# 🕯️ Brightvale | Emberdawn
### *Dark Fantasy & Post-Apocalyptic Lore Repository*

Welcome to the **Brightvale** lore repository, an expansive chronicling of a world choked by the [[Umbra]] and the remnants of the once-great Armisian Empire. This repository serves as the foundational knowledge graph for the Emberdawn setting, meticulously organized for use with [Obsidian](https://obsidian.md).

---

## 🌑 The Setting: A World in Eclipse
The world is defined by **The [[Apostasy]]**, a cataclysmic event that brought the crimson veil and the parasitic miasma known as the **[[Umbra]]**. Humanity and its allies now cling to the safety of **Brightvale**, protected by the holy fire of the **Beacon**.

### 🏰 Key Locations
- **[[Medsen]]**: The "City of the Beacon," bastion of civilization.
- **[[Emberfort]]**: The iron gates that guard the only pass into the valley.
- **[[Aeldenbarrow]]**: A hidden elven sanctuary tethered to ancient megaliths.

---

## ⚔️ Factions & Power Dynamics
The political landscape of Brightvale is a complex web of old blood and new industry:
- **The Council of Seven**: Modern governance struggling against ancient tradition.
- **The Noble Houses**: From the martial strength of **House Ravenne** to the arcane secrets of **House Aerincorvus**.
- **The Guilds**: Practical masters of the Vale, including the **[[Slayers]]**, the **[[Explorers' Guild]]**, and the **[[Emberguard]]**.
