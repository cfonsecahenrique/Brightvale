# [[Fae]]

The [[Fae]] are beings of the Otherworld — the Faewilds — whose fundamental nature sets them apart from every other creature the Enduring have encountered. They are **completely immune to the [[Umbra]]**. The shadow does not corrupt them, does not touch them, does not register their presence. In a world defined by the encroaching dark, the Fae exist outside its reach entirely.

This immunity is not understood. The [[Aetherweaver Academy]] has no theoretical framework for it. The [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretakers]] consider it theologically inconvenient. [[House Aerincorvus]], which has spent three generations documenting Fae-contact anomalies, considers it the single most strategically significant fact in the Vale — and the one most consistently ignored.

---

## The Light Fae

The Light Fae are beings of grief. They watch the mortal world wither and feel it — deeply, personally, in a way that resembles mourning but does not end. The forests they once walked are rotting. The glades they tended are shrinking. The peoples they once knew are huddled behind walls of stone and fire, slowly forgetting that anything else ever existed.

Most Light Fae have retreated entirely into the Faewilds. Those few who remain in the material world are profoundly rare and profoundly hidden — glimpsed behind waterfalls, reflected in old mirrors, felt as a presence in the deepest surviving groves where the canopy is still green. They do not seek contact. They endure their sadness in silence, watching from thresholds they no longer cross.

---

## The Dark Fae

The Dark Fae are something else entirely. Where the Light Fae grieve the world's decay, the Dark Fae **revel in it**. They are drawn to chaos, to entropy, to the unravelling of things. They are not corrupted by the [[Umbra]] — they cannot be — but they are deeply, consciously **connected** to it, and they are aware enough to manipulate it.

A Dark Fae does not spread the [[Umbra]]. It plays with it. It shapes it. It uses it as a tool, a weapon, a canvas. The distinction matters: the [[Umbra]] is mindless entropy; a Dark Fae working through the [[Umbra]] is entropy with intention, with cruelty, with a sense of humour.

Reports of Dark Fae activity in the Wastes are rare and difficult to verify — but the few credible accounts describe events that do not match standard [[Umbra]]-corruption patterns: mutations that are too precise, horrors that are too theatrical, destruction that seems designed to be witnessed.
