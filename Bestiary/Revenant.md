# Revenant

**Category:** Undead / Hollowed
**Habitat:** Ubiquitous across the Wastes

The **Revenant** is the most common and persistent threat faced by any Venturer stepping outside the walls of [[Medsen|Brightvale]]. They are the grim result of the [[Umbra]]’s final stage of corruption: the physical shell of a humanoid whose soul, memory, and personality have been entirely erased, replaced by the cold, animating will of the shadow.

## The Common Revenant
Standard Revenants are not the mindless, shambling undead of old folklore. They are terrifyingly capable. Driven by the hive-mind of the Umbra, they exhibit unnatural strength and cold, tactical cunning. They will flank, they will wait for a party's Light Bearer to exhaust their energy, and they will use weapons perfectly, though they have no memory of the hands that forged them. They feel no pain and do not retreat unless the Umbra hive-mind calculates a higher strategic value in doing so.

## The Arch-Revenant
A far more horrific variation exists, whispered about in the taverns of [[Emberfort]] and dreaded by every member of the Vanguard. These are the **Arch-Revenants**: mutated individuals lost to the Umbra—often former, highly skilled Venturers or the loved ones of those still living—who undergo a unique, localized mutation.

Unlike standard Revenants, an Arch-Revenant retains their mortal powers, their arcane or martial prowess, and a chillingly keen intellect. They are completely devoid of their original humanity and memories, operating entirely as generals for the Umbra. 

Arch-Revenants act as nodes, commanding localized hordes of beasts, standard Revenants, and Rotlords with terrifying military precision. Recent reports from the [[Explorers' Guild]] indicate that these Arch-Revenants are actively coordinating and pushing their armies slowly, inevitably, closer to Emberdawn.
