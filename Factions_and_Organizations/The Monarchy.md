# The Sun-Born Monarchy

The **Monarchy of Brightvale** is a bridge between the divine myths of the Dawnfather and the practical governance of the valley. Though its power has diminished significantly over the centuries, it remains a potent symbol of [[Humankind|human]] hope and the Armisian legacy.

### The Divine Lineage
The royal family claims to be the **Sun-Born** — direct descendants of the **Dawnfather**. Their legitimacy is historically rooted in their potent command over holy radiance, which they point to as proof of their celestial bloodline. For centuries, the Sun-Born kings ruled with absolute authority, their word as law as long as the Beacon stayed lit.

### The Scholastic Skepticism
The **Scholars’ Guild** (centered at the **[[Aetherweaver Academy]]**) remains privately skeptical of the "divine blood" claims. They argue that the Beacon’s constant radiance does not merely protect the city but *alters* it over generations. In their view, children born in the shadow of the Central Temple-Castle may simply be infused with its resonance from birth, making "divine lineage" a convenient political fiction that grants [[the monarchy]] power over the superstitious.

### The Decline of the Crown
The absolute power of [[the monarchy]] came to an end following a series of failed despots. Mismanagement of the valley's resources and ill-advised military ventures into the Wastes led to the **Great Famines**. To prevent a total collapse of social order, the **Council of Seven** was empowered to check the King's authority. Today, the King retains a seat on the Council, but his role is largely symbolic — a figurehead maintained to honor the city’s foundational myths.

### The Secluded Sovereign: [[King Alfwyn]]
The current ruler, **[[King Alfwyn]]**, has become a mystery to his own people. He has not Been seen in public for nearly seven years, having retreated into the labyrinthine halls of the **Central Temple-Castle**. 

Rumors abound regarding his seclusion:
*   **The Withered Light:** Some say the King is ill, his body literally withering away from the very light he claims to represent.
*   **The Terrible Truth:** Others whisper that he discovered a terrifying truth during a secret ritual within the Beacon's heart — a truth so profound that he can no longer look his people in the eye.

### The High Steward
In the King's absence, the **High Steward** manages the daily bureaucracy of the Crown. The Steward occupies the King's seat on the Council and acts as the sole voice of the sovereign. While the common folk continue to pray to the throne, many feel that [[the monarchy]] has become a "beautiful tomb" at the heart of the city.
