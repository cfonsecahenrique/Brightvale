# [[Order in the Vale]]

Brightvale is governed by **The Council of Five**, a body meant to balance the interests of the valley's major powers. However, the daily reality is a complex tug-of-war between the **Guilds**, the **Nobility**, and a dwindling **Monarchy**.

### The Council of Five
The Council serves as the supreme legislative and executive body. Its members typically include:
1.  **The King (or the High Steward)**: Representing the Sun-Born legacy.
2.  **The Arch-Aetherweaver**: Representing the [[Aetherweaver Academy]].
3.  **The Lord Commander**: Representing the [[Emberguard]] and city defense.
4.  **A Representative of the Noble Houses**: Currently a rotating seat among the Old Houses.
5.  **A Representative of the Guilds/Commerce**: Often the Grand Explorer or a leader of the Caretakers.


### The Armisian Legacy & Noble Houses

Humanity has struggled to retain the hierarchy of the ancient, forgotten **Armisian Empire**. Social status in [[Humankind|human]] settlements is defined by **Noble Houses**, divided into *Ancient* (pre-[[Apostasy]] lineages) and *New* (those who rose during the transition to the Vale).

The Great Houses maintain a **feudalistic system**, operating out of palazzos in [[Medsen]] while holding distributed rights over surrounding lands, forts, and fiefs. While these houses frequently engage in political maneuvering and internal feuds, they are often overshadowed by the **Guilds**, who hold the ultimate authority over the valley's resources and survival.
