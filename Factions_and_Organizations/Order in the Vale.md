# [[Order in the Vale]]

Brightvale is governed by **The Council of Seven**, a body meant to balance the interests of the valley's major powers. However, the daily reality is a complex tug-of-war between the **Guilds**, the **Nobility**, and a dwindling **Monarchy**.

### The Council of Seven
The Council serves as the supreme legislative and executive body of Brightvale. Its members consist of five permanent leaders from the valley's core institutions (High Caretaker, Lord Commander of the Emberguard, Arch Aetherweaver, First Slayer, and Grand Explorer) and two rotating seats reserved for the Noble Houses. For a detailed breakdown of its structure, elections, and political workings, see [[The Council of Seven]].


### The Armisian Legacy & Noble Houses

Humanity has struggled to retain the hierarchy of the ancient, forgotten **Armisian Empire**. Social status in [[Humankind|human]] settlements is defined by **Noble Houses**, divided into *Ancient* (pre-[[Apostasy]] lineages) and *New* (those who rose during the transition to the Vale).

The Great Houses maintain a **feudalistic system**, operating out of palazzos in [[Medsen]] while holding distributed rights over surrounding lands, forts, and fiefs. While these houses frequently engage in political maneuvering and internal feuds, they are often overshadowed by the **Guilds**, who hold the ultimate authority over the valley's resources and survival.
