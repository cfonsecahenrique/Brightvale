# House Drevorn (Placeholder Name)

**Motto:** *"What is held cannot be taken."*
**Status:** Recent House (Post-Apostasy)
**Seat:** The Ironweight Vault (Medsen)
**Heraldry:** A closed fist gripping a set of scales on a field of deep burgundy and grey.

**Overview:** 
The widely resented Drevorns achieved nobility not by blood or valor, but by outperforming the old elite in logistics during the initial Apostasy crisis. Evolving from mere merchants storing grain during the collapse, they now control Brightvale’s critical physical assets, trade routes, and a towering ledger of noble debt. They treat nobility as a business, viewing information and obligation as strict inventory.

**Strife & Ambition:** 
Despised by Old Houses for supposedly "buying" their status, Drevorn wields cold debt to neutralize social scorn. The ruthless current head, Corvin Drevorn, holds one intense ambition: securing a Drevorn appointment on the hallowed Council of Seven within his lifetime by leveraging twenty years of accumulated favors. 

**Key Relationships:**
- **Old Houses:** Widespread contempt from established lineages (Von Zoltraak, Iyengar), which Drevorn manages via financial blackmail.
- **Emberguard:** Purely cooperative supply relationship, though Commander Varis keeps it strictly professional. 
- **Explorers' Guild:** Co-financiers of expeditions in exchange for priority rights on recovered Waste goods.
- **House Ravenna:** A bitter cold war as both factions monopolize information and access, resulting in deep personal rivalries among heirs.

**Story Hooks:**
- A Venturer discovers their lineage owes an enormous inherited debt to the Drevorns through a middleman.
- The Ironweight Vault holds a catastrophic pre-Apostasy artifact recovered from the Wastes—and an external force wants it back.
- Corvin Drevorn hires Venturers to recover a stolen ledger holding the names and blackmails of half the Vale's nobility, avoiding an explosion of the Council's power balance.
