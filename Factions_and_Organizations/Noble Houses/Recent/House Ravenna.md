# House Ravenna

**Motto:** *"Every road leads somewhere. We know where."*
**Status:** Recent House (Post-Apostasy)
**Seat:** The Wayhouse — a deceptively modest building near [[Medsen]]'s courier district, indistinguishable from the surrounding offices except for the constant, quiet traffic of messengers and relay riders entering and leaving at all hours.
**Heraldry:** A crow in flight over a forked road on a field of charcoal and slate blue.

---

## Origins

[[House Ravenna]] was founded by **Ilara Ravenna**, a common-born scout who, in the first desperate years after the [[Apostasy]], single-handedly mapped the first stable routes through the Teardrop Mountains — routes that allowed the initial wave of survivors to reach Brightvale before the [[Umbra]]'s spread cut them off. Ilara was not noble, was not educated, and had no interest in titles. She was given her patent of nobility over her protests by the first provisional Council.

What Ilara understood that the founding Council did not was that the routes were only partly valuable. The *knowledge of the routes* — who used them, when, carrying what, and why — was where the power actually lived. She built a courier network originally intended for relief coordination, and never dismantled it once the emergency passed.

---

## Character & Culture

[[House Ravenna]] trades in **movement and information**. They operate the Vale's most comprehensive courier service — fast, reliable, and, for an additional fee, completely silent. Their riders know the mountain passes better than any other faction. Their clerks know more about who is communicating with whom than any other faction. They do not sell this information casually, but they retain it, and everyone who uses their service knows that the Ravenna knows.

This has a remarkable effect on behavior. People are more honest through Ravenna couriers than they are through any other channel, because lies require consistency and the Ravenna have a way of noticing inconsistencies. The house does not blackmail. They do not need to. The awareness that they *could* is sufficient.

The house is currently led by its eighth-generation scion, and leadership is always determined internally through a private evaluation process. Outside heirs are occasionally adopted into the family if they demonstrate the right combination of discretion, memory, and route-sense. This happens more often than the nobility is comfortable with.

## Strife Theme & Ambition

**Strife:** As the Vale's communication infrastructure, the Ravenna occupy a position that everyone depends on and no one trusts entirely. Every faction that uses their services wonders what information is leaking sideways. Every faction that attempts to build a parallel courier network finds it mysteriously inefficient. The house's cold war with [[House Drevorn]] is especially active: both operate in the space between information and access, and the current generation of scions from each house apparently have a personal dimension to the rivalry as well.

**Ambition:** To formally position the Ravenna's information network as a **Council intelligence service** — giving them official mandate to gather and analyze communications, in exchange for guaranteed state funding and legal protection. This would transform them from a private operation into an institution.

## Relationships

- **[[House Drevorn]] (Active Cold War):** Both houses operate in related domains — information and access — and actively work to undercut each other's networks while maintaining surface civility. The current scion rivalry is personal.
- **[[House Iyengar]] (Competitive Overlap):** The Iyengar trade in relationships; the Ravenna trade in information. The domains overlap constantly and the boundary is negotiated case by case.
- **[[Explorers' Guild]] (Operational Partnership):** Ravenna riders carry [[Explorers' Guild|Explorer]]s' dispatches, maps, and findings. Grand Explorer [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna|Mira]] has a closer relationship with the house than her professional role would strictly require — her surname is not coincidental.
- **[[Emberguard]] (Contracted Service):** The Guard uses Ravenna couriers for inter-settlement communication. Lord Commander [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner|Varis]] considers this a necessary dependency he intends to eventually reduce.

## Story Hooks

- A sealed Ravenna courier package addressed to an unknown recipient in [[Medsen]] has gone undelivered for six months because its intended pickup was never made. The package has been sitting in the Wayhouse. Someone just put a very large sum of money on the table for it, without explanation.
- Grand Explorer [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna|Mira Ravenna]]'s surname is Ravenna — but the house has never publicly acknowledged the connection, and Mira has never claimed it. Someone has obtained documentation suggesting the connection is direct, not coincidental, and that the house has been quietly influencing which Waste routes get explored and which do not.
- A Ravenna courier has gone missing on a routine inter-settlement run — the third in two months on the same route. The house is investigating privately. The route passes near the collapsed ruin where the Von Zoltraak clockwork sphere was reportedly recovered.
