# House Ashwood

**Motto:** *"The grove outlasts the storm."*
**Status:** Recent House (Post-Apostasy)
**Seat:** The Greenmantle Lodge — a sprawling, deliberately organic structure built into a living grove on the northern edge of the plateau, its walls overgrown with carefully cultivated ivy. Not a fortress. Conspicuously not a fortress.
**Heraldry:** A great oak with roots extending across the lower half of the crest on a field of deep forest green and earthy brown.

---

## Origins

[[House Ashwood]] began as a clan of common foresters — not nobles, not soldiers, barely citizens in the Armisian sense. When the [[Apostasy]] began transforming the forests around the plateau into [[Umbra]]-corrupted Warp-Groves, the Ashwood clan organized their neighbors, armed them with what tools they had, and defended a section of healthy woodland from the corruption for weeks while the early survivors were still establishing the initial settlement.

They did not win the engagement through superior training or magical ability. They won because they knew **exactly how the forest worked** — where the canopy caught the wind, where the ground drank the most light, where the undergrowth was too thick for the corrupted creatures to move quickly. Local knowledge, applied methodically, against enemies that didn't have it.

The Vale's provisional Council granted them a noble patent not for heroism, but because they controlled the only remaining healthy timber supply in the region, and the Council needed wood.

---

## Character & Culture

[[House Ashwood]] has evolved into the Vale's primary advocates for **sustainable ecology and wild-space preservation**. This puts them in recurring conflict with nearly every other faction that needs raw resources. They are not sentimental about it — they understand that the Vale needs timber, needs farmland, needs cleared ground — but they insist on operating within a framework that preserves enough functional ecosystem to maintain the plateau's long-term viability.

They are the primary suppliers of rare woods to the [[Aetherweaver Academy]], which requires specific timber species for the production of Lumen-infused materials. The Ashwood control which trees are harvested and in what sequence, giving them a quiet but consistent leverage over Academy supply chains.

The house is also deeply connected to the druidic traditions that persist in the Vale's wild spaces — not through elven heritage (they are predominantly human), but through generations of practical engagement with the same druids who maintain the hidden glades. They are not members of the druidic circles, but they are respected by them, which is a different thing entirely.

---

## Strife Theme & Ambition

**Strife:** [[House Ashwood]] is perpetually mediating between the Vale's resource needs and its ecological limits, in a society that increasingly does not want to hear about ecological limits. The house is accused of obstruction by the [[House Thalum|Thalum]], of sentimentality by the [[House Drevorn|Drevorn]], and of insufficient commitment by the [[House Aerincorvus|Aerincorvus]] elven faction. They are too pragmatic for idealists and too conservationist for pragmatists.

**Ambition:** To establish a formal **Forested Reserve** — a section of the plateau's remaining healthy woodland protected by Council charter from any logging or development, intended to serve as a permanent seed-source and ecosystem anchor. This is viewed by the resource-extraction houses as either environmentalism or land-grabbing, depending on the day.

---

## Relationships

- **[[House Thalum]] (Resource Conflict):** The Thalum consider the Ashwood's protected-forest policies an inexcusable obstruction of legitimate quarrying and terrain-clearing operations. The tension is structural and ongoing.
- **[[Aetherweaver Academy]] (Controlled Supply):** The Ashwood's hold on rare timber gives them Academy leverage. The Academy's progressive magic faction treats them more warmly than the Old Magic sect, which considers the supply relationship a Lugner-era arrangement they inherited.
- **[[House Aerincorvus]] (Sympathetic Alliance):** Both houses value the wild spaces and the druidic traditions. Their alliance is genuine but imperfect — the Ashwood's pragmatic resource-management philosophy sometimes clashes with the Aerincorvus's more absolute commitment to the Faewilds.
- **[[Explorers' Guild]] (Ecological Consultants):** The Ashwood maintain a liaison with the Explorers, providing botanical and ecological briefings before Wastes expeditions. Knowing which corrupted plants are recoverable and which are lost saves lives.

## Story Hooks

- A section of Ashwood-protected grove has developed a perfect circle of dead trees at its center — no Umbra corruption detected, no signs of disease. The Ashwood arborist who discovered it says the trees didn't die. They *emptied*, as if something consumed the cellular structure and left the form behind. The protected reserve is now generating a faint resonance that [[House Kaewdyn|Kaewdyn]]-sensitive scouts are picking up from three kilometers away.
- A lumber merchant with Thalum backing has obtained a legal dispensation from a minor Council official to harvest timber in an area adjacent to the Ashwood protected reserve. The paperwork is technically legitimate. The Ashwood suspect the dispensation was purchased and that the actual target is not the adjacent land.
- A druidic elder from the hidden glades has asked the Ashwood for something unprecedented: a formal introduction to the [[Order in the Vale|Council of Five]]. The druidic circles have been entirely non-political for generations. Whatever has prompted this request is clearly significant, and the elder has not explained it to the Ashwood yet.
