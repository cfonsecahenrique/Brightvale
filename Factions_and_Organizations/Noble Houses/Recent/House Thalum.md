# House Thalum (Placeholder Name)

**Motto:** *"The mountain holds. So shall we."*
**Status:** Old Armisian House (pre-Apostasy foundations, elevated post-Apostasy)
**Seat:** The Marrow Hall (Medsen's lower plateau)
**Heraldry:** A pickaxe and a mason's level crossed on a field of slate grey and iron brown.

**Overview:** 
Boasting lineage from the primal Armisian engineering corps, House Thalum essentially built Brightvale before the Apostasy even struck. Hyper-practical, unimaginative, and stubbornly reliant on pure physics over magic (which they meticulously log as unreliable), they maintain the Vale's walls, mines, and infrastructure. They claim ownership over the plateau's bedrock by right of first survey, grounding themselves as the unsentimental architects of survival.

**Strife & Ambition:** 
Their massive frustration originates from clueless politicians deferring crucial maintenance, a catastrophe they witness coming but cannot stop. Thalum’s ultimate ambition is breaking the Dwarven quarry hold on the eastern seams, aiming to monopolize the entire Vale's mineral extraction and construction resources.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Dwarvenkind of Hvergi:** An entrenched, formal rivalry over mining rights and conflicting technical philosophy, leading to endless administrative sabotage.
- **House Ashwood:** Controlled contempt. Thalum sees forests strictly as raw timber, while Ashwood protects them ecologically.
- **Emberguard:** Highly practical, transactional relationship to maintain Emberfort and valley defenses.
- **House Drevorn:** Wary, mandatory cooperation intertwining physical and financial infrastructures.

**Story Hooks:**
- Thalum engineers desperately sound the alarm on a fatal structural flaw in Emberfort’s eastern wall, ignored four times by politicians.
- Workers drilling deep in the Thalum quarry seams breach an undocumented, pre-Armisian labyrinth that radiates inexplicable heat.
- Rival Hvergi dwarves abruptly stop submitting mineral yields; Thalum suspects they hit an unearthed Cataclysm secret rather than a bureaucratic glitch.
