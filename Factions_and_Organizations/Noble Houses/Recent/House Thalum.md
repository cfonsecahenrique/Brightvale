# House Thalum

**Motto:** *"The mountain holds. So shall we."*
**Status:** Old Armisian House (pre-Apostasy foundations, elevated post-Apostasy)
**Seat:** The Marrow Hall — a building that is essentially a fortress dressed as a manor, embedded into the cliff-face of [[Medsen]]'s lower plateau. Its foundations extend deep into the bedrock, and its lower levels are as much mine as building.
**Heraldry:** A pickaxe and a mason's level crossed on a field of slate grey and iron brown.

---

## Origins

[[House Thalum]] — the **Mountain-Marrow** — traces its ancestry to the Armisian Empire's engineering corps: the surveyors, miners, and fortress architects who built the infrastructure of civilization. Where other houses trace lineages to kings or generals, the Thalum trace theirs to the first foreman who drove the first stake into the bedrock of what would become Brightvale. **They claim the ground itself is theirs by right of first survey**, a claim other houses find obnoxious and which the Thalum have produced extensive documentation to support.

When the [[Apostasy]] hit, the Thalum were already positioned in Brightvale because they had been contracted to extend the valley's fortifications. They didn't escape to safety. They were already there to build it.

---

## Character & Culture

[[House Thalum]] is *structural* in its values. They are direct, methodical, and deeply suspicious of anything that cannot be built, tested, or weighed. Abstract theorizing bores them. What interests them is load-bearing capacity, resource yields, and construction timelines. They are responsible for the maintenance of most of Brightvale's physical infrastructure — walls, aqueducts, bridges, mine shafts — and they take this responsibility with the deadly seriousness of people who know exactly what happens when stonework fails.

They have a **cold and professional rivalry with the [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarves]] of [[Hvergi]]** over quarrying and mining rights along the plateau's eastern face. The Thalum view the Dwarves as exceptional craftsmen whose preference for working their own seams represents a significant inefficiency and an unnecessary territorial complication. The Dwarves view the Thalum as competent enough but insufficiently respectful of the stone. Both sides respect the other's technical capability while disputing each other's claims.

The house dislikes magic on principled grounds — not religious, but practical. Magic fails unpredictably. Stone, properly cut and correctly placed, does not. They will use [[Aetherweaver Academy|Aetherweaver]] reinforcement on critical structures because they are not foolish, but they will document every applied enchantment, its failure rate, and its maintenance schedule with obsessive precision.

## Strife Theme & Ambition

**Strife:** The Thalum increasingly find that the Vale's political and resource allocation decisions are made by people who do not understand infrastructure at all — and that the inevitable consequences of deferred maintenance are blamed on the Thalum for not speaking up loudly enough in advance. They have spoken up. They have submitted reports. Nobody reads the reports.

**Ambition:** To break the Dwarves' control over the eastern quarry seams and establish a unified mineral extraction authority under Thalum supervision — which would give them effective control over the raw material inputs for every construction project and weapon produced in the Vale.

## Relationships

- **[[Dwarvenkind]] of [[Hvergi]] (Formal Rivals):** Competing resource claims, competing technical philosophies, and a rigid mutual disrespect maintained across generations. There is no active conflict, but any conversation between a Thalum overseer and a Hvergi mining clan elder will end with someone submitting a formal complaint.
- **[[House Ashwood]] (Controlled Contempt):** The Thalum consider the Ashwood's forest-protection to be an inexcusable waste of perfectly good timber and accessible terrain. The Ashwood consider the Thalum's quarrying habits an inexcusable disregard for living systems.
- **[[Emberguard]] (Practical Partnership):** The Emberguard relies on the Thalum for fortress maintenance at [[Emberfort]] and the Valley's wall network. The relationship is functional and entirely transactional.
- **[[House Drevorn]] (Wary Cooperation):** The Drevorn's financial infrastructure and the Thalum's physical infrastructure are interdependent. They cooperate where necessary and argue about everything else.

## Story Hooks

- The Thalum's engineering surveys have identified a structural weakness in the eastern wall of [[Emberfort]] that, if a specific type of Umbra-creature were to strike it at the right angle, would cause catastrophic collapse. They have submitted this report four times. It has been deprioritized four times. They are now considering whether to go public.
- Deep in the Thalum quarry seams, workers have broken through to a pre-existing tunnel system that does not match any known Armisian construction record. It goes down further than the surveys reach, and the air inside is warm when it should be cold.
- The Dwarves of Hvergi have stopped sending their weekly resource reports to the Vale's central ledger — quietly, without announcement, for the past three months. When a Thalum overseer investigated, the Dwarf foreman claimed administrative error. The Thalum suspect the Dwarves have found something significant and are not reporting it.
