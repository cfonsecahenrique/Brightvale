# House Aurelian

**Motto:** *"We remember the sky."*
**Status:** Old Armisian House
**Seat:** The Solarium — a grand, glass-roofed estate in the hills above [[Medsen]], oriented to catch every hour of weak sunlight. Its greenhouses grow crops that were nearly extinct, cultivated from seeds preserved across generations.
**Heraldry:** A radiant sun half-submerged below a horizon line on a field of pale gold and faded white.

---

## Origins

Before the [[Apostasy]], House Aurelian was the Armisian Empire's agricultural nobility — overseers of vast, sunlit wheat fields, granaries, and harvest festivals. They worshipped the sun not out of religious zeal but practical reverence: the sun was the source of all yield, all plenty, all life. When the [[Apostasy]] dimmed the sky and choked off the light, the Aurelians lost more than land. They lost their reason for being.

They have spent every generation since trying to get it back.

---

## Character & Culture

[[House Aurelian]] is defined by **grief dressed as optimism**. They speak of the Old Sun frequently — not as mythology, but as a lived memory passed down through family testimony and preserved agricultural records. The house maintains the Vale's most extensive seed vault and experimental greenhouse network, stubbornly cultivating crops that have no business surviving in the grey half-light of the post-Apostasy world. Some of these efforts fail. Some, through extraordinary care and aetheric supplementation, succeed.

Their obsession with the [[Aetherweaver Academy]]'s astronomical studies stems from a single hope: that the Auroras, those shimmering violet manifestations of the Weave seen from the Tower at [[Aurore]]'s Peak, might be connected to the original stellar light of the world before the catastrophe. If a pattern can be found, they believe, perhaps the sky can be restored.

This makes them beloved by common folk — who appreciate their investment in food production — and quietly viewed as romantics by the other Old Houses. The Aurelians do not mind the condescension. They are used to it.

---

## Strife Theme & Ambition

**Strife:** The Aurelians are in constant conflict with the reality that their core belief — that the sky can be restored — has no supporting evidence. They fund research others consider quixotic, maintain traditions others find nostalgic, and refuse to accept a permanent dimness as the new normal. This makes them vulnerable to exploitation by factions willing to tell them what they want to hear.

**Ambition:** To discover the cause of the sky's dimming as a separate research question from the [[Apostasy]] broadly, and to fund a project capable of creating a permanent agricultural ecosystem independent of the failing natural sunlight — a "Second Harvest" that does not require the Old Sun.

---

## Relationships

- **[[Aetherweaver Academy]] (Primary Funders of Astronomy):** The Aurelians jointly fund the Tower observatory with [[House Lugner]], but for entirely different reasons. The astronomers try to manage both patrons' expectations simultaneously.
- **[[House Lugner]] (Suspicious Alliance):** They share archival interest in the past but interpret it differently. The Aurelians believe the Old Magic might restore the sun; the Lugner believe that's an emotionally convenient misreading of the evidence.
- **[[Caretakers of the Beacon]] (Warm Relations):** Both houses find meaning in the idea of sacred, sustaining light. The Aurelians donate food stores to the Caretakers' charitable works regularly.
- **[[House Drevorn]] (Commercial Tension):** The Drevorn control grain distribution. The Aurelians grow the grain. The exact terms of this arrangement are renegotiated frequently and bitterly.

## Story Hooks

- An Aurelian greenhouse arcanist has successfully grown a variety of wheat that **glows faintly at night** — apparently photosynthesizing from the Beacon's light directly. The implications — for food production, for agriculture beyond Brightvale, for the Beacon's true nature — are enormous. The house is debating whether to publish the discovery or keep it proprietary.
- The seed vault beneath the Solarium contains a variety of crop extinct for two hundred years, preserved in a stasis chamber of unknown Armisian manufacture. The stasis effect is failing. They need either an arcanist who understands the old mechanism, or a way to germinate the seeds before they die permanently.
- A Von Zoltraak scholar has contacted the Aurelians privately with a claim: Armisian astronomical records suggest the sky's dimming was a gradual process that began *before* the [[Apostasy]] by roughly a century. If true, the Apostasy may be a symptom, not a cause. The Aurelians are not sure they want this to be true.
