# House Aerincorvus

**Motto:** *"Between two worlds, we are the door."*
**Status:** Old Armisian House (pre-Apostasy; of mixed Elven-Human lineage)
**Seat:** The Overgrown Palazzo (Medsen)
**Heraldry:** A crow perched on a branch of silver ivy on a field of deep forest black and iridescent green.

**Overview:** 
The Vale's only noble house of established Elven-Human heritage, Aerincorvus balances urban politics and druidic connections. Believing the Faewilds were sealed rather than destroyed during the Apostasy, they quietly operate the **Concordance**—a massive archive documenting Fae-contact anomalies to find patterns and potentially re-open the boundary.

**Strife & Ambition:** 
Their careful documentation of Fae phenomena is largely dismissed as folklore by the rest of the Vale. Their ambition is to compile irrefutable evidence of Faewilds contact to force the Council and Academy to treat it as a legitimate research priority, potentially seeking Fae allies untouched by the Umbra.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Druidic Communities:** Genuine two-way alliance and primary source of anomalies.
- **House Ashwood:** Close allies, uniting to protect and study wild spaces.
- **Aetherweaver Academy:** Friction over the Academy's skeptical dismissal of their anomaly research.
- **House Lugner:** Wary distance due to Lugner's selective view on Fae involvement in deep-weave traditions.

**Story Hooks:**
- A recent subject experiencing anomalous resonance contact is displaying a fading "Fae marker"—they don't know they have it.
- An investigator researching a glade where the "air tasted wrong" has mysteriously vanished.
- A progressive Von Zoltraak scholar believes his new aetherform matches Fae structural resonance and wants an uneasy collaboration.
