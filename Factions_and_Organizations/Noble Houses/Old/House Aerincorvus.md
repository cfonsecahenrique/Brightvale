# House Aerincorvus

**Motto:** *"Between two worlds, we are the door."*
**Status:** Old Armisian House (pre-Apostasy; of mixed Elven-Human lineage)
**Seat:** The Overgrown Palazzo — a grand but quietly crumbling building in [[Medsen]]'s upper tier, its walls consumed by magically sustained ivy that blooms in defiance of the grey sky. Inside, entire wings have been surrendered to climbing plants and nesting birds. The wing that functions as the house's working archive — the **Concordance** — is scrupulously maintained: shelved testimonies, indexed field reports, and annotated interview records line every wall.
**Heraldry:** A crow perched on a branch of silver ivy on a field of deep forest black and iridescent green.

---

## Origins

[[House Aerincorvus]] is the Vale's only noble house of established Elven-Human heritage — a lineage tracing back to a diplomatic marriage in the waning years of the Armisian Empire. The union was political; what it produced, across generations, was a house with one foot permanently in the urban political world and the other in the wild spaces, the druidic circles, and the quieter, stranger traditions that neither fully mapped onto Armisian governance.

Before the [[Apostasy]], this dual position gave them relationships that most of the imperial court could not form and did not value. After the [[Apostasy]], those same relationships gave them access to information that no other noble house was collecting: the testimony of people who had *seen* something, in the woods, in the glades, at the edge of the world's remaining wild places — something that did not match the established category of [[Umbra]]-corruption or natural phenomenon.

---

## The Faewilds: What Was Lost

The connection between the mortal world and the Faewilds was severed — or more precisely, *collapsed* — at some point contemporaneous with the [[Apostasy]]. This is not disputed. The [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elven]] communities who remember their relationship with the Fae most clearly describe the change as absolute and instantaneous: one day the boundary was permeable, humming with the faint resonance of the Other Side; the next, it was simply *gone*. Not thickened. Not obscured. Absent.

The [[Fae]] did not respond to this severance in any documented way, because access to the Fae — to know whether they still exist, whether they are aware of what happened, whether they are similarly confined on their side — is precisely what was lost.

## The Concordance

What [[House Aerincorvus]] has built in the decades since the [[Apostasy]] is not a magical solution or a diplomatic framework. It is a **case archive**.

They have maintained systematic contact with the druidic communities of the Vale's remaining healthy wild spaces and with the scattered [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elven]] settlements in the hidden glades, with a specific and narrow purpose: to collect, document, and analyze every reported instance in which someone claims to have had anomalous contact with the Faewilds — entering a space that behaves like the Fae's domain, perceiving an entity that matches the historical description of a Fae being, receiving communication that appears to originate from the Other Side.

These accounts are rare. Most are single incidents, experienced by individuals who have difficulty describing them precisely and who often retract or qualify their testimony when pressed. Some are clearly misidentifications of natural phenomena or [[Umbra]]-adjacent hallucinations. But some are not. And the ones that are not share structural consistencies that the house's archivists have been cataloguing for three generations.

Most of the Vale's established institutions treat these reports as folklore. The [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretakers]] consider Fae-contact accounts theologically irrelevant. The [[Aetherweaver Academy]] classifies them as anomalous resonance events lacking a mechanism. [[House Aerincorvus]] considers these responses to be the exact kind of institutional incuriosity that allows important patterns to go unrecognized.

The investigators who do this work — small teams operating quietly out of the Concordance, often traveling to druidic settlements for months at a time — do not describe themselves as believers in fairy tales. They describe themselves as people who know how to read a pattern when it appears in the data.

## The Working Hypothesis

The Aerincorvus position, held by the house's senior archivists and shared with only a small number of trusted outside scholars, is this:

The connection to the Faewilds was not destroyed by the [[Apostasy]]. It was **closed** — from one side or both — in response to it. And if it was closed, it can potentially be re-opened.

More significantly: if the Fae still exist on the Other Side, and if they were also affected by whatever caused the [[Apostasy]], then the Fae and the Enduring may share an interest in resolving it. The house does not romanticize this. They are not certain the Fae are benevolent, or that a restored connection would be safe, or that the Fae would be inclined to help even if asked. They are certain that an ally whose domain exists outside the [[Umbra]]'s current reach would be strategically significant.

This hypothesis has no official standing. The house pursues it without institutional sanction and without advertising it to the [[Order in the Vale|Council of Five]].

## Strife Theme & Ambition

**Strife:** The house occupies the uncomfortable position of taking seriously the thing that serious institutions dismiss. Their investigation is methodologically careful and their evidence is real; their subject matter is, to most of the Vale's power structures, the domain of credulous druids and superstitious villagers. The investigators within the Concordance are occasionally excellent scholars who quietly find the house's work more rigorous than what the Academy offers — and who cannot explain this to their colleagues without damaging their reputations.

**Ambition:** To accumulate sufficient documented evidence of Faewilds contact to force the [[Aetherweaver Academy]] and the [[Order in the Vale|Council]] to treat the question as a legitimate research priority — and, if possible, to generate a controlled re-opening of a Faewilds boundary point under conditions that can be studied rather than accidentally stumbled into.

## Relationships

- **[[Elves (Faefolk)|Druidic Communities]] (Primary Sources):** The house's relationships with the druidic glades and Elven settlements are long-standing, respectful, and genuinely two-directional. The druids bring testimonies to the house; the house shares its analysis with the druids. Neither is entirely comfortable with what that analysis currently suggests.
- **[[House Ashwood]] (Close Alliance):** The most natural alignment in noble society. The Ashwood protect the wild spaces; the Aerincorvus document what happens in them. They share findings regularly and operate as a quiet informal alliance on Council matters.
- **[[Aetherweaver Academy]] (Frustrated Distance):** The Academy's skepticism of Fae-contact reports is a source of genuine friction. Several individual Academy scholars, however, have made quiet contact with the Concordance on their own time.
- **[[House Lugner]] (Wary Distance):** The Lugner's Old Magic research has occasionally brushed against Aerincorvus territory — certain ancient Armisian inscriptions suggest the Fae were involved in the deep-weave tradition in ways the Lugner find inconvenient. The Aerincorvus find the Lugner's selective reading of this evidence unsurprising.

## Story Hooks

- The Concordance has identified three Faewilds-contact reports from the past two years that share a specific anomaly: in each case, the person who experienced the contact was later found to have a faint, non-Umbra resonance signature that fades over approximately six weeks. The house's archivist believes this is a marker — something the Fae are *doing deliberately*. She wants to find the marker before it fades in the most recent documented case. The person it's happening to does not yet know they're being looked at.
- An Aerincorvus investigator who has spent eight months in a druidic glade community has gone three weeks without sending her regular reports. The last report she sent described a location — about four hours' walk from the glade's nearest boundary — where the air "tasted wrong and the ground didn't answer." She was going to investigate. No one in the glade has seen her since.
- A member of the [[Aetherweaver Academy]]'s progressive faction ([[House Von Zoltraak|Von Zoltraak]]-affiliated) has approached the Concordance with a proposal: he believes one of the new progressive aetherforms he has been developing resonates at a frequency that matches structural elements in three of the Aerincorvus's documented contact reports. He wants to collaborate. The house is interested and suspicious in roughly equal measure.
