# House Volex

**Status:** Extinct — Armisian High Nobility
**Heraldry:** A dragon rampant with wings spread on a field of crimson and black — the oldest surviving heraldic image in the Vale's records.

---

[[House Volex]] was the preeminent **dragon-riding house** of the Armisian Empire, and by most historical analyses, its most militarily powerful. Their bond with their mounts was not ceremonial. It was biological — a deep attunement developed over generations that made Volex riders and their dragons effectively dual entities in combat.

They were the first noble house to collapse in the [[Apostasy]] — not to the [[Umbra]], but to the catastrophe that befell the dragons themselves. The nature of the draconic affliction is not fully documented, but the dominant account in the [[House Von Zoltraak|Von Zoltraak]] archive describes a progressive breakdown of the bond: dragons becoming erratic, then aggressive, then finally consumed by a transformation that the Umbra later completed into the corrupted mutations that now haunt the Wastes.

The Volex riders did not survive this. Those recorded in the final year of the house died with their dragons — some by choice, some as casualties of the animals' transformation.

---

## Legacy

[[House Volex]] is remembered with more complexity than most extinct houses. The common memory tends toward tragedy and grandeur: the last of the great riders, the end of an age. The academic memory — particularly among Von Zoltraak scholars — notes that the Volex were the empire's greatest military asset and that their loss in the opening stage of the [[Apostasy]] may have been a decisive factor in the civilization's inability to organize a coherent defense.

A more uncomfortable question, raised periodically and never cleanly resolved, is **whether the dragons' affliction was the Apostasy's first symptom or one of its causes**.

The [[Aetherweaver Academy]] reportedly holds the last confirmed surviving dragon eggs in a secured vault below the [[Aurore|Aurore Peak]] campus — a custody they assumed after the Volex line collapsed. Whether they have attempted to hatch them, what condition the eggs are in, and what a hatched dragon might become in a world now saturated with [[Umbra]] resonance are questions the Academy has not published answers to.
