# House Rjioha

**Status:** Extinct — Armisian High Nobility
**Heraldry:** A ship's prow cresting a wave, with a star compass above — field of deep sea-blue and silver.

---

[[House Rjioha]] was one of the most influential houses of the Armisian Empire, and the one most responsible for the empire's expansion beyond its continental borders. The Rjiohans were a seafaring people — amphibious in their ambitions, convinced that the empire's future lay not in consolidation but in contact. They advocated for trade and diplomatic engagement with distant cultures, including the [[Dwarvenkind]] mariners who arrived at the empire's shores from across unknown seas.

They funded and organized the empire's most ambitious exploratory voyages, returning with knowledge, technologies, and relationships that no other house understood the value of. Much of this accumulated knowledge — entire libraries of sea charts, cultural documentation, and diplomatic agreements with civilizations that may no longer exist — is now lost. The Rjioha were caught outside the Vale when the [[Apostasy]] fell, their fleet still at sea or in foreign ports. No Rjiohan ship returned.

Their extinct lineage survives only in the draconic blood that passed through an ancestral marriage union into [[House Kaewdyn]] — a genetic legacy that the Kaewdyn have found far more useful in the Wastes than any of the Rjioha's diplomatic achievements.

The [[Explorers' Guild]] considers [[House Rjioha]] a patron saint of sorts — explorers who vanished before they could return with what they found. A small shrine to the Rjioha hangs in the Explorers' Guild hall at [[Emberfort]].

---

## What Was Lost

The most significant thing lost with House Rjioha is **knowledge of the world beyond the Vale**. The Rjioha knew that other civilizations existed — in forms the Armisian Empire had never seen. Whether those civilizations survived the [[Apostasy]], and whether they might be or already are encountering the Enduring in the ruins below the plateau, is entirely unknown.
