# House Rjioha

**Status:** Extinct — Armisian High Nobility
**Heraldry:** A ship's prow cresting a wave, with a star compass above on a field of deep sea-blue and silver.

**Overview:** 
House Rjioha was once the mighty seafaring arm of the Armisian Empire, responsible for all intercontinental exploration, diplomacy, and trade. Viewing isolationism as a death sentence, they allied with external civilizations like the seagoing Dwarves. Unfortunately, their entire fleet was caught at sea or in distant ports when the Apostasy struck. Not a single vessel ever returned, wiping the house from existence. 

**Legacy:**
With their disappearance, the Enduring lost profound knowledge of the world beyond the Vale, including complex diplomacy with lost races. Whether external civilizations survived and wander the Wastes is a terrifying unknown. Their genetics remain only via an ancestral marriage to House Kaewdyn, gifting them the draconic resonance. Modern Explorers regard House Rjioha as patron saints of the lost expedition, reserving a small shrine for them at Emberfort.
