# Main Vibes of this Project
Order vs Chaos (Life vs Unlife, or Civilization vs Ruin). The Umbra is the encroaching force of Entropy, and life is the strife against it. The Gods brough Order through a careful system/cycle of life-death-afterlife, in which souls are recycled into divine energy and power. But after the Gods left, for unknown reasons, the cycle has been breaking down, and the Umbra is encroaching on the world.

Cyclical History: the world goes in ages, each age ends with a cataclysm, and a new age begins with the survivors. How have the ancients survived previous apocalypses and why they happen are major mysteries.

## Umbra and Apostasy
Umbra is like a natural force that naturally gravitates towards magical beings and magical expression, essentially, carriers of the Aetherweave. During the great Spell-wars, very powerful wizards were quickly consumed by Umbra, just like magical beings like dragons, unicorns, and other magical creatures.

Umbra-touched creatures lose their minds, but instead of creating mindless zombies, they retain their magical, combat or battle instincts and prowess. It is not unusual to see an Umbra-touched wizard casting powerful spells, or an Umbra-touched dragon breathing fire, but with no sense of self or reason. Fallen Generals, through instinct alone, retain their tactical genius, and can be seen leading hordes of Umbra-touched creatures, making them a constant threat to civilization.


# GM Secrets for major mysteries
## The Beacon
Its power burns the souls of all living beings, before it is consumed by the Umbra. It stops the afterlife circle. 
## Beacon Dust
Ash from the beacon is turned into both fertilizer and currency.
Ashcoins, also known as Ashgeld, are used as currency in Brightvale. 
#### Secret of Beacon Dust
It is made of the bones of the dead, namely the Marked (like the Kings of Brightvale).

# Present
Brightvale is currently at a moment of post-equillibrium. 
Food stocks dwindle, population is growing, and the Beacon is dimming or flickering. This is what causes the main drive towards pushing Venturers into the wilds.

# Religions
Different Religions and cults exist, namely in how they regard the Beacon and the cycle of life and death.
Some see the Beacon's light as the absolute divinity, that should not be touched, whose flickering is a sign of displeasure.
Some see it as untouchable and that its ups and downs must be weathered by the devout.
Some see it simply as divine energy left here for civilization to master, use and harness.

# Fae 
Light vs Dark Fae
Fae beings are unphased by the Umbra, and are immune to its effects.
But while Light Fae are consumed by sadness of the withering world around it, often retreating to the Faewild, very rarely appearing in our material world, hidden behind waterfalls, mirrors, and deeper enclaves of surviving glades, the Dark Fae revel in the chaos and decay, and are deeply connected to Umbra, conscious enough to manipulate it. 

# Dwarves
## Before Apostasy
Before Brightvale, Dwarves were sidelined communities that settled along the Empire's Coast. They brough seafaring expertise, trade, mining knowledge, but also a strong form of militarization through Vikingr-like raids. Eventually, non-bellic relations were formed between dwarven communities and the Armisian Empire. But a sense of distrust remained between dwarves and men. 

During the Apostasy, led by the Dawnfather, they joined forces in carving a way up the Teardrop Mountains of the North, seeking refuge from the encroaching Umbra. But once settled in Brightvale, dwarves gravitated towards a more isolationist stance, distrusting the men of the Empire, and focusing on their own survival. 

They mined crystals in the deep mines of Hvergi, used by Aetherweavers to focus and manipulate the Light of the Beacon. Some are also known to contain latent magical energy, which can be used to create magical items, or released in a burst of energy.

## After Apostasy
In Hvergi, the Dwaves are (almost secretly) mining a tunnel to the Northeast, creating tension with Medsen. They want to control one of the exits/entrances of Brightvale. They also believe such Northeastern exit might lead to less Umbra-touched lands.
