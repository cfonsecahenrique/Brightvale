# Three Bridges

## Overview
Three Bridges is a thriving, bustling village situated precisely where the Coldrun and the Swiftwater rivers violently converge. It stands at the vital chokepoint entrance to Brightvale's interior, located along the **Venturer's Trail** just north of the looming shadow of **[[Emberfort]]**. Because of its strategic location, nearly all Venturers pass through Three Bridges before making their final march to Emberfort and out into the Wastes, and it serves as the first true bastion of civilian safety they reach upon their return. 

It is one of the most culturally diverse settlements in Brightvale, boasting a considerable and harmoniously mixed population of [[Humankind|Humans]], [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]], [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarves]], and [[Halflings]]. Its numerous inns and taverns are beloved by Venturers. They offer the warm hearths and strong drinks necessary to thaw the chill of the Umbra after grueling expeditions, as well as the worldly comforts one partakes in before heading out into the deadly Wastes, potentially for the last time. Here, stories are traded like currency, and news from the outside world is first spread to the valley.

## Geography
The village takes its name from three massive stone bridges that span the converging rivers, connecting the divided landmasses. Because it straddles these critical arteries, Three Bridges is a major economic hub. Supplies, lumber, and grain from across the valley flow through here before being taxed and sent down to the garrison at Emberfort.

## History: The Battle of Three Bridges
The village is not just a tavern hub; it is the most sacred and blood-soaked battleground in modern Brightvale history. 

During the brutal **[[The Brightblood War]]** (293–297 AA), Three Bridges became the site of the civil war's climactic conflict. A coalition of reformist Noble Houses had finally managed to corner the Royal Loyalist army here, pinning the Crown's forces against the freezing rivers. The civil war was poised to end in the total slaughter of the Royalists.

However, the sheer bloodshed and emotional resonance of the war attracted a massive Horde of Umbra-touched beasts, which managed to breach the Venturer's Trail and bypass Emberfort's weakened defenses. The Horde poured into Three Bridges, forcing the warring human factions to unite instantaneously or face total annihilation. 

The ensuing **Battle of Three Bridges** was an absolute massacre, but the unified defense held the line and saved the interior of Brightvale from the Umbra. The battle famously cost the life of the reigning monarch, **King Aldhelm**, who died defending the Ironspan alongside the very rebels who had sought to dethrone him. His royal sacrifice forced a bitter but enduring truce—dubbed the Golden Reprieve—that established the current, uneasy political balance between the Monarchy, the Council of Five, and the Noble Houses. Today, a charred stone monument to King Aldhelm and the unified fallen stands at the center of the village crossroads.
