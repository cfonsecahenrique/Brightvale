# [[Aeldenbarrow]]

Most **[[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]]** reside in secluded settlements, sequestered within hidden glades and stone druidic circles. Constructed from living timber and carved earth mounds, these communities center around megaliths arranged in perfect circles—sacred geometries believed to be potential gates to the lost **Faewilds**. Here, they seek to restore the connection severed by the [[Apostasy]] through communal meditation and natural alignment. Protected by shifting illusions and ancient glamours, these sites remain notoriously difficult for outsiders to find, appearing as nothing more than untouched forest to the untrained eye; one cannot hope to find their way into these sanctuaries without being guided by a resident. While primarily inhabited by [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]], these glades are also home to [[Halflings]] and occasional members of other races who are intrinsically connected to natural magic, the [[Fae]], or the primal rhythms of nature.
