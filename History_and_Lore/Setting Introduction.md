# Setting Introduction

In a dying world immersed in relentless shadow and nightmarish monsters, a small community holds fast to the light, hoping those who endure can find a way to save this broken realm before all hope is lost.

The Age of [[Umbra]] is a dark and broken age within the realm known as the Halcyon Domain, which rots in the wake of ancient and forgotten misdeeds of past gods and civilizations. Those who remain rebuild what they can among the ruins of the Old World, referring to themselves as the Enduring, who huddle within reinforced battlements around the sacred Beacon that illuminates and wards the last city: Emberdawn. All Enduring are trained to survive and defend themselves in this bleak land from a young age, many honed into fierce warriors and Aetherweavers through hardship and necessity by the time they reach adulthood. Bands of Venturers brave the cracked and twisted landscape beyond their walls each day to gather sustenance, recover materials, uncover ruins, attempt to shed light into time-lost mysteries and connect the dots of events of the past — and maybe, a way to undo it.

Emberdawn is a claustrophobic pressure cooker that has expanded in defiance of the dying world. Currently, records count close to two thousand Enduring living in the city, divided into Citizens, Guildmembers and Venturers, offspring of several generations of people confined to its walls. The community is small enough that most people are familiar with those from their district, but large enough that most outsiders are still considered strangers.

Outside Emberdawn lies wilderness, commonly referred to as The Wastes, The Wilds, or simply "outside". It is a dark and gloomy world filled with a shadow-like miasma, called the [[Umbra]]. Whatever it touches, fauna or flora, it corrupts, resulting in horrific monsters that prowl the wastes. Sentient beings, however, become something even more terrible: revenants, hellbent on commanding the forces of shadow against the light and everything touched by it.

## The Present Day

Brightvale has passed the point of equilibrium.

For generations, the Vale sustained itself — barely, carefully, through rationing and discipline and the Beacon's steady warmth. That balance is no longer holding. The population has grown beyond what the plateau's agriculture can reliably feed. Food stores are thinning. Rationing has quietly tightened. The civic infrastructure that once felt secure now creaks under the weight of too many people and not enough harvest.

And the Beacon itself is **flickering**.

Not failing — not yet — but dimming in ways the [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretakers]] cannot explain and the [[Aetherweaver Academy]] cannot measure with confidence. The light that has burned without interruption since the Dawnfather's sacrifice now wavers, and every fluctuation sends a tremor through the institutions that depend on it.

This convergence — dwindling food, growing population, uncertain light — is the engine driving Brightvale's current age of expansion. The [[Council of Five|Council]] is pushing more Venturers into the Wilds than at any point in the Vale's history, funding expeditions that would have been considered reckless a generation ago. The calculus is simple and grim: the answers are not inside the walls. If the Enduring do not find new resources, new knowledge, and new solutions in the ruins beyond the plateau, they will not survive what is coming.
