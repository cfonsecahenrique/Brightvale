# [[Umbra]]

# A Treatise on the Breath of the Void, written by Scholar Ehre of [[House Thalum]]

The **[[Umbra]]** is a sentient, hive-like parasitic miasma birthed from the **[[Apostasy]]**. It manifests as an indigo-black shroud, behaving as a drowning liquid that clings to the world’s lowest scars.

### I. The Nature of the Shroud

The [[Umbra]] is inherently soul-chilling and oppressive, drinking the warmth of the living and muting the world’s song. In the lowlands, it settles into "Shadow-Pools" where the air becomes a suffocating poison. Only the **Beacon’s** holy light and the altitude of Brightvale keep the **Sacred Valley** pure, though "Red Morn" fogs still bleed across the plateau with the seasons.

### II. The Discordant Hum and the Warp

The Shadow emits a **Malignant Echo**—a low, rot-heavy chime that pierces through stone and skin to unravel the spirit. Prolonged exposure induces "Flesh-Discord," a slow undoing of the natural form:

- **The Blight:** A sickness of the marrow, the shedding of skin, and a ghastly translucency of the body.
- **Twisted Blooom:** The budding of extra sight-spheres, jagged bone-spurs, and a sickly violet ichor within the veins.
- **Unnatural Melding:** In the darkest depths, the resonance can fuse distinct lives into a single, agonized knot of meat and shadow.
### III. The Blighted Wilds and the [[Umbra]]-Touched

All places outside the walls of [[Medsen|Emberdawn]] are commonly referred to as "The Wastes" or "The Wilds" — bleak landscapes where little light permeates through the crimson veil above and the black forest canopies below. Here, the [[Umbra]] seeks to erase the boundaries of life, folding all things into a singular entropic fold. Flora and fauna mutate, acting as one through a nexus hive mind.

- **The Woods & Beasts:** Trees turn to brittle black glass or become weeping nexuses that broadcast the Shadow’s song. Animals lose their original shapes, mutating into nightmarish beasts of numerous tentacle limbs that grasp and spear, their flesh dissolved into sensory filaments and "Void-Flux."
- **The Rotlords:** The [[Slayers]] speak in hushed tones of these Great Horrors. They are massive nexuses of forest will and dark magic — towering cathedrals of rotting flesh and fungal might that are constantly increasing their mass, amplifying the Malignant Echo to warp all life in their path.
- **The Fallen Host:** Perhaps most disturbing is the talk of blasphemous angelic beings — remnants of old, forgotten pantheons that were left behind and corrupted by the shadow, now serving as cruel heralds of the void.
### IV. The Hollowing of the Soul

The subversion of a mind to the Dark, known as **Hollowing**, is the total erasure of the self:

1. **Memory-Sifting:** The Echo shatters the mind’s history into **Cold-Ash**.
2. **Extinction:** The personality withers, the vital spark dies, and the body becomes a necrotic vessel.
3. **The Revenant:** The resulting shell reanimates. Standard Revenants exhibit unnatural strength and cold, tactical cunning, remaining hollow slaves to the Void.
4. **The Arch-Revenants:** What makes the mightiest [[Slayers]] lose their sleep are those who were once powerful Venturers or loved ones lost to the [[Umbra]]. These unique Revenants retain their mortal powers, magic, and keen intellect, but are completely devoid of their original memory and humanity. They act as generals of the dark, leading vast hordes of Umbra-Touched beings against expeditions, and reports suggest these coordinated armies are slowly closing in on [[Medsen|Emberdawn]].
### V. The Prophylactic Paradox

The **Embermark** produces a holy counter-hymn that nullifies the Warp. However, this protection is a treacherous balance; to push back the Dark, the Emberborne must flare their internal fire, risking **Shatter-Warping**—where the body is broken by the very Light intended to save it.

### VI. The Umbra's Hunger for Magic

The [[Umbra]] is not indiscriminate. It gravitates toward **magical beings and magical expression** — toward anything that carries the Aetherweave. The greater the arcane potency, the faster the shadow comes. During the great Spell-Wars that followed the [[Apostasy]], the most powerful wizards were consumed first, their vast reserves of channelled energy drawing the [[Umbra]] like a beacon of their own. Dragons, unicorns, and other inherently magical creatures suffered the same fate — their very nature made them irresistible to the dark.

This affinity explains a pattern that the [[Slayers]] and the [[Emberguard]] have long observed: the [[Umbra]] does not produce mindless husks. An Umbra-touched creature loses its mind — its memory, its identity, its capacity for reason — but **retains its instincts**. A corrupted wizard still casts spells, drawing on muscle memory and arcane reflex with terrifying precision. A corrupted dragon still breathes fire, still wheels through the sky in combat formations etched into its flesh by generations of draconic evolution. The body remembers what the soul has forgotten.

Most dangerous of all are the **Fallen Generals** — former military commanders and tacticians whose strategic instincts survived the Hollowing intact. Through pure reflex and ingrained battlefield intuition, they organize and lead hordes of Umbra-touched creatures with a coordination that should be impossible for beings without consciousness. They are not thinking. They are *performing* — executing the patterns of a lifetime's training on an empty stage. The fact that this performance is indistinguishable from genuine tactical intelligence is what makes them a constant, existential threat to every settlement in the Vale.
