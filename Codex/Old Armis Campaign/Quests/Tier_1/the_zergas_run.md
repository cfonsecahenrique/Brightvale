# The Zergas Run

**Quest ID:** q_zergas_run
**Type:** Voyage / supply-and-survey
**Difficulty:** Easy–Moderate (suggested levels 1–3; 3–4 sessions)
**Destination:** Zergas-by-Azure, the Empire's outpost on the northern point of the Titan's Sword
**Posted by:** Imperial Admiralty contract-boards, the Lower Terraces, Pattis

> GM-facing brief. This is the tutorial voyage: real sea, real stakes, low lethality. It teaches the rhythms of an Armisian crossing — watches, rationing, Weave discipline — and ends with the player's name in the Guild books. Keep wonder high. The arc closes with the return report; every thread it plants belongs to a later quest.

## Premise

A supply-and-survey haul down the well-sailed western lane: carry trade-goods and Admiralty stores from Pattis to Zergas-by-Azure, take on the outpost's reports and return cargo, and sail home. Routine for a seasoned crew — which is why a green hand is trusted with it. The real education is the crossing itself, and the trouble waiting at the far end.

## The Crew

- **Sailing Master Byrne** — the ship's commander. Runs the watches, sets the rations, expects both kept. Dry humor under a perpetual frown. Tests the player without explaining why; respects competence and dislikes questions that should be obvious.
- **Faelan** — Elven pilot who reads the sea by an art the Aetherwrights don't respect and can't match. Quiet, accurate, and unsettling to people who can't explain how he knows things. The player's most reliable ally if they earn his trust.
- **Aetherwright-Officer Vaela Sinn** — responsible for the ship's Weave-reserve. Formally composed, privately anxious. Her anxiety runs deeper than professional caution.

## Arc Structure

**Provision → Cross → Make Landfall → Resolve the Trouble → Return & Report.**

### Session 1 — The Crossing

Out the harbor, Byrne drills the player: watches, rationing, never stray from the Weave-light. Set the wonder of a planar-powered voyage — the Air-plane pulling the sails on a calm day, the Water-plane parting swells under the hull. Then spring the first real test: a squall that demands action, or a becalming that tempts the crew to spend Weave early. Let the player make the call.

The **teaching fight** arrives in a stretch of thin ward-coverage: a small pack of **Azurian Reef-Scavengers** (low-CR reskin — giant crabs, reef hunters) that emerge from shallower water beside the lane. Winnable, instructive, dangerous enough to matter. NPC crew soften the fight but let the player make the decisive moves.

Vaela Sinn reports the Weave-reserve lower than it should be. She has no explanation. Byrne notes it. Keep moving.

### Session 2 — Zergas-by-Azure

Landfall at a mud-and-timber frontier port that smells of salt, pine, and old grievance. The supply handoff is routine. The port is not.

Three things press on the player simultaneously:

- **The Dwarven Traders.** A delegation from Athregal is at the port, led by clan-elder **Unn Greykeep**. They're owed something — the Admiralty's records are vague — and they're not leaving until it's addressed. Byrne tells the player to stall them. The Dwarves are immovable but not yet hostile. The player can stall, negotiate in good faith, or lie. What the Dwarves are actually owed is unresolved here; how the player handles Greykeep sets the terms for *The Dwarven Debt*.
- **The Missing Crew.** A merchant-frigate at anchor is short four hands. The surviving crew drink too much and say too little — but Isle fever is in their eyes and their evasion. Press them and they'll eventually say *the Isle took them* without saying more. A first whisper.
- **The Weave-Leak.** The reserve is lower than any routine usage explains. Investigation reveals that Vaela Sinn has been quietly bleeding small amounts from the reserve — not for herself, but to feed a ward-stone hidden in her quarters, one she has maintained since before the voyage. The ward keeps someone she loves alive, somewhere, at a cost she has not disclosed to anyone. A small crime with real human weight.

### Session 3 — Resolution & Return

The Dwarven situation is deferred: the player hands off a formal Admiralty pledge of follow-up. The missing crew cannot be recovered. Vaela's transgression must be handled — the player can report her, protect her, or extract a promise. Each choice has weight with Byrne, with Vaela, and with the Admiralty later.

The return crossing draws on a lighter reserve. Faelan earns his pay. Arrive at Pattis, submit maps and report. The player's name goes in the books.

## Complications

- The Dwarven delegation is angrier than Byrne let on; Greykeep's patience runs out before the player is ready.
- Vaela's ward-stone is failing; she may have to choose between the voyage and whoever it protects.
- The missing crew left something at Zergas — a note, a half-drawn map, a name — and it is now the player's problem to decide what to do with it.

## Combat

Light and calibrated to levels 1–3 with NPC support. The teaching fight is the arc's only designed encounter; additional skirmishes (a harbor brawl, a Dwarven standoff that goes wrong) are optional escalations.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Clean run:** Admiralty standing; Faelan as a named contact; Vaela Sinn's loyalty or enmity depending on the player's choice; the player's name in the Guild books.
- **Dwarven situation handled well:** Greykeep remembers; opens *The Dwarven Debt* on better terms.
- **The Isle thread acquired:** a name, a map fragment, or a rumor — not actionable yet, but real.
- Modest pay + return cargo shares.
