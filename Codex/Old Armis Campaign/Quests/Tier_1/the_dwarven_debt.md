# The Dwarven Debt

**Quest ID:** q_dwarven_debt
**Type:** Diplomacy / investigation (with combat)
**Difficulty:** Moderate (suggested levels 3–5; 3–4 sessions)
**Destination:** Zergas-by-Azure and the Athregali settlement at Greykeep's Claim
**Posted by:** Imperial Admiralty, direct commission (not the public board)

> GM-facing brief. This quest is what the Admiralty has been avoiding: a debt it made and would rather forget. The player is sent to resolve it quietly — but quietly is not the same as honestly, and the Dwarves of Athregal are very good at telling the difference. Let the player choose what the Empire's word is worth.

## Premise

During the first Armisian contact with the Dwarves of Athregal — a contact that began as war and ended as uneasy trade — the Empire made a promise it has not kept. Clan-elder **Unn Greykeep** and her delegation have been waiting at Zergas-by-Azure for that promise to be honored. The Admiralty's official position is that the matter is under review. The Dwarves' position is that review has run three years, and patience has a price.

The player is dispatched with a sealed commission: resolve the situation without conceding what the Admiralty does not want to concede. What they actually owe is left deliberately vague in the briefing. The player must find out.

## What the Debt Actually Is

The Empire promised Clan Greykeep a seat at the trade council governing the Zergas landing — the body that sets tariffs, land-use, and port access on the Titan's Sword. In practice this would give Athregal a veto on Armisian expansion northward, which the Council has no intention of honoring. What the Dwarves were told and what the Admiralty's records say are two different documents. One is the truth.

The player discovers this discrepancy during the investigation portion (Session 2). How they handle it defines the arc.

## The Crew

- **Admiralty Liaison Pell Orantes** — a meticulous bureaucrat sent to ensure the player says nothing that constitutes a formal admission of liability. He is not a villain; he is a man doing a job that requires him to be something close to one.
- **Faelan** — present if the player has his trust; the Dwarves respond to Elves differently than to humans. A useful wildcard.
- **Unn Greykeep** — clan-elder, not a diplomat in the Armisian sense. She says exactly what she means and expects the same in return. The player will find this either refreshing or alarming.

## Arc Structure

**Commission → Arrival → Learn What Is Actually Owed → Negotiate → Return with the Answer.**

### Session 1 — The Commission & Arrival

Orantes briefs the player on the journey: resolve Greykeep's grievance, preserve the Admiralty's position, and do not make any promises the Council hasn't cleared. The commission is written carefully enough that it says none of this in plain language.

Zergas-by-Azure: Greykeep has made herself immovable at the harbor-master's lodge. She is not hostile, but she has stopped being polite. Orantes attempts the standard diplomatic deflection; Greykeep ends the meeting in under four minutes by pointing out that she speaks Armisian fluently and can read the deflection for what it is.

### Session 2 — The Investigation

The player must find out what the Dwarves were actually promised. This requires:
- Accessing the port archive at Zergas — with or without Orantes noticing.
- Interviewing surviving crew from the original contact expedition, some of whom are now settled at Zergas.
- Optionally, speaking honestly with Greykeep herself, who will tell them exactly what she was promised if they ask sincerely rather than diplomatically.

The discrepancy is real and documented. Someone in the Admiralty rewrote the agreement after it was made. Orantes may know this; he may not. The player has to decide whether it matters.

### Session 3 — The Negotiation

Three resolutions are available:

- **Honor the debt:** give Greykeep the seat. The trade council changes; Armisian expansion north slows; the Dwarves of Athregal become a genuine partner rather than a managed nuisance. Orantes files a report that does not reflect well on the player. Greykeep respects them forever.
- **Offer a substitute:** negotiate something the Admiralty will accept giving — trade concessions, harbor access, a formal apology with no legal weight — in place of the seat. Greykeep knows a substitute when she sees one. She may accept if the substitute is real enough; she may refuse and escalate.
- **Bury it again:** find a pretext to defer, delay, or discredit Greykeep's claim. Orantes is useful for this. The debt remains unpaid. The Dwarves remember, and Athregal will remember. Something will collect this debt eventually.

### Session 4 (if escalation) — The Reckoning

If negotiation fails or the player chooses the third path, Greykeep takes action: a Dwarven ship blockades the Zergas landing. Not a war — she is too careful for that — but an unmistakable signal. The player must either break the blockade (a fight that will cost them Athregal permanently), find a genuine resolution under pressure, or return to Pattis and let the Admiralty handle the fallout.

## Combat

Not the primary tool here, but available: a blockade confrontation, a skirmish with Dwarven guards during the archive break-in, or a street fight if Orantes's methods provoke a response. Calibrate to levels 3–5.

## Complications

- The Admiralty official who rewrote the agreement is still active — and has connections to a Noble House that profits from northern expansion.
- Greykeep's delegation includes a young warrior, **Bjarn**, who wants war and will find a pretext if the player takes too long.
- Faelan knows something about Athregal that neither the Empire nor the Dwarves have said aloud. He'll share it if the player has earned it.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Debt honored:** Athregal stands as a genuine ally; Armisian-Dwarven trade normalizes; the player has real enemies in the Admiralty.
- **Substitute accepted:** a fragile peace; Greykeep's grudging respect; the debt is technically closed but not truly paid.
- **Debt buried:** short-term Admiralty approval; a future crisis the player may have to face at higher level; Greykeep's enmity and Athregal's long memory.
- Diplomatic standing with **Clan Greykeep** shifts permanently based on outcome.
