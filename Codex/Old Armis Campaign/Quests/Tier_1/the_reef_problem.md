# The Reef Problem

**Quest ID:** q_reef_problem
**Type:** Voyage / infiltration & intrigue (with combat)
**Difficulty:** Moderate–Hard (suggested levels 3–5; 4–5 sessions)
**Destination:** Borkum Reef, two days' sail west of the Titan's Sword
**Posted by:** Imperial Admiralty contract-boards, the Lower Terraces, Pattis

> GM-facing brief. This is a mission with a conscience built into it: the contract is clean and the target is not. The Admiralty wants the Reef-Born "dealt with"; the player will discover that the Reef-Born are the Empire's own castoffs, and that the cleanest solution is the cruelest. Let the moral weight land without preaching. The arc ends when the player delivers their report to Navarch Thorne — not before.

## Premise

The pirates of Borkum Reef are hardening into a real power astride the western sea-lanes. Navarch Kassia Thorne's contract is blunt: the Reef "must be dealt with" — crushed, infiltrated, or turned against itself. The player's crew is to reach the Reef, learn who truly leads it, and carry out the Admiralty's will. *How* is left to them. That latitude is the whole adventure.

## The Crew

- **Admiralty Envoy Caston Drell** — there to see the contract fulfilled and to report how the player fulfills it. Loyal to Thorne, not to the player. Reads the mission as a straightforward suppression operation; discovering otherwise will unsettle him.
- **Faelan** — the Reef's channels are a navigational maze. Without a reader of waters, a ship runs aground before it reaches the islets.
- **Mira Voss** — a Reef defector the Admiralty is using as a guide. Her loyalty runs in exactly two directions and she knows it.

## Arc Structure

**Approach → Get Inside → Learn the Truth → The Choice → Carry It Out → Answer For It.**

### Session 1 — The Approach

Two days' sail to Borkum Reef, with Mira Voss serving as pilot and uneasy conscience. She describes the Reef not as a pirate fortress but as a community: ship-timber taverns, salvage markets, refugee families, a few deserter Aetherwrights who fled the Helm and never looked back. Drell hears piracy. The player can hear something else.

Arrival: the Reef is a labyrinth of low islets and wreck-sheltered coves. Faelan gets the ship in without losing the keel. The crew enters under a cover the GM and player establish. The Reef's people are not welcoming but are not immediately hostile.

### Session 2 — Getting Inside

The Reef is a confederation, not a kingdom — there is no single head to cut off. Three captains hold real influence:

- **Captain Oryn Sabe** — the oldest Reef-Born, a former Admiralty lieutenant who was cashiered and stripped of his patent after the western campaign. The Admiralty most wants him dead. He is the most reasonable person the player will meet here.
- **Captain Tirel Ash** — young, ambitious, and genuinely dangerous. Would sell the Reef to the right buyer if it meant personal power. The Admiralty's best tool for a divide-and-betray play.
- **The Aetherwrights** — a loose cell of three deserters from the Aether-Helm operating out of a salvage shed. They keep the Reef's wards running and know things about the Weave the Empire suppresses. They are not captains, but every captain needs them.

The player investigates, maneuvers, and decides whom they believe. What they learn shapes what "dealt with" comes to mean.

### Session 3 — Learning the Truth

The Reef's full picture emerges: it was built by people the Empire discarded — failed colonists, broken soldiers, indebted sailors, Elven navigators the Helm stopped hiring. Its "piracy" is mostly toll-taking on lanes the Admiralty calls its own but cannot police. The community the "clean" solution would destroy has children and gardens and a functioning shrine.

Drell pushes for resolution. Mira Voss is watching what the player chooses to see.

### Session 4 — The Choice

Four paths remain open:

- **Crush it:** a coordinated fight the player can win and will not enjoy. Captain Sabe's last words, if it goes that way, are not curses.
- **Betray from within:** use Tirel Ash to fracture the confederation from the inside. Ash gets what he wants; the Reef fractures; the violence is distributed and deniable. Drell considers this a clean outcome.
- **Turn it:** convince the captains — including Ash — that the Reef's best future is as a licensed waystation rather than a pirate hold. Requires out-maneuvering Drell and Ash simultaneously. Sabe will help if the player has treated him honestly.
- **Protect it:** betray the contract, deliver a false report, and leave the Reef standing. Mira Voss will cover for the player if they've earned it. Drell will know. Someone always pays.

### Session 5 — Answer For It

Back at Pattis. Navarch Thorne reads the report. The Council hears her summary. The player's standing with the Admiralty, the Reef-Born, and whoever witnessed their choice shifts here — and shifts permanently.

Whatever path the player took, two things follow the player home: the deserter Aetherwrights' knowledge (if the player spent any time with them), and the first clear signal of the secession current the Reef-Born are part of.

## Combat

Real and expected, but not the point. Boarding actions, a tavern brawl, a running fight through the channels, or a clash between Reef factions the player has set against each other. Calibrate to levels 3–5 with NPC support; the player should feel the jump from the Zergas run.

## Complications

- Mira Voss is not fully a defector — she is watching to see what the Empire will do, and her report goes somewhere other than the Admiralty.
- A Reef community will be destroyed by the "clean" solution; the player sees it before they choose.
- The deserter Aetherwrights have a cache of research the Helm wants suppressed, and they will give it to the player if the player earns their trust.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Contract fulfilled the Admiralty's way:** strong Admiralty standing; coin and shares; a mark the world will remember.
- **The Reef turned or spared:** weaker Admiralty standing; a genuine ally on the western lanes; the Reef-Born's gratitude and Mira Voss's loyalty.
- Either way: the deserters' Weave-knowledge is in the world now, seeding *The Deserters' Cache*; and the secession current has a new chapter, seeding *The Secession*.
