# The Contested Isle

**Quest ID:** q_contested_isle
**Type:** Grand expedition / three-faction confrontation (exploration, combat, revelation)
**Difficulty:** Very Hard (suggested levels 9–12; 6–8 sessions)
**Destination:** The Contested Isle — the interior, beyond any prior expedition's reach
**Posted by:** Joint Imperial-Armisian Commission / direct Admiralty commission / player initiative

> GM-facing brief. This is the arc where everything the player has learned about the Isle converges into a single reckoning. The three empires are all here now, competing openly, and the Isle is responding to the accumulated intrusion in ways no expedition has survived long enough to report. The dead primordial may stir. The player's accumulated choices — with Athregal, the Reef-Born, the Ziramati, the Helm — all bear weight here. End the arc with a fact that cannot be unmade.

## Premise

The race for the Contested Isle has reached a tipping point. Armisian extraction camps, Athregali mining operations, and Ziramati sacred-ground expeditions now occupy overlapping territory on the island's coast, and open confrontation between the three powers is weeks or days away. The player arrives — as part of a major expedition, as an independent agent, or as the Admiralty's senior operative — with more knowledge of what the Isle actually is than any official expedition commander.

What they do with that knowledge, in the face of an international crisis and a dead primordial whose body is being excavated, defines the arc.

## The Factions on the Isle

- **The Armisian Extraction Fleet** — commanded by **Admiral Caeran Lys**, a career officer whose primary concern is the quantity of residuum his ships go home with. He knows about the Isle-Touched problem; he has decided it is an acceptable operational loss.
- **Clan Greykeep's Expedition** — led by **Bjarn Greykeep** (if he survived prior quests, now a commander in his own right), with full clan backing and significantly better equipment than anyone expected. Athregal wants the Isle for its residuum *and* for the claim it represents. Their relationship with the player determines their first move.
- **Isharr's Delegation** — the Ziramati envoy, now with a full sacred-expedition behind her, here to witness what becomes of the grave. She is not here to mine. She is here to prevent the worst possible outcome, and she does not think she can. She will work with the player if the player is the only option left.
- **The Natives** — coordinated, purposeful, and no longer retreating. They have been watching every expedition for decades. They have decided something. Isharr is the only person the player has met who might speak their language.

## Arc Structure

**Arrival → The Three-Way Standoff → Into the Interior → The Primordial Stirs → The Reckoning.**

### Session 1 — Arrival & The Standoff

The player arrives to find three armed expeditions in a standoff on the coast. Lys wants to begin the next extraction phase; Bjarn's expedition has blockaded the primary seam access; Isharr's delegation is between them. A wrong move from any party starts a war on the Isle's shore.

The player has relationships that no one else does. This is the moment those relationships become operational.

### Session 2 — Navigating the Factions

The player has days before someone fires first. Three tracks run simultaneously:

- **The diplomatic track:** build a temporary multi-faction agreement that allows the player's expedition to proceed into the interior without an immediate war breaking out behind them.
- **The intelligence track:** each faction has information about the interior the others don't. Pooling it — which requires trust the factions don't have — reveals a clearer picture of what's at the Isle's center.
- **The Natives track:** Isharr can open communication. The natives have a request. It is not a request the Admiralty will approve.

### Sessions 3–4 — The Interior

Whatever arrangement the player made holds or doesn't. The expedition moves inland — further than any prior expedition has reached.

The interior of the Isle is wrong in ways that compound at depth: the ground is warm underfoot, the air carries a subsonic resonance that affects sleep, the residuum seams are more dense and more wrong the further in they go. One expedition member begins the conversion process by Session 4. The player saw this at the Residuum Run; everyone else is seeing it for the first time.

**The center of the Isle** is a depression in the black rock, kilometers wide, where the primordial's body has been slowly dissolving into the Aether for however long it has been dead. The residuum pools here in concentrations that affect consciousness. The dead thing's magic still moves at the bottom of it — not purposefully, but not randomly either.

**The Natives live at the rim** of the depression, tending it. They are not hostile to the player's expedition. They are hostile to what the expeditions have been doing to the edge of the grave for years. They have a word for it that Isharr translates as: *eating the dead*.

### Session 5 — The Primordial Stirs

If enough residuum has been extracted across enough expeditions — and it has — something shifts. The GM decides the nature and scale: the dead primordial is not waking, but something is responding to the extraction. The Isle-Touched transformations in proximity to the center accelerate. The ground reshapes faster. The depression breathes.

Bjarn's expedition begins an unauthorized deep extraction. This is the trigger, not the player; the player must decide how to respond.

### Sessions 6–7 — The Reckoning

The player's accumulated choices converge:

- **Stop the extraction:** requires confronting Admiral Lys and Bjarn Greykeep simultaneously — diplomatically or otherwise. Lys will not stop without force or a political lever the player has to have built.
- **Let it continue:** the player has seen what the end state is. They go home wealthy. The Isle continues to consume its excavators.
- **The Natives' request:** they have asked, through Isharr, for one thing: the expeditions leave and do not come back, and in exchange, they will show the player something at the center that they have been protecting since the primordial fell. It is not a weapon. It is a memory — the primordial's last moment, preserved in the Aether, which is the truest account of what the Apostasy was and where it came from. *This is the setting's deepest available truth at this tier.*

Whatever happens, the Isle is changed. The factions that remain on it are changed. The player leaves with something — residuum, or the memory, or Isharr's alliance, or a dead crew, or all of the above — that the world will be different for.

## Combat

Significant and multi-front: Isle-Touched encounters in the interior, a full confrontation between expedition forces if diplomacy fails, one direct engagement with the primordial's residual defensive magic (treat as an environmental hazard with damage expressions and saves, not a monster fight). Calibrate to levels 9–12 with mixed NPC support.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Extraction stopped, Natives honored:** the Isle is withdrawn from the race; the three-power scramble redirects; the player has the memory and Isharr as a permanent ally.
- **Extraction completed:** maximum material gain; political victory for the Admiralty; the player knows what they've done and what it costs. The memory is lost.
- **Faction war triggered:** chaotic resolution; some faction is destroyed on the Isle; the political map of the western reaches changes permanently.
- **Whatever the outcome:** the player holds the deepest knowledge available in the campaign. What they build with it, or what they bury, is the campaign's final question.
