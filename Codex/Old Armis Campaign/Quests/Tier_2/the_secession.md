# The Secession

**Quest ID:** q_secession
**Type:** Political crisis / naval (with combat)
**Difficulty:** Hard (suggested levels 5–7; 4–5 sessions)
**Destination:** Borkum Reef and Pattis
**Posted by:** Imperial Admiralty direct commission — not on the public board

> GM-facing brief. This quest pays off the Reef Problem. The player's earlier choice shapes the situation they arrive in; what they did at the Reef is now a political fact. The Reef-Born are declaring themselves a free confederation, and the Empire must respond. Let the player's prior relationship with Captain Sabe and the Reef determine how much room they have to maneuver.

## Premise

Three months after the events of *The Reef Problem*, the Reef-Born have made a move: a jointly signed Declaration of Confederation, circulated to every Admiralty port, proclaiming Borkum Reef and its affiliate communities a sovereign free state under Captains' Council law. It is not legally coherent by Armisian standards. The Admiralty treats it as piracy. The Reef-Born treat it as the founding of a nation.

Navarch Kassia Thorne has commissioned the player specifically — because the player was at the Reef — to resolve the crisis before the Council orders a full naval suppression. The window is narrow.

## The Situation the Player Finds

The Reef situation differs based on the outcome of *The Reef Problem*:

- **If the Reef was crushed:** a successor Captains' Council, radicalized by the destruction, has united in a way the old confederation never managed. The player's history here is a liability. Captain Sabe is dead or absent; someone angrier is in his seat.
- **If the Reef was turned or spared:** Sabe is alive and in a complicated position — he wants the secession to succeed but knows the Empire can destroy it. He'll work with the player because he has to, not because he trusts that it ends well.
- **If the contract was betrayed for the Reef:** the player has credit here that no other Admiralty agent would have. Sabe will meet them. What the player does with that trust defines the arc.

## The Crew

- **Navarch's Adjutant Pira Conn** — Thorne's representative; authorized to negotiate within limits Conn will reveal as the situation demands them. She is not unsympathetic to the Reef-Born and she is not allowed to say so.
- **Faelan** — present if the player maintains the contact. His Elven heritage gives him standing with the older Reef-Born communities.
- **Mira Voss** — if she survived *The Reef Problem* on good terms, she is the player's best channel to the current Captains' Council.
- **Tirel Ash** — if he survived and was elevated, he is the primary obstacle. He wants the secession declared so he can sell the resulting chaos to the highest bidder.

## Arc Structure

**Commission → Reach the Reef → Read the Room → The Negotiation → The Reckoning.**

### Session 1 — Commission & Approach

Thorne is blunt: the Council has authorized a naval response in thirty days. If the player cannot produce a resolution before then, the Admiralty will produce one of its own, and it will not look like diplomacy. Conn has been given real authority — not unlimited, but real. Go.

The Reef is louder than the player remembers. Word of the Declaration has brought sympathizers — other exile communities from the western lanes, a few Elven navigators who have no love for the Empire, one Dwarven ship that is officially neutral.

### Session 2 — Reading the Room

Three days at the Reef. The player maps the political terrain:

- The Captains' Council is not unified. Sabe (if alive) wants a negotiated status; Ash wants leverage; a third captain named **Renne Fol** wants peace and will give up the Declaration if the Reef-Born are offered a formal trade charter.
- The sympathizer ships are a complication: some are genuine; one is an Armisian intelligence asset running a false flag. Identifying it may matter.
- The Aetherwright deserters are still here, if they survived. Their presence gives the Reef a defensive capability the Navy's estimates do not include.

### Session 3 — The Negotiation

The player negotiates between the Captains' Council and Conn's mandate. Three real outcomes are on the table:

- **Formal charter:** Borkum Reef becomes a licensed free port under an Armisian trade charter — sovereignty in practice, subordination in name. Renne Fol supports this; Ash opposes it; Sabe accepts it reluctantly.
- **Full recognition:** a harder ask. The Reef-Born are recognized as a free confederation; the Empire trades with them as a foreign power. Outside Conn's mandate; requires the player to go over Thorne's head to the Council directly.
- **Suppression by proxy:** use the false-flag ship and Tirel Ash to destabilize the confederation from within, manufacturing a pretext for the Navy to intervene "at the Reef's request." Conn knows how to run this. The Reef-Born who trusted the player will know what happened.

### Session 4 — The Reckoning

Whatever the player chose, it lands:
- The charter is signed, or the Council recognition is achieved, or the false flag is run.
- If the false flag: the Navy's intervention is ugly. Sabe's response, if he's alive, is not.
- If the charter or recognition: the Reef-Born have a future. The secession current doesn't stop here — it becomes a precedent, and the Council knows it.

Return to Pattis. Report to Thorne. The player's standing with the Admiralty, the Reef-Born, and Faelan shifts based on what they chose to be.

## Combat

Expected if the false-flag approach is used or if Ash forces the issue: boarding actions, a harbor confrontation, a fight in the Council chamber that spills into the docks. Calibrate to levels 5–7 with NPC support.

## Complications

- Ash has reached out to an outside party — not Athregal, not Ziramat — and someone is funding his resistance to any settlement. The player can trace this if they look.
- One of the sympathizer ships is carrying Reef families from an outlying community being displaced by Admiralty expansion. Their presence makes "just dissolve the Reef" politically impossible if the player surfaces it.
- The Aetherwright deserters will leave if the settlement doesn't protect them. Their knowledge leaves with them.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Charter or recognition achieved:** the Reef-Born survive as a political entity; the secession current becomes a lasting feature of the western lanes; the player has a named ally in the west.
- **Suppression by proxy:** Admiralty approval; Reef-Born enmity; the deserters scatter with their knowledge into the wider world.
- Reputation with **The Reef-Born**, **Imperial Admiralty**, and **Tirel Ash** (as a power, if he survives) shifts permanently.
- Significant pay from Thorne regardless of method; political capital depending on outcome.
