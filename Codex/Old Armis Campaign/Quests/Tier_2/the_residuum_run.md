# The Residuum Run

**Quest ID:** q_residuum_run
**Type:** Voyage / expedition (exploration, combat, horror)
**Difficulty:** Hard (suggested levels 5–8; 5–6 sessions)
**Destination:** The Contested Isle, deep west past the Azurian
**Posted by:** Imperial Admiralty / Aether-Helm joint commission, Pattis

> GM-facing brief. This is the arc where the Isle stops being a rumor. The player goes, sees what the residuum actually is, and returns changed. Do not explain the Isle — it does not explain itself. Show the wrongness of the land, the hunger of the three-way scramble, and the cost of drawing on something that should not be drawn. The arc closes when the player is back in Pattis with what they know. What they do with it is a longer story.

## Premise

The Contested Isle bleeds a raw Aether-residuum found nowhere else in the known world — the prize driving the three-way race between the Empire, Athregal, and Ziramat. The Aether-Helm has sponsored a serious extraction expedition: reach the Isle, establish a forward camp, mine a viable quantity of residuum, and return. The player is crew on the most significant western voyage yet attempted.

What the Isle actually is — why the land fights back, why the natives are bound to its power, why Ziramati navigators treat the approach as a funeral rite — none of that is in the briefing.

## The Crew

- **Expedition Commander Sela Dorn** — an Aether-Helm senior wright, brilliant and inflexible. She believes the residuum is a mineral phenomenon and will not update this belief easily. Her arc is whether she updates it at all.
- **Faelan** — present if the player has his contact. His reading of the Isle's waters will save lives the instruments cannot.
- **Athregali Observer Bjarn Greykeep** — a young Dwarven warrior attached under whatever diplomatic settlement came out of *The Dwarven Debt*. Has a reason to be here that he has not fully disclosed.
- **Ziramati Envoy Isharr** — a dragon-folk scholar who joined the voyage at Pattis under a formal watch agreement. She refers to the Isle as *Tiâmtu* and refuses to explain the word. She is afraid in a way the others are not.

## Arc Structure

**Provision & Departure → The Azurian Crossing → First Landfall → Into the Interior → The Cost → Withdrawal.**

### Session 1 — The Azurian Crossing

The longest uncharted ocean stretch the player has attempted. Dorn runs the Weave-reserve hard — too hard, Faelan thinks. The crew is good; the equipment is excellent. Three days out, the sea starts being wrong: a current that runs against the wind, bioluminescence at noon, a smell of earth on an empty ocean. Isharr begins making marks on the hull that she will not explain to Dorn.

Arrive at the Isle's outer shelf. The sky is different here — not dangerously, just wrong enough to notice.

### Session 2 — First Landfall

The Isle from the shore: dense green interior, black-stone coast, air that tastes faintly of burnt iron. The forward camp goes up by the book. First survey parties move inland.

**The land fights back in small ways.** No dramatic attack — the ground shifts under a step that should have been solid, a bird call that is almost right, compass needles that want to point inward toward the interior instead of north. A survey party returns short one member who fell into a sinkhole that is not on any map and is not, on closer inspection, geological. Dorn notes it. Continues.

**The natives make contact** on the third day — not an attack, but a presence. They appear at the edge of vision and withdraw. Isharr recognizes them as bound to the Isle's power in a way that matches nothing in Armisian bestiary records. She wants to speak to them. Dorn says there is no time.

### Session 3 — Into the Interior

The residuum seams are a day's march from the coast. They are extraordinary: raw Aether bleeding visibly from fissures in the black rock, collecting in pools that shimmer and pull at the senses. Dorn's instruments go wild. The extraction crew begins work.

**Two things happen simultaneously:**
- A Dwarven sub-expedition, arriving separately and earlier than the Admiralty knew, has already established a camp near the best seam. Bjarn was expecting them; his disclosure of this ranges from awkward to damaging depending on the player's relationship with him.
- A crew member who drew on the residuum directly — not through equipment, just a hand into a pool, a moment of curiosity — begins to come apart in a way that has no medical description. The process is slow, visible, and terrifying. This is the Isle-Touched in its early stages.

### Session 4 — The Cost

The player now knows what the residuum does to a person who channels it. Dorn knows. The question is what they do with that knowledge while the extraction continues.

The Isle escalates: the land actively reshapes itself against the camps now — not with monsters but with geography. Paths close. The forward camp is three kilometers from where it was yesterday, by a measure no one disputes. The natives move closer. Isharr finally speaks with one and returns pale. She will tell the player what she learned; she will not tell Dorn.

**What Isharr learned:** the Isle is a grave. The power in the ground is a dead thing's blood. The natives are what the dead thing's magic made. The residuum is not a mineral; it is an act of desecration, and the Isle knows it.

The player must decide: complete the extraction, abort, or find a third path the briefing didn't offer.

### Session 5 — Withdrawal

Whatever the player chose in Session 4 defines this session. The withdrawal is hard regardless: the Isle will let them leave but not easily, and the Azurian crossing with a lighter-than-expected reserve and a crew that has seen what they've seen is its own ordeal.

Arrive at Pattis. Dorn files her report, which reflects the expedition's operational results. The player files theirs. The two reports are not identical. Someone in the Helm reads the discrepancy.

## Combat

Real and escalating: the Isle-Touched (stat block: `the_isle_touched.md`), a confrontation with the Dwarven camp that goes wrong, and one full engagement with the Isle's natives if the player pushes too deep. The land itself functions as a dungeon whose architecture the GM adjusts between sessions.

## Complications

- The Isle-Touched crew member can be slowed but not cured; the player has to decide what to do with them before the return voyage.
- Bjarn's Dwarven contact at the interior camp has residuum samples the Empire doesn't know about.
- Isharr offers the player a choice: take her findings back to Ziramat — giving the dragon-folk what they came for — or keep them. Her queen will know either way.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Full extraction completed:** a hold full of residuum that will make the Aether-Helm very powerful and very dangerous; the player knows what they've brought home.
- **Partial or aborted extraction:** less residuum, more credibility with Isharr and the Ziramati; Dorn's hostility.
- **Either way:** the player now carries the truth of the Isle. What they do with it — whom they tell, whom they protect, what they build toward — shapes the rest of the campaign.
- Significant pay; potential access to residuum-enhanced equipment (which has a cost the player now understands).
