# The Deserters' Cache

**Quest ID:** q_deserters_cache
**Type:** Investigation / pursuit (with combat)
**Difficulty:** Hard (suggested levels 6–8; 4–5 sessions)
**Destination:** Pattis, the western lanes, and the Reef-Born territories
**Posted by:** Aether-Helm Internal Commission — classified, not on any public board

> GM-facing brief. Three Aetherwright deserters from the Helm have been running unauthorized research on the residuum's effects on living Weave-users. Their findings are accurate, dangerous, and directly contradict the official position that residuum extraction is safe. Someone in the Helm wants them found — but the player should arrive at the question of *why* before they decide what "found" means.

## Premise

Three senior Aetherwrights left the Helm's service two years ago under circumstances classified at the highest level. The official record says desertion; the unofficial word in the Helm is that they were asking questions about the residuum's biological effects that the Commission found inconvenient. The player is hired — discreetly — to locate them and recover their research. Whether to recover the researchers is left to the player's judgment.

## The Researchers

- **Oran Veth** — the eldest, a former Helm master-wright who was the most dangerous Aetherweaver of his generation before he left. He is not hiding because he is afraid; he is hiding because he needs more time. He will speak to the player if they approach without threat.
- **Danu Sael** — a younger wright, Oran's former apprentice, who drew on raw residuum once in a controlled experiment and has been slowly changing since. She does not know how long she has. She is the proof of concept they never wanted.
- **Rekar** — an Elven wright whose connection to the Fae gives him a different angle on what the residuum is doing; he suspects the truth is older and worse than Oran's biochemical model suggests.

## The Research

Oran's cache contains three years of observations on residuum exposure in living subjects, synthesized into a finding no Helm scholar has reached because no Helm scholar was allowed to ask the question:

*Residuum does not merely damage living Weavers. It replaces their own planar connection with the Isle's — slowly, completely, and irreversibly. The Isle-Touched are not corrupted; they are converted. At the end of the process, the Isle has another native.*

This is not a safety concern. It is a cosmological one. And it is the most dangerous document in the western reaches.

## Arc Structure

**Commission → The Trail → Finding Oran → Danu & Rekar → The Decision.**

### Session 1 — The Commission & The Trail

The Helm's handler is **First Wright Saer** — calm, meticulous, and lying about something the player will have to identify by watching her closely. Her stated goal: recover the research; bring the deserters back for a "review" she describes as administrative.

The trail begins in Pattis: a safe-house that was abandoned in a hurry, contacts who know less than they're saying, a letter that went to Borkum Reef. The player follows the evidence west.

### Session 2 — The Western Lanes

The deserters have been moving between Reef-Born-adjacent communities and Zergas-by-Azure, doing field research at each stop. The player follows the trail through two or three locations — each visit surfaces a piece of their findings and a person who has reason to protect them.

One interview subject is a former Admiralty sailor who participated in an early residuum extraction and has been quietly changing since. He doesn't know what's happening to him. Oran's notes describe his case exactly.

### Session 3 — Finding Oran

Oran Veth is in a hidden location — a decommissioned waystation or a Reef-Born harbor they've been allowed to use in exchange for keeping the community's wards running. He knows the player is coming; he has had time to prepare an argument rather than a defense.

He is not hostile. He will present his findings clearly and in full if the player gives him the chance. He wants to know: who sent the player, and what do they plan to do with what he's about to show them?

**Danu Sael** is present. She is visibly changed — not dangerous, not yet Isle-Touched, but the Weave around her behaves differently than it should. She demonstrates, quietly, what three years of residuum proximity does, and what drawing on it even once did.

**Rekar** is absent — tracking a thread he won't share by messenger. He returns by Session 4.

### Session 4 — Rekar's Return & The Decision

Rekar returns with a piece of information that recontextualizes everything: the residuum extraction ships are already carrying Isle-Touched cargo back to Pattis — trace amounts, contaminated equipment, a storage worker who touched the wrong crate. The conversion is already in the city. Slowly.

**First Wright Saer's actual goal** becomes clear to any player who has been watching: she wants the research destroyed and the deserters silenced, not because the findings are wrong, but because the Helm has already committed too much to the extraction program to reverse it without destroying the Helm's political position. The research being public would end careers. The research being suppressed would end people.

The player chooses:
- **Return the research to the Helm:** the deserters likely don't survive the administrative review. The findings disappear. The extraction continues.
- **Make the findings public:** a scandal the Helm may or may not survive; the extraction program halts or is reviewed; Danu Sael becomes politically impossible to disappear.
- **Find a third channel:** give the findings to someone outside the Helm who has the standing to act on them — Isharr and the Ziramati, Athregal, a sympathetic Council member. The information survives; the deserters may.

## Combat

The Helm's retrieval team arrives in Session 3 or 4, depending on pacing: a professional extraction squad sent by Saer when she realizes the player's approach is going to take longer than a simple recovery. Fighting them is not the only option, but it is an option. Calibrate to levels 6–8.

## Complications

- Danu Sael doesn't want to be saved — she wants to finish the research. She is lucid now, and the window is closing. Keeping her alive and free may require taking her somewhere the Helm can't reach.
- Rekar's Fae contacts have a different interpretation of the residuum that agrees with Oran's conclusions and adds something neither of them expected: the dead primordial's conversion process has a will in it, even now.
- Saer has leverage on the player from a prior commission. She'll use it.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Research returned:** Helm approval; the deserters' fate; the extraction program continues unimpeded. The player knows what they helped bury.
- **Research publicized:** political crisis; the extraction program under scrutiny; Danu Sael alive and visible; the Helm hostile.
- **Third channel used:** the information is in the world in a form the Helm can't easily suppress; the deserters may survive; the player has a named enemy in the Helm.
- Access to Oran's research as a worldbuilding artifact; Rekar as a contact if he survives; Danu Sael's situation as an ongoing thread.
