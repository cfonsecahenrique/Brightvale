# New Armis Campaign — Worldbuilding Overview

A **prequel** campaign set during the golden age of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]**, centuries before the [[Apostasy]] that shattered the world of the *Brightvale* setting. Where Brightvale is dark, post-apocalyptic survival, this campaign is its mirror image: a **bright, vibrant, sea-faring age of discovery** at the height of empire — Hellenic golden age crossed with the ancient Phoenician thalassocracy.

> **Tone:** exploration, wonder, ambition, and the first cracks of hubris. The audience knows what is coming; the characters do not.

**The campaign begins in `994 AF`** — 994 years after the founding of [[Armis]], at the height of empire. See [[Timeline (Armis)]].

> **★ The central frontier:** **[[Adamantis]]** — a dangerous, unexplored island in the western ocean that all three great powers ([[The Armisian Empire|Armis]], [[Athregal]], [[Ziramat]]) are racing to colonise. It fights back, and it bleeds raw Aether. This is where the venturers go.
>
> **★ The driving agenda:** see **[[Plot Hooks]]** for the Empire's expansionist designs — the engines of the campaign.

## Core Premise
- Two and a half centuries ago (**744 AF**), the Empire achieved **[[Aetherweave Mastery|mastery of strongly flowing Aetherweave]]** — the breakthrough that launched its golden age.
- Its seafaring runs on **command of the Water and Air planes**: planar-powered fleets cross the **[[The Azurian Ocean|Azurian Ocean]]**, returning each year with exotic artefacts, animals, and riches from the [[The Western Reaches|new world]].
- The Empire sponsors a constant stream of new-world expeditions, most launched from the island-metropolis of **[[Pattis]]** — a city built both above and beneath the waves.
- **[[Dwarven Clans]]** have only recently made contact with the Empire — a relationship that began in bloodshed and is now an uneasy trade.

## The Empire
- **[[The Armisian Empire]]** — the central hub note. A confederation more than a monolith: an imperial core surrounded by proud, semi-independent city-states.
- **Capital:** the twin cities of **[[Armis]]** and its subjugated neighbour **[[Caen]]**, now slowly merging into one great metropolis.
- **Free city-states:** independent polities that pay the Empire lip service and little else. Chief among them is **[[Pattis]]**, the westernmost city.

## The Map (see [[The Known World]])
The Empire rings the **[[The Central Sea]]**:
- **Northern coasts (heartland):** **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]** (twin capital), **[[Lyx]]**.
- **Roaming the sea:** **[[Pattis]]** — a *moving* island-metropolis that favours the western edges.
- **Southern coasts:** the subjugated necromantic empire of **[[An'Hiseret]]** (ancient-Egyptian flavour), conquered after the Awakening.
- **The west:** the great landmass of **[[The Titan's Sword]]**; beyond it the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]** leading to the new world ([[The Western Reaches]]). Across the ocean: the Dwarven realm of **[[Athregal]]** to the north, and the crimson desert empire of **[[Ziramat]]** (newly contacted) to the south.

## Key Locations
- **[[Armis]]** — imperial capital; seat of power.
- **[[Caen]]** — subjugated twin of Armis, merging with it.
- **[[Pattis]]** — island-metropolis and gateway to the new world. (Numenor / Atlantis inspiration.)
- **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]** — far-western outpost on the [[The Titan's Sword]]; site of first contact with the Dwarves.
- **[[Adamantis]]** — the dangerous, Aether-rich island at the heart of the three-way colonial race; the campaign's expedition frontier.

## Peoples
- **[[Dwarven Clans]]** — recent arrivals to imperial society; first contact was warlike, now an uneasy trading partnership. Small Dwarven communities are appearing in [[Pattis]] and [[Armis]].

## Dating Convention
This campaign uses **AF (After Founding)** dating, counting up from the founding of [[Armis]]. The present is **994 AF**. See **[[Timeline (Armis)]]**.
