# Bestiary — Index

Creatures of the Armisian age — note the **tonal difference** from *Brightvale*: this is a bright, pre-cataclysm world. Expect sea-beasts, leviathans, exotic new-world fauna, and living dragons rather than Umbra-corrupted horrors.

## Monsters of the Elder Days
The curses, servants and spawn the gods left behind when they withdrew. See **[[_Heroes and Legends]]**.
- **[[The Bull-King of the Horned Court]]**: the oath-breaking king, bull-headed and deathless beneath [[Ambrakis and the Horned Court|Ambrakis]].
- **[[The Hydra of Enneara]]**: lesser brood of Tiamat, lying across the short lane to [[Adamantis]]. It hunts Aether.
- **[[The Cyclopes of the Pyrrhai]]**: Hevrek's abandoned forge-giants, and the blind Eldest who remembers the theft.
- **[[The Gorgon Ysmene]]**: the mother whose prayer for vengeance was answered, in the [[The Garden of Stone|Garden of Stone]].
