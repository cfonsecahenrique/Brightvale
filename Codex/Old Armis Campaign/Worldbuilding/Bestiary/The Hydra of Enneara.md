# The Hydra of Enneara

The nine-throated serpent of **[[Enneara]]**, the island that sits on the short sea-lane between the [[The Titan's Sword|Titan's Sword]] and **[[Adamantis]]**. It killed **Alkaon the Unwearied**, the greatest hero of the Elder Days, and it has been growing ever since. *(The tale: **[[_Heroes and Legends#III. The Labours and Death of Alkaon|The Labours and Death of Alkaon]]**.)*

> **Kind:** *Spawn of the Deep* — a piece of primordial hunger in serpent form. It is not a person and cannot be bargained with.

## What It Is
- **Nine heads on one great body.** The body is a bloated serpent-trunk as long as a war-galley, coiled through the tidal caves under the island. Each head is the size of a horse's body. In the oldest tellings there were nine. Sailors who have seen it lately count more and stop counting.
- **Two for one.** Cut a head away and two grow from the stump within the hour. Alkaon learned this at the cost of his life. The stories say fire on the stump stops the regrowth, and Ziramati sailors think *their* fire might. Nobody has tried it on purpose.
- **Its blood is poison.** The shallows around Enneara are milk-green and lifeless. Fish die on the tide-line, and nothing grows below the high-water mark. A blade wetted in its blood keeps the poison for years, and Alkaon's last arrows were said to kill anything they scratched.
- **Its breath is a fog.** At dawn the island hides in a green-white haze that smells of rotting kelp. Breathe enough of it and your lungs blister.

## It Hunts Aether
This is the thing the Empire hasn't worked out yet. *(GM truth, discoverable in play.)*
- **Aether draws it.** The Hydra is lesser brood of [[Tiamat]], and like its mother's dragons it feeds on the world's magic. Wooden hulls under plain sail sometimes slip past Enneara in a calm, unnoticed. **Aether-driven Armisian ships don't.** The heads come up from the green water toward the planar engines as surely as sharks toward blood.
- **It has grown since the Awakening.** Before 744 AF Enneara was a bad-luck island that sailors gave a wide berth. In two and a half centuries of Aether-hulls passing on the western lane, the Hydra has fed and swelled and grown more heads. In the Empire's own records the island goes from *"avoid"* to *"impassable"* in that time. Nobody has put the two facts together.
- **It may smell Adamantis.** On still nights the heads turn west-southwest, toward the Isle and its bleeding residuum, all at once.

## How It Is Feared
- **The Long Lane.** Armisian convoys to [[Adamantis]] do not take the short lane past Enneara. They swing far north, which adds three days, extra water, and one more stretch of open sea for the [[The Reef-Born|Reef-Born]] to raid. Every captain on the western run has an opinion on those three days, and none of them will vote to give them up.
- **Sailors' signs.** A green slick on the water means you've gone too far south. Dead fish floating in a line mean *turn now*. If you hear more than one hiss, don't count them.
- **"Gone to Alkaon."** It is western-sea slang for a ship lost without a trace.

## The Truth (GM)
- **Whoever kills it owns the short lane.** That's three days off every crossing to Adamantis. In a three-way race for the Isle it is worth more than a fleet, and [[Athregal]] and [[Ziramat]] both know it as well as the Empire does.
- **Killing it may be impossible by ordinary means.** It needs fire on every stump, the Ninefold Bane, or something stranger. Starving it might work too: an Aether-dark approach, ships with their engines quiet, crews rowing by hand as heroes did.
- **Killing it is also the wrong lesson.** A dead Hydra proves to the Empire that it can kill the old powers. That is a step down the road to the [[Apostasy]].

## Hooks (GM)
- **The Admiralty's wager.** A Pattisian syndicate will pay a fortune to the crew that can prove the short lane passable, and pay less, quietly, to the crew that proves the *Athregali* have found a way through.
- **Recover the Ninefold Bane** from the landing-beach while the Hydra sleeps in the fog. *(See [[Enneara]].)*
- **A Ziramati captain** proposes a joint hunt. Dragonfire and Armisian steel, the spoils split. Neither side trusts the other to stop when it's dead.
