# The Cyclopes of the Pyrrhai

The one-eyed giants of the volcanic **[[The Pyrrhai|Pyrrhai]]**, smiths to the forge-god [[Hevrek]] in the Age of Heroes. When the gods withdrew, nobody told the Cyclopes to stop. They have kept the god's forge burning for an age, waiting for a master who doesn't come, and they remember exactly what mortals did to them while they waited. *(The tale: **[[_Heroes and Legends#IV. The Smiling Thief and the Blinded One|The Smiling Thief and the Blinded One]]**.)*

> **Kind:** *Abandoned Servants* — made for a god's work and never dismissed. They are a people, not beasts: they have names, grudges, laws, and a craft no mortal can match.

## What They Are
- **Giants of the forge.** They are three times a man's height, broad and soot-black, each with a single great eye that glows faintly red in the dark, like a coal. Their skin is scarred by sparks from a thousand years at the anvil and doesn't burn easily.
- **Few, and fewer every age.** Perhaps a few dozen remain. No young have been seen. They tend huge, rank-smelling flocks of sheep on the island slopes and work the forge in shifts that never stop.
- **Ourkon the Eldest.** Ourkon is their patriarch, the one Ereskes blinded. He no longer works the anvil. He sits on the black-sand beach of the largest island, listening for oars, and the rest of the Cyclopes do as he says.

## Their Craft
- **The god's own forge.** The Pyrrhai's central vent is Hevrek's first anvil-fire, and the Cyclopes still work it. They make things no Venagillium smith can make: bronze that will not dent, edges that will not dull, and hinges and gears for Daedrion's old automatons, the Bronze Watchmen.
- **They forged the heroes' arms.** Half the weapons hanging at **[[Hoplonesos]]** were made here. The Cyclopes remember each one and who carried it, and they consider every one of them still *theirs*.
- **They are still making something.** Ships that pass at night hear the hammers. No one has asked what the Cyclopes forge, for a god who has been gone an age.

## How They Are Feared
- **Nothing leaves the islands.** That is the law of the Cyclopes since the theft. Shipwrecked crews are kept, as labour or as food, depending on the season and on Ourkon's temper. Ships that land for water lose their water-party. Ships that land for plunder are found, months later, beached on the far side of the Central Sea, with every crewman's single left eye put out.
- **The ember tribute.** Every spring the Smiths' Guild of [[Venagillium]] sends a small ship east carrying a sealed bronze lamp lit from the Forgehall's never-dying fire. It is the stolen ember, returned in token, as an apology the city has made every year for longer than it has kept records. The Cyclopes take the lamp, and in exchange they do not come west. Every year **one of the emissaries does not come home**. The Guild chooses the crew by lot and does not publish the list. *(See [[The Pyrrhai]].)*
- **Black smoke.** When the central vent smokes black instead of grey, the Cyclopes are angry. Fishing villages along the far-eastern coast pull their boats up, douse their hearths, and don't light a lamp until the smoke runs grey again.

## The Truth (GM)
- **They want their god back more than anything.** They are servants who have lost their master, not monsters. A venturer who carries real news of [[Hevrek]], or who can prove they have stood before a **withdrawn god** at [[The Edges of the World|the edges of the world]], would be received as a herald, not a thief.
- **Ourkon wants Ereskes.** Failing Ereskes, he wants Ereskes's blood. A descendant of the Smiling Thief in a landing party is in mortal danger, and is also the only person who could make the apology Ourkon has waited an age for.
- **What they are forging** is the GM's to decide. It could be a weapon for the god's return, a cage, or a hull. If the Empire finds out, it will want it.

## Hooks (GM)
- **The emissary lot falls on a friend**, a patron's child, or a player character's apprentice. This year the Cyclopes have asked for *two*.
- **Commission the impossible.** An [[Aetherweave Mastery|Aetherwright]] needs a component only Hevrek's forge can make, and someone has to go and ask.
- **A Bronze Watchman's heart.** The Cyclopes will trade one working piece of Daedrion's work for a single thing: the true name of the island where Hevrek withdrew.
- *Use the `generate-daggerheart-adversary` skill for Bruiser/Leader stat blocks. Ourkon works well as a Solo who cannot see but hears everything.*
