# Adamantis

> **Neutral GM title.** The island has **no shared name** — each culture calls it something different (see below). "Adamantis" is the campaign's neutral handle.

A **large, dangerous, unexplored island** in the western waters — **southwest of [[The Titan's Sword]]** and **northwest of [[Ziramat]]**, out where the three Azurian powers' reaches overlap. It is the central frontier of the age, and very likely the central stage of the campaign.

## The Isle's Three Names
Each power names the Isle in its own tongue, and the name reveals how that culture *sees* it:
- **Armisian — *Adamantis*** ("the Untamed"). The Hellenic-Phoenician scholars' name: an unconquerable land that refuses to be subdued. Pattisian sailors mutter darker bynames for it.
- **Athregali — *Reiðrey*** (anglicised *Reidrey*, "the Wrath-Isle"). The Norse-tongued Dwarves name it for its fury — an island that rages against any who set foot on it.
- **Ziramati — *Tiâmtu*** (anglicised *Tiamtu*). The dragon-folk name it for **[[Tiamat|Tiamat, the World-Mother]]** herself — sensing in the Isle a sleeping primordial power of the same ancient blood as dragons, older even than their **[[Azhdashar|Dragon-Goddess]]**. Whether a lost dragon-sibling sleeps here, or the World-Mother's own relic, is the Isle's deepest secret. *(See [[Tiamat]].)*

## The Three-Way Scramble
All **three great empires** are here at once, each landing from a **different side** of the island and each holding a single fortified **base-town**:
- **[[The Armisian Empire]]** — approaching from the northeast, off [[The Titan's Sword]]. *(Base-town: TBD.)*
- **[[Athregal]]** (the Dwarves) — approaching from the north. *(Base-town: TBD.)*
- **[[Ziramat]]** — approaching from the southeast. *(Base-town: TBD.)*

None has penetrated far. Each clings to its foothold and probes the interior, racing the others — and the island itself.

## The Island Fights Back
The Isle is not passive ground. It **resists the explorers**:
- **The natives** are rarely seen — elusive, violent, and deeply **synchronised with the primordial powers of the island**. They strike and vanish; almost nothing is known of them.
- The land itself seems to turn against intruders, as though the Isle and its people move as one.

## The Prize: Aether-Heavy Residuum
The Isle is the source of a powerful **Aether-heavy residuum** — a concentration of raw Aether unlike anything in the known world. Every power covets it: for the Armisians it is fuel for **[[Aetherweave Mastery]]** beyond anything achieved at home. The scramble is, at bottom, a scramble for this.

## The Truth (GM secret)
**The Ziramati are right.** The Isle is the resting place of one of **Tiamat's four lost [[Primordial Dragons|primordial dragons]]** — and it is, almost certainly, **dead**. Everything strange about the Isle follows from that one fact:
- The **residuum** is the dead primordial's magic still **bleeding into the earth** — a dead god's leaking lifeblood, not a mineral. The powers racing to mine it are desecrating a corpse-god.
- The **hostile ecosystem** and the land "turning against" intruders are the dragon's world-shaping magic still **reshaping the Isle** in its own image, with no living will to direct it.
- The **natives** are the people that magic made — bound to the dead primordial, "synchronised with the primordial powers" because those powers *are* the air they breathe.
- *Tiâmtu*, the Ziramati name, was never poetry: they sense their lost aunt/sibling in the ground. *(See [[Tiamat]], [[Primordial Dragons]].)*

> GM dial: is it truly dead, merely dormant, or *dying* — and does anything remain that could **wake**? Whatever the answer, exploiting it is the seed of the [[Apostasy]].

## Hooks & Foreshadowing (GM)
- This is the **expedition engine** of the campaign — the place player venturers are sent, the prequel's answer to the *Brightvale* Wastes.
- The three-way colonial race is a pressure cooker: rival expeditions, sabotage, uneasy alliances, and shared danger.
- **An island that fights back, primordial native powers, and a flood of raw Aether that everyone wants to exploit** — handle with care: this is exactly the kind of hubris that could one day birth the [[Apostasy]].
