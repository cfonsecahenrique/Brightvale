# Nanobanana Map Prompts — Old Armis Campaign

Prompts for generating parchment-style illustrated maps of the campaign's main locations, matching the reference world map (aged parchment, hand-inked cartography, sepia/teal wash, ornamental cartouches).

Workflow: paste the **Style Block** into every prompt, then append the location-specific block for the place you want. Swap in the reference image itself as a style/composition reference image if the tool supports image-conditioning alongside text.

---

## Style Block (common to all prompts)

```
Style: hand-drawn fantasy cartography on aged, weathered parchment. Warm sepia and ochre paper tones with soft brown foxing stains and a darkened, deckle-edged border. Fine black ink linework in the manner of a Renaissance-era survey map — cross-hatched shading for relief, stippled texture for terrain. Muted, desaturated watercolor wash restricted to a narrow palette: sea-glass teal/blue-green for water, warm tan/umber for land, dull brown for mountains and buildings. Labels in an elegant engraved serif or blackletter display caps, slightly worn. Include a compass rose (or simplified wind-rose) in one corner, fine gold-brown decorative corner flourishes/scrollwork on at least one corner, and small hand-drawn iconography for landmarks (miniature towers, ships, waves, trees) rendered as tiny pictorial glyphs rather than modern map symbols. No modern typography, no gloss, no bright saturated color, no photographic elements — the whole piece should read as a single, slightly worn physical artifact, corners softly curling, ink a little faded in places. Aspect ratio 4:3 landscape.
```

## Armis-Caen

```
[STYLE BLOCK], drawn as a flat top-down schematic city map — the same diagrammatic bird's-eye-from-directly-above perspective as the rest of this set, not a painted or angled aerial view. Line-and-wash cartography, not a painterly scene.

Subject: a detailed top-down map of Armis-Caen, twin capital of the Armisian Empire — a vast circular walled Bronze Age Hellenic/Phoenician metropolis, half-merged from two once-rival cities, its impossibly tall, polished white limestone walls drawn as concentric rings narrowing toward the crowded center. Signal-fire towers dot the outer wall's crest at regular intervals. Show, laid out as distinct labeled districts:
- **Harbour quarter** on the coastal edge: the Cothon, a small circular enclosed military port lined with vaulted stone ship-shed (neoria) arches and dark war-galley glyphs, its channel-mouth marked with a chain; the separate, busier Emporion of quays and warehouses; a tall white octagonal lighthouse at the harbour mouth.
- **Civic heart** near the middle ring: the Great Agora as a large open plaza ringed by colonnaded stoas, a small semicircular council chamber beside it, and a tiered open-air theatre cut into a slope facing the agora.
- **Temple district**, occupying the upper/inner tier of the city and drawn as its own dense, clearly bounded quarter: twelve individually distinct temple footprints, no two alike in shape or size, one for each god of the Armisian Pantheon — Anaru (sky/thunder/kings, the largest, most central), Thalassos (sea/storms/sailors, oriented toward the harbour), Aetheon (war), Hevrek (forge/craft, near the smithing district), Aesaris (desire/beauty), Kalvris (trade/luck/thieves, near the Emporion), Setharis (wisdom/oracles), Morvath (death/oaths, angled toward the necropolis road), Maeris (childbirth/mothers, smaller, tucked among the poorer blocks), Rethis (scavengers/spies, unobtrusive, at the district's edge), Aelun (moon/tides/dreams), and Terra (harvest/earth). Render this quarter so the sheer variety of temple shapes reads clearly even at map scale — it should look like a crowded architectural argument, not a uniform row.
- **The Palace**: a sprawling, many-winged, labyrinthine complex at the city's high center, drawn as an irregular sprawl of courtyards and light-wells rather than a single symmetrical building.
- A **processional avenue** lined with small victory-column glyphs running from the main gate inward toward the temple district and palace.
- A **necropolis** of round beehive-tomb glyphs on a hill just outside the walls, facing the sea, linked to the city by its own road.
- A scatter of small galley/ship glyphs in the harbour and open water, and a faint line of green-gray, snow-capped mountains as a distant background terrain feature beyond the landward wall.

Palette note: keep the set's standard sepia/ochre/teal parchment wash as the base, with restrained warm accents (a little terracotta, bronze, and Tyrian purple) reserved for the temple district and palace roofs only, so those districts pop as the city's richest, most consecrated ground without breaking the flat map style.

Label the city "ARMIS-CAEN" in large banner lettering, with smaller labels for the Cothon, Emporion, Agora, Temple District, Palace, and Necropolis.
```

## Pattis

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a map of Pattis, the moving island-metropolis of the Armisian Empire — a circular disc-shaped city floating on open water, designed in perfect concentric rings (unlike organically-grown mainland cities), half its structure rising in tiers above the waterline and half implied beneath it via faint dotted linework showing submerged domed districts. Show:
- A central circular military harbor (the Cothon) at the exact heart of the disc, with a single narrow gated channel and ship-sheds ringing its inner rim.
- A commercial harbour district (the Emporion) ringing the Cothon on the seaward side.
- A ring of twelve small distinct temple glyphs forming a "Sacred Ring" around the upper tier.
- A domed or glass-paned structure set apart and drawn partially submerged/underwater with wave-line hatching over it, labeled as the Temple of Thalassos.
- Stepped terrace rings on the outermost band representing wealthy quarters facing the open sea.
- A small tower motif near the center marking the Aether-Helm, the chamber that steers the city.
- Faint current/wave lines and a few small ship glyphs in the surrounding sea, since the whole city drifts on the water.
- Label the city "PATTIS" in large banner lettering at the top.
```

## Zergas-by-Azure

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a map of Zergas-by-Azure, a rough far-western Armisian frontier outpost town at the northernmost point of a long peninsula (the Titan's Sword), facing the open ocean. Show a small, unwalled or lightly-palisaded colonial port — a modest harbor with a handful of ships and simple quays, timber-and-stone buildings scattered without the geometric planning of the imperial capital, a market/trading square, a garrison blockhouse, and a scatter of prospector camps and supply-yards at the edges. Include small dwarven-craft details (stone/timber structures, forge-smoke glyphs) mixed among the more improvised Armisian buildings, hinting at the uneasy Dwarven trade contact here. Surrounding terrain: rocky coastal cliffs, sparse windswept trees, and open ocean waves to the north and west. Label the settlement "ZERGAS-BY-AZURE," with a small compass note toward "THE AZURIAN OCEAN."
```

## Athregal

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a regional map of Athregal, the Norse-inspired Dwarven homeland — a cold northern realm of fjords, glaciers, and stone-hold clan-holds running up into icebound mountains. Show:
- A great mountain hold/city carved into cliff faces and stone, with forge-smoke glyphs, longhouse roofs, and runic banner-style lettering for the settlement name.
- Several smaller clan-hold glyphs (small fortified stone settlements) scattered along fjord coastlines, connected by mountain passes.
- Jagged glacier and ice-field terrain to the north, rendered with sharp hatched peaks and pale blue-white ice texture within the map's muted palette.
- Small warning glyphs (crossed axes, a crude giant footprint) marking the northern glacier frontier where the war against the giants is fought.
- Fjords and cold coastal waters to the south/west with a few longship glyphs.
Label the region "ATHREGAL" in a bold, angular rune-influenced display lettering.
```

## An'Hiseret

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a regional map of An'Hiseret, the subjugated ancient-Egyptian-inspired river empire on the southern coast of the Central Sea, now under Armisian rule. Show:
- The great river Seretis running from an unmapped, unexplored interior in the south up to the coast in the north, its banks lined with a strip of cultivated, fertile land distinct from the surrounding desert.
- Several river-town glyphs along its length, labeled Saidz, Xol, Atnas, and Atir, each drawn as small clusters of flat-roofed stone buildings with an obelisk or temple-pylon silhouette.
- Desert terrain flanking the river in the map's muted tan palette, with sparse dune-hatching.
- One or two monumental tomb/temple glyphs (temple-pylon or small pyramid forms) near the largest town, hinting at the god-princes and the necromantic Weave.
- A coastline at the map's northern edge opening onto "THE CENTRAL SEA," with a few Armisian ship glyphs offshore signifying occupation.
Label the region "AN'HISERET" and the river "THE SERETIS."
```

## Ziramat

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a regional map of Ziramat, a crimson desert empire on a great bay, ruled by dragon-folk — Akkadian/Persian architecture under a draconic overlay. Show:
- Cliff-cities carved directly into red sandstone canyon walls around a wide bay, drawn as tiered facades cut into the rock rather than freestanding buildings, with small dragon-glyph motifs (wings, coiled tails) worked into the linework near the capital.
- The capital city, Ziramat, at the head of the bay, larger and more elaborate than the others.
- Smaller cliff-city glyphs labeled Elikrys, Hiraks, Arakses, and Yaviks scattered along the coastline and canyon country.
- A desertic, crimson-tinted interior — use a slightly warmer rust/ochre wash than the standard palette to suggest red rock, with dune and canyon hatching.
- The bay itself opening onto "THE TOPAZ SEA," rendered in the same muted teal water tone as the rest of the set.
- A small coiled-dragon cartouche or sigil worked into one corner in place of (or alongside) the compass rose.
Label the region "ZIRAMAT."
```

## Adamantis

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a map of Adamantis (the "Untamed Isle"), a large, dangerous, unexplored island where three empires are racing to colonise from three different coasts. Show:
- A rugged, mostly-unmapped island interior: dense forest/jungle hatching giving way to blank, sparsely-detailed terrain toward the center, with a few ominous unexplained glyphs (broken ruin shapes, a coiled shadow) suggesting the sleeping primordial beneath the land, without depicting a literal dragon.
- Three small fortified base-town glyphs on three different coasts: one Armisian-style (white stone, circular walls) on the northeast shore, one Dwarven-style (heavy timber-and-stone, forge-smoke) on the north shore, and one Ziramati-style (cliff-cut, red stone) on the southeast shore — each drawn distinctly per that culture's architecture.
- Faint, incomplete coastal survey lines that trail off and become dashed/uncertain further inland, as if the mapmakers themselves never finished charting it.
- Small hazard glyphs scattered near the coastline clearings: crude native-camp markers, warning symbols, and a raw-residuum vein motif (small jagged crystal glyph) near the island's heart.
- Open ocean surrounding the isle in the standard teal wash, labeled toward "THE AZURIAN OCEAN" and "THE TOPAZ SEA" at opposite edges.
Label the island "ADAMANTIS," with the two alternate names ("Reiðrey" and "Tiâmtu") in smaller script beneath, as if added by different cartographers.
```
