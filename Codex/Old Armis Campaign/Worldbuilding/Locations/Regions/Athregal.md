# Athregal

The realm of the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]** — the homeland of the Dwarven people who recently made contact with the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]**. The two met as rival colonisers near **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]** on the **[[The Titan's Sword]]**.

> **Cultural inspiration (GM):** **Norse.** A cold, clan-based, seafaring warrior culture of feuding houses, raiders, and skalds — hardened by their endless war against the giants of the ice.

## Location
Athregal lies **north of the [[The Azurian Ocean]]** — beyond the open western ocean, off the edge of the Empire's familiar map, a cold realm running up into **northern glaciers**. Its clans reached **south** to colonise the western frontier of the **[[The Titan's Sword]]**, where they collided with the Empire pushing west, meeting at **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]**.

## The War in the Glaciers
Athregal's defining struggle is its long, grinding war against the **[[Glacier Giants|giants]]** of the **northern glaciers** — an ancient enemy that presses down on the Dwarven homeland from the ice. This northern pressure is part of what drives the clans southward and outward, and it is one of the few things capable of forcing the feuding clans to set aside their quarrels and unite.

## The Race for the Isle
Athregal is one of three powers — with **[[The Armisian Empire]]** and **[[Ziramat]]** — colonising **[[The Contested Isle]]** in the western ocean, each from its own base-town. The Dwarves approach the Isle from the north.

## Religion
The **[[_Athregali Pantheon|Athregali Pantheon]]** — a Norse faith of forge, frost, war, and fate, whose gods are themselves bound up in the war against the [[Glacier Giants|giants]].

## The Hidden Forge (GM — looking forward)
Athregal has been quietly carving its own residuum from **[[The Contested Isle]]** since before the Empire even noticed it doing so. When the Armisian elites turn the **[[The Amun-Orb|Amun-Orb]]** toward deathless rule during the **[[Timeline (Armis)|Dark Reign]]**, Athregal reads the warning correctly: a stretched, immortal-led empire conquering its way outward will reach the glaciers eventually. Rather than arm its own clans and risk open war it cannot yet win, Athregal forges residuum-weapons in secret and gives them to the one people already inside the Empire's reach with a grudge to spend them on — the conquered **[[Aldari]]**. It is Athregali iron and Athregali residuum-craft, as much as Aldari fury, that lets the plateau retake itself and march on **[[Armis]]**.

## Clan Warfare & Imperial Meddling (GM / [[Plot Hooks]])
The Dwarves of Athregal are **bellicose and fractured**, their clans forever feuding — yet when they **unite**, they become a serious thorn in Armisian plans. The Empire's standing answer is **divide-and-rule**: send envoys to **back rival clans against the ruling ones**, and keep Athregal too divided to ever stand as one. *(See hook #5 in [[Plot Hooks]].)*

## Hooks (GM)
- The northern war is leverage: the Empire could arm the giants' enemies, broker against them, or quietly *prolong* the conflict to keep Athregal busy and divided (see the divide-and-rule agenda above).
- A Dwarven realm across the Azurian is a natural destination for the great expeditions, and a power the Empire does not yet truly know.
- The Hidden Forge contradicts the official, *official* line that Athregal "doesn't get involved" in inner Armisian politics. A foreign agent or Aldari survivor who can prove it could shake either realm.
