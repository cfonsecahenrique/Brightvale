# The Blood Empire

A great civilisation of **blood-sorcery** at the far-eastern edge of the world, past the **[[The Eastern Steppes|Great Grass-Sea]]** — square, walled cities "of purity" ruled by an **ancient dragon**, at the foot of the Mountains where the world is said to end. No Armisian has ever stood in it; the **[[The Eastern Steppes|Orcenfolk]]** carry the only reports.

> **Cultural inspiration (GM):** an ordered, ancestor-and-blood civilisation of walled cities — alien, disciplined, and powerful — with a draconic god-ruler in the deep-time register of **[[Ziramat]]**.

## What the Orcs Tell
Past even their grass, the Orcenfolk speak of a great power dwelling in **massive, square, walled settlements "of purity,"** running on **blood-magic** and ancestor-rite, and ruled — they insist — by **another ancient dragon.** To the **far north-east** of it rise the **Mountains of the World**, so vast and unbroken the Orcs hold that the world itself *ends* there.

## A Lost Dragon, Found
> **GM truth.** The Blood Empire's dragon-ruler is **one of [[Tiamat|Tiamat's]] five primordial brood** — almost certainly one of the **lost three** (see **[[Primordial Dragons]]**). With **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]** in [[Ziramat]] and the dead one in **[[The Contested Isle]]**, this places a **third** of the five — leaving **two** still genuinely unaccounted for. The blood-sorcery of its cities is its god-ruler's work, exactly as Azhdašar's crimson desert is hers: a primordial remaking its domain in its own image.

## Status
- **Wholly uncontacted.** The Empire knows it only as Orcenfolk rumour; reaching it means crossing the **[[The Eastern Steppes]]** first.
- Kin, in some sense neither power grasps, to **[[Ziramat]]** — two dragon-ruled civilisations at opposite edges of the same world.

## Hooks (GM)
- The first Armisian road east is a prize worth an empire's ambition — and a reckoning with a power and an agenda older than the Armisian calendar.
- What the player learns east could change the whole western race: the ancient dragons are a **brood with their own scattered design**, and the three-way scramble for the Isle is a small part of something far larger.
