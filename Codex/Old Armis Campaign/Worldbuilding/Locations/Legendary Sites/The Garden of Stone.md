# The Garden of Stone

A high, hidden valley in the hills **inland of [[Lissipos]]**, on the southeastern coast of the [[The Central Sea|Central Sea]]. It's a sheltered bowl of wild olive, thyme and limestone, and it is crowded with **statues**: hundreds of them, standing, kneeling, reaching, fleeing, every one perfectly lifelike and every one once alive. This is the home of **[[The Gorgon Ysmene|the Gorgon Ysmene]]**, and it has been for longer than the Empire has existed.

## The Valley
- **One way in.** A single goat-path climbs from the village of **Oinoe** through a narrow cleft. At its top stands an ancient **shrine of [[Kythera]]**, roofless, its altar worn hollow. This is where Ysmene prayed. Past it, the valley opens.
- **The statues.** They stand among the olives in no order, wherever they were when they met her eyes. Some are weathered almost featureless, ancient beyond reckoning. Some are recent enough that the chisel-marks of their sandal-straps are still sharp. Moss grows on the oldest. Lizards sun on the newest. Every one of them is looking toward the same place.
- **Phaedra.** Near the centre stands a young woman in bronze-age armour, one hand raised in greeting, her face turned toward the Gorgon's house with an expression of terrible kindness. A tarnished round shield lies at her feet, still polished on its inner face. This is **Phaedra Moon-Shield**, daughter of [[Aelun]], and the shield is hers. *(The tale: **[[_Heroes and Legends#VII. Phaedra's Pity|Phaedra's Pity]]**.)*
- **The house.** At the valley's head stands a low stone farmhouse with a sheepfold, a loom under an awning, and a child's grave with fresh wildflowers on it.

## Who Lies Here
Most of the statues are not heroes. A few are: armoured, armed, bearing the weapons of the Elder Days and of every age since, which is why relic-hunters keep coming. But most are **ordinary people**: soldiers, a magistrate, a slaver still holding his chain, a woman with her hands over her face. They climbed the path at night after the singing because they had done something they couldn't live with, and wanted someone to see it.

## Oinoe — the Village Below
A hamlet of shepherds and bee-keepers at the foot of the goat-path, a day's walk inland from Lissipos.
- **Eyes to the ground.** Children learn to walk the high pastures with their eyes on their feet before they learn their letters. Shepherds wear a fold of linen across the brow that can drop over the eyes in a heartbeat.
- **Offerings, not tribute.** The village leaves honey, bread, and wool at the Kythera shrine at the top of the path. Ysmene leaves cheese and woven cloth in return. Nobody in Oinoe would call her a neighbour, and nobody would let an outsider call her a monster in their hearing either.
- **The village fears heroes more than her.** Every few years someone arrives with mirrors, hired swords, and a patron's purse. Oinoe's elders try to talk them out of it, and then go indoors and close the shutters. Ysmene has never come down the mountain in anger. Nobody wants to find out what would make her.

## Hooks (GM)
- **Recover Phaedra's shield.** The shield is still a working mirror against the Gorgon's gaze, and it is lying at the feet of a statue thirty yards from Ysmene's door.
- **The latest statue.** A missing person the party is tracking is up there. Is there any way to bring someone back from stone? *(GM: perhaps, if they can be made to forgive themselves, which is a harder quest than any hero has tried.)*
- **The singing reached Lissipos** last week, for the first time in living memory. Something has changed up the mountain. *(See [[The Gorgon Ysmene#The Truth (GM)|the Truth]]: the last of the lord's line may be close.)*
