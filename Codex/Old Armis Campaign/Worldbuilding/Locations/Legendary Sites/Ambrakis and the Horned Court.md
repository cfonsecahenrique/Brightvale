# Ambrakis and the Horned Court

A small, old cliff-town on the **eastern coast of [[The Titan's Sword]]**, facing back across the [[The Central Sea|Central Sea]] toward the Empire it has never much belonged to. Ambrakis was a kingdom once, in the Age of Heroes. Now it is a colonial backwater of a few thousand souls, one bad harbour, and a very famous basement.

Under the old acropolis lies the **Horned Court**: the palace of King Asteros, walled in by Daedrion's maze, with **[[The Bull-King of the Horned Court|the Bull-King]]** still inside.

## The Town
- **Older than the Empire.** Ambrakis was a city before [[Armis]] was founded, and its people are quietly, stubbornly proud of it. Its walls are cyclopean, its frescoes faded, and its dialect almost unintelligible in the capital. The Empire took it two centuries ago, mainly as a water-stop for ships bound for [[Zergas-by-Azure]], and has thought about it as little as possible since.
- **Built *around* the hill, never *on* it.** The old acropolis at the town's heart is empty. It's a bare crown of rock and broken columns where nothing has been built for a thousand years. The town rings it like a crowd keeping its distance.
- **No cellars, no wells inside the old wall.** Water comes from cisterns on the cliff-top and from a spring outside the town. *(See [[The Bull-King of the Horned Court#How He Is Feared|How He Is Feared]].)*
- **A temple to the wrong god.** Ambrakis's one temple belongs to **[[Thalassos]]**, the god who cursed its king. The townsfolk serve him with a devotion that is half piety and half apology. Every year at the Opening of the Sea they drive a white bull into the maze, paying a debt that was never theirs.

## The Horned Court
- **The Bull Gate.** The only known entrance is a bronze-sheathed gate at the foot of the acropolis, carved with a bull's head whose horns form the lintel. It is barred from *outside*. The bar is renewed every generation, and a priest of Thalassos holds its key.
- **Daedrion's Maze.** Inside, walls of fitted stone, frescoed corridors and collapsed light-wells spiral down through the old palace and below it. The maze was built so that no path leads out the way it led in. The few who have come out describe rooms that were not there on the way in, and a throne room they could never find again.
- **It is growing.** Over the last two centuries, sounds have been heard under streets well beyond the acropolis. A house on the harbour road collapsed into a passage last winter. The town council voted to say it was subsidence.

## The Sentence "to the King"
The colonial magistrates of the Titan's Sword, and on occasion the courts of [[Pattis]] when the moving city lies close, sentence convicted **perjurers and oath-breakers** to the Horned Court. The condemned is given a lamp, a loaf, and a ball of red thread, and walked through the Bull Gate at dusk. The gate is barred behind them.

Perhaps one in ten comes out, days later, by ways the town cannot account for, wild-eyed and unharmed. Ambrakis holds that Thalassos forgave them. The returned are given a red cord to wear and are never asked to swear an oath again, because the town considers that they already have.

## People of Note
- **The Barkeeper.** This is the hereditary priest of Thalassos who holds the key to the Bull Gate. The current Barkeeper is an elderly woman who has walked the first three turns of the maze more times than anyone alive and won't say what she does in there.
- **The Thread-Mender.** A blind old man sits by the gate and sells red thread to the condemned and the curious. He came out of the maze forty years ago and hasn't seen anything since. He says he saw too much on the way out.

## Hooks (GM)
- **A sentenced witness** whose testimony the party needs has been walked through the Bull Gate tonight.
- **The new tunnel** under the harbour road leads somewhere. Scholars want it mapped, the council wants it filled, and the bellowing from it is getting louder.
- **The white bull of Thalassos.** Ending the curse means finding a bull worthy of a god. Legend says such bulls still walk out of the sea at one beach on the Sword, once in a lifetime. *(See [[The Bull-King of the Horned Court#The Truth (GM)|the Truth]].)*
