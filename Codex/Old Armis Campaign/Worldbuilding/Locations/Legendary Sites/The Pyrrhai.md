# The Pyrrhai

*"The Fire-Isles."* A cluster of volcanic islands in the **far-eastern [[The Central Sea|Central Sea]]**, off the mountainous coast where the sea narrows toward [[The Eight Forts]]. This is the home of **[[The Cyclopes of the Pyrrhai|the Cyclopes]]** and of the first forge of [[Hevrek]]. It is the most feared water inside the Empire's own sea, and the reason the eastern shipping lanes keep to the northern shore.

## The Islands
- **Seven isles around a burning one.** Six barren black islands ring the seventh, a smouldering cone the sailors call **the Anvil**, whose central vent has not gone out in recorded memory. Its smoke is visible for two days' sail in any direction: grey when the Cyclopes are calm, black when they are not.
- **Black beaches, giant flocks.** The outer isles are grazing ground. Sheep the size of oxen crop the sparse grass of the slopes, tended by Cyclops shepherds who carry crooks the length of a mast.
- **The Forge.** Inside the Anvil, reached through a rift in its flank, is the god's own smithy: a cavern-hall lit by the living mountain, ringed with anvils and quench-pools, hung with tools too large for any mortal to lift. The hammers never stop. Ships passing at night hear them across the water.
- **Ourkon's Beach.** On the largest outer isle, a long strand of black sand faces west. Here the blind Eldest sits, day and night, listening for oars.

## No One Has Taken Them
[[An'Hiseret]] never conquered the Pyrrhai: the dead do not swim, and the God-Kings had no fleet worth the name. Nor has the Empire, which has the fleet and hasn't found a reason that outweighs the cost. The islands appear on imperial charts as a ring with a warning-mark and a single word: ***ἄβατον*** — *untrodden*.

## The Ember Tribute
Every spring a small, plainly-rigged ship sails east out of [[Venagillium]] carrying a sealed bronze lamp lit from the Forgehall's fire. This is the ember Ereskes stole, returned in token, every year, for as long as anyone has records. Its crew are chosen by lot from the Smiths' Guild.

The ship beaches on Ourkon's Beach. The lamp is set on the sand. The crew wait, eyes down, until a hand larger than their boat takes it up. Then they sail home, all but one. **Every year, one emissary stays.** The Guild doesn't say what becomes of them. The Cyclopes have never explained the custom, and Venagillium has never asked.

## The Coast Opposite
The fishing villages on the eastern mainland shore live in the Anvil's shadow. They keep no lamps lit after dusk when the smoke runs black. They never pick up driftwood that has been worked by a tool. They pay a share of every catch into the sea, "for the shepherds." Once in a generation a Cyclops wades ashore, takes something (a boat, a herd, a child who was out after dark), and wades back. The villages don't complain to the governor. The governor wouldn't come.

## Hooks (GM)
- **The emissary lot.** This year the lamp-ship sails with a party member aboard, or someone they care about. This year the Cyclopes have asked for two.
- **Hevrek's forge** can make what no mortal smith can. An Aetherwright or a hero-hunter has a commission, and someone must carry it ashore and live.
- **Something is being forged.** The hammers have changed rhythm. The smoke has been black for a month. Something in the Anvil is nearly finished.
- **Weapons of the Elder Days** that went missing from [[Hoplonesos]] may have been *reclaimed* by their makers rather than stolen. The Cyclopes consider every blade they ever made still theirs.
