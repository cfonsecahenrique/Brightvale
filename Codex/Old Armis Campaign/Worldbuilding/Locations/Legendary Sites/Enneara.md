# Enneara

*"Nine-Throats."* A low, fog-shrouded island in the **[[The Azurian Ocean|Azurian Ocean]]**, lying square across the short sea-lane from the southern [[The Titan's Sword|Titan's Sword]] to **[[Adamantis]]**. It is the lair of **[[The Hydra of Enneara|the Hydra]]** and the grave of **Alkaon the Unwearied**. Every chart in the Empire marks it, and every captain steers around it.

## The Island
- **Low, grey and wrong.** Enneara is a long whale-back of grey rock and salt-scrub, a few miles end to end, honeycombed with sea-caves at the waterline. Fog sits on it at dawn and dusk. In the hours between, it looks like any other barren islet, which is how ships get caught.
- **The green shallows.** For a mile around, the sea is a milky, poisonous green. Nothing lives in it. The tide-lines are thick with bleached fish-bones, and the rock below high water is stained the colour of verdigris.
- **The tidal caves.** The Hydra coils in the drowned caves beneath the island and surfaces through blowholes all across it. A landing party never knows which hole a head will come out of.

## The Spear in the Rock
On the island's single landing-beach, a crescent of black shingle on the eastern side, **the Ninefold Bane** stands where Alkaon drove it two ages ago. It is a hero's spear of dark bronze, taller than a man, sunk a third of its length into living rock. Wrapped around its shaft is **a skeleton too large to be human**, still standing, its bones green with the Hydra's poison.

Sailors who've passed close enough to see it say the spear-head **gleams**, untarnished, in the fog. No one has gone ashore to take it. Several have tried.

## How the Western Sea Lives With It
- **The Long Lane.** Armisian convoys to Adamantis swing far north around Enneara: three days added, more water carried, more open sea for the [[The Reef-Born|Reef-Born]] to raid. Captains argue about those three days in every harbour tavern on the western run.
- **"Gone to Alkaon."** It's western slang for a ship lost without a trace.
- **Others take the risk.** [[Athregal]]'s ships run plain sail, without Aether, and sometimes slip through the short lane in a calm. Their captains believe it's the Dwarves' luck. The Armisian Admiralty has not yet asked itself why. *(GM: the Hydra hunts Aether. See [[The Hydra of Enneara#It Hunts Aether|It Hunts Aether]].)*
- **Offerings on the wind.** Ships passing within sight of the fog throw a coin and a cup of wine overboard for Alkaon, so that he'll keep the Hydra's eyes on him and off them.

## Hooks (GM)
- **The Admiralty's prize.** Proving the short lane passable, or impassable but crossable by some trick, is worth a fortune and a name.
- **Recover the Ninefold Bane.** It's a heist under the fog, with the Hydra sleeping beneath the beach. Whoever draws the spear from the rock has done what no one has done in two ages. They have also taken up a weapon its owner never laid down. The keepers of **[[Hoplonesos]]** will want to know where it goes.
- **Wreck-salvage.** Two centuries of Aether-ships lost on the short lane lie in the green shallows: cargoes, charts, an admiral's seal, and every one of their planar engines still faintly humming. The engines are what keep the Hydra here.
