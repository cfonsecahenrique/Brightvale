# The Edges of the World

Where the charts run out, the marvels begin. The margins of **[[The Known World]]** are where the **old monsters, the withdrawn gods, and the wreckage of fallen Ages** still wait — and where the defining dread of the age, the **unknown itself**, lives.

> **A Herodotus principle governs this note:** reliability decreases with distance. The heartland is fact; the Reaches are report; the true edges are sailors' oaths and old men's dreams. *(And the GM knows some of those dreams are true.)*

## The Margins
- **The Far West — [[The Hesperine Isles]].** The furthest place anyone returns from, and they return *disagreeing* — on the location, the duration, whether time ran true. A thin place strewn with broken stone tables, builderless graves, and drowning pyramids.
- **The Far North — [[The Frozen North]].** Past **[[Athregal]]** and the **[[Glacier Giants]]**, the true arctic: ice giants who rule the high glacier, and a withdrawn **God of Winter** on his lonely seat.
- **The Southwest — [[The Eye of the Richat]].** Concentric rings of mountain rippling out from a rocky centre the sand-dwellers call the eye of a *primordial* set into the earth.
- **The Far East — [[The Eastern Steppes]] & [[The Blood Empire]].** Past the Great Grass-Sea of the free Orcenfolk lies a civilisation of blood-sorcery ruled by **another ancient dragon** (see **[[Tiamat]]**), at the foot of the Mountains where the world is said to end.
- **The Deep South — beyond [[Ziramat]] and the [[The Seretis|Seretis]].** Headless men, tribal peoples in animal hides, and the sand-buried statues of a **lion-rider empire** that died before An'Hiseret ever rose. No one knows where the **[[The Seretis|Seretis]]** even *begins* — it falls over a dozen cataracts from an unmapped south.
- **Beneath the Waves.** The Central Sea has its own drowned cities and a vast something the Aetherwrights of **[[Pattis]]** are careful never to disturb.

## The Pattern Beneath the Margins
> **GM-only.** The recurring shapes — drowned pyramids, sand-buried empires, broken tables, burial grounds without builders — are the **wreckage of previous Ages**, the cyclical collapse glimpsed by people who don't know what they're seeing. And the edges are precisely where the **withdrawn gods** still dwell: the lonely peaks, the deep arctic, the Eye, the floor of the sea. To sail to the margins is, without knowing it, to sail toward the gods — and toward the proof that this golden age is one of many, and not the last. (See **[[Apostasy]]**.)

## Hooks (GM)
- Every report here is a voyage waiting to be sailed; the unknown becoming known is the campaign's deepest engine of wonder and dread.
- Three of **[[Tiamat]]**'s five brood can now be placed — **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]** in [[Ziramat]], the dead one in **[[Adamantis]]**, and the blood-magic dragon of **[[The Blood Empire]]** — leaving **two** still lost in these margins.
