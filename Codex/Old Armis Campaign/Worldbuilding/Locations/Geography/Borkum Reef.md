# Borkum Reef

An **archipelago** lying north of [[The Titan's Sword]]** and **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]**, roughly two days' sail west from the Capital of [[Armis]], on the threshold of the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]**. 

## A Gathering of the Marginalised
Borkum Reef has become a haven for those the Empire has cast off or pushed out: **deserters, exiles, smugglers, and marginalised communities** are flocking to its islands. What began as scattered refuges is coalescing into a **proper pirate faction** — the **[[The Reef-Born]]** — astride the very sea-lanes the Empire's western expeditions depend on.

## The Imperial Problem (GM / [[Plot Hooks]])
A pirate power at the mouth of the western trade is intolerable to Armisian ambition. The Reef "**must be dealt with**" — by sword, by infiltration, or by turning its fractious communities against one another. *(See hook #7 in [[Plot Hooks]].)*

## Hooks (GM)
- A morally grey target: many Reef-folk are victims of the Empire's own expansion. Player venturers may be sent to crush them — or sympathise with them.
