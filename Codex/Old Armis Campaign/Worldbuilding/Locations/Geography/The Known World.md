# The Known World

A map-orientation note for the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian]]** golden age. Three great waters and one great landmass define the geography of the campaign.

## The Seas
- **[[The Central Sea]]** — the heart of the Empire and its civilisation (Hellenic / Phoenician in flavour). Most of the Empire rings its coasts.
  - **Northern coasts** — the imperial heartland: **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]**, **[[Lyx]]**, and most of the Empire.
  - **Southern coasts** — the **subjugated necromantic empire of [[An'Hiseret]]** (ancient-Egyptian in flavour).
  - **Roaming the sea** — the **moving island-city of [[Pattis]]**, favouring the western edges.
- **[[The Azurian Ocean]]** — the open western ocean **beyond** the Titan's Sword. The road to the new world; where the great expeditions sail. The Dwarven realm of **[[Athregal]]** lies to its **north**.
- **[[The Topaz Sea]]** — the southern sea **below the Azurian**, holding the bay-empire of **[[Ziramat]]** (newly contacted) — the domain the Dragon-Goddess [[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]] was set to rule.
- **[[Adamantis]]** — a large, dangerous, unexplored island **southwest of the Titan's Sword** and **northwest of Ziramat**, where all three western powers race to colonise. The campaign's central frontier.
- **[[Borkum Reef]]** — an archipelago ~two days' sail **west of the Titan's Sword's northern tip**; a rising pirate haven (the **[[The Reef-Born]]**).
- **[[The Hesperine Isles]]** — the **westernmost known place**, a mystical temperate archipelago far past Adamantis, where the reports stop making sense.

## The Edges of the Map (unknown)
The Empire's ambition outruns its charts. Beyond the known lie blanks the Empire means to fill. *(See [[Plot Hooks]].)*
- **South of [[Ziramat]]** — unknown.
- **West past the [[The Hesperine Isles]]** — unknown, and the Isles themselves may not stay put.

## The Great Landmass
- **[[The Titan's Sword]]** — a large landmass forming the western edge of the known world. The **[[The Central Sea]]** lies to its east; the **[[The Azurian Ocean]]** to its west.
  - Near its **northern tip** sits the far-western outpost of **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]**, where the Empire first met the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]**.
