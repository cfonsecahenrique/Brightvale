# The Central Sea

The inland sea at the heart of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]** — the cradle of its civilisation and the highway along which nearly all its power, trade, and culture flow. In flavour it is the Empire's Mediterranean: warm, busy, ringed by ancient cities. (See the [[The Known World|world map]].)

## The Coasts
- **Northern coasts — the imperial heartland.** The twin capital of **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]** and the cities of **[[Lyx]]**, **[[Orierba]]**, **[[Venagillium]]**, **[[Ezaro]]**, and **[[Triverium]]** ring the northern shore.
- **Southern coasts — [[An'Hiseret]].** The subjugated necromantic empire of the south — its towns **[[Saidz]]**, **[[Xol]]**, **[[Atnas]]**, and **[[Atir]]** — brought under Armisian dominion but far from erased.
- **Roaming the sea itself — [[Pattis]].** The moving island-metropolis drifts across these waters, favouring the western edges.

## Geography
- To the **west**, the Central Sea is closed off by the great landmass of the **[[The Titan's Sword]]**; beyond it lies the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]**.

## Hooks (GM)
- A sea of trade routes, naval rivalries, pirate coasts, and the constant traffic between a confident north and a resentful south.
