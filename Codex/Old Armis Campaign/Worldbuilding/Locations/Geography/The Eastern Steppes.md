# The Eastern Steppes

The **Great Grass-Sea** east of the imperial heartland — an endless steppe of the **free Orcenfolk**, the one neighbour the **[[The Armisian Empire|Empire]]** never brought to heel, and the long road toward the far-eastern edge of the world.

> **Cultural inspiration (GM):** the steppe horse-nomad register — felt tents, horse-hosts, sky-worship, and a great uniting khan the settled world learns to fear.

## The Grass and Its People
The steppe is a sea of grass crossed by **warring nomadic Orcenfolk tribes** — magnificent horse-barbarians who own no roof and worship the open sky. In poor years they quarrel among themselves; the danger is the year a **Great Kha** unites the grass into a single horse-host and forces the proud heartland to **buy peace** with caravans of "gifts" that are tribute in all but name. The Empire never conquered them; it learned to pay them.

## The Road East
The Orcenfolk are also the gatekeepers of the road east. Past their grass lie reports of the eastern edge of the world: the **[[The Blood Empire|Empire of Blood-Sorcery]]** in its square walled cities; and beyond that, the **Mountains of the World** — ranges so vast and unbroken that, the Orcs say, the world itself must *end* there. To cross the grass and reach the east, an expedition must win passage tribe by tribe, or earn a war-host's enmity.

## Hooks (GM)
- An overland expedition where steppe politics and distance are the adversaries, and a road east is the prize — or the war.
- The Orcs remember every slight; a crossing won by force closes the road won by treaty.
