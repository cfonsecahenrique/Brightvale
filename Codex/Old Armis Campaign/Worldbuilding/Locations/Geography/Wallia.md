# Wallia

The mountainous northern shield of the [[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]], bordering the barbarian tribes of the Wildlands. Its high passes and the fortress of [[Wulfsgard]] are the gate between the heartland and the north.

During the empire's collapse, Wallia becomes the centre of the Aldari rising. Its mountains are armed with Athregali anti-airship weapons for the [[The Battle of Wulfsgard]].
