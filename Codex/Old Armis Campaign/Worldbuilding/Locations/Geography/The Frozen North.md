# The Frozen North

The **true arctic** beyond **[[Athregal]]** — a waste of advancing ice, freezing ocean, and black fjords, ruled by giants and, past even them, by a **withdrawn god of winter** on his lonely seat at the top of the world.

> **GM-only.** The Ice Giants are the god's court or his wardens; the **God of Winter** is one of the withdrawn powers who keep the **[[The Edges of the World|edges of the world]]**. Reachable; not conquerable.

## Giants Upon the Ice
Past Athregal and the **[[Glacier Giants]]** of the known war lies the high arctic, where the Athregali speak of **giants who rule over the ice** — lords of the high glacier, seen only by the most daring Armisian explorers to sail those waters and return. Whether they are greater kin of the Glacier Giants, something older, or the same tale grown in the telling, no Armisian can say.

## The Seat of Winter
Past the giants, where the cold has a centre, stands the lonely seat of a **withdrawn God of Winter** — a power that has not spoken to mortals since the Age of Heroes, and that the ice giants answer to. A venturer who reaches it may meet a presence the whole campaign has only hinted at from the margins; what it says, or refuses to say, is the prize.

## The Dwarven Road
The **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves]]** of **[[Athregal]]** sail these waters as no Armisian dares, and mount their own expeditions north — for reasons of their own that they do not fully explain. A crew that goes north goes with the Dwarves, and the ice itself is the deadliest thing on the field: pack ice, killing cold, storms that erase the way home.

## Hooks (GM)
- A voyage where the ice is the dungeon and the god is the reward — survival, not conquest, is the worthy end.
- A god's notice is a dangerous gift. What a player carries south from the arctic may be watched. (See **[[Apostasy]]**.)
