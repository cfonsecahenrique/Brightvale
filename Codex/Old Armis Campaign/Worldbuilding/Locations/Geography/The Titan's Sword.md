# The Titan's Sword

A large landmass forming the **western edge of the known world** — the barrier between the Empire's inner **[[The Central Sea]]** (to its east) and the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]** (to its west). The blade-like shape that gives it its name runs roughly north–south. (See the [[The Known World|world map]].)

## Strategic Role
- **The western frontier.** A contested colonial expanse where the **[[The Armisian Empire|Empire]]** pushes its reach — and where it first collided with the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]**.
- **The gateway west.** Crossing or rounding the Titan's Sword is the threshold between the safe inner sea and the open ocean road to the new world.

## Settlements
- **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]** — a far-western Armisian outpost near the landmass's **northern point**, facing the Azurian. Site of **first contact** with the Dwarves of Athregal.

## Hooks (GM)
- A frontier of rival claims, half-mapped interiors, and whatever (or whoever) held this land before either the Empire or the Dwarves arrived.
