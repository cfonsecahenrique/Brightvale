# Atir

A temple-city of **[[An'Hiseret]]**, the subjugated necromantic empire of the **southern coasts** of the **[[The Central Sea]]** — now under **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian]]** overlordship, but ancient-Egyptian in character and far older than its conquerors. Atir is the great seat of **Amun-Atir, the Death God** (see the **[[_An'Hiseretian Pantheon|An'Hiseretian Pantheon]]**).

## The City of the Death God
Where **[[Xol]]** is the seat of the bound prince, Atir is the seat of the **mother** — the temple-city of **Amun-Atir**, divine source of the deathless royal line and the central power of the death-cult. Its great mortuary-temples and judgement-halls are the theological heart of An'Hiseret: the place the weighing of souls and the rites of the deathless were codified, and the doctrine that *death is a throne one can hold* was first taught.

> **GM-only.** Amun-Atir is the god the **[[Apostasy|Century of Blood]]** will see *erased from existence*, unmade by her own sons' loosed knowledge turned against the gods. Atir is where the cult that her death dooms still burns brightest. See **[[The Return of Xol-amun]]**.

## Hooks (GM)
- The doctrinal centre of the death-cult under occupation — proud, ancient, and quietly certain the conquerors are a passing season.
- Rivalry and old kinship with **[[Xol]]**: the city of the mother and the city of the bound son, two poles of the Amun line.
