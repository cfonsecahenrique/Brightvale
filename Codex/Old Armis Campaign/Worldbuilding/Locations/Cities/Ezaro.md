# Ezaro

A city of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]** on the **[[The Central Sea]]**, among the heartland cities of the Empire alongside the twin capital **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]** and **[[Lyx]]** — at **994 AF**, an ordinary northern port. It will not stay one.

> **GM-only.** A century and a half from now, Ezaro is the city the resurgent **[[Aldari]]** capture and rebuild as their own capital after sacking **[[Armis]]**-Caen (see **[[Timeline (Armis)]]**). Its Armisian bones — walls, harbour, forum — survive under Aldari rule and culture, which is exactly why the **[[The Armisian Empire|Empire]]**'s central-European-flavoured survivals into the *Brightvale* era trace through here rather than through Armis itself.

## Overview (994 AF)
- **An unremarkable northern port.** Ezaro trades grain and timber down the coast to **[[Armis]]**–Caen; nothing about it yet marks it for greatness or ruin.
- **Closer to the plateau than it knows.** Of the Empire's heartland cities, Ezaro sits nearest the Aldari highlands — a detail no one in 994 AF thinks twice about.

## Looking Forward (GM)
- **~1144 AF — captured.** As the Aldari march south after sacking Armis-Caen, Ezaro falls with comparatively little resistance — its garrison gutted by a century of feeding the Empire's deathless elites.
- **~1145 AF — reborn.** The Aldari crown Ezaro their new capital. Over the following **[[Timeline (Armis)|250 cold-peace years]]**, Armisian stone and Aldari rule fuse: **[[Athregal]]**'s mastery of iron and residuum, gifted to its allies, makes Ezaro a genuinely advanced city — Mediterranean foundations under a Germanic, medieval order.

## Hooks (GM)
- At 994 AF, Ezaro is mundane on purpose — let players pass through without a second glance, so its later fate lands harder in hindsight.
- A noble house's seat here is a quiet bet on the wrong horse, or the right one nobody's made yet.
