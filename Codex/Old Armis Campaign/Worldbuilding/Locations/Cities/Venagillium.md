# Venagillium

A city of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]** on the **[[The Central Sea]]**, among the heartland cities of the Empire alongside the twin capital **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]** and **[[Lyx]]**. Venagillium is the Empire's forge — the patron city of **[[Hevrek]]**, god of the forge, craft, fire, and [[Aetherweave Mastery|Aetherweaving]] — and it wears the mark of the god's favour in the permanent haze of smoke over its rooftops.

## Overview
- **Patron of [[Hevrek]].** Venagillium's one temple is the **Forgehall**, built directly over the city's oldest and deepest smithing pits — less a sanctuary in the usual sense than a working foundry that happens to be sacred, its altar-fire never separated from the furnace-fires around it. Every other god of the [[_Armisian Pantheon|pantheon]] keeps a shrine somewhere in the city; none dares raise a temple in Hevrek's own house.
- **Where the fleet is made.** Venagillium supplies a substantial share of the Empire's naval ironwork, bronze fittings, and — increasingly — the aetherically-laced hull components that ships built at [[Pattis]]'s Weave-Yards require. A ship's captain who has never set foot in Venagillium has still trusted her life to something forged there.
- **The Anaxos' conquests.** The **[[Armis-Caen#The Temple-Tomb of the Anaxos|Temple-Tomb of the Anaxos]]** in the capital memorialises, among others, the Anaxos who conquered Venagillium and brought its forges under imperial control — a conquest old enough now that Venagillium's own smiths tell the story with more pride than resentment. They make the Empire's weapons. They have made their peace with what that means.
- **A guild city in all but name.** The Smiths' Guild of Venagillium holds informal authority that rivals the city's own magistrates — apprenticeships are hereditary in all but law, trade secrets are guarded as fiercely as any temple mystery, and a master smith's word on the quality of a hull-fitting is taken over a suffete's.

- **The stolen ember.** The Forgehall's fire is said to descend, unbroken, from the ember **Ereskes** stole from the forge of the **[[The Cyclopes of the Pyrrhai|Cyclopes]]** in the Age of Heroes. Every spring the Smiths' Guild sends a lamp lit from it east to **[[The Pyrrhai]]** as a token of return, and every year one of its emissaries doesn't come home. (See [[_Heroes and Legends]].)

## Hooks (GM)
- **Aetherweave hull-components** are Venagillium's newest and most lucrative export — and the least understood by the smiths making them, who follow Aetherwright specifications without fully grasping the planar theory behind them. A batch gone subtly wrong could ground a fleet, or worse.
- **Guild secrets** worth stealing, buying, or blackmailing someone over are Venagillium's stock in trade — this is the city for a heist built around metallurgy rather than treasure.
- **Hevrek's temple never goes cold.** A pilgrim who wants an audience with the god's priesthood must be willing to work the forge alongside them first. The god does not deal with idle hands.
