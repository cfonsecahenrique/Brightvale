# Orierba

A city of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]** on its northern frontier, among the heartland cities listed alongside the twin capital **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]**, **[[Lyx]]**, and **[[Ezaro]]** — but unlike them, Orierba was not Armisian-founded. It was **theirs** first.

> **GM-only.** Orierba was the largest town of the **[[Aldari]]**, conquered and absorbed into the Empire's heartland generations before the campaign present — exactly as the Empire's elven subjects were "subjugated and assimilated" (see **[[The Armisian Empire]]**). It is *why* Armisians call the Aldari barbarians: a subject people, paying tribute on land that was theirs first.

## Overview
- **A conquered capital.** Aldari-built, Aldari-named, now garrisoned and taxed by Armisian governors who treat its older halls as provincial curiosities.
- **994 AF — uneasy quiet.** At the campaign present, Orierba is a sullen, watched city: old Aldari shrines stand half-converted to Armisian cults, and informants are paid well to listen in its taverns.
- **The flashpoint to come.** When the Empire's elites turn to the **[[The Amun-Orb|Amun-Orb]]** for immortality (see **[[Timeline (Armis)]]**), it is Orierba's tribute and conscripts that pay for it first — and Orierba's revolt, a century later, that begins the Aldari's march on **[[Armis]]** itself.

## Hooks (GM)
- An Aldari elder in Orierba already knows the old roads to **Alder** and **Wulfsgard** that Armisian maps don't show — useful to a party that earns trust, dangerous to one that doesn't.
- A garrison commander quietly suspects the "pacified" city is anything but, and is right.
