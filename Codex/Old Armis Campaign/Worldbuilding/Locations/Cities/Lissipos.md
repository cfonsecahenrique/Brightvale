# Lissipos

A city of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]**, on the **southeastern coast** of the **[[The Central Sea]]** near **[[Aelunos]]** — a populous, close-knit port known across the Empire for the strength of its households and the length of its grudges.

## Overview
- **Patron of [[Kythera]].** Lissipos's one temple belongs to Kythera, goddess of childbirth, mothers, children, and vengeance — a pairing of domains that outsiders sometimes find strange until they have spent a season in the city. To the Lissiotes, protecting a child and avenging a wrong done to one are simply the same duty, seen at two different moments.
- **The Birthing Halls.** Attached to Kythera's temple are the Empire's most respected birthing halls, staffed by priestess-midwives whose training is sought after in every port from here to Armis-Caen. A Lissiote midwife commands a fee and a deference that few other tradeswomen in the Empire can match.
- **The Widows' Registry.** Less publicly discussed but universally understood: Kythera's priesthood also keeps an informal accounting of wrongs done to Lissiote mothers and children — debts owed, oaths broken, harm inflicted — that the temple has been known to see settled through means the civic magistrates prefer not to examine too closely. It is not law. It is older than law, and the city treats it with the same seriousness.
- **A city that remembers children by name.** Every child born in Lissipos under the temple's care is entered into a permanent roll, kept for centuries. Genealogists and inheritance-lawyers from across the Empire pay well for access to it; the priesthood grants it selectively, and always for a price that has nothing to do with money.

- **The first entry.** The oldest line on the Widows' Registry, older than the city's walls, belongs to the Gorgon herself: **Ysmene**, the shepherd's wife from the hills behind Lissipos, whose prayer to Kythera was answered too well. It is still marked *unsettled*. Her valley, the **[[The Garden of Stone|Garden of Stone]]**, is a day's walk inland, and no Lissiote mother lets her child hum the lullaby that drifts down from it. (See [[The Gorgon Ysmene]].)

## Hooks (GM)
- **A debt on the Widows' Registry** left unsettled for a generation is a superb slow-burn plot hook — Kythera's justice does not expire, and the temple has a very long memory.
- **The birthing halls** are neutral ground in Lissipos in a way almost nothing else is — even feuding families set aside quarrels within its walls. Violating that neutrality would be a serious act, with serious consequences.
- **The child-roll** holds inheritance and lineage secrets that could upend a noble house's claim decades after the fact.
