# Xol

A temple-city of **[[An'Hiseret]]**, the subjugated necromantic empire of the **southern coasts** of the **[[The Central Sea]]** — now under **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian]]** overlordship, but ancient-Egyptian in character and far older than its conquerors. Xol is the seat of the demi-god prince **[[The Return of Xol-amun|Xol-amun]]**, and the site of the rite the Empire interrupted.

## The High Sanctum
Xol's heart is the **high sanctum** where the deathless priesthood performed the great binding-rites of the Amun line — and where, in the chaos of the conquest (**~900 AF**), Armisian troops stormed in and **interrupted Xol-amun's soul-binding** before it could be finished. The sanctum still stands, occupied but never truly secured; the priesthood that tends it has not stopped its work, only hidden it.

> **GM-only.** Xol-amun did not die and was not reborn — his bound soul endures, dragged across the desert aboard **[[The Wandering Necropolis]]**, and the priests of Xol are clergy preparing a resurrection. See **[[The Return of Xol-amun]]**.

## Hooks (GM)
- A restless temple-city under occupation, where the conquerors hold the streets and the death-cult holds the dark beneath them.
- The starting thread of the Necropolis mystery: it is from Xol that the unfinished god began his long pilgrimage into the deep desert.
