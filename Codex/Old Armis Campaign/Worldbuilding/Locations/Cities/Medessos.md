# Medessos

A small, unremarkable village in a nested mountain valley **west of [[Venagillium]]** — a terraced settlement of a few score households, spring-fed and easily overlooked, in the heartland's quiet upland country. In the golden age it is a pleasant nothing: good water, defensible ground, and a stubborn, self-sufficient people who never depended on the Weave the way the great cities do.

> **GM-only.** Medessos is the future **Brightvale**. *Medessos → Medsen*: the village becomes the great walled refuge-city of the post-Apostasy age, the last place the light holds. Everything that makes it forgettable now makes it survivable when the world ends. See **[[The Vale to Come]]**. Plant the geography, never the prophecy.

## The Vale
A ringed, spring-fed valley with clean water and defensible high ground — the kind of place the Empire's grand powers have no reason to notice. Its folk are mountain-farmers and herders, plain and hardy, with an old local faith and a habit of minding their own.

## Hooks (GM)
- A quiet human interlude on the way between [[Venagillium]] and the heartland — rest, resupply, a name learned. Let the players feel the calm. The weight is the GM's to carry, never theirs to be told.
