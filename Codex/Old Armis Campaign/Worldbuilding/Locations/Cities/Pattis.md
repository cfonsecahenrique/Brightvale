# Pattis

The **westernmost city** of **[[The Armisian Empire]]** and the great gateway to the new world — and, uniquely, a **moving city**. Pattis is an **island-metropolis** that drifts under its own power across the **[[The Central Sea]]**, most often holding to the sea's **western edges**, near the passage around **[[The Titan's Sword]]** into the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]**. It guards its independence jealously: a free city-state that pays the Empire trade and prestige but not obedience.

> **Inspiration:** **Carthage** (a Punic, sea-mastering rival-within-the-empire) by way of **Númenor and Atlantis** — a proud, brilliant, sea-crowned city at the apex of its golden age, with all the foreboding that comparison implies.

## A Moving City Above and Below the Waves
Pattis is the Empire's supreme work of **[[Aetherweave Mastery]]**. It is not anchored to any coast: ceaseless command of the **Water** and **Air** planes carries the whole island-city through the [[The Central Sea]] at will. Half of it rises in tiers above the surface; the other half is built **beneath the waves**, its districts, ways, and gardens held dry and breathable by that same planar mastery. To live in Pattis is to depend, every moment, on the Weave holding — and to wake, some mornings, to a different horizon.

## Overview
- **Where the expeditions begin.** Most great new-world voyages are launched, crewed, and financed from Pattis's harbours.
- **Independent.** One of the city-states that retains real sovereignty within the imperial confederation.
- A landing-point for the new **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarven]]** communities entering Empire society.

## Secession (GM / [[Plot Hooks]])
The halls of Pattis and **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]** seethe with intrigue. Some hold that the **Pattisians are a separate people** entirely, who would **secede** from the greatest empire of any age — a fracture made all the more dangerous by the fact that Pattis can simply *sail away*. *(See hook #6 in [[Plot Hooks]].)*

## Looking Forward (GM)
Pattis outlives Armis-Caen — but not by enough. When the deathless elites' century of rule (see **[[Timeline (Armis)|the Dark Reign]]**) breaks the Empire in civil war, it is Pattis that survives the sack of the old capital and declares itself the Empire's sole legitimate heir, growing for 250 wary years against the Aldari realm at **[[Ezaro]]**. It is Pattis's own Master Aetherweavers who, centuries later, push the Amun-Orb past immortality and toward true godhood — the act that finally ends the age. The glorious-overreach theme below is not just flavour; it is the city's actual fate.

## Hooks (GM)
- Numenor/Atlantis framing invites a theme of glorious overreach — exploration shading into hubris.
- The undersea districts — and the city's very motion — are only ever one failure of the Weave away from catastrophe.
- A city that can *move* is a city that can flee, blockade, or vanish: enormous strategic and political leverage over the capital ([[Armis]]) and the western trade.
