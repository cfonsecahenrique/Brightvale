# The Five Archon Priests

The **scaled high-clergy of [[Ziramat]]** — the five priest-statesmen who hold the Crimson Empire together in the name of **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]**, and the first targets of any Armisian plan to break it from within.

> **Role:** Ruling priesthood of [[Ziramat]], beneath the god-queen · **Affiliation:** The cult and dragon-rites of [[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]

## The Machinery Beneath the Queen
Azhdašar rules supreme but **remote** — a god throned in the deep red heart of her greatest cliff-city, who does not govern the day to day. That work falls to her **five Archon Priests**: dragon-blooded matriarch-clergy, each holding a portion of the empire's cities, temples, and sacred dragon-rites. Together they are the load-bearing structure of Ziramat; the queen is its sun, but the Archons are its spine.

## Not Cartoon Villains
The Archons are the **priest-statesmen of a civilisation that understands more about the age's coming doom than [[The Armisian Empire|Armis]] does.** Ziramat keeps being *right* about things the Empire dismisses, and at least some of the Archons read the signs of the **[[Apostasy]]** truer than any Armisian scholar. To move against them is a real and consequential act, not a clean one.

## The Target
> **GM-only.** Imperial intelligence has mapped this hierarchy and concluded: remove the Archons and Ziramat stumbles. An Armisian strike — infiltrating as **[[Borkum Reef|Reef]]** pirates or **[[An'Hiseret|An'Hiseretian]]** traders to assassinate all five — is a major move in the great game, and a heavy one: it may decapitate the one power that saw the doom coming.

## Hooks (GM)
- Five distinct targets — where each sits, what guards them, who hates them, and what each *knows*. At least one should be able to offer the player the **truth** instead of a fight.
- Each Archon is a window into Ziramati faith, the dragon-rites, and what the cliff-cities believe about **[[Tiamat]]** and the lost brood.
