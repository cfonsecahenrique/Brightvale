# Hazi Winter Falcon

Nomadic warlord of the **Winter Horde**. Sent by Armis to help pacify the Aldari highlands, Hazi instead becomes bound to the land and its people after fighting beside King [[Wulf von Lugner]].

Hazi marries Wulf's daughter, Hilde, refuses the Empire's recall, and fights beside the Aldari at the [[The Battle of Wulfsgard]].
