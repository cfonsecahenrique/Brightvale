# Aldari

The **tribes of the northern plateau** — a people who held the highlands northwest of the Empire's heartland, and the elder coast besides, for longer than **[[The Armisian Empire|Armis]]** has existed at all. Armisians call them barbarians; the Aldari call themselves the people of **Alder**, the eldest of their towns and the root of their own name.

> **Cultural inspiration (GM):** **Germanic.** Clan-and-hearth tribes of the hill country — oath-bound, forest-wary, slower to trust iron than the sea-peoples south of them, and old enough to remember when the **[[Elves]]** ruled the land outright.

## Overview
- **Older than the Empire's neighbours.** The Aldari were rooted in their plateau before **[[Athregal]]** ever made contact with Armis — they are not newcomers like the Dwarves, but a people the Empire simply never bothered to name until it wanted their land.
- **Kin to the Elves.** Long before the Armisian conquest, the Aldari coexisted peacefully with the **[[Elves]]** of the northern reaches — trading, intermarrying, and in places sharing hearths outright. Several Aldari clans carry visible elf-blood, and elf-Aldari kinship is not a scandal among them the way it would be read in [[Medsen]]'s halls centuries later.
- **Conquered, not converted.** **[[Orierba]]**, the largest Aldari town, was absorbed into the Empire's northern heartland generations before the campaign present — its people subjugated and put to work much as the Empire's own elven subjects were. Alder itself, smaller and higher in the hills, was never fully digested.
- **The long grudge.** Subject status under Armis is the wound every Aldari clan nurses, openly or in secret, at **994 AF**.

## Towns of the Plateau
- **Alder** — the eponymous ancestral seat, a half-sacred grove-town in the high hills that gives the Aldari their name. Even at the height of Armisian conquest it was never more than nominally held.
- **[[Orierba]]** — the largest Aldari town, the one the Empire actually annexed and counts among its northern heartland cities.
- **Wulfsgard** — a warrior-clan hold in the high passes, seat of the plateau's fiercest war-leaders.
- **Drosselfen** — a riverside trade town, the Aldari's main point of (grudging) commerce with Armisian merchants.
- **Eichenholt** — a grove sanctuary where elf-Aldari kinship runs deepest, tended jointly by Aldari elders and elven kin.

## Faith

The Aldari keep a **[[_Aldari Pantheon|faith of earth, grove, and ancestor]]** — centred on **Elda**, the Ancient, the land herself given presence. The Empire calls her *"the Goddess"* because she stands alone above all the lesser powers with no divine rival above her. Sacred authority rests with the **Eldamothren** — clan-mothers who tend grove sanctuaries rather than temples. There are no male priests; the eldest woman of each clan *is* its religious authority.

The faith bleeds at its edges into the **[[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Elven tradition]]** — at **Eichenholt**, tended jointly with elven kin, the old paths to the Otherworld are still open. The Aldari are the only humans of whom that can be said.

The Empire's annexation of Aldari land is, in this faith's terms, *blasphemy*: Elda owns the land, and Armisian deed-law treating it as chattel is heresy the Eldamothren are counting.

## Hooks (GM)
- The Aldari are this campaign's quiet powder keg: a subjugated, longer-rooted people whose grievance the Empire has never taken seriously. *(See [[Timeline (Armis)]] for what they eventually do about it.)*
- **[[Athregal]]**, watching the Empire's ambitions grow, may find more sympathy with the conquered Aldari than with Armis itself — a thread worth seeding early.
- An Aldari character (or one with Aldari/elf-blooded kin) carries a grudge the Empire doesn't know it's earned yet.
