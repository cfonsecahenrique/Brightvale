# The Closing Doors

The **Iron Tide is closing the Otherworld.** As Dwarven iron spreads through the land, the thresholds to the Faewilds thicken and die, one by one — and the Empire is severing one of the world's old protections **blind**, calling the iron that does it progress.

> **GM-only.** The **[[Elves]]** and their druids half-know this; no one with power believes them. Show it as a dread, never a lecture.

## What Iron Is Really Doing
Cold iron is the Fae's bane — it breaks glamour and seals the thresholds between the world and the **[[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Faewilds]]**. This is folk-belief to the Elves and literal truth in the setting. The consequence no Armisian has connected: as the iron of the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves]]** of **[[Athregal]]** spreads through the legions, the fleets, and every nail of every new building, the veil thickens and the Fae withdraw. The old country's thresholds are going quiet and dead.

## A Wound of the Age
The Faewilds are part of the **old order of the world** — one of the balances that held before the young Empire's planar Weave and the death-cult's necromancy. Closing the doors is one more way the golden age **cuts through the foundations it stands on**, alongside the residuum, the dependence on the Weave, and the broken death-cycle of **[[An'Hiseret]]**. When the **[[Apostasy]]** comes, the protections that might have softened it — the old thin-place magic, the bargains, the Fae's own stake in the living world — will already be half-severed. The Empire will have locked one of its own escape doors from the inside.

## Keepers of the Cycle's Memory
The deepest Elven secret: **the Elves, and the Fae they commune with, remember previous Ages ending.** The standing stones and barrows the Elves tend are, in places, older than the Elves themselves — and the Fae are older still. Through threshold-communion, the Elves carry a memory no human archive holds: that **this has happened before**, that golden ages rise and fall in a turning wheel, and that the signs read true again now. A druid who says "we have seen this before, and we know how it ends" is dismissed as a bitter mystic. He is the most reliable historian in the world.

## Hooks (GM)
- How far has the closing gone — a creep barely begun, or thresholds dying within a venturer's lifetime?
- Do the **Fae** know they are being exiled, and have any begun to act against the iron? A Fae faction with a stake in stopping the Iron Tide is a rich hidden player.
