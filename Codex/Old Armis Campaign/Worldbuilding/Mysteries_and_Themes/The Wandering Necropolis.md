# The Wandering Necropolis

A **city that walks** — a vast tomb-city of the deathless south, dragged across the deep desert by hordes of the labouring dead, never twice in the same place. It is the anchor of a bound god and the one prize the Empire cannot reach.

> **GM-only.** The Necropolis is the death-cult's holiest engine and the moving prison of **[[The Return of Xol-amun|Xol-amun's bound soul]]**. Pace its revelation slowly.

## What It Is
A working death-city of **[[An'Hiseret]]**'s old order, mobile and self-sustaining: tens of thousands of the labouring dead hauling its tents, temples, and tomb-barques across the sands under priests who never stopped practising the **necromantic Weave** the conquest was supposed to have ended. At its heart, in the great tomb-barque, the bound soul of **[[The Return of Xol-amun|Xol-amun]]** is venerated — perhaps the very engine of the city's deathless march.

## Why It Walks
The priests are dragging their unfinished god across the desert toward the one holy site where his binding could be completed — a slow processional pilgrimage measured in generations. So long as it rolls on, his return stays possible.

## The Trap for the Empire
**The Necropolis keeps to the deep southern desert, and Armisian power is naval power.** The fleets that drowned the death-empire's coastal legions cannot sail dry sand; no river reaches the interior routes. The Empire conquered the shore and can **never catch the walking tomb**. To take what is inside it, the **[[The Armisian Empire|Empire]]** must send not an army but a small, seasoned, capable team.

## The Master Aetherweavers' Designs
The Master Aetherweavers of **[[Pattis]]** covet what the Necropolis carries — a bound divine soul is precisely the seed they need for the **[[The Amun-Orb|Amun-Orb]]** and the **[[Apostasy|Bid for Divinity]]**. They will not say so plainly. Taking the soul does not just rob a temple; it may stop a city — and it puts a god's fragment in the hands of mortals who mean to become gods.

## Hooks (GM)
- A herculean task no army can do: find a moving city in a trackless desert, breach it, take the soul, and carry it home pursued by everything that wants it back.
- An'Hiseret's conquered factions and the south's deathless powers will not forgive the theft of a sacred relic — the player makes an enemy of a civilisation.
