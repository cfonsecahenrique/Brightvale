# The Return of Xol-amun

The deepest secret of the conquered south: a **bound god in waiting**, an interrupted resurrection, and the loosed knowledge of how to **truly unmake a divine soul** — the death-cult's eschatology, and a thread that runs straight into the **[[Apostasy]]**.

> **GM-only.** Players should discover this through play — the death-cult's purpose, the bound god, and the loosed knowledge are slow-burn revelations, never exposition.

## The Bound God
When the Armisians stormed the high sanctum of **[[Xol]]** in the chaos of the conquest (**~900 AF**), they interrupted **Xol-amun's soul-binding** — the rite meant to seat the victor of the An'Hiseretian civil war in a restored, eternal body. The rite was never finished:

**Xol-amun did not die, and was never reborn.** His soul remains — bodiless, conscious, bound to the material plane, caught between unmaking and apotheosis. He is a god in waiting: aware, patient with the patience only the deathless can afford, and unable to act until his binding is completed. The surviving death-priests of **[[An'Hiseret]]** know this. Their resistance is not rebellion — it is *eschatology*. They are clergy preparing a resurrection.

## The Amun Brothers' War
Xol-amun and his brother — demi-god princes, sons of **Amun-Atir, the Death God** (see **[[_An'Hiseretian Pantheon]]**) — fought a civil war over which would be made eternal. To win it, each brother had to learn the most dangerous art in the world: how to **truly and permanently unmake a soul-bound being** — to end even a deathless god.

## The Loosed Knowledge — the God-Killing Weapon
That unmaking-knowledge survived the war and the Armisian conquest, scattered and hidden, and it is among the most dangerous things in the world:
- It is the only known way to *truly* destroy a souled undead — Xol-amun himself included.
- Anyone who fears death or covets power over it will kill to possess it.
- In the **Century of Blood** it is plausibly the very weapon by which Aether-empowered mortals **erase Amun-Atir, the Death God** — the An'Hiseretian method of unmaking a deathless soul, turned at last against the gods. (See **[[Apostasy]]**.) It is also what makes slaying a living primordial like **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]** thinkable.

## The Tremor Before the Quake
**[[An'Hiseret]] industrialised the interruption of death** — corpses denied their passage, souls bound against the cycle, the boundary between living and dead made a workforce — for centuries. This is not merely *like* the catastrophe that ends the age; it is **a literal early tremor of it.** Every bound soul still held against its passage — Xol-amun chief among them — is a small open wound in the order of death.

## The Anchor
Xol-amun's bound soul is dragged across the desert aboard **[[The Wandering Necropolis]]** — toward the one holy site where his binding could be completed. So long as the Necropolis rolls on, his return remains possible, and the south can never be truly secured.

## Hooks (GM)
- **Can Xol-amun return** — a restored demi-god of death in the conquered south — or is his binding *uncompletable*, leaving him an eternal, scheming, bodiless presence?
- **Where is the unmaking-knowledge now?** Lost, hoarded by a death-cult faction, in deep archives, or drifting toward someone who should never have it (the Master Aetherweavers of **[[Pattis]]**?).
