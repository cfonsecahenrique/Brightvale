## The Campaign Engine
The central frontier is **[[The Contested Isle]]** — the dangerous, Aether-rich island all three western powers are racing to colonise. Expeditions launched from **[[Pattis]]** (and the Empire's island foothold) are the prequel's answer to *Brightvale*'s Venturer system: player venturers probe the Isle's interior, contend with its elusive natives and primordial powers, and compete with rival Dwarven ([[Athregal]]) and [[Ziramat|Ziramati]] expeditions for the **Aether-heavy residuum**.
