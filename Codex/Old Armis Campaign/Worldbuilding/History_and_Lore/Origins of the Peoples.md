# Origins of the Peoples

The peoples of the world are not separate creations. They are **divergent branches of a single stock** — the descendants of one extinct ancestral people, scattered and reshaped until they became as different as Dwarf from Dragonkin. What drove them apart was not time alone but **the world itself**: environments that were never merely natural, but molded by primordials, by gods, by cosmic events, and by the deep currents of the **[[Aetherweave Mastery|Aetherweave]]**. The peoples are fantasy-evolution's answer to a world where magic shapes the land that shapes the flesh.

> Most of this is **deep lore** — myth, druidic memory, and scholarly speculation rather than common knowledge. The Elves hold the truest account; everyone else has fragments.

---

## The Common Ancestor — Inheritors of a Fallen Age

The world moves in **Ages**, and each ends in cataclysm. The shared ancestor of all the peoples was the root stock of a **prior Age** — a people shattered when their world fell. The survivors did not rebuild as one. They scattered into the refuges that would keep them alive, and over the long dark between Ages, each refuge — and the supernatural force that ruled it — **remade them in its own image.**

So the peoples are siblings who no longer recognise the kinship: every one of them a different answer to the same question, *how does what is left of us survive what the world has become?*

## The Molding Forces

Each branch was shaped by where it sheltered and what shaped that place:

### Elves — Remade by Annwn
The first to rebuild a true civilisation, in the green heartlands — and the branch that sheltered closest to the **Otherworld**. Their long communion with the [[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Faewilds]] and its magic reached into them and *rewrote* them: the Elves became **deathless**, ageless beings, the elder people in the most literal sense. This is why they remember the deep past — an Elf alive today may have *witnessed* the [[Age of Heroes and the Withdrawal of the Gods|Age of Heroes]] with their own eyes. Their immortality is Annwn-fed, not innate — and as the spreading iron seals the doors, it is quietly **waning**.

### Humans — Sheltered by the Gods
The branch that stayed **closest to the ancestral stock** — short-lived, without innate magic, born in the **southern continent** beyond the present maps. They should have been outcompeted by their more magical cousins. They were not, because they were **sheltered**: the gods who walked the earth took humanity under their guidance, overseeing its development with deliberate care, and in time **intermarrying** with it to sire the demi-gods who ruled in the Age of Heroes. Humanity's edge was never magic. It was patronage.

### Dwarves — Forged by the Ice
Sheltered in the **northern fjords and mountains**, the Dwarves were shaped by the hardest refuge of all: a low, strong centre of gravity for cave and abyssal mountain-path, deep cold-endurance, and bodies and tempers tempered by an **endless war with the [[Glacier Giants]]** of the high ice — a strife that is itself half-supernatural, and that has never let the clans soften.

### Dragonkin — Marked by the Primordials
Not shaped by a place but by a **power**: the Dragonkin are a mutation induced by the presence of the **[[Primordial Dragons|primordial dragons]]** — Tiamat's brood. They are kin, in blood and being, to those world-makers. This is why the Ziramati feel the **dead primordial of the [[The Contested Isle|Contested Isle]]** as lost family and mourn it as a grave: it *is* their kin.

### The Others — the Framework Extends
The same logic reaches every people, and these threads are open for development:
- **Orcenfolk** — shaped by the open **[[The Eastern Steppes|Eastern Steppes]]**, as the Dwarves were by the mountains: horse-bonded, hardy, free.
- **Halflings** — shaped by gentle, sheltered green country, the branch that survived by being overlooked.
- **Infernis** — shaped by a *fiendish or infernal* touch — a primordial of fire and ruin, or an early scar of the same entropy that later becomes the [[Umbra]].

## Why It Matters

- **One family, many conquests.** The Empire's subjugations — of Elves, of the south, soon of others — are, beneath everything, a shattered family preying on itself. None of them remember they were ever one.
- **The Elves are the memory.** Deathless and ancient, they hold the account no human archive does — and are dismissed for it.
- **The south is a lost cradle.** Humanity's birthplace lies past the edge of the maps, forgotten by the people who came from it. Someone, someday, will go back.
