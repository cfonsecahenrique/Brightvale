# The Pattisian Wars

The **Pattisian Wars** are the final civil wars of the Armisian Empire: a struggle between **[[Pattis]]** and [[Armis]]–[[Caen]] that turns an overstretched empire into a collection of hostile camps.

## The Fracture

The [[Ziramat|Ziramatians]] conspire to fund Pattis's rebellion against the imperial centre. Their support does not create the resentment that drives Pattis, but it gives the island-metropolis the resources to turn grievance into open war. City-states, vassals, and old imperial houses are pulled into one side or the other.

The conflict strips the Empire of its grip on [[Adamantis]]. While the Armisians fight each other, Athregal and Ziramat expand their exploration and influence on the island.

## The Northern Front

The [[Aldari]] formally remain neutral, though both sides use Aldari companies as mercenaries. Repeated imperial demands and northern incursions make neutrality increasingly impossible: the Aldari must spend men and material holding their own highlands while an empire at war still expects tribute and service.

Armis responds by allying with nomadic warlords. Their hordes keep [[An'Hiseret]] under imperial control and fight Pattisian forces on land. One such force, led by **[[Hazi Winter Falcon]]**, is sent to the Aldari highlands as a show of power and an instrument for pacifying the northern frontier.

## Consequences

The civil war leaves no imperial force strong enough to answer the Aldari rising after [[The Battle of Wulfsgard]]. The sack of [[Armis]]–[[Caen]] follows within a year, and Pattis emerges to claim the inheritance of the Empire it helped destroy.
