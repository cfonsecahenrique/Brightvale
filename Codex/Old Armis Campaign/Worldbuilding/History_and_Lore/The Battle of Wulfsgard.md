# The Battle of Wulfsgard

The **Battle of Wulfsgard** is the turning point of the Aldari rising and the immediate prelude to the sack of [[Armis]]–[[Caen]].

## The Refusal

After helping **King [[Wulf von Lugner]]** defeat the Great Northern Coalition of hobgoblin tribes, the nomadic warlord **[[Hazi Winter Falcon]]** marries Wulf's daughter, **Hilde**. Hazi chooses to remain in [[Wallia]], adopts its customs, and binds the Winter Horde to the Aldari cause.

When Armis orders Hazi's legion back to the capital, Hazi refuses. The imperial court demands Hazi's head. Wulf instead warns the other Aldari kings to prepare for war: *come and get it.*

## The Battle

The Empire dispatches a fleet of skyships to break Wulfsgard. Athregal, which has quietly chosen its side, sends dwarven Aether-weapons built to bring down the flying leviathans. Wulfsgard's mountain shield in Wallia is packed with these anti-airship engines.

The imperial foot legions cannot force the Wallian mountain passes, where Aldari warriors and the Winter Horde hold them. Only two skyships reach Wulfsgard itself. Both are destroyed after a fierce battle.

## The March South

Wulfsgard's victory turns rebellion into a united campaign. Wallians, Aldari, Aurelians, Orierbans, and the Winter Horde descend from the highlands, ravage imperial territory, and sack Armis–Caen after a swift year of war. They then establish a new imperial capital at [[Ezaro]].
