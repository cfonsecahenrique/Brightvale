# The Armisian Empire

*The central hub of the [[Overview|New Armis Campaign]]. Link any faction, location, NPC, or lore note from this prequel back to here.*

The **Armisian Empire** is a bright, vibrant, sea-faring civilisation at the height of its golden age. It is a culture of merchants, explorers, philosophers, and shipwrights, defined by its restless hunger to map and trade with the world beyond the horizon.

> **Cultural inspiration (GM):** **Bronze Age Mediterranean.** The heartland reads as the **Phoenician** and **Hellenic** city-states; the moving city of **[[Pattis]]** leans **Carthaginian**.

**The campaign present is `994 AF`** (994 years after the founding of [[Armis]]). See [[Timeline (Armis)]].

## Character of the Empire
- **A thalassocracy.** Power flows along sea lanes, not land borders. The Empire is a web of ports, trade routes, and colonies rather than a contiguous territory.
- **An age of discovery.** The state and its wealthy houses sponsor near-constant expeditions westward across the **[[The Azurian Ocean]]**. Each returning fleet brings exotic artefacts, animals, and riches that fuel the next voyage.
- **Confederation, not monolith.** The imperial core rules directly, but many **free city-states** retain real independence, bound by trade, treaty, and prestige rather than conquest.

## The Source of the Golden Age
The Empire's zenith rests on **[[Aetherweave Mastery]]**. In **744 AF** — 250 years ago — Armisian arcanists unlocked mastery of *strongly flowing* Aetherweave, the deep planar currents. Their seafaring supremacy is, in truth, command of the **Water** and **Air** planes: it powers their fleets, sustains the undersea half of **[[Pattis]]**, and underwrites every voyage to the new world.

## Geography
The Empire rings the **[[The Central Sea]]** (see the [[The Known World|world map]]):
- **Northern coasts — the heartland:** the twin capital **[[Armis]]–[[Caen]]** and the cities of **[[Lyx]]**, **[[Orierba]]**, **[[Venagillium]]**, **[[Ezaro]]**, and **[[Triverium]]**.
- **The moving city of [[Pattis]]** — an island-metropolis that drifts across the Central Sea, favouring its western edges.
- **Southern coasts:** the subjugated necromantic empire of **[[An'Hiseret]]**, conquered after the Awakening.
- **The west:** beyond the great landmass of **[[The Titan's Sword]]** lies the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]** and the road to the new world.

## The Twin Capital
The heart of the Empire is the merging twin cities of **[[Armis]]** and its subjugated neighbour **[[Caen]]** — once rivals, now growing into a single sprawling metropolis as their walls and districts knit together.

## The Free Cities
The most important independent polity is **[[Pattis]]**, a **moving island-metropolis** that roams the [[The Central Sea]] and favours its western edges. From Pattis sail most of the great new-world expeditions.

## Foreign Contact
The Empire has recently encountered the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]** at **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]** on **[[The Titan's Sword]]**, where both powers reached to colonise. First contact was warlike; an uneasy trade now follows, and the first Dwarven communities are settling in [[Pattis]] and [[Armis]]. Further west, the Empire has also just made contact with the crimson empire of **[[Ziramat]]**, south across the [[The Azurian Ocean]].

## The Race for the Isle
The Empire is one of three powers — with **[[Athregal]]** and **[[Ziramat]]** — racing to colonise **[[Adamantis]]**, a dangerous, Aether-rich island in the western ocean. The Empire works it from a single fortified foothold; the prize is its **Aether-heavy residuum**, fuel for [[Aetherweave Mastery]] beyond anything achieved at home. This is the great venture of the age — and the campaign's frontier.

## Peoples of the Empire
The Empire is human-ruled, but not human-only. The **[[Elves]]** — the *elder* people who reigned **before humankind**, Brythonic-Celtic in culture — were long ago **subjugated and assimilated**, and remain most numerous in the Empire's **north-eastern reaches**. The **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]** are the newest arrivals (see Foreign Contact).

## Religion
The Empire keeps the **[[_Armisian Pantheon|Armisian Pantheon]]** — a Bronze Age Mediterranean family of gods of sea, sky, craft, and war. Its elven subjects keep the far older **[[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Elven faith]]**. See [[_Mythology Index|Religions & Mythology]].

## Looking Forward (GM)
This is the civilisation that will one day fall in the [[Apostasy]] and leave the Noble Houses and ruins of the *Brightvale* era as its inheritance. Plant the seeds of that collapse here.
