# Religions & Mythology — Index

The hub for the **myths, gods, and faiths** of the campaign's cultures. Each civilisation has its own complex pantheon, shaped by its character and its origins; beneath them all lies an older, primordial layer.

## The Pantheons
- **[[_Armisian Pantheon|Armisian Pantheon]]** — Bronze Age Mediterranean; a Phoenician-Hellenic family of gods of sea, sky, craft, trade, and war. The faith of [[The Armisian Empire]].
- **[[_An'Hiseretian Pantheon|An'Hiseretian Pantheon]]** — ancient-Egyptian; gods of the river, the sun, and above all **death and the deathless**. The faith of [[An'Hiseret]].
- **[[_Athregali Pantheon|Athregali Pantheon]]** — Norse; gods of forge, frost, war, and fate, sung in the halls of the Dwarves of [[Athregal]].
- **[[_Aldari Pantheon|Aldari Pantheon / The Erde Synthesis]]** — Continental Germanic; earth-goddess, sacred grove, and ancestor-communion. A matrilineal faith headed by **Elda** — known to outsiders simply as *"the Goddess"*. After the Sacking of Armis-Caen (1144 AF), the faith absorbs [[Terra]]'s Ezarian cult and evolves into **Erde** — the dominant, near-monotheistic religion of the Aldari Realm across the Cold Centuries (1145–1495 AF).
- **[[_Ziramati Pantheon|Ziramati Pantheon]]** — Akkadian-Persian with a draconic core; a faith centred on the **living god-queen [[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]** and the dragon-blood. The faith of [[Ziramat]].
- **[[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Elven Faith & the Fae]]** — Brythonic-Celtic; not a pantheon but **communion with the Fae** and the Otherworld. The elder faith of the [[Elves]], older than all the rest.
- **[[_The Primordial Powers|The Primordial Powers]]** — the older-than-gods layer: dragons, the deep, and the slumbering force the Ziramati name *Tiâmtu* (see [[Adamantis]]).

## How to Use This Section
- Each pantheon has its **own folder** with an **index note** (the overview) plus one note per deity, cult, or myth as they are written.
- **Deity notes** should link to their **pantheon index** + their **culture** (e.g. [[An'Hiseret]]) + 1–2 thematically close peers — not to every other god (keep the graph clustered).
- Cross-pantheon echoes (the same primordial seen through different faiths) belong in **[[_The Primordial Powers]]**.
