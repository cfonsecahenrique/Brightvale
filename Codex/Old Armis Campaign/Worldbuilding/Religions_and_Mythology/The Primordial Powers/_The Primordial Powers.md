# The Primordial Powers

The layer **beneath the pantheons** — the older-than-gods forces the four cultures each glimpse, name, and misunderstand in their own way. Where the Armisian, An'Hiseretian, Athregali, and Ziramati faiths are the religions of *peoples*, these are the powers that were here first: dragons, the deep, fate, the slumbering things in the unexplored dark.

> **Flavour & purpose:** this is the home for **cross-pantheon echoes** — one true primordial seen through four cultural lenses — and for the cosmic substrate that ties the campaign to the eventual [[Apostasy]].

## The Primordials
- **[[Tiamat|Tiamat, the World-Mother]].** The creator dragon-goddess of [[_Ziramati Pantheon|Ziramati]] myth: she made the world and bore **five dragons** to dominate it. The deepest named power in the cosmology.
- **[[Primordial Dragons]].** Tiamat's five offspring — incomprehensibly magical world-shapers whose power remakes whole landscapes and **bleeds into the earth even after death**. [[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]] is the living one (her crimson desert is her own work); a **dead** one lies in [[Adamantis]].
- **The Slumbering Deep — *Tiâmtu*.** The Ziramati name for **[[Adamantis]]** invokes Tiamat herself — and **rightly**: the Isle holds a dead primordial dragon, and the **Aether-heavy residuum** everyone covets is its leaking magic. The other cultures name the Isle too (Armisian *Adamantis*, Athregali *Reiðrey*) without understanding it.

## Still Open (scaffold — fill in)
