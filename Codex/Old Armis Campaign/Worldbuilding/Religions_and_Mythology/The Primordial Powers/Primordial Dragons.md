# Primordial Dragons

The **five dragon offspring** of **[[Tiamat|Tiamat, the World-Mother]]** — and the deepest true power of the setting. Where mortal "dragons" are merely great beasts, the **primordials** are forces of creation: incredibly magical, world-shaping entities set by their mother to **dominate the world she made**. **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]** is the only one the living world knows by name.

## Their Nature
- **World-shapers.** A primordial dragon does not merely *live* in a land — it **remakes** it. Its magic bends the landscape, the climate, and the ecosystem around it into its own image over ages.
- **Magic in the earth.** Their power **soaks into the ground** itself. The land near a primordial becomes saturated with raw Aether, its flora and fauna warped into something of the dragon's character.
- **It outlives them.** Crucially, this seepage **does not stop at death.** A dead primordial's magic keeps flowing into the earth, reshaping it long after the dragon itself is gone — a wound in the world that never closes.

## Seen in the World
- **Azhdašar & [[Ziramat]] (living).** The **crimson desert** and red-rock cliffs of Ziramat are no accident — they are the living mark of a Red primordial reshaping her domain on the **[[The Topaz Sea]]**.
- **[[Adamantis]] (dead/dormant).** The Isle *is* the resting place of one of the **lost dragons**. Its hostile, self-defending ecosystem, its elusive native peoples, and above all its **Aether-heavy residuum** are all the work of a primordial's magic still bleeding into the land — almost certainly **after its death**. *(GM truth — see the Isle's note.)*
- **[[The Blood Empire]] (living).** Far east past the **[[The Eastern Steppes|Great Grass-Sea]]**, a second living primordial rules a civilisation of **blood-sorcery** from walled cities "of purity" — a third sibling placed, its land remade in its own image exactly as Azhdašar's is. *(GM truth — see [[Tiamat]].)*

## The Stolen Secret of Hatching
When **[[Ziramat]]** is finally ground down across the **[[Timeline (Armis)|Cold Centuries]]**, both successor empires — **[[Pattis]]** and the **[[Ezaro|Aldari Realm of Ezaro]]** — come away with something more lasting than plunder: the Ziramati secret of **dragon hatching**, guarded for an age and surrendered only in the empire's death throes. Each side breeds and bonds dragons of its own in the centuries after, and each raises its own **Dragon-Rider Houses** as a result — a deliberate, mutually-assured-destruction echo of Azhdašar's own brood. It is these houses, risen in both realms, that the *Brightvale*-era **[[Dragons]]** bestiary entry remembers as "the Empire's" Dragon-Rider Houses, decimated together when the Apostasy turned every dragon on its rider in a single night.

## Hooks (GM)
- The **residuum** every power is racing to exploit is, in truth, a **dead god's leaking lifeblood**. Mining it is desecration — and exactly the hubris that could birth the [[Apostasy]].
- Where are the **other two** primordials? With Azhdašar, the Isle's dead one, and the dragon of **[[The Blood Empire]]** now placed, two of the five remain unfound — each a land-shaping cataclysm waiting somewhere on the map. *(See [[Tiamat]].)*
