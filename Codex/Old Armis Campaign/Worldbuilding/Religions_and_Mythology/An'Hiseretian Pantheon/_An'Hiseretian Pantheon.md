# An'Hiseretian Pantheon

The gods of **[[An'Hiseret]]** — an **ancient-Egyptian** faith of the river, the sun, and above all **death and the deathless**. This is the theology that let god-princes and high priests rule for centuries: in An'Hiseret, death is not an ending but a throne one can hold. Worship centres on the great river **[[The Seretis|Seretis]]** and the necromantic mysteries woven through it.

> **Flavour:** animal-headed gods, mortuary cults, divine kingship, the weighing of souls — and a uniquely literal traffic between the living and the dead.

## The Amun Line — the Death God and Her Sons
At the centre of the cult stands **Amun-Atir, the Death God**, divine mother of the deathless royal line, whose seat is the temple-city of **[[Atir]]**. Her sons, the demi-god princes, fell into a **civil war** over which would be made eternal — and from that war came the most dangerous knowledge in the world: how to *truly* unmake a soul-bound, deathless being. One son, **[[The Return of Xol-amun|Xol-amun]]**, throned at **[[Xol]]**, had his eternal soul-binding interrupted by the Armisian conquest (~900 AF) and waits, bodiless and bound, for it to be completed.

> **GM-only.** The Amun line is the thread that runs straight into the **[[Apostasy]]**: the loosed soul-unmaking knowledge becomes the **god-killing weapon** of the Century of Blood, and in that war Amun-Atir herself is *erased from existence.* See **[[The Return of Xol-amun]]**.

## Hooks (GM)
- The **upriver civilisations** (see [[The Seretis]]) may worship older or stranger gods — a chance to introduce a faith even An'Hiseret considers heretical or primordial.
- A death-cult that blurs life and death is potent [[Apostasy]] foreshadowing.
