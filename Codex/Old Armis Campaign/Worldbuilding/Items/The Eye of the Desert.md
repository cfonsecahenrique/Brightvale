# The Eye of the Desert

**The third key to godhood** — a Ziramati relic looted in the long grinding-down of **[[Ziramat]]**, and the last piece the Pattisian Aetherweavers believe they need to complete the **[[The Amun-Orb|Amun-Orb]]**'s true purpose.

> **GM-only.** Reveal it as late as the Orb itself. It is not the Bid's engine — it is what convinces Pattis the engine can finally be finished.

## What It Is
A sphere of fused red glass and bone, said to be cut from the desert floor where one of **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]**'s ancestors first bled into the sand — a fragment of primordial residuum so old and so concentrated that the Ziramati never dared mine the ground around it, and instead built a cult to *watch* it. To the Ziramati it was a warning made object: proof of what raw godhood looks like, and an argument for never reaching after it.

## How Pattis Gets It
As **[[Ziramat]]** is ground down across the **[[Timeline (Armis)|Cold Centuries]]** by both successor empires, its temple-cult of the Eye falls with everything else. Pattisian Aetherweavers recognise it for what the Ziramati always knew it was — and where the Ziramati saw a warning, Pattis sees a missing component.

## Why It Matters
Adamantis residuum gives the Orb its fuel; the slain **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]**'s primal core gives it a *living* god's pattern to imitate. The Eye is neither — it is proof, in matter, that a primordial's power can be held in a fixed, stable form instead of bleeding away into the earth. Study it long enough, and the Pattisian Masters convince themselves they can stop *borrowing* divinity through the Orb and instead **become** it. That conviction is what finally moves the gods to act. (See **[[Apostasy]]**.)

## Hooks (GM)
- Whoever carries the Eye out of a dying Ziramat carries the last piece of the Apostasy's spark, and likely has no idea.
- A Ziramati survivor who remembers what the Eye-cult actually believed could undercut Pattis's confidence — or confirm its worst fears.
