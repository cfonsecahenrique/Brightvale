# The Amun-Orb

**The engine of apotheosis** — a bound divine soul wed to the leaking lifeblood of a dead god, and the artifact through which the Master Aetherweavers of **[[Pattis]]** mean to make mortals into gods.

> **GM-only.** The Amun-Orb is the mechanism of the **[[Apostasy|Bid for Divinity]]**. Reveal it late and never explain it fully.

## What It Is
The Orb fuses two of the most dangerous things in the world:
- a **bound demi-god soul** — the unfinished, deathless soul drawn from **[[The Wandering Necropolis]]** and the line of **[[The Return of Xol-amun|Xol-amun]]**, held against its passage by An'Hiseretian death-art; and
- raw **[[Adamantis|residuum]]** — the bleeding lifeblood of a dead **[[Primordial Dragons|primordial dragon]]** —
focused through the highest planar craft of **[[Aetherweave Mastery]]**.

Together they let its wielders **undo the line between life and death itself** — to break the cycle the gods once kept, and let humanity grasp at divinity and immortality.

## Why It Matters
The Orb is how the **[[Apostasy]]** *begins* — but not all at once. Its first true use, a century after the campaign present, is smaller and more selfish than apotheosis: Armisian elites learn to carve **personal immortality** from it, and that century of deathless lords (see **[[Timeline (Armis)]]**) is what actually tears the Empire apart. Only centuries later, fed with the slain **[[Azhdashar]]**'s primal core and **[[The Eye of the Desert]]**, does it offer Pattis the real Bid — true godhood, not borrowed life.

## The Edge That Cuts Both Ways
The Orb's deepest secret, understood by only its most dangerous students, is that it can **unmake** what it grants as readily as grant it: an immortality carved from it can be carved back out, a divinity bid for can, in principle, be undone. This is the only reason the **[[Ezaro|Aldari Realm]]** and **[[Pattis|the Pattisian Empire]]** hold their **[[Timeline (Armis)|250-year cold peace]]** at all — each knows the other could, in theory, reach the Orb's secrets and unmake its rival's lords wholesale. Mutual dread of that reversal is the only treaty that matters.

## Hooks (GM)
- Whoever holds the Orb holds the question of the age: should mortals become gods? Let the player be its shield, its thief, or its destroyer.
- The Orb is a fragment of a god held against its will; what **[[The Return of Xol-amun|Xol-amun]]** wants done with it is its own dark thread.
- An Aldari or Pattisian scholar who works out the unmaking-edge before their rivals do holds a weapon neither side has dared use yet.
