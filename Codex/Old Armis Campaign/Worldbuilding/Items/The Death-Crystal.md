# The Death-Crystal

An **An'Hiseretian necromantic focus** — a worked crystal that channels the death-Weave of the conquered south. The kind of relic An'Hiseret is forbidden to make under occupation, and quietly still does.

> **GM:** A small, dangerous, *legal*-grey artifact — a way to put the deathless south's power into a player's hands (or a Senator's cargo) early, and to make the Aether-Helm very interested.

## What It Is
A focus of the **necromantic Weave** — the tradition entirely unlike the Empire's planar **[[Aetherweave Mastery|Aetherweave]]** — cut and consecrated in the death-houses of **[[An'Hiseret]]**. To Armisian eyes it is a curiosity and a contraband; to the death-priests it is a working tool of a craft the conquest scattered but never ended. Drawing on it is potent and perilous, and prolonged use marks the user.

## Why It Matters
The death-crystal is a small, portable proof that the south's mastery over the boundary of life and death did not die with its conquest — one more thread in the **[[The Return of Xol-amun|held-open death]]** that is a tremor of the **[[Apostasy]]** to come. The Master Aetherweavers of **[[Pattis]]** and the Aether-Helm both covet samples of An'Hiseretian death-Weave; a crystal that reaches Pattis intact is a real political and arcane asset.

## Hooks (GM)
- A retrieval prize an occupied province does not want leaving the south — and that more than one Imperial faction wants for itself.
- A curious owner who learns what the crystal really does holds knowledge the Empire would rather suppress.
