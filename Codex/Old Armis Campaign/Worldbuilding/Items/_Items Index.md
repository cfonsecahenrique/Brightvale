# Items — Index

Artefacts, relics, and exotic goods of the Armisian age — especially the treasures hauled back from the **[[The Western Reaches|new world]]** across the **[[The Azurian Ocean]]**.

## Artifacts of the Doom
- **[[The Amun-Orb]]** — the engine of apotheosis: a bound divine soul wed to dead-god residuum, the mechanism of the **[[Apostasy|Bid for Divinity]]**.
- **[[The Death-Crystal]]** — an An'Hiseretian necromantic focus; the deathless south's power in a single worked crystal.
- **[[The Eye of the Desert]]** — a fused relic of red glass and bone looted from a dying **[[Ziramat]]**; the third key, with the Amun-Orb and Azhdašar's primal core, to the Pattisian Bid for Divinity.
