# The Beacon — GM Secrets

> **This document contains GM-only information. Players should not have access to these details.**

---

## The Beacon's True Mechanism

The Beacon does not merely ward off the [[Umbra]]. It **burns the souls of all living beings within its reach** — slowly, imperceptibly — consuming the spiritual residue of the living before the [[Umbra]] can claim it. The Enduring do not feel this process. They experience the Beacon's warmth as protection, as comfort, as home. They do not know that every day spent within its light shortens something they cannot name.

The critical consequence: **the Beacon interrupts the afterlife cycle entirely.** Souls consumed by the Beacon do not pass into whatever divine architecture once existed. They do not recycle. They burn, and they are gone. The Caretakers' Last Rites do not send the dead onward — they ensure the dead are fuel.

This is why the Gods' departure matters. The cycle of life-death-afterlife that once sustained the world has been replaced by a closed loop of consumption. The Beacon keeps the Enduring alive by devouring what should come after life.

## Beacon Dust & Ashgeld

The ash produced by the Beacon's eternal fire is collected by the [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretakers]] and processed into two forms:

- **Fertilizer:** Beacon Dust is spread across the Vale's agricultural fields, where it produces unnaturally productive harvests. The crops of Brightvale grow in conditions that should not support them — dim light, poor soil, shortened seasons — because the Dust carries residual vital energy drawn from the Beacon's consumption.
- **Currency:** The same ash is pressed and minted into **Ashcoins**, commonly called **Ashgeld**. These small, pale coins are the standard unit of trade in Brightvale. They carry a faint warmth and are said to glow slightly in complete darkness.

The population accepts both uses without question. Beacon Dust is holy residue, a gift of the divine fire. Ashgeld is stable currency backed by the most reliable institution in the Vale.

## The Secret of Beacon Dust

The source material for Beacon Dust is not abstract divine energy.

It is **bone**.

Specifically, the ground and incinerated remains of the **Marked** — those individuals who bore the deepest connection to the Beacon's power during their lives. The Kings of Brightvale, the Sun-Born line, are the most prominent among the Marked, but they are not the only ones. High-ranking [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretakers]], powerful [[Aetherweaver Academy|Aetherweavers]] who have spent decades channelling the Beacon's light, and certain individuals who develop an unusually strong resonance with the fire — all of them are, upon death, rendered into the Dust.

The Caretakers who manage this process are few. The knowledge is held at the highest levels of the Order, shared with no outside institution and no monarch. Whether the current [[King Alfwyn]] knows is uncertain.

The crops grow because the dead feed them. The coins circulate because the dead back their value. Brightvale's economy and its survival are, at their foundation, built on the consumption of its own people.
