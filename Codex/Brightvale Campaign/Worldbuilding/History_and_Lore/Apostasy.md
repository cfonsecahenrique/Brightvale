# [[Apostasy]]
![[Pasted image 20260420135252.png]]
The [[Apostasy]] is a legendary event shrouded in mystery, that ultimately led to the world as it is today. It is said the the Gods abandoned the world, allowing darkness and chaos to seep into the world. 

Slowly, endless gloom engulfed the skies, leaving nights black as pitch and even the zenith of day a dreary haze. The somber grays of the mist and sky are occasionally broken by heavy rains, snowfall or strange erratic weather events. 

The Apostasy is known was the moment civilization was left by the gods, but what happened right before is unknown to those in the present. What is known is that it happened in a moment of great strife, that continued, even worse, for the next 100 years of the [[Umbra]] slowly setting in, corrupting all life. This century was marked by chaos-inflicted wars supernatural wars -- such as great magical beings being driven insane and ravaging cities and towns -- political wars due to the famine that set, and different ideals on how to survive these trying times. 

![[Pasted image 20260422164406.png]]

This century was also marked by great heroes fighting back against the Umbra and those it touched. Heroes with great pre-Apostasy powers, some even stronger due to absence of the gods, led inhabitants of the "civilized" world against the dark.

But the dark consumed everything, and when such a powerful hero fell, they would come back. Touched by the dark. Umbra-touched. Their bodies ever-more deformed. Their fatal wounds the grounds for another terrible limb, horn or tentacle to grow. And then the fallen heroes became humanity's worst nightmare. Humanity's own strength would turn against itself, devouring the world as we knew it.
