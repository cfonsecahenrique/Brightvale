# [[Emberfort]]

**[[Emberfort]]** does not announce itself gently. It sits wedged into a narrow gorge of the Teardrop Mountains — the **Idol Hollows**, where the rock closes in on both sides and the sky becomes a pale strip far overhead, too distant from [[The Beacon]] and too hemmed by peaks for its light to reach with any warmth — with the posture of something that has held on through sheer stubbornness. Walls of aether-threaded stone are fused directly into the rockface on both sides; ramparts bristle with ballistas against whatever the Wastes might send skyward; and at its highest point, the Crow's Nest burns a single lit window watching the lowlands for signals no one wants to see. The settlement climbs in both directions at once: upward along the gorge rim under open sky, where the Lower Town Markets push coal smoke and ozone into the cold mountain air, and downward through the Tunnels — a multi-levelled undercity of vaulted Armisian stone where half the population eats, sleeps, and barters by firelight. It is a small city and a dense one, built into every available inch of crag and cliff, smelling of forge heat, cheap stew, and the peculiar metallic tang that lingers on gear that has been near the [[Umbra]] and survived. What light the Beacon cannot provide, Emberfort makes itself: forge fires, tunnel lanterns, the Chapel's pale flame, the torches along the Rampart Walk — all burning without pause, turning the gorge into something that never fully sleeps. Day and night blur into a permanent dim dusk, somber and smoke-touched, and somehow always alive. To the south, the **Venturer's Trail** descends from the Gate of Souls into the [[The Somber Gorge|Somber Gorge]] and the Wastes beyond it. A pale lantern burns above the Chapel of the Amber Light at all hours — for the departing, for the returning, and for the ones the Ossuary Wall is still waiting to name.

### The Sentinel of the Vale
Initially built as a simple defensive bastion, [[Emberfort]] has grown into a bustling, rough-edged settlement. It is the primary base for the **[[Emberguard]]**, who monitor all who enter or exit the valley. Its walls are thick, reinforced with aether-infused stone, and lined with ballistas capable of taking down the largest flying monstrosities.

### The Venturer Hub
More than just a military outpost, [[Emberfort]] is the heart of the **Venturer** community. It is the primary headquarters for:
*   **The [[Explorers' Guild]]**: Who maintain their drafting rooms and equipment sheds here, planning their descents into the Wastes.
*   **The [[Slayers]]**: Who use the fort's training yard (the "Pit") to drill recruits and refine the "Verse of the Blade."

The fort's lower town is a chaotic collection of smithies, reagent markets, and taverns. The air is always thick with the smell of coal smoke and the ozone-tang of magical gear being repaired. It is a place of high stakes and short lives, where the wealth recovered from the Old World ruins flows freely before being sent up to the noble courts of [[Medsen]].

### The Lower Town Markets

The most active commercial district in Emberfort is the **Lower Town Markets** — a dense, loud stretch of stalls, storefronts, and makeshift workshops that caters almost exclusively to Venturers and the people who outfit them.

*   **Ashwick's Provisions**: The go-to stop for any Venturer heading into the Wastes. Run by **Bruna Ashwick**, a no-nonsense [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarf]] with a reputation for stocking exactly what you need and not a single thing you don't. She keeps a tight inventory of adventuring gear, rope, torches, ration packs, field medkits, and basic alchemical supplies. Bruna has heard every sob story from every underfunded Venturer who's passed through, and she's not moved by any of them.
*   **The Last Ember**: The most frequented inn in Emberfort, known for its battered furniture, surprisingly decent stew, and the kind of atmosphere where people don't ask too many questions. It draws a nightly crowd of Venturers, off-duty Emberguard, and the occasional wandering merchant. Rumours, contracts, and bad decisions are its most popular exports.

### The Pit

Further into the Lower Town stands **The Pit** — a sunken, open-air arena that serves as the permanent headquarters of the **[[Slayers]]' Guild** in Emberfort. It is where recruits are drilled, where guild business is conducted, and where candidates present themselves for admission.

## Notable Locations

Emberfort occupies a narrow gorge in the Teardrop Mountains, its outer walls fused into the rockface on both sides. Space is scarce and the settlement is sharply vertical. The **upper fort** clings to the gorge rim under open sky — garrison, markets, training grounds, and the Gate itself. Below it, carved into the mountain over generations, are **the Tunnels**: a multi-level undercity of vaulted corridors and stone-cut chambers that predates the fort by centuries, likely of Armisian origin. Roughly half the city lives and works above ground. The other half has mostly made peace with the dark.

### Above the Stone

*   **The Gate of Souls** — The iron-bound double gate at the gorge mouth that opens onto the Venturer's Trail. Manned around the clock by [[Emberguard]] checkpoint officers who log every party going out and every survivor returning. The name is informal; it has been long enough that nobody remembers the formal one.
*   **The Irongate Watch** — The [[Emberguard]]'s command post, built into the gorge wall immediately behind the Gate of Souls. Passes are issued here, identities verified, and the watch rotation managed. [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner]]'s authority is absolute within these walls, which is one reason certain people prefer the Tunnels.
*   **The Parade Ground** — A broad expanse of compacted stone between the Gate and the Lower Town. Expedition parties assemble here before departure; the Emberguard drills here at dawn. On some mornings both happen at once, which pleases neither group.
*   **The Rampart Walk** — The fortified wall-top walkway ringing the upper fort, bristling with ballistas. Sentinels rotate through it day and night. On clear evenings, off-duty Venturers come up to watch the [[Umbra]]-flux shimmer in the lowlands — a habit considered a bad idea and widely practiced regardless.
*   **The Crow's Nest** — The high [[Explorers' Guild]] observatory at the north end of the Rampart Walk, used to monitor weather, Umbra-flux density, and movement in the Wastes below.
*   **The Lower Town Markets** — The dense commercial strip running from the Pit to the tunnel entrances: smithies, reagent stalls, gear merchants, food vendors. Ashwick's Provisions and The Last Ember are both here.
*   **The Pit** — The [[Slayers]]' sunken open-air training arena. Guild headquarters, drill ground, and admission stage. The sound of steel on stone carries up the gorge walls before dawn.
*   **The Chapel of the Amber Light** — A small [[Caretakers of the Beacon]] shrine tucked against the eastern gorge wall, identifiable by a pale lantern burning above its door at all hours. Brother Eddan tends it. Venturers come for departure blessings; the dead receive Last Rites here before their names are taken down into the Tunnels.

### Below the Stone — The Tunnels

The Tunnels predate the fort by centuries and may be Armisian in origin. They were converted to habitable use as the gorge above filled up. The quality of stonework varies sharply by depth — upper corridors are wide and vaulted, carved by skilled hands; the lower warrens are narrower and rougher, expanded as needed by whoever had a pickaxe and a reason.

*   **The Venturer's Hall** — The largest single chamber in the Tunnels: a converted Armisian vaulted hall that now serves as the communal hub for Venturers between expeditions. The ground floor holds long tables, a permanent fire, and expedition contract boards maintained jointly by both guilds. The upper levels — mezzanine galleries carved into the chamber walls — are bunk dormitories. A Venturer with coin sleeps at The Last Ember. A Venturer with no coin sleeps here. Most Venturers sleep here regardless, because contracts are posted on these boards before they reach the surface.
*   **The Weighing Room** — An [[Explorers' Guild]]-administered chamber where recovered Waste goods are catalogued, assessed, and valued before being packaged for the climb to [[Medsen]]. Issa Drevorn maintains a desk in the corner, at the House's insistence and at nobody's invitation.
*   **The Field Station** — A compact [[Aetherweaver Academy]] outpost one level below the Venturer's Hall: a room of flux-measurement arrays, scroll racks, and humming instruments pointed toward the Gate of Souls above. Ysa Vael works here alone. Guild members occasionally knock on the door with items they don't understand. She charges a consultation fee and spends it on better instruments.
*   **The Knot** — The four-way intersection at the centre of the Tunnel network, where the Slayers' corridor, the Explorers' corridor, the Slag District access, and the Venturer's Hall all converge. Loudest during shift changes. Quietest at the third hour of night, which is precisely when the more discreet transactions tend to occur.
*   **The Slag District** — The deepest operational level, where forge heat vents up through the stone rather than out into the gorge. Half a dozen smithies operate here, including Mallet's forge. The air is permanently hot, orange-lit, and loud. Things that need repairing urgently — in Emberfort, most things do — end up here.
*   **The Ossuary Wall** — A long, low corridor descending from the Chapel of the Amber Light into the Tunnels. Both walls are carved with the names of Venturers who did not return. The oldest inscriptions have been worn smooth by hands running along them in the dark. New names are added by Brother Eddan using a chisel he keeps for exactly this purpose. If the body was not recovered, he leaves a blank space beside the name.

## Notable Locals

A selection of the colourful, rough-edged people that Venturers will encounter on any given day in Emberfort.

| Name | Description | Allegiance |
|------|-------------|------------|
| **Bruna Ashwick** | Dwarf owner of Ashwick's Provisions. Practical, unsentimental, stocked with exactly what you need. Has heard every underfunded Venturer's plea and remains unmoved by all of them. Keeps a Beacon-ward bolted above the door — "good for business," she says, which is as close to piety as she gets. | Independent |
| **Corvin** | Innkeeper of The Last Ember. Thick-armed, grey-bearded, few opinions and fewer questions. Keeps the ale cold and the peace — mostly by knowing exactly when to stop refilling someone's cup. A Stoic litany is carved into the bar's edge: *the light endures, and so do we*. He put it there the year his wife didn't come back from an expedition. | Independent |
| **Brother Eddan** | The [[Caretakers of the Beacon]]' resident priest at Emberfort's small Beacon shrine. Performs Last Rites on Venturers who don't return whole — a task he has never fully hardened to. A devout Orthodox believer who reads the Beacon's dimming as divine warning. Gives departing expeditions a blessing whether they want one or not, and quietly notes the faces of those who decline. | [[Caretakers of the Beacon]] |
| **Ysa Vael** | A second-year [[Aetherweaver Academy]] field researcher stationed at Emberfort to measure Umbra-flux near the Gate of Souls, commissioned by the Academy's Progressive faction. Openly Pragmatist: she calls the Beacon "the most interesting unsolved engineering problem in the known world." She and Brother Eddan have established a mutual schedule of deliberate non-interaction. | [[Aetherweaver Academy]] |
| **Duvrak Sigr** | Acting captain of the local [[Slayers]] chapter, filling in during [[Makjar]]'s absences. A compact, battle-scarred Dwarf who runs the Pit with cold efficiency. Doesn't raise his voice. Doesn't need to. An Orthodox man who believes the Beacon flickers because too many of his recruits have forgotten what sacrifice costs. | [[Slayers]] |
| **Petra** | A silent veteran Explorer who has run expeditions alongside Grand Explorer [[Mira Ravenna]] herself. Never wastes words, never wastes steps. Whether the stillness in her is Stoic faith or something older and quieter, no one has ever asked her directly. | [[Explorers' Guild]] |
| **Kaze Swift-Arrow** | Orc Slayer veteran, named for his speed with a shortbow. Boisterous at the tavern, dependable in a fight. Collects trophies from Umbra-touched creatures. Lights a Stoic candle on the first of every month for the expedition partners he's outlived — calls it superstition, means it as prayer. | [[Slayers]] |
| **Kaze "Kita" Deep-Delver** | Kaze's older sister — methodical, quiet, and unsettlingly calm underground. An Explorer specialist in below-grade ruins; others request her on cave-heavy expeditions. She doesn't speak of faith, but carries a small worn figure in her pack whose origin she's never explained. | [[Explorers' Guild]] |
| **Oswin "Patches" Brale** | Retired Venturer who lost his sword hand to an Umbra-touched creature and now runs a cramped pawnshop dealing in recovered relics. Once Stoic. Now just bitter: *the light endures, sure, but it didn't endure for my hand.* Encyclopedic about the Wastes regardless. | Independent |
| **Seln** | An Elven healer who set up a low-cost treatment post near the Pit. Treats Slayers and Venturers with equal detachment and keeps careful notes on every wound she sees. Openly Pragmatist on faith: she does not pray over wounds, she stitches them. Has not attended a single Caretaker blessing since she arrived. | Independent |
| **Tomas "Two-Coin" Hecht** | Emberfort's unofficial bookie. Takes bets on expedition outcomes, knows which Venturers are overconfident, and has an unnerving talent for knowing who isn't coming back. Claims the Beacon "runs on math, not miracles — same as my odds." Some consider this blasphemy. He takes bets on that too. | Independent |
| **Mallet** | A massive, scar-covered Orc blacksmith who works the forge closest to the Pit. Speaks rarely, works constantly, best in Emberfort for Umbra-resistant repairs. His years in the Wastes are a topic he does not entertain. The only decoration in his forge is a small veiled-woman carving he deflects questions about. | Independent |
| **Finney Ashpike** | A young Emberguard lookout posted to the Crow's Nest. Talks constantly — weather, lowland sightings, half-heard rumours. Prays before every watch and after every one he survives; keeps a Beacon-ward hung from the spyglass. Genuinely useful, if you can sort signal from noise. | [[Emberguard]] |

Orthodox in name only; he believes the Beacon was an act of supreme arcane engineering, and that calling it a miracle has cost the Enduring two centuries of progress. | [[House Lugner]] |
