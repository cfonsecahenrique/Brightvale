# Nanobanana Map Prompts — Brightvale Campaign

Prompts for generating parchment-style illustrated maps of Brightvale's main settlements, using the same reference-map style as the [[Old Armis Campaign]] set, adjusted for this setting's colder, dimmer, post-apocalyptic tone. Paste the **Style Block** into every prompt, then append the location-specific block.

---

## Style Block (common to all prompts)

```
Style: hand-drawn fantasy cartography on aged, weathered parchment. Warm sepia and ochre paper tones with soft brown foxing stains and a darkened, deckle-edged border. Fine black ink linework in the manner of a Renaissance-era survey map — cross-hatched shading for relief, stippled texture for terrain. Muted, desaturated watercolor wash restricted to a narrow palette: sea-glass teal/blue-green for water, warm tan/umber for land, dull brown for mountains and buildings. Labels in an elegant engraved serif or blackletter display caps, slightly worn. Include a compass rose (or simplified wind-rose) in one corner, fine gold-brown decorative corner flourishes/scrollwork on at least one corner, and small hand-drawn iconography for landmarks (miniature towers, ships, waves, trees) rendered as tiny pictorial glyphs rather than modern map symbols. No modern typography, no gloss, no bright saturated color, no photographic elements — the whole piece should read as a single, slightly worn physical artifact, corners softly curling, ink a little faded in places. Drawn as a flat top-down schematic map (bird's-eye-from-directly-above), not a painted or angled aerial view. Aspect ratio 4:3 landscape.

Brightvale tonal shift: this is a dark, post-apocalyptic setting under a slowly dimming protective light, so cool the standard palette down — trade some of the warm ochre for cold slate-grey and pale amber/gold accents reserved for anything touched by the Beacon's light (temple windows, ward-lines, lantern glyphs). Where a location borders the Wastes or the Umbra, let the ink linework grow rougher and the wash darken toward a bruised violet-grey at that edge of the map, as if the parchment itself is uneasy there.
```

## Medsen (Emberdawn)

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a top-down map of Medsen, also called Emberdawn — the vertical capital of Brightvale, built into the base of the Frost-Veil Peaks and organized as a steep stack of tiers climbing the mountain rather than a city spread flat. Render it with a strong sense of elevation even in top-down view — use tighter contour/hatch lines and layered building-glyph density to read as "climbing," and label each tier clearly:
- **The High Tier (the Aether-Reach)**, at the city's peak: the Central Temple-Castle, a labyrinthine white-stone fortress built directly over the Beacon, drawn larger and more elaborate than any other structure, its core glowing with a small warm gold-light glyph or ward-pattern distinct from the rest of the cold palette; clustered around it, the palazzos of the Ancient Noble Houses and the Council chambers.
- **The Mid Tier (the Crucible)** below it: a denser band of guildhall and workshop glyphs, forges, and market rows, marking Medsen's commercial and industrial heart.
- **The Low Tier (the Shadows)** at the base and spilling up the surrounding slopes: crowded, irregular favela-like settlement glyphs climbing the mountainsides in switchback terraces (a La Paz/Rio-de-Janeiro-style vertical slum), rendered rougher and denser than the planned center tiers, thinning out where the Beacon's light barely reaches — shade this outer band toward cold shadow.
- Three departing roads clearly marked at the city's edges: southeast toward Veikryss (a wide "Crown Highway"), northeast toward Hvergi (a treacherous mountain path through the Frost-Veil Peaks), and south toward Aeldenbarrow (a winding road through the Whisperwood Valley).
Label the city "MEDSEN (EMBERDAWN)" in large banner lettering, with smaller labels for the Aether-Reach, the Crucible, and the Shadows.
```

## Aurore

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a top-down map of Aurore, a smaller mountain range at the geographic heart of the Vale, crowned by a single imposing summit and drawn as a natural node for the valley's magical currents. Show:
- At the summit: the Aethertower, a tall vertical structure of cobalt-blue stone (the one place on the map allowed a cool blue accent beyond the standard palette), marked as the primary hub for magical research and warding.
- Carved directly into the cliffs below the tower: the sprawling Aetherweavers' Academy, rendered as tiered structures cut into the rock face rather than freestanding buildings, connected to the tower by a narrow stair-path.
- A small palazzo glyph on the mid-slopes marking the seat of House Von Zoltraak.
- The surrounding peaks lightly washed with faint violet-blue streaks or a subtle aurora-glow motif in the sky/border area above the range, hand-inked as delicate curtain-like linework rather than a literal light effect.
- Two departing paths: northeast toward Veikryss through the Arcane Peaks, and west toward Aeldenbarrow through the Whisperwood Valley.
Label the range "AURORE" in large banner lettering, with smaller labels for the Aethertower and the Aetherweavers' Academy.
```

## Hvergi

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a top-down map of Hvergi, a Dwarven enclave carved into the eastern mountain-roots of Brightvale's plateau, functioning as mine, fortress, and cultural heartland at once. Show:
- A compact, heavily fortified surface settlement at the mountain's base — squat stone halls, a fortified gate, and modest above-ground structures, rendered in sturdy, blocky Dwarven-style architecture glyphs rather than the more vertical human style of Medsen.
- Beneath and behind it, a layered cutaway/underground-mine motif (consistent with the map's flat top-down style, shown as a dashed or hatched "beneath the stone" inset) indicating the Deep Mines and their crystal seams — small crystal-cluster glyphs marking the richest veins.
- A secret tunnel heading northeast, drawn fainter and more roughly than the settlement's other features — partially obscured by hatching, as if the cartographer only half-knows it's there, trailing off the edge of the mapped area without a clear endpoint.
- Two marked roads: southwest to Veikryss (the main Mountain Road) and southwest also to Medsen (a direct path through the Frost-Veil Peaks, drawn as a separate route from the Veikryss road), and a steep path south to the high-altitude village of Orska.
Label the enclave "HVERGI" in bold, blocky display lettering distinct from the more flowing script used for human settlements.
```

## Emberfort

```
[STYLE BLOCK]

Subject: a top-down map of Emberfort, a fortress-settlement wedged into a narrow gorge of the Teardrop Mountains (the Idol Hollows), the Vale's sentinel against the Wastes. This location sits at the map's darkest tonal edge — let the palette shift furthest here toward cold slate and bruised violet-grey, with small warm lantern/forge-glow glyphs (the Chapel's pale flame, forge fires, tunnel lanterns) picked out as the only warmth on the page. Render it as two layered bands within the gorge, both visible on the flat map:
- **The upper fort**, along the open gorge rim: the Gate of Souls (a fortified double-gate glyph at the gorge mouth, opening south onto the Venturer's Trail), the Irongate Watch just behind it, the Parade Ground as an open cleared space, the Rampart Walk as a walled walkway studded with small ballista glyphs ringing the upper fort, the Crow's Nest as a watchtower at its northern end, the Lower Town Markets as a dense cluster of stall/smithy glyphs, the Pit as a sunken circular arena, and the Chapel of the Amber Light as a small shrine glyph against the eastern gorge wall marked with a single lantern icon.
- **The Tunnels**, drawn as a lower underground inset (dashed border, as with Hvergi, to signal "beneath the stone" within the same flat-map convention): the Venturer's Hall as the largest chamber, the Weighing Room, the Field Station, the Knot as a four-way junction at the network's center, the Slag District glowing faintly orange at the deepest level, and the Ossuary Wall as a long narrow corridor lined with tiny name-glyphs leading down from the Chapel above.
- Walls of aether-threaded stone fused into the rockface on both gorge sides, and the Venturer's Trail marked descending south from the Gate of Souls toward the Somber Gorge and the Wastes beyond — let the ink and wash grow rough and dark at this southern edge, marking the boundary of the Vale's protection.
- The route northwest to Three Bridges marked ascending out of the gorge onto the plateau.
Label the settlement "EMBERFORT" in large banner lettering, with smaller labels for the Gate of Souls, the Lower Town Markets, the Pit, and the Tunnels.
```
