# Halfling Hearthlands

## Overview
The Halfling Hearthlands is the agricultural heart of Brightvale, situated in a fertile, sacred valley. Here, the [[Halflings]] maintain the lush farmlands that provide a significant portion of the valley's sustenance, utilizing ancient communal storytelling and agrarian rituals to keep the land thriving under the Beacon's light.

The Hearthlands have no lord's seat and no market town — only farmsteads scattered the length of the valley and, at the water's edge, **[[Thistle Falls]]**: a village of a hundred people that serves as the region's only centre. The tithe-grain is counted there, disputes are settled there, and the harvest rites are spoken on its green.

## Connections
*   **East ([[Three Bridges]]):** The Sacred Valley road, running down through [[Thistle Falls]] and out of the valley toward the bridges.
*   **Within the valley ([[Thistle Falls]]):** The Hearthlands' one village, on the headland where the river opens out.
