# Thistle Falls

## Overview
**Thistle Falls** is the main village of the **[[Halfling Hearthlands]]** — which is to say it is the closest thing the sacred valley has to a capital, and it is a hundred people. The Hearthlands are not organised the way the rest of Brightvale is organised: there is no lord's seat, no garrison, and no market town, only farmsteads scattered across the valley floor and, at the water's edge, the one settlement where everyone eventually comes. Tithe-grain is counted at Thistle Falls. Disputes are settled at Thistle Falls. The Hearthlands' harvest rituals are spoken at Thistle Falls, and the valley's whole year is organised around a green the size of a barn yard.

A visitor from [[Three Bridges]] — two days east, and twenty times the size — invariably underestimates the place. The [[Halflings]] are content to let them.

## The Village

![[thistle falls map.webp]]
> *Thistle Falls, pop. 100 — the headland, the green, and the long bridge.*

The village occupies a **low green headland** where the river opens out into still, pale water. The land here is a blunt tongue of pasture and orchard running out between the channel on one side and the broad water on the other, and **every building in Thistle Falls stands on it**:

- **The Green.** At the headland's tip, a **round open green of pale trodden earth**, and around it, facing inward, the dozen-odd cottages that are the village proper — timber under thatch and plank, with a couple of slate roofs among them for the families that can afford the difference. This is where the counting, the judging, and the singing all happen, on the same ground, often on the same day.
- **The Long Bridge.** From the green, a **narrow timber footbridge** runs out straight across the water to the far bank — far longer than it has any business being, laid on trestles, and wide enough for one cart or two people. It is the village's single connection to the road that runs along the northern shore, and it is rebuilt after nearly every hard winter.
- **The Upper Crossing.** Two miles upstream, where the channel is still narrow, a second and much shorter bridge carries the **Sacred Valley road** over the water. This is the way wagons come, and the reason the village has a road at all.
- **The North Shore.** Along the far bank, strung out beside the road, a **thin line of farmsteads and drying-sheds** — not part of the village in any way the villagers would acknowledge, but the first thing an arriving traveller sees.

Around and behind all of it: **fields in the Hearthlands' patchwork** — pale gold standing grain, dark ploughed strips, hedged orchards and copses dividing one holding from the next all the way up the valley. There is no wall, no palisade, no watchtower, and no space set aside for any of the three.

**The falls.** The cataract the village is named for is not here. It lies upstream and out of sight, where the river comes down off the valley shoulder in a stand of thistle and rock; the villagers walk out to it for certain days in the year and otherwise leave it alone.

## Hooks (GM)
- A hundred people, no defences, and the valley's entire grain tally sitting in sheds on an undefended headland. Everyone in Brightvale knows this. Nobody has yet had to think about it.
- **The Long Bridge is the village's only quick way out**, and it is one cart wide.
- The Hearthlands' agrarian rites are spoken here, and the [[Halflings]] do not explain them to outsiders. Whether they are folk tradition or something older is exactly the sort of question **[[Aeldenbarrow]]** would like answered.

## Connections

*   **West / Upvalley ([[Halfling Hearthlands]]):** Farm tracks into the scattered holdings of the sacred valley, and the path to the falls.
