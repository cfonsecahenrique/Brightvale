# [[Orska]]

## Overview
**Orska** is a small, rugged village perched on the eastern edge of Brightvale. Situated at a considerable altitude in a stark, highly mountainous landscape, it features steep inclines and thin, biting air.

Unlike the densely agricultural lowlands, Orska is defined by its sweeping high-altitude pastures where cattle graze freely across the slopes. It is a vital exporter of cattle-based products to the rest of the valley, supplying much of Brightvale's leather, cured meats, and bone.

The settlement is small, unwalled, and built entirely against the weather rather than against men. **[[House Drevorn]]** maintain a keep above it to oversee the lucrative livestock trade and consolidate their monopoly over the valley's supply chains.

## The Village

![[orska map.webp]]
> *Orska, pop. 300 — under snow, which is most of the year.*

There is no square, no green, and no crossroads. Orska is **one long lane switchbacking up the mountainside**, with its houses set in tight ranks along it and along the few short spurs that branch off: **narrow stone longhouses under steep roofs**, byre and dwelling under a single ridgeline, gable-end to the wind and packed close enough that a man can cross from one doorway to the next without losing his footing in a drift. Perhaps four dozen of them, holding three hundred souls and rather more cattle.

**Dark conifer forest crowds the village on three sides**, coming down the slope almost to the rooftops — the treeline is the village's windbreak, its firewood, and the reason nothing here is built of anything but stone and spruce. Above the trees the land opens out into the high summer pastures; below, the lane drops away southwest toward the lowlands. Snow lies over all of it from first frost to late thaw, and the roads out of Orska leave the settled ground as **dotted traces** rather than roads — lines of stakes and cairns that a herder follows when the drifts have erased everything else.

The keep of **[[House Drevorn]]** does not stand among the houses. It sits apart and above them, on the shoulder of the mountain beyond the last of the trees — a small, severe hold with a clear view down the whole length of the lane and of every beast that moves on the pastures. The Drevorn factor lives in the village; the family visits.
