# [[Orska]]

## Overview
**Orska** is a small, rugged village perched on the eastern edge of Brightvale. Situated at a considerable altitude in a stark, highly mountainous landscape, it features steep inclines and thin, biting air.

Unlike the densely agricultural lowlands, Orska is defined by its sweeping high-altitude pastures where cattle graze freely across the slopes. It is a vital exporter of cattle-based products to the rest of the valley, supplying much of Brightvale's leather, cured meats, and bone.

The settlement is small but heavily fortified against the harsh mountain elements. It consists of roughly a dozen stone houses clustered together for warmth and defense, all situated beneath the looming shadow of a small, severe castle. This keep belongs to **[[House Drevorn]]**, who maintain a presence here to oversee the lucrative livestock trade and consolidate their monopoly over the valley's supply chains.

## Connections
*   **North ([[Hvergi]]):** A steep climbing path winding upward toward the dwarven enclave of Hvergi.
*   **Southwest ([[Three Bridges]]):** A descending trail that connects back to the bustling hub of Three Bridges.
