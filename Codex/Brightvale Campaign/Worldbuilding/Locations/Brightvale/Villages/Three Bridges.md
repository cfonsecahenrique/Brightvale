# Three Bridges

## Overview
Three Bridges is a thriving, bustling village situated precisely where the Coldrun and the Swiftwater rivers violently converge. It stands at the vital chokepoint entrance to Brightvale's interior, located along the **Venturer's Trail** just north of the looming shadow of **[[Emberfort]]**. Because of its strategic location, nearly all Venturers pass through Three Bridges before making their final march to Emberfort and out into the Wastes, and it serves as the first true bastion of civilian safety they reach upon their return. 

It is one of the most culturally diverse settlements in Brightvale, boasting a considerable and harmoniously mixed population of [[Humankind|Humans]], [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]], [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarves]], and [[Halflings]]. Its numerous inns and taverns are beloved by Venturers. They offer the warm hearths and strong drinks necessary to thaw the chill of the Umbra after grueling expeditions, as well as the worldly comforts one partakes in before heading out into the deadly Wastes, potentially for the last time. Here, stories are traded like currency, and news from the outside world is first spread to the valley.

## Geography

![[three bridges map.webp]]
> *Three Bridges, pop. 720 — the confluence and its three quarters.*

The village takes its name from three massive stone bridges that span the converging rivers, connecting the divided landmasses. The **Coldrun** comes down out of the northern woods in a hard curve; the **Swiftwater** runs in from the west; they meet at the village's eastern edge and leave as one broad channel bending away southeast toward Emberfort. Three Bridges is therefore not one village but **three quarters on three separate pieces of ground**, each reachable from the others only by a span.

- **The North Bank** — the wedge of land between the two rivers above the confluence. The newest and most orderly quarter: straight lanes of timber houses with a scatter of slate-roofed halls among them, hemmed on every side by ploughland and orchard strips that run right up to the doorsteps. The northern road leaves from here for Veikryss.
- **The South Bank** — the largest and oldest quarter, a fan of lanes spreading uphill and away from the water, packed tighter than anywhere in the valley outside Veikryss. At its southern edge is the **crossroads**, where King Aldhelm's charred monument stands and where the Venturer's Trail peels off for Emberfort. The **millhouse** and its great wheel sit beside it, the loudest building in the village.
- **The West Reach** — a long ribbon of houses strung out along the Swiftwater's southern bank, backed immediately by open field. This is the poorest and least defensible quarter, and the one the Sacred Valley road enters from the west.

**The spans themselves.** Three are the great ones — heavy stone piers carrying a timbered deck wide enough for a laden wagon, the **Ironspan** among them. The lesser plank crossings that now thread the rivers upstream and down were added piecemeal as the village outgrew its own name; the tollmen watch the three, and everyone knows which of the small ones can be walked at night without being seen.

**The Strand.** Inside the bend of the confluence, where the two currents drop their silt, lies a pale crescent of sand and gravel — the village's landing beach. Flatboats are drawn up here, and the valley's lumber and grain are weighed on the Strand before they are taxed and sent down the Trail to the garrison. It is also, by long custom, where the village holds its fairs, its fistfights, and its funerals.

**And no walls.** Not a palisade, not a ditch, not a gate. Three Bridges has never been fortified — the rivers were held to be wall enough, and the ploughland runs unbroken from the last house to the treeline in every direction. Anyone who has read the account of the Battle understands exactly what that cost, and the village has still not built one.

Because it straddles these critical arteries, Three Bridges is a major economic hub. Supplies, lumber, and grain from across the valley flow through here before being taxed and sent down to the garrison at Emberfort.

## History: The Battle of Three Bridges
The village is not just a tavern hub; it is the most sacred and blood-soaked battleground in modern Brightvale history. 

During the brutal **[[The Brightblood War]]** (293–297 AA), Three Bridges became the site of the civil war's climactic conflict. A coalition of reformist Noble Houses had finally managed to corner the Royal Loyalist army here, pinning the Crown's forces against the freezing rivers. The civil war was poised to end in the total slaughter of the Royalists.

However, the sheer bloodshed and emotional resonance of the war attracted a massive Horde of Umbra-touched beasts, which managed to breach the Venturer's Trail and bypass Emberfort's weakened defenses. The Horde poured into Three Bridges, forcing the warring human factions to unite instantaneously or face total annihilation. 

The ensuing **Battle of Three Bridges** was an absolute massacre, but the unified defense held the line and saved the interior of Brightvale from the Umbra. The battle famously cost the life of the reigning monarch, **King Aldhelm**, who died defending the Ironspan alongside the very rebels who had sought to dethrone him. His royal sacrifice forced a bitter but enduring truce—dubbed the Golden Reprieve—that established the current, uneasy political balance between the Monarchy, the Council of Seven, and the Noble Houses. Today, a charred stone monument to King Aldhelm and the unified fallen stands at the center of the village crossroads.
