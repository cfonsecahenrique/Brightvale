# [[Halflings]]

### [[Halflings]] (Guardians of Joy)

Like [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]], [[Halflings]] retain a strong connection to **Nature and the [[Fae]]**. They manage the farmlands through communal storytelling, a ritual they believe keeps the spirits of the [[Fae]] from fading entirely from the Vale.

Their deepest cultural contribution to Brightvale is their central role in maintaining [[The Concordance]] — the vast, decentralized compendium of folk magic and druidic primeval lore that predates the [[Apostasy]]. Halfling elders carry large portions of it in memory, embedded in song, seasonal ritual, and the names they give to days and weather.
