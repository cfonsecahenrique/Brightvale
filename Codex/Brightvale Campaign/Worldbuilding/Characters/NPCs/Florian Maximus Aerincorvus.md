As evident by my silver-white hair and green eyes, I am a cousin of the Aerincorvus patriarch. I am deeply connected to the family goals: my older sister Aelia joined the Concordance as an expert Aetherweaver, while I joined the Venturer Aetherweaving branch, hoping to bring back Concordance related artefacts. I have studied how the Fae have a unique connection to the Umbra -- some accept it while some are unnafected -- something that fascinates me. My internal debate is whether to bring back these items for study in the Aetherweaver Academy or in the Concordance's Vaults, all while the Caretakers strongly oppose both options. I am now eager to join my first expedition and get a sense of the wastes for myself, but I am slightly anxious about being treated differently by other Venturers and Veterans due to my noble heritage.
