# Revenant Shamble

*They were people once. The clothes still fit. The weapons are still held correctly. Nothing else remains. They move with the patient, coordinated economy of soldiers who have forgotten why they were trained but not how.*

**Category:** Undead / Hollowed
**Related:** [[Revenant]], [[Umbra]], [[Apostasy]]

---

## Motives & Tactics

Revenant Shambles do not act alone if they can avoid it. The Umbra's hive-mind drives them to flank, to separate, to position so that a PC engaging one finds another at their back. Against a spread-out party they are genuinely dangerous; against a disciplined group that stays close they fall quickly. They actively exploit anyone who moves away from the group to deal with the Dirge or escape the Warden.

Used in a pair, they apply constant positional pressure and make every PC feel like they cannot ignore anything.

## Tier 1 — Standard

**Type:** Undead / Hollowed | **Role:** Standard | **Tier:** 1
**Difficulty:** 11 | **Thresholds:** 6/10 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +1 | **Hollow Strike:** Melee | 1d8+1 physical
**Experiences:** None

### Features

- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Shamble critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Pack Cunning - Passive:** The Shamble gains +2 to attack rolls for each other Revenant within Close range of its target (maximum +4).
- **Flanking Drive - Action:** **Spend a Fear.** This Shamble and one Revenant ally within Close range both reposition into Melee of the same target, then each makes a standard attack against that target. If both attacks succeed, the target becomes _Vulnerable_ until the start of their next turn.

### Soft Move

Both Shambles reposition in concert — move them to opposite sides of a single PC, activating Pack Cunning for both. They haven't attacked yet. They're just standing there. On either side of someone.

---

## Design Notes

- **Pack Cunning** is the Shamble's entire threat: alone, it is a Difficulty 11 enemy with modest damage. With one Revenant ally nearby it reaches +3 to attack; with the Pale Dirge's Resonance Field also active it reaches +5. Players quickly learn to separate Shambles or kill them fast — exactly the movement decision a tutorial encounter should teach.
- **Flanking Drive** is now explicitly two separate standard attacks (this Shamble and one ally each attack), which is clearer than the previous wording. Both must succeed to trigger _Vulnerable_ — at Tier 1 this won't always land, but the repositioning itself is valuable.
- HP corrected to 4 (was 3, minimum for Tier 1 Standard is 4). Stress corrected to 3 (was 2, minimum is 3). Difficulty corrected to 11 (was 10). Damage corrected to 1d8+1 (avg 5.5, within 4–6 range and matches example dice pool; was 1d8+4 at avg 8.5, badly out of range). Pack Cunning cross-reference to the Dirge removed from feature text — design context belongs in Design Notes only.
- **Encounter composition:** Use exactly two Shambles alongside one Warden and one Dirge. A third Shamble tips the encounter from "slightly easy" to "standard." A single Shamble loses the Pack Cunning payoff.
- **Encounter tip:** Start the Shambles at Close range behind or beside the Warden. They should not advance until the Warden engages — then they move to exploit whoever is distracted by it. If a PC breaks off toward the Dirge, both Shambles should converge on that PC immediately using Flanking Drive.

## Recommended Encounter — The Revenant Patrol

*Act II, Encounter 1 | 4 PCs at Level 5 | Difficulty: Slightly Easy*

| Adversary | Role | Tier | Count |
|:---|:---|:---:|:---:|
| Revenant Warden | Bruiser | 2 | 1 |
| Pale Dirge | Support | 1 | 1 |
| Revenant Shamble | Standard | 1 | 2 |

**Opening position:** Warden at Close range, advancing. Shambles flanking behind it. Dirge at Far range, already keening.

**Encounter lesson:** Identify the Dirge as priority. Kill it and the math shifts immediately — Pack Cunning drops, Shadow Mend disappears, and the Warden and Shambles become straightforward. Ignore it and the encounter escalates steadily.

**Narrative beat:** One of the Shambles is wearing a tattered Explorers' Guild patch on its coat. Someone came this way before and did not return.
