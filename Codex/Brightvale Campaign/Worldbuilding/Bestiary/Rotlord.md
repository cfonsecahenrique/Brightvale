# Rotlord

**Category:** Great Horror / Aberration
**Habitat:** Darkest reaches of the Wastes, deeply corrupted forests

A **Rotlord** is a walking nightmare—a massive nexus of corrupted forest will, dark magic, and amalgamated flesh. To call it an animal or a plant is inaccurate; it is a mobile ecosystem of rot.

## Appearance
Rotlords appear as towering, lumbering cathedrals of rotting flesh, brittle black glass-wood, and fungal might. They are formed from the bodies of beasts, revenants, and the flora of the Wastes, all fused together by the [[Umbra]]’s Malignant Echo in the deepest Shadow-Pools. What makes them truly terrifying isn't just their size, but the fact that they are constantly increasing their mass, absorbing any organic matter they stumble across.

## Behavior and Threat
A Rotlord does not hunt in the traditional sense; it simply *assimilates*. It acts as a mobile amplifier for the Umbra, broadcasting the shadow's song into the surrounding environment and causing spontaneous mutation and Flesh-Discord in any living thing that gets too close. 

Fighting a Rotlord is an undertaking usually reserved for entire companies of [[Slayers]] supported by heavy artillery or [[Aetherweaver Academy|Aetherweaver]] strikes. Proximity to the beast means dealing with suffocating spores, grasping tentacle-roots, and the sheer crushing weight of a monster that feels no pain.
