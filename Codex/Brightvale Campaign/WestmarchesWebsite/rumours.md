# Rumours & Whispers
## A Compendium of What People Are Saying

*Collected from the common rooms of Emberfort's Guild-Hall, the courier posts of the Wayhouse, the less reputable establishments of Medsen's lower tiers, and one very talkative merchant outside the Halfling Hearthlands who refused to say how he knew any of it.*

*Accuracy is not guaranteed. Interestingness is.*

---

## Within Brightvale

**"The King hasn't been seen in seven years. Seven. Nobody's that busy with paperwork."**
King Alfwyn retreated into the Central Temple-Castle shortly after his coronation and stopped all public appearances. The High Steward speaks for the crown. The theories range from illness to something he witnessed inside the Beacon's core that broke him entirely. Nobody has disproven any of them because nobody has been allowed close enough to try.

---

**"House Aurelian's new wheat glows in the dark."**
A wheat variety grown in the Aurelian experimental greenhouses photosynthesizes off the Beacon's light — and at night, it glows faintly blue. The Caretakers have dispatched observers to the greenhouses. They have not yet explained to House Aurelian what they're looking for.

---

**"There's a sealed courier package sitting in the Wayhouse with no pickup. Six months, enormous bounty, anonymous sender. The recipient hasn't been seen since winter."**
House Ravenna's clerks are professionally obligated not to discuss their clients. The fact that they haven't opened the package themselves suggests either impeccable ethics or genuine fear of what's inside.

---

**"Corvin Drevorn is hiring. Very quietly. Someone stole a ledger from the Ironweight Vault and he needs it back before whoever has it figures out what's written in it."**
The ledger allegedly contains the names and specific blackmail material for approximately half the Vale's nobility. Drevorn is willing to pay a considerable sum to avoid finding out who they'll sell it to, and to whom.

---

**"The Thalum engineers have flagged a structural failure in Emberfort's eastern wall. Four separate reports in two years. The Council keeps deferring the vote on repairs."**
The Thalum engineers' estimates on when the wall might fail without intervention vary depending on which engineer you ask. None of the estimates are comfortable.

---

**"There's something moving through Medsen's lower tiers at night. Looks human. Drains people."**
Caretaker healers have noted a pattern: patients presenting with extreme vitality loss, no visible wounds, and no memory of the previous night. The official position is Umbra-adjacent illness. The pattern doesn't match anything in the standard Umbra exposure literature.

---

**"An Iyengar Paladin is being quietly tried by the Caretakers' tribunal for channeling divine power 'outside orthodox sources.' The Paladin says she's always drawn on personal conviction, not the Beacon. The Caretakers say that's precisely the problem."**
The trial hasn't been made public. It has become public anyway, through the fastest information network in the Vale: people talking about it in the market at three different stalls simultaneously.

---

**"House Aerincorvus had an investigator go missing. She was documenting a glade where 'the air tasted wrong.' That's all the last report said."**
The Aerincorvus Concordance has catalogued eleven sites in the Vale's western edge with similar descriptions. The missing investigator had developed a theory connecting them. The theory is presumably in her notes. The notes are with her, wherever she is.

---

**"Someone has been attending open lectures at the Academy who doesn't appear on any registry. Asks extremely precise questions about Umbra containment. Registers magically as 'not present.'"**
The Academy's administrative staff have confirmed this three separate times. The Tower's Arch-Aetherweavers are aware. They have not publicly acted on this information, which is itself information worth noting.

---

**"The Hvergi Dwarves stopped submitting quarterly mineral yield reports six weeks ago. House Thalum filed a formal inquiry. The Dwarves haven't responded."**
This is unusual — Hvergi bureaucracy is meticulous to the point of tedium. House Thalum suspects they struck something in the deep seams they don't want to share. Hvergi suspects that if they do share it, Thalum will use the information to justify a territorial claim. Both suspicions are probably correct.

---

**"The yearly tournaments are coming. Three houses have already submitted challengers for the Blade-Ring. There's an unofficial betting pool on whether the Slayer's Pit champion will enter."**
The tournaments are equal parts genuine competition and barely veiled political theater. The betting is enthusiastic, the odds are calculated by people who know more than they should, and First Slayer Makjar has neither confirmed nor denied the rumor of her participation.

---

**"House Von Zoltraak built a portable Umbra suppression device. It works. The failure mode is that it rebounds the Umbra at double density into the containment zone."**
The device has not been submitted for Council review. House Von Zoltraak maintains it needs more testing. People who have heard about the failure mode agree with this assessment.

---

**"A cult in the lower city is trying to bring back a vampire lord's lover. The vampire lord in question is reportedly still alive — or something like alive — and is funding the operation."**
The Caretakers have categorically denied the existence of vampires in Brightvale. They have denied it with some specificity, which is considered suspicious by people who have noticed these things.

---

## Outside Brightvale — The Wastes

**"There's a city out there. Six, maybe seven days south. Empty — no Umbra-touched inside its walls. Nothing inside its walls at all."**
First reported by Venturers in 395 AA. Multiple subsequent expeditions have confirmed the city exists. None have found an explanation for why the Umbra-touched avoid it entirely. The Explorers' Guild has classified this as a Priority Site. An investigation is being assembled.

---

**"Someone came back from the Wastes talking about a lost Armisian legion. Still at their posts. Silver armor, weapons that glow. Waiting for orders that will never come."**
The Venturers who filed this report were coherent, unHollowed, and consistent in their testimony. The Explorers' Guild categorized it as "unconfirmed but serious." The Slayers filed it under "things that will eventually require our attention."

---

**"The Southern Quake opened up something beneath the plateau. Explorers are calling it the Chasm — a massive underground complex, multiple entrances, no map of the interior. Whatever's down there has been down there a long time."**
The Academy has dispatched scholars to the entrance points. The Explorers' Guild has dispatched Venturers. The Caretakers have asked both parties to report anything that looks like a shrine. Nobody has asked the Caretakers why.

---

**"Kaewdyn scouts are reporting their Umbra-sensitivity triggering at levels that don't match any threat they can identify. Their commander says the signal feels 'structured.' Not chaotic. Structured."**
House Kaewdyn's military hierarchy is taking this seriously. The Slayers, when asked, said that a structured signal would imply coordination at a level that should not be possible. They did not elaborate. They looked like people who had elaborated on this topic privately and found it unpleasant.

---

**"The Frost-Veil Pass leads somewhere. Explorers found a route through the northern peaks twelve years ago. There are Elven ruins beyond the ice deserts. Nobody has come back with a complete picture of what's there."**
The Guild maintains this as a Technical Route — available to elite parties, not standard expeditions. What built the ruins, when, and why they're in the frozen north are unanswered questions. Elf scholars at Aeldenbarrow have declined to speculate publicly.

---

**"Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna has been quietly suppressing certain route maps. Removing them from the master cartography after they come in. The Venturers who filed the originals have noticed."**
House Ravenna filed an intelligence report on this. The Grand Explorer hasn't moved against the source of the leak. The suppressed routes all lead toward the same general region of the southern Wastes. Nobody who has asked the Grand Explorer directly about this has received a direct answer.

---

**"There are dragon eggs in the Wastes. Wild ones — not the clutch in the Academy vaults. Found in the deep southern ruins. Nobody knows if they're viable."**
Multiple independent Venturing parties have reported fragments consistent with dragon egg casings. The Academy is very interested. The Slayers are interested in a different way. Nobody's definition of "viable" is the same.

---

**"Some of the ruins in the deep Wastes don't match Armisian architecture. Or any architecture anyone can identify. They were built before the Empire. The question is by what — and where did they go?"**
The Academy's research archives have been compiling this data for thirty years. The patterns suggest at least two prior civilizations, both of which ended in ways that rhyme uncomfortably with the Apostasy.

---

**"The Umbra-touched aren't just expanding — they're organizing differently. Arch-Revenants coordinating in ways Slayer veterans say they haven't seen before. Covering flanks. Adjusting to counter-tactics."**
Three independent Slayer field reports in the last year. First Slayer Makjar has reviewed all of them personally and has made no public statement. The Slayers who filed the reports are still on active duty. The Guild is watching this carefully enough that the watching is itself becoming visible.

---

**"A Venturing party came back reporting a working settlement in the Wastes. Lights. Smoke. No contact made. The Explorers' Guild is not authorized to confirm or deny whether this has been verified."**
Filed by a single party. Not dismissed. The Grand Explorer did not laugh when the report was presented, which is considered significant by everyone who was in the room.
