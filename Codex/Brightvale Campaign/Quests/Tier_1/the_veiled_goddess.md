# The Veiled Goddess

**Quest ID:** q_veiled_goddess
**Type:** Investigation / social (with combat)
**Difficulty:** Moderate (suggested levels 4–5; 3–4 sessions)
**Destination:** The Venturer's trail, deep past Ember 7
**Posted by:** Explorers' Guild task board (surface framing); the real commission is from the Caretakers of the Beacon, routed through the Council

> GM-facing brief. This quest surfaces the fourth religious position — the veiled goddess — as a lived community rather than a rumor. The Caretakers want it suppressed; the community the player finds is not a threat in any conventional sense. Let the moral weight build slowly. The arc closes when the player decides what to report and to whom; the veiled goddess herself never appears directly and should never be fully explained.

## Premise

The veiled-feather symbol — scratched into trail markers, burned into supply cache lids, occasionally left in the soil near Checkpoint walls — has been appearing with increasing frequency on the Venturer's trail. The official Guild posting frames it as a survey task: record the locations, assess whether it indicates organized activity in the Wastes, and report. The Caretakers' private briefing to the player is blunter: find whoever is doing it, and end it.

## What Is Actually Out There

Past Ember 7, in a limestone hollow the maps mark as unoccupied, a community of thirty-odd Wastes-dwellers has established a functioning settlement around a pre-Beacon shrine. They are not cultists in any aggressive sense: they are people who did not make it to Medsen, who found the shrine still standing, and who have organized their survival around it. The shrine predates the Apostasy. The symbol carved above its entrance is the same veiled feather.

The shrine does something. Not dramatically — it does not ward, does not glow, does not perform miracles by any visible measure. But people who spend time near it sleep better and die less frequently than proximity to the Umbra should allow. The community knows this and does not know how to explain it.

## The Community

- **Silvan** — the settlement's de facto leader, a former Venturer who left the trail after losing their party and found the hollow by luck or fate. Practical, suspicious of Medsen, not interested in becoming a political symbol. Will speak honestly to a Venturer who approaches without hostility.
- **Maren** — the shrine's keeper, an elderly woman who arrived before Silvan and has been tending the shrine for years. She does not claim to understand it. She only says that the Caretakers' Last Rites are not the only way through death.
- **Deylan** — a young man who grew up in the hollow and has never been to Medsen. He is the one who started putting the feather marks on the trail. He was hoping other lost people would find them. He didn't know it would attract official attention.

## Arc Structure

**Task Commission → Into the Wastes → Finding the Community → Learning the Truth → The Report.**

### Session 1 — Commission & Journey

The Guild briefing is routine. The Caretaker who approaches the player afterward is not: she is specific about what she wants — the symbol suppressed, the source identified, and the activity ended before it reaches Medsen's lower wards, where a fourth religious position is already circulating. She uses the word *heresy* once and then puts it away.

The trail beyond Ember 7 is marked with feathers at intervals. They're not threatening. They lead somewhere.

### Session 2 — The Community

Arrival at the hollow: the community is visible, functional, and immediately wary. Silvan meets the player at the edge. What happens next depends on how the player presents themselves.

If the player is honest about the commission, Silvan asks them to look first and report after. If they lie, Deylan will eventually reveal what the feathers were for and the deception will cost them something.

Three days with the community. The shrine is examined; Maren speaks about it in terms that are neither orthodox nor heretical — she simply describes what she has observed. The Umbra is thinner near the shrine. She doesn't know why. The player can investigate the shrine directly: it is pre-Apostasy construction, a style the Aetherweaver Academy has no record of, with an inscription that no living scholar has translated.

### Session 3 — The Complication

The Caretaker who commissioned the player arrives, separately, with a Slayer escort. She was not confident the player would act quickly enough. She intends to destroy the shrine and scatter the community.

This is the arc's turning point. The player has three to four days with this community and a growing sense of what it is. The Caretaker's action is legal; the Guild commission technically supports it; the community has done nothing violent. The player can:

- **Stand aside:** the shrine is destroyed. The community scatters. Deylan disappears into the Wastes. Maren does not survive the cold.
- **Intervene:** put themselves between the Caretakers and the shrine. This has immediate political consequences and may require a fight.
- **Negotiate:** find a resolution that satisfies the letter of the commission without destroying the community — report the symbol as a navigation marker, or argue that the shrine's apparent Umbra-suppression properties make it a Venturer asset worth studying.

### Session 4 — The Report

Back at Medsen or Emberfort. The player files whatever they file. The Caretaker files hers. The two reports may not match.

What the veiled goddess actually is remains unanswered. The shrine stands or does not. The community survives or does not. Deylan's feathers are still on the trail markers, or they aren't. The player carries whatever they decided to carry.

## Combat

Not the primary mode; optional and political. The Slayer escort is a combat encounter if the player intervenes at the shrine. Umbra creatures are present on the trail but secondary. Calibrate to levels 4–5.

## Complications

- The inscription on the shrine is in a script that predates the Armisian Empire. The Aetherweaver Academy would want to see it; the Caretakers would not want them to.
- A second Venturer party was commissioned for the same task independently. They are ahead of the player and their approach has been less careful.
- One of the community members carries the Beacon's mark — a person who should have died of Umbra exposure months ago and did not.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Community protected:** a named contact in the hollow (Silvan); Caretaker hostility; a mystery the player is now personally invested in; the shrine intact.
- **Community scattered:** Caretaker approval; Guild standing; Deylan's location unknown; Maren dead or missing; the shrine destroyed and the player the only one who saw what it was.
- **Negotiated outcome:** partial approval from all parties; the shrine studied rather than destroyed; the community moved rather than scattered — a fragile peace.
- Guild pay; the translation of the inscription if the player pursued it (a handout that feeds *The Dimming Beacon* and the Apostasy mystery).
