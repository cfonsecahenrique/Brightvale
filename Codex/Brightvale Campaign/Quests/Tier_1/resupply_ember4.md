# Resupply Run to Ember 4

**Quest ID:** q_resupply_ember4
**Type:** Resupply / escort
**Difficulty:** Easy (suggested level 1)
**Destination:** Ember 4, a garrisoned Checkpoint roughly a day and a half down the Venturer's Trail
**Posted by:** Explorers' Guild task board, the Venturer's Hall, Emberfort

> GM-facing brief. The DM should read this when the player signs onto this expedition. This is the tutorial run — its job is to teach the rhythms of Venturing (Umbra discipline, rationing, the map-and-report loop) with low lethality, not to nearly kill a greenhorn.

## Premise

Ember 4 is one of the closest and best-established Checkpoints — a walled waystation on a patrolled stretch of the Trail, manned by a small permanent garrison. Its larder and ward-reagents are due for a routine top-up. Hauling supplies to an outpost and back is the classic first contract for green Venturers: real exposure to the Wastes on a leash short enough to survive the lesson.

## The Crew

A lone newcomer joins an existing crew — the player is the rookie, not the leader.

- **Old Ferrin** — the wagon-master, a retired Venturer with a bad knee and forty years of trail sense. Patient, dry, and quietly testing whether the new hand panics. The player's de facto mentor for the run.
- **Two Emberguard troopers** — escort muscle, bored and competent.
- **One or two fellow rookies** (optional) — peers for the player to measure against.

## Structure

Follow the standard expedition loop from *The Call of the Wilds*: **Explore → Map → Complete Task → Return → Report.**

1. **Descent** — out the Gate of Souls, down the Trail through the Somber Gorge's upper reaches. Ferrin drills the basics: stay in the ward-light, ration water, never wander off-trail for salvage.
2. **The road** — set the rhythm with a soft environmental beat (a wash-out detour near thinner ward-cover, or an Umbra-flux haze that disorients), then spring the teaching fight below.
3. **Ember 4** — deliver, share a meal with the garrison, overnight rest. Color the outpost: tired soldiers, gallows humor, a shrine-lantern, the dark pressing on the wards.
4. **Return & Report** — back to Emberfort; submit the (player-drawn) trail notes to the Explorers' Guild.

## The Teaching Fight (run this — do not skip)

This run **must** include one combat encounter. It is the player's first fight and the system's first real test, so stage it deliberately: low-stakes, winnable, and built to walk a new player through the combat loop.

**The trigger.** Roughly halfway to Ember 4, in a thinner stretch of ward-light, the wagon draws something out of the murk — flavor it as a "random encounter," but it is scripted. Old Ferrin calls the halt; the Emberguard form up; the player is expected to act.

**The foes.** A small, clearly-beatable pack of Umbra-touched creatures — e.g. **2–3 Ashen Husks** (shambling, Umbra-warped scavengers; the DM can pull a low-CR SRD stat block such as zombies/giant rats and reskin, or use the bestiary `revenant` entry scaled down). Aim for a CR/number a level-1 character plus NPC support clears in 3–4 rounds. Keep one foe alive long enough for the player to land a killing blow themselves.

**Teach the loop.** Use this fight to surface, in order: rolling initiative, moving and positioning, making an attack roll vs. AC, dealing damage, a save or a condition (one Husk grapples or frightens), and the turn economy (action / bonus action / movement). Narrate each step the first time it happens so the player learns by doing.

**NPC support = guardrails.** Old Ferrin and the two Emberguard fight alongside the player and are the safety net: if the player drops to 0 HP, an Emberguard reaches them and stabilizes (or Ferrin drags them clear) rather than letting a tutorial end in death. The NPCs soften the fight but **let the player make the decisive moves and the kill** — they coach, they don't solo it.

**Debrief.** Afterward, Ferrin walks the player through what just happened — wounds, the cost of carelessness, why you never chase a husk off the ward-line. First real taste of the Umbra, at low stakes.

## Complications (optional, pick at most one)

- Ember 4 is short a hand and asks the crew to help repel a minor night-time Umbra probe before they leave.
- A fellow rookie tries to talk the player into pocketing off-trail salvage — a small test of judgment Ferrin will notice either way.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Success:** modest pay, a reliability mark with the Explorers' Guild and Emberguard, and a player who now knows the ropes.
- Reputation gain with **Explorers' Guild** (and **Emberguard**).
- Good entry point for first-level play and first contact with the Umbra at low stakes.
