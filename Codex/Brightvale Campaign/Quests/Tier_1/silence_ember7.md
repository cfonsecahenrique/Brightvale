# The Silence at Ember 7

**Quest ID:** q_silence_ember7
**Type:** Investigation (with combat)
**Difficulty:** Moderate (suggested level 2–3)
**Destination:** Ember 7, a remote Checkpoint several days down the Trail, past the well-patrolled stretch
**Posted by:** Explorers' Guild task board, the Venturer's Hall, Emberfort

> GM-facing brief. Read when the player signs on. This is a mystery with teeth: the journey builds dread through evidence, and the arrival pays it off with a fight that the clues should have let a careful player half-anticipate. Pick a cause from the menu below and seed it from the road.

## Premise

Ember 7 reports by runner-relay every two weeks without fail. It has now been **silent for a month.** The Guild needs to know whether the Checkpoint still stands, whether its garrison lives, and whether the link can be restored — or whether Ember 7 has to be struck from the network. The crew is to reach it, find the truth, fix what can be fixed, and bring back the rest.

## The Crew

- **Petra** — veteran Explorer, expedition lead and navigator. Speaks rarely; when she changes the marching order, follow it.
- **Kaze Swift-Arrow** — Orc Slayer, the muscle; reads a fight before it starts.
- **A support caster** (pick one): **Seln** (Pragmatist field-healer) or **Brother Eddan** (Caretaker — brings Last Rites, which may matter here).

## Structure

**Explore → Map → Complete Task → Return → Report**, but the "task" is *find out what happened* and the answer shapes everything after.

1. **The road out** — past the last friendly Checkpoint, ward-cover thins and the Umbra grows bold. Lay the dread.
2. **Signs** — escalating evidence the player reads: missed relay-markers; a relay-runner's pack with no runner; ward-stones gone dark; tracks that are wrong; the veiled-feather scratched into a marker by someone afraid.
3. **Arrival** — Ember 7, gates open or breached, no challenge called. The investigation proper: read the scene before something reads you.
4. **The truth** — see the menu. Resolve, recover survivors if any, then **withdraw and report**.

## What Happened at Ember 7 (pick one)

- **Overrun (default):** an Umbra-flux surge spawned a Revenant infestation; the garrison was killed or turned. Survivors, if any, are barricaded and frightened — and one or more may be quietly corrupting (a moral problem for Brother Eddan).
- **Hollowed:** prolonged exposure broke the garrison's minds before any monster did; they abandoned the post, or worse, and the Checkpoint stands eerily intact and empty.
- **Buried:** a rockfall or structural failure severed the relay and trapped the garrison; this becomes rescue under time pressure rather than a straight fight.
- **Tended:** the garrison is alive but has stopped reporting on purpose — they've found (or built) something near the outpost marked with the veiled-feather, and they don't want Medsen to know. (Ties hard to the Cult of the Goddess thread; use sparingly and as a slow burn.)

## Combat

Real and expected: Revenants and Umbra-touched creatures (the DM can pull appropriate SRD undead/aberrations via the rules tools). Calibrate to a level 2–3 crew with NPC support; the player should feel the difference from the Ember 4 milk run.

## Complications

- A survivor begs not to be "given the Rites" — they don't believe they're turning. They might be right.
- The cause is still present and follows the crew onto the road home.
- Petra makes a hard call (abandon, burn the post, leave someone) and watches how the player takes it.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Contact restored / post saved:** strong reputation with **Explorers' Guild**; Ember 7 stays on the map.
- **Post lost, truth recovered:** still a success — the Guild needed to *know*. Reputation, better pay than Ember 4.
- Whatever the cause, the crew likely returns with a thread that feeds Venturer rumor and the wider fracture (the Beacon's dimming emboldening the Umbra; or veiled-feather evidence the Order would rather suppress).
