# The Noble House's Claim

**Quest ID:** q_noble_house_claim
**Type:** Intrigue / retrieval (with combat)
**Difficulty:** Moderate (suggested levels 4–5; 3–4 sessions)
**Destination:** Medsen and the Wastes
**Posted by:** House Lugner, through an intermediary — not publicly posted

> GM-facing brief. A Noble House wants the relic recovered from Zothu's crypt. This is not a simple theft — House Lugner believes the relic is a pre-Apostasy Old Magic artifact that could advance their mission of Old Magic recovery, and they may be right. The player is caught between the House, the Explorers' Guild (which holds the relic), and the question of whether something that predates the Apostasy should be in private hands at all.

## Premise

A relic recovered from the Crypts of King Zothu is currently held in the Explorers' Guild's secured archive pending formal cataloguing. House Lugner — the Pale Sanctum's tiefling-blooded magisters, champions of Old Magic recovery — has identified it as a pre-Apostasy artifact of the type their research has been seeking for years. They want it, they are willing to pay for it, and they are willing to acquire it through other means if a legitimate purchase is refused.

The player is approached by **Magister Vel Lugner**, a mid-rank member of the House, with a commission that is carefully worded to sound like research assistance and is actually a recovery operation.

## The Relic

A small stone vessel, sealed with a wax-and-resin compound that has not degraded in the time since the Apostasy. The Guild's cataloguers have been careful not to open it. House Lugner's analysis of the external markings suggests it contains a preserved memory — an Apostasy-era record of something that happened in Zothu's crypt complex. What memory, and who recorded it, is unknown until the vessel is opened.

The vessel cannot be easily duplicated or faked. House Lugner wants the original.

## The Players

- **Magister Vel Lugner** — earnest, brilliant, and operating at the edge of what the House has authorized. He believes genuinely that Old Magic recovery is necessary for Brightvale's survival. He also has a secondary motive he hasn't disclosed: his House's patron is pressing for results, and a pre-Apostasy memory vessel would be the most significant discovery in a generation.
- **Guild Archivist Pova Senne** — the cataloguer responsible for the relic. She suspects it is significant; she is procedurally scrupulous; she has not yet understood how badly House Lugner wants it. She can be an ally if the player approaches her honestly.
- **House Drevorn's Agent** — Drevorn, the debt-wielding logistics house, has also identified the relic's existence through their information network and has made a quiet bid to acquire it. They don't care about Old Magic; they want the leverage of holding something the Academy and House Lugner both want.

## Arc Structure

**The Commission → Access & Investigation → Competing Claims → Resolution.**

### Session 1 — The Commission

Vel Lugner approaches the player through an intermediary at a Venturer's gathering. His pitch is legitimate enough: he wants the player's help navigating Guild protocols to examine an artifact the House believes is historically significant. The commission fee is substantial.

What he has not said: the Guild has already declined a formal purchase request from House Lugner; House Drevorn has made a competing offer the Guild is considering purely for political reasons; and Vel Lugner's authority to spend what he's spending is not fully sanctioned by the House.

### Session 2 — The Guild Archive

The player gains access to the Guild archive — legitimately (through Senne's cooperation), through social maneuvering, or by other means. Examining the relic confirms Lugner's assessment: the vessel is pre-Apostasy, the markings match no current scholarly catalog, and whatever is inside has been preserved with a care that suggests the original depositor considered it critically important.

The player also discovers:
- Drevorn's agent has been in the archive before them. Something has already been examined or disturbed.
- The relic's markings include a symbol that appears in the inscriptions the player may have seen at the Zothu crypt — a direct link to whatever Zothu was.
- Senne has figured out what Lugner wants and is not happy about it. She will help the player if they tell her the truth.

### Session 3 — Competing Claims

Three factions converge:

- **House Lugner** wants the vessel and will escalate if denied.
- **House Drevorn** wants to use the vessel as leverage; they don't need to open it.
- **The Guild** (via Senne) wants it studied properly and not removed from Guild custody without Council review.

The player navigates the politics. Options include:
- **Legitimate transfer:** build a case for Council review and push for a formal loan or purchase that satisfies Guild protocols. Slow but clean.
- **Private handover to Lugner:** bypass the Guild process. Senne's cooperation ends here; Drevorn may expose the transaction.
- **Expose Drevorn's interference:** giving Senne evidence of Drevorn's unsanctioned access shifts political leverage toward the Guild and away from both Houses. Drevorn will remember.
- **Open the vessel:** doing so in the archive, without authorization, reveals the memory inside — making it simultaneously less valuable as a sealed artifact and more valuable as information. What the memory shows is a fragment of the Apostasy-era crypt complex, from the perspective of someone who was there. It is disturbing and inconclusive, and now the player carries it.

### Session 4 — Resolution

The vessel's fate is determined. So is the player's relationship with House Lugner, House Drevorn, and Guild Archivist Senne.

If the vessel was opened: the memory-fragment it contained feeds directly into *Beyond the Sealed Door* — the player now knows there is more to find in Zothu's complex and has seen a piece of what it is.

## Combat

Not primary: a confrontation with Drevorn's agent if they move to physically secure the relic, or a hired intervention if Vel Lugner's patience runs out. Calibrate to levels 4–5.

## Complications

- Vel Lugner's House patron is **Lord Casivur Lugner**, who is less scrupulous than his subordinate; if Vel fails, Casivur sends someone the player will not enjoy meeting.
- The relic is not the only thing from the Zothu crypt that has attracted attention. A second item from the same expedition has already been quietly purchased by an undisclosed buyer.
- Senne has been cataloguing the relic long enough to have her own opinion about what it is — and her opinion differs from House Lugner's in a way that matters.

## Outcomes & Rewards

- **Relic to Lugner:** significant pay; House Lugner alliance; Guild coolness; the memory-fragment's contents unknown unless the player was present for the opening.
- **Relic to Guild custody:** Senne's trust; Guild standing; Lugner's frustration; the vessel properly studied in time.
- **Vessel opened by player:** the memory-fragment in hand; political fallout from the unauthorized opening; direct fuel for *Beyond the Sealed Door*.
- **Drevorn exposed:** a political chip against House Drevorn; Senne's gratitude; Drevorn's enmity.
