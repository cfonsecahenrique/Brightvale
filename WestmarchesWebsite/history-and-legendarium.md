# A History of the Enduring
## Legendarium & Timeline of Brightvale

---

## Part One — The Legendarium

*Key terms every resident of Brightvale knows. Newcomers who get these wrong are usually from somewhere they shouldn't be.*

**The Apostasy** — The catastrophe. Roughly four hundred years ago, the gods departed without explanation. The crimson veil sealed the sky. The Umbra was unleashed. The Armisian Empire collapsed in months. The Apostasy is year zero — all dates are measured from it.

**After Apostasy (AA)** — The calendar. The current year is **400 AA**.

**The Armisian Empire** — The great human civilization that dominated the world before the Apostasy. Its ruins, relics, and secrets fill the Wastes. The Noble Houses are its surviving aristocracy.

**The Enduring** — The collective name for all peoples who survived the Apostasy and built Brightvale. A term of identity and defiance, not a specific ancestry.

**Brightvale** — The protected plateau in the Teardrop Mountains. The last confirmed safe habitation in the known world.

**The Beacon** — The pillar of holy fire at the heart of Medsen, ignited by the Dawnfather's sacrifice. It keeps the plateau clear of Umbra. It is slowly dimming.

**The Dawnfather** — The martyr who sacrificed his soul to create the Beacon at the Apostasy's darkest hour. The Sun-Born monarchy claims descent from him.

**The Umbra** — The sentient, parasitic darkness that fills the world beyond the plateau. *(See: The Umbra)*

**The Wastes / The Wilds** — Everything outside Brightvale's walls. Umbra-choked, geographically unstable, and full of things that once were not dangerous.

**Revenant** — A person fully Hollowed by the Umbra: dead inside, reanimated as an Umbra-driven shell. They retain muscle memory and instinct but not self.

**Arch-Revenant** — A Revenant who retained extraordinary capability — magic, tactics, leadership — through the Hollowing. The most dangerous class of Umbra threat.

**Hollowing** — The full subversion of a soul by the Umbra. Total erasure of self.

**Flesh-Discord / The Warp** — The physical mutation caused by prolonged Umbra exposure. Ranges from skin-shedding to complete bodily transformation.

**Ashgeld** — The standard currency. Coins pressed from Beacon ash; warm to the touch, faintly luminous in total darkness. Stable, backed by the most reliable institution in the Vale.

**Lumen** — Distilled Beacon light, infused into wood or stored in Censers. Used to push back the Umbra in the field. Produced by the Aetherweaver Academy.

**Venturers** — Those who descend into the Wastes on expedition. Not soldiers, not scholars, not saints — usually a mixture of all three.

**The Venturer's Trail** — The primary mountain pass descending from the plateau into the Wastes, guarded at its head by Emberfort.

**Ashlines** — Beacon ash mixed into paint, applied to fortifications to generate passive Umbra wards.

**Sun-Born** — The royal bloodline, claiming divine descent from the Dawnfather. The current king, Alfwyn, is Sun-Born — or claims to be.

**The Brightblood War** — The civil war (293–297 AA) that nearly destroyed Brightvale from the inside. A wound the Vale has not finished healing.

**Memory-Bargain** — A last-resort survival technique: sacrificing personal memories to the Umbra's resonance to avoid Hollowing. The memories do not return.

**The Malignant Echo** — The low, constant vibration emitted by the Umbra and Umbra-touched creatures. Extended exposure without protection degrades the mind.

**Embermark** — The silvery-gray ritual mark burned into Emberguard members by the Beacon's Cold-Flame. Provides minor resistance to the Malignant Echo.

## Part Two — A History of the Enduring

*The official timeline as maintained by the scribes of Medsen. What is not recorded here is considered lost.*

---

### Year 0 — The Apostasy
The gods departed. The crimson veil sealed the sky. The Umbra consumed civilization. The Armisian Empire, which had stood for centuries, collapsed within months. Dragons fell. Cities burned. The few who survived began to walk north.

### ~Year 5 — The Sacrifice and the Beacon
A man history calls the **Dawnfather** gave his soul to ignite the Beacon on the Teardrop plateau. Survivors established the first settlement at **Medsen** in the Beacon's light. It was a refugee camp. It became a city.

### Year 25 — Hvergi Founded
Dwarven refugees consolidated in the eastern peaks, founding **Hvergi** and beginning the deep-seam mines that still power Brightvale's industry.

### Year ~87 — The Seared Oracles *(disputed)*
A sect of Beacon followers was condemned by the early Caretakers for uncovering something the authorities would not name. The sect was sealed alive in a vault beneath the Dawnfather Temple. Official records on the matter were destroyed. Caretaker orthodoxy teaches that the sect fell to heresy. The specifics remain deliberately struck from the archive.

### Year 164 — The First Archive
Arch-Mage **Elowen the Wise** — First Steward of the early Medsen government — ordered formal recording of all surviving history after scholars noticed the memory-eroding effects of Umbra exposure on the population.

### Years 168–284 — The Age of Cynred the Bitter
King Cynred ruled for 116 years through deliberate isolation and institutional neglect. He closed borders to survivors, refused audiences, and allowed Brightvale to calcify. When he died, his grandson King Aldhelm inherited a structurally intact realm with a politically poisoned court.

### Year 252 — The Slayers' Guild Founded
Lord Eadmer, the "Paragon of Steel," organized elite monster hunters following the first major breach of Emberfort's South Palisades.

### Year 270 — The Scholastic Schism
First Steward Elowen resigned following a bitter dispute over "Deep-Weave" scholarship — the recovery and study of pre-Apostasy human magic. The dispute split the scholarly community and directly led to the Academy's refounding.

### Year 271 — The Aetherweaver Academy Founded
The Scholars' Guild rebranded as the **Aetherweaver Academy**, relocating to the cliffs of Aurore's Peak and committing to teaching Aetherweaving to all capable initiates.

### Years 293–297 — The Brightblood War
Civil war between the Crown's Coalition and a Reform Coalition of Noble Houses. Four years of urban combat in Medsen, ending when the Beacon flickered, Emberfort fell to a Hollowed general named **Lord-Warden Theron**, and both sides were forced to unite against the Horde at **Three Bridges**. King Aldhelm died there, his Sun-Born radiance burning through the Horde and through himself. The war is considered Brightvale's deepest wound. The sides, technically, are still remembered.

### Years 297–308 — The Golden Reprieve
**Queen Wendreda** pardoned lesser rebels and sacrificed the most dangerous Reform leaders to the Beacon. A decade of recovery produced **Ashlines**, **Lumen Censers**, and the Warding Expansion that doubled Medsen's safe living zone.

### Year 329 — The Explorers' Guild Founded
King Oswin "The Venturer" formalized expeditions into the Wastes. The Guild was born.

### Years 354–356 — The Great Famine
Three years of crop failure under King Raedfryd's mismanagement. Ended when Raedfryd sacrificed himself to the Beacon's Great Pyre. The people did not grieve publicly.

### Year 357 — The Council of Seven Formed
In the wake of the royal self-sacrifice, the Council was established to permanently check the monarchy's authority. The crown became ceremonial.

### Year 385 — Mira Ravenna Becomes Grand Explorer
The current leader of the Explorers' Guild assumes command following the death of Grand Explorer Lorenzo.

### Year 393 — The Silent Sovereignty
King Alfwyn is crowned and almost immediately retreats into the Central Temple-Castle, ceasing all public appearances. He has not been seen in seven years. The High Steward speaks in his name.

### Year 399 — The Southern Quake
A massive earthquake reshapes the southern Wastes. New routes are opened. Old ones are buried. What the quake disturbed in the deep lowlands is not yet known.

### Year 400 — Discovery of the Chasm
*Now.* Landslides from the quake's aftermath have revealed multiple entrances to **The Chasm** — an ancient underground complex beneath the plateau itself. No map of the interior yet exists. Origin unknown. Explorers' Guild expeditions are ongoing.
