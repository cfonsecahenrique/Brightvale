# The Five Guilds of Brightvale

*The Guilds are the spine of the Vale. The Houses own the estate. The Guilds own its survival.*

---

## The Caretakers of the Beacon
### *Order of the Eternal Hearth*

The oldest institution in Brightvale — predating the Council, predating the Monarchy in any formal sense, predating nearly everything. The **Order of the Eternal Hearth**, commonly called the Caretakers, maintains the Beacon. Without them, the holy fire dims. Without the holy fire, everything else is academic.

Their responsibilities extend far beyond the literal flame. The Caretakers perform the **Last Rites** — the rituals that prevent the dead from rising as Revenants, which is considerably more urgent in Brightvale than in most places. They conduct **Awakening Rituals** for the Emberborne. They distill the Beacon's light into **Lumen-Casks**, the portable light-vessels that keep Venturing parties alive in the Wastes. They run Brightvale's most extensive charity network, providing healing and sustenance throughout the city. They are ubiquitous, they are essential, and they ask, in exchange, only that you not question what they do behind closed doors. These are considered spiritual matters.

The Caretakers represent the **Orthodox** theological position: the Beacon is divine, sacred, and not to be studied or tampered with. This position supports their institutional authority. It is also, they would say, simply correct.

**Current Leader:** High Caretaker **Kaelen**, an Infernis of severe bearing and genuine radiance. Kaelen and the Order's senior priests are often partially blind from years of staring into the Beacon's heart. They believe the city survives through divine sacrifice — which, in a sense, is technically true.

**Seat:** The Dawnfather Temple, Medsen.

## The Emberguard
### *Shield of the Vale*

The Emberguard is Brightvale's internal military force: its police, its border patrol, its civil order, and its first response when something comes down from the mountains that shouldn't be there. They are headquartered at **Emberfort** — the great bastion at the head of the Venturer's Trail — and maintain **"Glow-Stations"** (smaller outposts) along the valley's major routes and within Medsen itself.

Every Emberguard member undergoes the **Ashen Oath**: a ritual involving the Beacon's Cold-Flame that leaves a silvery-gray **Embermark** on their forearms and grants minor resistance to the Umbra's Malignant Echo. Their tactical doctrine is built around tight formations and overlapping defensive auras — the **Chain of Flame** — designed to create a temporary pocket of Beacon-adjacent protection in the field.

Their roles within the Vale:
- **Border Defense** — First line against mountain-dwelling horrors and Umbra-tainted beasts approaching the plateau.
- **Civil Policing** — Law enforcement within settlements; enforcement of rations and the "Sanctity of Light."
- **Mobilization** — When a threat is detected, they determine whether it requires their rank-and-file, a Slayers contract, or Venturer support.

They are not the Vale's most celebrated institution. But when something goes wrong at three in the morning, they are the ones who show up.

**Current Leader:** Lord Commander **Varis Lugner**, an Infernis of House Lugner, recognizable by his gray-blue skin and sweeping dark horns. A veteran of the Crimson-Gloom incursions, credited with modernizing Emberfort's ballista defenses. His strict enforcement of the Common Ration has earned him friction with newer houses. Never seen without his ancestral plate armor, which is said to hum faintly in harmony with the Beacon.

**Seat:** Emberfort.

## The Slayers
### *Elite of the Wastes*

The Slayers are the specialists called when the Emberguard determines that what has come down the mountain requires something beyond soldiers. They hunt the great abominations: Rotlords, Arch-Revenants, Warp-Nests, and things the Wastes produce that don't yet have names. They are, without serious dispute, the most dangerous people in Brightvale.

### The Ingress of Ash
Slayer training begins with the **Ingress of Ash**: controlled exposure to concentrated Umbra-resonance and alchemical mutagens derived from Beacon light. Those who survive emerge with superhuman reflexes, enhanced senses, and a minor immunity to the Umbra's corrupting influence. They are no longer entirely human. They are finely tuned instruments of destruction who also possess an empirical knowledge of the Umbra — its anatomy, its patterns, its weak points — that surpasses anything taught in the Academy's lecture halls.

### The Verse of the Blade
The dominant philosophy among current Slayers is the **Verse of the Blade** — an Orcish tradition that treats combat as high art. Its premise: a warrior who fights without soul is no better than the beasts in the Wastes. Hollowing does not only come from outside. A mind that becomes pure violence eventually loses what makes it worth saving. Expression — in movement, in craftsmanship, in how you choose to face what's trying to kill you — is what keeps the edge sharp on the inside.

This philosophy has spread well beyond Orcish Slayers. It is now the cultural dominant of the entire Guild.

### Admission
Joining the Slayers is not a matter of signing a ledger or passing a sparring test. A candidate must **Venture into the Wastes at least once** and return with a **new piece of knowledge about the Umbra-touched** — something the Guild does not already know. Hearsay is not sufficient; the knowledge must be specific, firsthand, and actionable. The candidate then presents their findings to assembled Slayers at **The Pit** in Emberfort, who vote on whether the candidate demonstrates the quality of mind the Guild demands. Surviving the Wastes matters. Knowing what you saw there matters more.

**Current Leader:** First Slayer **Makjar One-Eyed**, an Orc who lost her eye during a calculated sacrifice in a three-day pursuit of a Rotblood. Foremost master of the Verse of the Blade. Considers keeping warriors' souls sharp to be as important as keeping their blades so.

*"The Scholars talk of the Umbra as a darkness to be feared. We know it as a beast to be bled. You can't understand the Void until you've tasted its ichor on your blade."*

**Seat:** Emberfort (The Pit).

## The Explorers' Guild
### *Trailblazers of the End-Times*

The Explorers' Guild is where the Venturers officially live. They set the task boards, maintain the checkpoint network in the Wastes, record the maps, and push the Council to be more aggressive about reclaiming what was lost. They are obsessed with the past — the ruins, the relics, and the buried knowledge of the Armisian Empire — and with the possibility that the world beyond the valley might, someday, be survivable again.

### What They Do
- **Waste Mapping** — The geography of the Wastes shifts constantly under Umbra-flux and lingering spell-scars. Keeping accurate maps is a survival matter for every expedition that follows.
- **Checkpoint Establishment** — They build and maintain magically-shielded outposts in the Wastes where parties can rest, resupply, and find emergency supplies. Expanding the checkpoint network is a standing priority.
- **Artifact Recovery** — Pre-Apostasy relics are the Guild's primary funding source and the Academy's primary research material. Bringing them back is core business.
- **Mountain Surveillance** — They monitor and map the Teardrop Mountain passes, ensuring glaciers remain impassable to Umbra-touched and searching for routes into uncharted regions.

### Technical Routes
The Guild maintains tight records on **Technical Routes** through the mountains' sheer faces — paths too demanding for the mindless Umbra-touched to navigate. These "Brave-Paths" allow elite Venturers to enter the Wastes from unexpected angles. They are the Guild's most closely guarded operational secrets.

### Culture
The Guild has the highest mortality rate of any institution in Brightvale. This produces a certain kind of personality: rowdy, charismatic, fatalistic, and deeply loyal to the people who chose to walk into the same dark places. The Guild-Hall at Emberfort is the warmest building in the coldest profession.

**Current Leader:** Grand Explorer **Mira Ravenna**, a veteran whose name is synonymous with gambling on survival and winning. She has participated in Memory-Bargains to survive Umbra exposures that should have Hollowed her, losing significant personal memories — including the face of her own child — in exchange for returning to lead her people. She carries this with a sharp, dark humor that fools nobody who looks closely.

*"The Wastes don't just take your life. They take your Tuesday afternoons and your first kiss, too. At least I've forgotten why I'm supposed to be sad."*

**Seat:** Emberfort (the Guild-Hall).

## The Aetherweaver Academy
### *The Light Was Made — What Was Made Can Be Understood*

The Academy sits carved into the cliffs of **Aurore's Peak**: a sprawling complex of cobalt stone and silver-veined marble, tiered balconies overlooking the central valley, ozone-scented laboratories where the Vale's mages are trained, and a Tower at the summit housing the Arch-Aetherweavers and a grand multi-lensed observatory for reading the winter Auroras.

The Academy teaches Aetherweaving — structured, formulaic arcane magic — to all capable initiates regardless of ancestry. It also houses the Vale's primary arcane research operation: ward reinforcement, Lumen production, and the enduring effort to understand what the Beacon actually is and how it actually works.

### Two Patrons, One Battlefield
The Academy is the site of the Vale's most significant ongoing ideological conflict, funded from opposite directions by two noble houses.

**House Lugner** backs the **Old Magic** faction — scholars working to recover the pre-Apostasy human tradition of instinct-based, unstructured magic. Their premise: the Armisian Deep-Weaves were not what caused the Apostasy. Their loss was. Recovering them is the path forward.

**House Von Zoltraak** backs the **Progressive Aetherweaving** faction — scholars who argue that new arcane forms, developed in direct response to current conditions, are categorically more useful than reviving a tradition that failed to prevent the Apostasy in the first place.

Individual scholars spend considerable energy trying to secure grants from both factions without antagonizing either. A growing number have simply relocated to the **Crucible** — Von Zoltraak's independent laboratory complex at Aurore's mid-tier — to avoid the politics entirely. The Arch-Aetherweavers of the Tower maintain public neutrality, which satisfies no one.

**Seat:** Aurore's Peak (main campus); the Crucible (Aurore mid-tier, independent labs).
