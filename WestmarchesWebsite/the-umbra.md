# The Umbra
## What Every Venturer Needs to Know

*The following is adapted from "A Treatise on the Breath of the Void" by Scholar Ehre of House Thalum — required reading for all Explorers' Guild initiates before first descent.*

---

## What It Is

The **Umbra** is not mere darkness. It is an **ancient, sentient miasma** — a hive-minded shroud of indigo-black that moves through the lowest places of the world like a drowning tide. It was not always present. Before the Apostasy, the natural cycle of life, death, and divine maintenance held it in check. When the gods departed, those mechanisms failed. The Umbra expanded to fill the void they left.

It manifests as a clinging liquid darkness that settles into lowlands, ravines, and the spaces beneath things. Only altitude and the Beacon's light push it back. Outside the plateau, it is everywhere. In the Wastes, the air itself is its territory.

---

## What It Does to the Living

Prolonged exposure to the Umbra induces **Flesh-Discord** — a slow, agonizing transformation of the natural form. Scholars have identified three stages:

**The Blight** — The earliest signs: a sickness of the marrow, skin beginning to shed, a ghastly translucency spreading beneath the surface. At this stage, withdrawal to clean air and Beacon light can halt progression.

**The Warp** — The budding of extra sensory organs, jagged bone-spurs breaking through skin, a sickly violet ichor replacing blood in the veins. Reversal at this stage is possible but difficult, requiring significant Caretaker intervention.

**Unnatural Melding** — In the deepest concentrations of heavy Umbra, the resonance can fuse distinct living things into a single, agonized mass of flesh and shadow. No recorded reversal exists.

---

## The Umbra Hungers for Magic

The Umbra is not indiscriminate — it **gravitates toward magical beings and magical expression**. The greater the arcane potency, the faster it comes. During the collapse following the Apostasy, the most powerful wizards were consumed first. Dragons, beings of inherent magical nature, suffered the same fate.

As a practical matter: the more actively you channel magic in the Wastes, the more attention you draw. This is not a reason not to use magic. It is a reason to be deliberate about when and how.

---

## The Malignant Echo

The Umbra emits a constant low vibration — a **Malignant Echo** — that pierces stone and skin alike. It is felt more than heard: a rot-heavy chime at the edge of perception that unravels the spirit over time. Extended exposure without protection degrades focus, memory, and eventually coherent thought.

This is not metaphor. The Echo is measurable. Experienced Slayers can identify the type and approximate size of an Umbra-touched creature simply by the specific frequency of the Echo it emits.

---

## The Hollowing

The full subversion of a mind to the Umbra is called **Hollowing**. It is total.

1. **Memory-Sifting** — The Echo shatters the mind's history. The victim begins to lose personal memories: faces, names, the reasons they started doing things.
2. **Extinction** — The personality dissolves. The vital spark dies. The body becomes a necrotic vessel.
3. **The Revenant** — The shell reanimates with unnatural strength and cold, instinctive cunning. It remembers nothing of who it was, but it retains every combat reflex, every skill, every ingrained pattern of movement. A Hollowed soldier still fights like a soldier. A Hollowed mage still casts.

**Arch-Revenants** are the category that keeps experienced Slayers awake at night: former Venturers or powerful individuals whose Hollowing preserved their full capabilities — magic, tactics, combat skill — while erasing everything human about them. They act as generals, organizing lesser Umbra-touched into coordinated forces with a discipline that should be impossible for beings with no consciousness. They are not thinking. They are *performing* — executing the patterns of a lifetime's training on an empty stage. Reports suggest organized Arch-Revenant commands are converging on the plateau.

---

## Memory-Bargains

In extremis, Venturers have survived deep Umbra exposure through **Memory-Bargains** — sacrificing personal memories to the Umbra's resonance in order to avoid full Hollowing. The sacrifice buys time and distance. It does not cure the exposure. The memories do not return.

Some Venturers have returned from the Wastes missing years of their lives — unable to recognize family members, carrying grief they cannot explain because they no longer remember why they are sad. Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna is the Vale's most prominent practitioner. She has forgotten the face of her own child, and she carries this with a sharp, dark humor that fools nobody.

---

## Surviving It

**The Beacon's light** is the primary defense. The plateau is clean. The closer to Medsen's centre, the stronger the ward.

**Ashlines** — paint mixed with Beacon ash, applied to walls and structures — generate a passive ward. Emberfort's rebuilt walls are treated. Checkpoint outpost perimeters are maintained with Ashlines.

**Lumen Censers** — portable vessels of distilled Beacon light — provide limited but life-saving protection in the field. Standard equipment for Venturing parties.

**The Embermark** — a ritual burn left on Emberguard members by the Beacon's Cold-Flame — provides minor resistance to the Malignant Echo. Slayers develop a more robust version through the Ingress of Ash.

**Altitude helps.** The Umbra settles low. Staying above treeline reduces exposure, though it does not eliminate it.

## The Creatures It Creates

Beyond the Hollowed, the Umbra shapes the natural world into something worse.

**The Wastes' flora and fauna** mutate under the Umbra's hive influence. Trees become brittle black glass or weeping resonators that broadcast the Echo. Animals lose their original forms, merging into nightmarish multi-limbed predators.

**Rotlords** — massive, mobile ecosystems of rotting flesh and fungal growth, constantly expanding, amplifying the Malignant Echo to warp all life in their vicinity. The Slayers hunt them. It is not easy work.

**Shatter-Wing Angels** — blasphemous, corrupted remnants of forgotten divine beings. They serve as heralds of the void, their original sacred purpose inverted into something that cannot be named without discomfort.

**Umbra-Touched Fiends** — corrupted extraplanar entities trapped in the Wastes since the Apostasy. They retain their infernal instincts and considerable power.

**Dragons** — believed extinct as a species. What remains are massive Umbra-touched mutations in the deepest Wastes. The Aetherweaver Academy reportedly holds the last safe eggs in stasis. What a dragon *becomes* when fully Hollowed is one of the Vale's most feared theoretical scenarios, and one that several scholars believe is no longer theoretical.
