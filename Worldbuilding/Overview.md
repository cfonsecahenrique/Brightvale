# Brightvale Worldbuilding Overview

This document provides a summary of the current worldbuilding progress for the **Brightvale** campaign setting. The setting is structured as a dark fantasy, post-apocalyptic world recovering from a cataclysmic event, focused around a single safe valley.

## Core Lore & History
*   **The [[Apostasy]]**: The central cataclysmic event. A crimson veil choked the sky, leading to winters and darkness. It spawned the [[Umbra]] and Revenants, corrupting the land. The world moves in cyclical Ages, each ending in cataclysm — the Apostasy is the most recent collapse.
*   **The [[Umbra]]**: A sentient, parasitic miasma that gravitates toward magical beings and expression. It induces "Flesh-Discord" and mutates flora and fauna. Umbra-touched creatures retain their combat instincts and magical prowess despite losing all sense of self.
*   **The Dawnfather & The Beacon**: A legendary hero who sacrificed himself to create the Beacon, a pillar of holy fire protecting the valley from the [[Umbra]]. The Beacon's true nature and the economy built on its ash (**Ashgeld**) hold darker secrets.
*   **[[Religions]]**: Three primary theological positions vie for authority — the Orthodox, the Stoic, and the Pragmatist — each shaping how the Beacon is understood and who controls it.
*   **[[The Brightblood War]]**: A devastating civil war (293–297 AA) between Crown loyalists and a reform coalition of Noble Houses. Ended when a Horde invasion forced both sides to unite at the Battle of Three Bridges, at the cost of King Aldhelm's life. The aftermath — the Golden Reprieve — produced the Lumen Censers and Ashline wards.

## Locations
The setting is centered on **Brightvale**, a protected plateau.
*   **[[Medsen]]**: The capital city built around the Beacon.
*   **[[Emberfort]]**: A bastion guarding the main pass (Venturer's Trail) into the valley.
*   **Other Settlements**: [[Aurore]], [[Hvergi]].
*   **[[Aeldenbarrow]]**: A druidic/elven settlement connected to megaliths, hidden by glamours.

## Factions & Organizations
The society is a tug-of-war between Noble Houses and Guilds.
*   **The Council of Seven**: Governing body of Brightvale.
*   **The Monarchy**: Symbolic rule of the **Sun-Born** kings, currently led in seclusion by **[[King Alfwyn]]** via his **High Steward**. (Detailed in [[Kings of Brightvale]])
*   **Noble Houses**: Remnants of the Armisian Empire. They operate on a feudal system.
    *   **Old Houses**: Aerincorvus, Aurelian, Iyengar, Kaewdyn, Lugner, Von Zoltraak.
    *   **Recent Houses**: Ashwood, Drevorn, Ravenna, Thalum.
    *   **Extinct Houses**: Rjioha, Volex.
*   **Guilds**: Hold practical power and resources.
    *   [[Caretakers of the Beacon]]
    *   **[[Emberguard]]**: Based at [[Emberfort]]; the policing and internal safety force of the Vale, led by Lord Commander [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner]].
    *   **[[Slayers]]**: Elite Remnant Hunters; maintain the South Palisades and train the city guard. Combat is considered a high art. Led by First Slayer [[Makjar One-Eyed, First Slayer]] ([[Orcenfolk|Orc]]).
    *   **[[Explorers' Guild]]**: Trailblazers and artifact-hunters. Map the Wastes and the Teardrop Mountain passes. Led by Grand Explorer [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]].
    *   [[Aetherweaver Academy]]

## Ancestries (Races)
*   **[[Humankind]]**: Numerous, hierarchical, obsessed with Noble Houses.
*   **[[Elves (Faefolk)]]**: Secretive, living in hidden glades (like [[Aeldenbarrow]]), seeking connection to the lost Faewilds.
*   **Other Ancestries**: [[Dwarvenkind]], [[Halflings]], [[Orcenfolk]], [[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]]/[[Infernis_Tieflings|Tieflings]].

## Bestiary
*   **[[Revenant]]**: Standard hollowed threats, and highly dangerous Arch-Revenants that retain intellect and powers.
*   **[[Rotlord]]**: Massive mobile ecosystems of rotting flesh and fungal might.
*   **[[Shatter-Wing Angel]]**: Blasphemous, corrupted remnants of old forgotten pantheons.
*   **[[Umbra-Touched Fiends]]**: Corrupted devils and demons trapped in the Wastes since the Apostasy.
*   **[[Dragons]]**: Considered extinct, reduced to [[Umbra]]-touched mutations in the Wastes. The [[Aetherweaver Academy]] reportedly holds the last safe eggs.
*   **[[Fae]]**: Their connection was severed completely after the [[Apostasy]], leading [[Elves (Faefolk)|elves]] and Aerincorvus investigators to seek traces in ancient stones to reopen it.

## Themes & Mysteries
*   **Major Themes**: The true nature of the [[Apostasy]], the Dawnfather, the [[Umbra]], reconquering the world, the diminished Faewilds, the discovery of the Undercity, and [[The Beacon|the Beacon's secrets]].
*   **Minor Themes**:
    *   *Modern Vampires of the Vale*: A cult trying to resurrect a vampire lord's lover.
    *   *Dragon Eggs*: Searching for remnants of [[dragons]].
    *   *Tournaments*: Yearly martials and magical events.

## Legendarium (Key Terms)
*   **[[Apostasy]]**: The cataclysm.
*   **[[Umbra]]**: Sentient shifting shadow/miasma.
*   **Revenants**: Undead husks animated by the [[Umbra]].
*   **Dawnfather**: Martyr whose soul powers the Beacon.
*   **Brightvale**: The protected valley.
*   **[[Medsen]]**: The capital city.
*   **Flesh-Discord / Warp**: Mutation caused by the [[Umbra]].
*   **Hollowing**: The process of having one's soul erased by the [[Umbra]] to become a Revenant.
*   **Armisian Empire**: The ancient, fallen [[Humankind|human]] empire.
