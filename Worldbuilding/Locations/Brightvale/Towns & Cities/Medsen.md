# [[Medsen]]

**[[Medsen]]** (also known as **Emberdawn**) is the vertical capital city of Brightvale, built into the base of the**Frost-Veil Peaks** at the northern end of the plateau. It is a city of white stone, soaring arches, and deep shadows, anchored by the divine light of the **Beacon**.

![[Pasted image 20260420134238.png]]
### The Central Temple-Castle
Dominating the city's highest tier is the **Central Temple-Castle**, a labyrinthine fortress of white stone built directly over the heart of the Beacon. It serves as both the spiritual center of the valley and the claustrophobic seat of the **Monarchy**. It is here that **[[King Alfwyn]]** has remained in seclusion for seven years, speaking only through his High Steward. The Temple itself is tended by the **[[Caretakers of the Beacon]]**, who manage the focusing lense and the distribution of warmth to the city below.

### Tiers of the City
The city is organized into distinct tiers, representing the social hierarchy of the Vale:
*   **The High Tier (The Aether-Reach):** Home to the Temple-Castle, the palazzos of the Ancient Noble Houses, and the primary Council chambers.
*   **The Mid Tier (The Crucible):** The heart of [[Medsen]]'s commerce and industry. Here, guilds maintain their workshops, and the "Recent" Houses hold their influence.
*   **The Low Tier (The Shadows):** The most crowded and industrial section of the city, where the common citizenry dwells in the flickering edges of the Beacon's reaches.

![[Pasted image 20260420134303.png]]
### Governance
[[Medsen]] is the seat of the **Council of Seven**, where the ultimate authority of the Vale is weighed. Despite its symbolic importance to [[the Monarchy]], the city’s daily administration is a complex web of Guild influence and Noble house maneuvering.
