# Zorael Lugner

**Status:** Living, barred from active venturing (as of 400 AA)
**Role:** Aetherweaver, former Venturer; survivor of the [[Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission|Aldred Commission]]
**Affiliation:** [[House Lugner]] (cousin to [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner]]); [[Aetherweaver Academy]]

---

## Overview

A [[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]] sorceress of [[House Lugner]], Zorael is one of the Academy's celebrated **Instinctuals** — Old Magic wielders who shape spells by visualisation rather than formal weave-craft. Her speciality was wide-area destructive magic; her field name among Venturers was *the Horde-Razer*, after a series of expeditions in which her spells cleared paths through Umbra hordes that would otherwise have buried her companies.

She is impatient. She is often read as cold. Her relationship with her cousin [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner|Varis]], the House heir, is rocky — she considers his abandonment of arcane study for the Emberguard a kind of intellectual cowardice; he considers her vanity a kind of recklessness. They have not shared a table in seven years.

## The Aldred Commission

Zorael was seated on the 387 AA expedition at the [[Aetherweaver Academy]]'s insistence, over the [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Order]]'s preferred roster. She survived the engagement at the unnamed temple because [[Theodor Stjormund]] and [[Cillian the Old]] held the line while she and [[Yuriko Ruinweaver]] fled. The flight back to [[Brightvale]] was a gauntlet she does not, in any record, describe.

On returning to the gates of [[Emberfort]], she and Yuriko were detained under the Order's quarantine protocol. They were held for months. They were released only after the Caretakers were satisfied that what they had seen had not been spoken aloud to anyone who would believe them.

## Since 387 AA

Officially, Zorael returned with her mind heavily corrupted by [[Umbra]] exposure and has been restored to "somewhat functional" status through holy treatment. The Order has barred her from future expeditions. She receives a small Caretaker stipend.

Unofficially, her mind is intact. She has not spoken of the temple — not directly — to anyone outside a very small circle. She has, however, **introduced certain motifs into her aetherwork**: a feather rendered in spell-residue, a veiled face suggested by the arc of an ignition. The Academy's progressive scholars have noticed. The Caretakers, who do not read aetherwork well, have not.

She is one of the cult's most consequential silent patrons. She has never used the word *cult* aloud.
