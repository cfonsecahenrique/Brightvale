# Theodor Stjormund

**Status:** Deceased (387 AA)
**Role:** Dwarven Guardian, Venturer of the [[Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission|Aldred Commission]]
**Affiliation:** [[Hvergi]]; second son of **Storvald Stjormund** of the Stjormund clan

---

## Overview

The second son of Storvald Stjormund, Theodor was a renowned [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarven]] warrior with extensive field experience in the Umbra and the Wastes. By the time of his selection for the 387 AA expedition, he had become one of the most charismatic younger figures in Hvergi — a brawler in the field, a charmer at the long tables, and an increasingly suspected unifier of the three dwarven clans.

He was selected for the **[[Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission|Aldred Commission]]** for two reasons. The first was his genuine fitness for the work. The second, less spoken, was that his father's clan considered him **expendable** as a second son, and Hvergi's senior council considered him **dangerous** as a potential unifier — sending him on a prestigious expedition was both an honour and a quiet way to lower the risk that he would, given another decade, draw enough banners to do something the clans were not prepared to allow.

On his departure, the other two dwarven clans presented him with a **Hjalrim Crystal Gauntlet**, forged of Sigr crystal, as a gesture of goodwill. The gift was widely read in Hvergi as either a sincere blessing or a clean political bribe. Both readings were probably correct.

## Death

Theodor was killed at the temple in the southwestern Wastes during the descent of the Umbra-touched Shatter-Wing Angel and the breach of the surrounding [[Umbra]]-hordes. He chose to remain at the threshold with [[Cillian the Old]] to buy [[Zorael Lugner]] and [[Yuriko Ruinweaver]] time to flee. The Crystal Gauntlet was not recovered.

In Hvergi, his death was received with a public grief that the senior clan-council privately considered convenient. Storvald Stjormund has not commented in public on his son's death since the official report was issued. He has commented, twice, in private — once to a Senior Caretaker, and once to a [[Concordance]] envoy. The content of neither conversation is recorded.
