# Aldred Gwyn

**Status:** Deceased (387 AA)
**Role:** Senior Caretaker; nominal liaison to the [[Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission|Aldred Commission]]
**Affiliation:** [[Caretakers of the Beacon]]

---

## Overview

A mid-ranking Caretaker bureaucrat in his fifties at the time of his death, Aldred Gwyn served the Order for the better part of three decades — most of it spent at desks rather than at lanterns. He attended **seven Oracle cycles** as a scribe and second, and dutifully transcribed the words "metaphor" and "degradation" into the margins of his journals every time the [[The Blinded Ones|Sealed Oracles]] said something the senior Vision Readers preferred not to hear.

In the months before the 386 AA cycle, Aldred began to dream of a face he had never seen and a chamber he had never entered. The dreams did not stop. By the time the Council convened to discuss the 386 vision, Aldred had volunteered himself for the field expedition with an enthusiasm his superiors interpreted as careerism. They were wrong about that.

## On the Expedition

To the four Venturers selected by the Council, Aldred presented himself as a flustered bureaucrat: mispronouncing names, dropping notes in puddles, asking unnecessary questions about supply counts. The performance was deliberate. He needed the party to underestimate him, and he needed the Order's observers — who would read his reports — to record nothing alarming.

At the temple, he ended the performance and **lit the Ashgeld fire** that opened the channel to the Oracles, against every standing instruction of his Order. He died in the same minute, struck down by the Umbra-touched Shatter-Wing Angel that descended through the broken ceiling. He met its gaze without flinching.

## After His Death

The Order has canonised Aldred in its public account of the expedition (see *[[Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission]]*) as the lone martyr of a venture compromised by external political meddling. His name is read at every Lighting of the Lesser Lanterns in the parishes of [[Medsen]] and [[Emberfort]].

The truth — that he disobeyed his Order, that he received private visions for four months, that he was the reason the Oracles spoke clearly at all — is held by only the highest Caretakers and by the two Venturers who survived him.

He was not a hero. He was a man who, for once, did not look away.
