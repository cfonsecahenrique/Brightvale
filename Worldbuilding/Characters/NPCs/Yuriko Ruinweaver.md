# Yuriko Ruinweaver

**Status:** Living, barred from active venturing (as of 400 AA)
**Role:** Orcen Seraph, former Venturer; survivor of the [[Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission|Aldred Commission]]
**Affiliation:** Formerly [[Caretakers of the Beacon]] (expelled); independent Venturer

---

## Overview

An [[Orcenfolk|Orcen]] Seraph raised within the [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Beacon's]] religious order rather than in the nomadic tradition of her people, Yuriko was trained as a **Paladin of the Beacon's Flame** from early childhood. She showed promise — divine wings manifested unusually early; her sworn ideals burned bright enough to draw senior attention.

She was **expelled** from the Order in her late twenties. The official record cites "doctrinal incompatibility." The real cause was her persistent, formal questioning of two specific subjects: the **Oracle Cycle** and the so-called **Sealed Chamber** in which she suspected (correctly, as it turned out) that something the Order would not name was being kept.

After her expulsion, Yuriko discovered that her divine wings and powers had not left her. She built a successful freelance Venturing career and quietly nicknamed herself *Ruinweaver* — for the Sealed Chamber she had once, in her own words, *woven the ruin of*.

## The Aldred Commission

The political machinations that placed Yuriko on the 387 AA expedition remain unclear. The [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Order]] would not have nominated her; the Council seated her over their objections. Her combat capability was the official justification. Whether some faction at the table wanted her gone, and used the expedition to that end, is among the persistent rumours of the Aldred Commission.

She survived the temple alongside [[Zorael Lugner]] because [[Theodor Stjormund]] and [[Cillian the Old]] bought their flight at the cost of their lives. On returning to the walls of [[Brightvale]], she was detained under quarantine and held alongside Zorael for months.

## Since 387 AA

The Order's public account holds that Yuriko's mind was broken by Umbra exposure and that the Caretakers' treatment restored her to partial function. The Order has barred her from active venturing.

In private, Yuriko has been the **more openly seditious** of the two survivors. Her divine powers have not faded, which she takes as confirmation that whatever empowered her was never the Beacon to begin with. She speaks carefully and rarely, but she speaks. In the lower wards of [[Medsen]] she is sometimes recognised; in the [[Explorers' Guild]] she is welcome at certain tables.

She wears no cult mark openly. Her left forearm carries an old scar she will not explain. The shape of it is a feather.
