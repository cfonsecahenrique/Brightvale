# [[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]]/[[Infernis_Tieflings|Tieflings]]

### Origins of the Shard-Kin

The **[[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]]**, commonly known as **[[Infernis_Tieflings|Tieflings]]**, are a race that did not exist before the **[[Apostasy]]**. Their emergence was the direct result of desperate bargains struck during the cataclysm's earliest, darkest years. To survive the encroaching [[Umbra]] or to maintain their status, various noble lineages and desperate individuals formed pacts with extra-planar entities. The unforeseen consequence for these "Pact-Holders" was a permanent alteration of their bloodlines, passing down devilish or demonic traits to all their descendants.

### The Two Hues of the Cursebairn

The physical manifestation of an [[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]] reveals the nature of the original pact:
- **Red-Skinned & Horned:** Descended from those who struck bargains with **Devils**. These lineages are often associated with themes of order, contracts, and rigid hierarchy.
- **Gray/Blue-Skinned & Horned:** Descended from those who struck bargains with **Demons** (the phenotype found in **[[House Lugner]]**). These lineages are often associated with raw power, primal resonance, and the chaotic survivalism of the [[Apostasy]].

Despite their "cursed" appearance, the [[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]] are often viewed with a complex mix of awe and reverence, being seen as a living connection to the divine at a time when traditional gods, demons, and devils have long since vanished from the world.

### Naming Traditions and Faith

Tieflings are as much a race as people with red hair, they are simply a forced mutation on random people, with genetic traits passed down through generations.
Thus, there are no "true" tiefling communities and naming conventions.
Their names usually follow Human naming conventions.
At most, those who devote their life to The Beacon may take on Latinate names to signify their devotion to the Light, such as:
*Dante, Maximus, Beatrice, Gabriel, Vergil, Lucia, Augustus, Lucian, Cassius, Sylvia, Orpheus.*


[[Infernis_Tieflings|Tiefling]] communities are rare, with most [[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]] evenly distributed among [[Humankind|Human]] settlements.

## Warlock Magic — The Specialization Tradition

The extraplanar entities the original Pact-Holders bargained with — Devils and Demons, now long vanished from the world — practiced magic through **obsessive specialization**. They chose one domain and studied it until understanding became instinct, and instinct became something harder to name: a certainty so total that reality simply cooperated.

This tradition, called **Warlock magic**, lives on in the Infernis bloodline. It does not function like Elven formulaic magic, which builds universal frameworks. A Warlock picks a single subject — transmutation, binding, force, shadow — and devotes everything to it. They begin with theory. They go further. Eventually, the distinction between knowing something and *being able to do* it collapses entirely.

The most disorienting expression of Warlock magic is in questions of physical law. A Warlock who has spent decades mastering movement — who has interrogated, at every level, how bodies interact with the world — will eventually stop asking *how* to move through air, and begin asking instead why they *couldn't*. You walk without consciously directing each muscle. You do not reason through gravity with each step. Flight, to a Warlock who has understood movement deeply enough, is not a feat. It is simply an absence of the assumption that it isn't possible.

Infernis practitioners do not universally follow this path. But those who do often find it comes more naturally to them than to practitioners of other ancestries — whether through inherited resonance with their ancestors' tradition or something deeper in the bloodline is a question the [[Aetherweaver Academy]] has not resolved.
