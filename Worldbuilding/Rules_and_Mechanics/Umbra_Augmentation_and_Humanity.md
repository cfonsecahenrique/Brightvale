# The Umbral Bargain: Augmentation vs. Sacred Humanity

In the world of Brightvale, the Umbra is not just a monster outside the walls; it is a creeping corruption that some Venturers choose to invite into their own flesh to survive the horrors of the Wastes. This choice represents a fundamental trade-off between the spiritual purity of **Sacred Humanity** and the terrifying power of **Umbra-touched Augmentation**.

## I. The Taint: Adopting the Umbra

A character can choose to **Embrace the Umbra** when they are in a moment of extreme desperation. This typically occurs during a **Failure with Fear** on a critical roll, or as an alternative to a **Death Move** when they mark their last Hit Point.

### The Cost: Sacrificing the Self
To anchor the Umbra, a piece of the character's past must be given up. You must permanently sacrifice one of the following:
*   **A Connection:** Permanently erase a bond with another PC or NPC. This memory is gone, replaced by static and cold shadow.
*   **An Experience:** Permanently cross out one of your +2 or +1 Experiences. You can no longer spend Hope to add this bonus, as the skill and the memory of learning it have been consumed.

### The Gain: Umbra Ranks
Each time you make a Bargain, you gain one **Umbra Rank**. For each rank, you choose one **Umbra Augmentation** from the list below.

---

## II. Disadvantages: The Weight of Shadow

As the Umbra takes root, it chokes out the character's capacity for Hope.
*   **Hope Atrophy:** Your **Maximum Hope** is reduced by **1** for every Umbra Rank you possess.
*   **Sustaining the Shadow:** When you roll a **Success with Fear**, the GM gains **2 Fear** instead of 1. The Umbra within you feeds the darkness around you.
*   **The Hollowing:** If your Maximum Hope is ever reduced to **0**, the character is fully consumed. They become an NPC Revenant, lost to the Wastes.

---

## III. Sacred Humanity: Staying Pristine

Characters who possess **0 Umbra Ranks** are considered "Pristine." They represent the purest light of the Beacon.
*   **The Beacon's Encouragement:** When a Pristine character spends Hope to **Help an Ally**, they roll a **d8 Advantage Die** instead of a d6.
*   **Spiritual Aegis:** Pristine characters gain a +1 bonus to all **Reaction Rolls** made to resist Umbral mental influence or corruption effects.

---

## IV. Umbra Augmentations

When you gain an Umbra Rank, choose one of the following:

| Augmentation         | Mechanical Effect                                                                                                                                         |
| :------------------- | :-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Obsidian Sinews**  | Your muscles harden into dark stone. Permanent **+1 to Strength** and **+1 to Armor Score**.                                                              |
| **Whisper-Step**     | Your shadow moves before you do. You can mark a **Stress** to instantly teleport to any location within **Close Range** as part of your movement.         |
| **Revenant’s Vigor** | You no longer feel pain. Gain **+2 Hit Point slots**. However, you cannot be healed by mundane means; only Lumen-infused or magical healing works on you. |
| **Hollow Sight**     | Your eyes turn pitch black. You can see through magical darkness and gain **Advantage** on Instinct rolls to track or perceive creatures in the dark.     |
| **Leeching Blade**   | When you deal **Severe Damage** with a melee attack, you may clear **1 marked Hit Point** as you draw the life-force out of your foe.                     |

---

## V. Recovery and Redemption

Can the Umbra be removed?
*   **The Great Purification:** Removing an Umbra Rank is an arduous task requiring a long-term project at the **Aetherweaver Academy** or a miracle from the **Caretakers**. It typically requires rare components (like a Pure Lumen Shard) and a high-difficulty Knowledge or Instinct roll.
*   **Lost Forever:** Even if the Rank is removed, the **Experience or Connection** sacrificed to gain it is gone forever. Some parts of the soul cannot be retrieved.
