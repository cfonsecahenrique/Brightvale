# Scholastic Schism

A historic academic dispute over the nature of Armisian magic, dividing scholars between those who blamed the [[Apostasy]] on the overreach of Old Magic and those who blamed its abandonment. The schism fractured the early [[Aetherweaver Academy]] and continues to echo in the rivalry between [[House Lugner]] and [[House Von Zoltraak]].
