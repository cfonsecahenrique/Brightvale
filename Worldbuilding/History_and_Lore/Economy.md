# The Economy of Brightvale

Brightvale's economy is a closed, fragile system defined by survival, logistics, and the central distribution of resources. The geographic isolation of the valley dictates strict flows of goods between agricultural centers, industrial hubs, and the demanding capital city.

## Agriculture and the Breadbasket
The massive capital of **[[Medsen]]** is a dense metropolis with an ever-growing appetite. It consumes the vast majority of food produced across Brightvale, acting as the valley's largest economic sink.

To sustain this, the **[[Halfling Hearthlands]]** acts as the definitive breadbasket of the valley, producing the lion's share of crops. Their unparalleled agricultural yield is achieved not just through halfling agrarian techniques, but through a vital, regulated exchange: Medsen supplies the Hearthlands with unpacked **Ashgeld**. The divine ash from the Beacon, before being compacted into protective wards, functions as an incredibly potent and magical fertilizer. 

While the Hearthlands provide the bulk of the valley's food, other smaller towns and villages across the plateau manage to produce smaller yields, often achieving agricultural self-sufficiency for their own populations.

## Mountain Exports: Cattle, Stone, and Steel
Closer to the Frost-Veil Peaks and the eastern ranges, the rugged terrain favors different industries.
- Mountain-adjacent towns such as **[[Hvergi]]** and **Orska** dedicate their outer foothills and plateaus to herding. They export the vast majority of Brightvale's cattle and cattle-based products (including leather, cured meats, and bone tools).
- **[[Hvergi]]**, being the great Dwarven enclave, is the undeniable industrial heart of the Vale. From its deep mines and massive forges, it produces almost all of the valley's required stone, metalwork, and fine artisan crafted goods.
- While [[Medsen]] also produces its own share of high-end artisan and luxury goods, it fundamentally relies on consuming Hvergi’s raw materials to fuel its local trade.

## Timber and Venturer Trade Dynamics
Forestry and lumber are primarily sourced from towns situated near the valley's wooded areas. **[[Veikryss]]**, the central hub, is a major producer of timber, acting as the primary supplier of wood for construction and commercial use across the region.

However, the most dynamic economic engine outside of raw material production is the **Venturer Support Logistics**.
The settlements arranged along the primary southern arteries—**[[Veikryss]]**, **[[Three Bridges]]**, and **[[Emberfort]]**—experience massive, bustling trade uncharacteristic of standard rural towns. Because they serve as the logistical funnel leading toward the Venturer's Trail, their local economies are supercharged by:
- Housing, feeding, and supplying departing [[Venturers]] and the large entourages that support them.
- Facilitating the flow of heavy logistical caravans that continuously transport supplies back and forth to maintain the outer Checkpoints and Venturer outposts established deep in the Wastes. 

The constant flow of guild funding, specialized gear, recovering Venturers, and the influx of highly valuable ancient artifacts retrieved from the Wastes makes these three settlements critical, wealthy nodes of commerce outside the capital.
