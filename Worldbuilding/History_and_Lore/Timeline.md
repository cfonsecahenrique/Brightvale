# The Chronicled Years (After [[Apostasy]])

This [[timeline]] represents the official record of the **Enduring** as maintained by the scribes of [[Medsen]]. Dates are measured in **AA (After [[Apostasy]])**, marking the years since the world was veiled in crimson and the Dawnfather made his sacrifice.

The current year is **400 AA**.
