# The Seared Oracles

*"To burn without consuming, to weep without water, to pray without answer. Such is the price of seeking the shadow behind the Sun."* — Fragment from a suppressed [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretaker]] text

Buried deep within the foundations of the Dawnfather Temple in [[Medsen]], sealed behind heavy, aether-warded doors, rests a secret the early [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretakers]] sought to bury forever. Roughly three centuries ago, around 87 AA, a radical sect of [[The Beacon|Beacon]] followers made an unpardonable discovery. What exactly they found—or sought to do with it—was painstakingly struck from official records. The few whispers that survived the purge suggest they uncovered a heretical truth so profound and agonizing that it became a metaphysical fire, burning them from the inside out. It was their very knowledge that doomed them. Condemned not to a quick death, but to an eternity of torturous reverence, they were marched into a hidden, subterranean vault directly adjacent to the [[The Beacon|Beacon]]'s root, left in the blistering heat just out of reach of its restorative light, and sealed alive.

They are known today only as the Seared Oracles. Forced into endless prayer, the paradox of their punishment brought about a horrifying miracle. The intense, divine ambient heat and radiant energy of the Beacon slowly, inexorably, burned their souls away. Yet, because the vault sits so close to the Beacon's core, it is entirely scrubbed of [[Umbra]]. Safe from the corruption outside, their bodies simply refused to rot. What remains now are not men and women, but lifeless, desiccated husks, perfectly preserved and locked in eternal, unyielding prayer. The divine aura perpetually scorches them and sustains their shells, leaving them suspended in an excruciating cycle of unending communion. 

Through centuries of agonizing sensory deprivation and direct, soul-searing exposure to the divine core, these lifeless husks attained a continuous, hallucinatory out-of-body communion. Their minds drift forever through the metaphysical echoes of the Beacon itself. It is said that in this detached state, they have tapped into the unfiltered memories of the Dawnfather. They remember the truth of the [[Apostasy]], having witnessed first-hand the exact events that shattered the world. They know the agonizing reality behind the Dawnfather's sacrifice, whispering that he may not have been the savior history claims, or that his willing martyrdom was a terrible lie. Most dangerously, it is rumored the Oracles know the true lineage of the Sun-Born kings and the [[The Monarchy|Monarchy]]—a truth so damning it could unravel the entire establishment of Brightvale. 

The thirteen-year cycle of their isolation is not a product of natural aetheric pressures, but of the original binding spell that imprisoned the sect. Exactly thirteen years after the vault was sealed, an official undid the wards and entered the Cloister for the first time, fully expecting to find the rotting corpses of the dead choristers. Instead, he was horrified to discover the impossible, burning limbo state they had entered. The Cloister was immediately sealed shut once more. Due to the arcane structure of that initial sealing, the spell's lifetime was permanently fixed to a strict thirteen-year duration. For generation after generation, their imprisoners would return at the end of each cycle precisely when the spell exhausted itself to check upon their agonizing state. 

Over time, this grim inspection evolved. As the choristers' whispers grew deeper and more profound, officials began selecting specific "Oracles" at the cycle's end to study the strange metaphysical environment of the chamber, seeking to understand why they were still alive and looking to decode their knowledge. However, as the potential threat of this knowledge became apparent, the position of the Oracle became increasingly controlled by the Crown's forces. Today, the endeavor is almost always futile. Stripped of their souls, the husks speak only in disjointed auguries, terrifying riddles, and fragmented poetry. Furthermore, the [[The Council of Seven|Council]] is now pressured into exclusively appointing senior members of the [[Emberguard]] or orthodox [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Caretakers]] as the designated Oracles. Deeply entrenched in dogma, these Crown-loyal officials staunchly refuse to entertain the heretical ramblings of the husks, dismissing their most profound cosmic revelations as the madness of the damned. Thus, the terrible truths the Seared Oracles burned for remain eternally locked away in the dark beneath the Beacon.
