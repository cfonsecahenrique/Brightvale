# [[Apostasy]]
![[Pasted image 20260420135252.png]]
The [[Apostasy]] is a legendary event shrouded in mystery, that ultimately led to the world as it is today. It is said the the Gods abandoned the world, allowing darkness and chaos to seep into the world. 

Ever since the [[Apostasy]] happened, endless gloom has engulfed the skies, leaving nights black as pitch and even the zenith of day a dreary haze. The somber grays of the mist and sky are occasionally broken by heavy rains, snowfall or strange erratic weather events. There are beliefs among the Enduring that the highest mountain peaks can pierce the gloom and offer new homelands free of turmoil.
