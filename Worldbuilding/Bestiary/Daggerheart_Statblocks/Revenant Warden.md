# Revenant Warden

*A massive Hollowed figure in corroded plate, still moving with the unhurried precision of a soldier who has never needed to hurry. Its eyes are sealed shut with dark ichor. It does not run. It does not need to.*

**Category:** Undead / Hollowed
**Related:** [[Revenant]], [[Umbra]], [[Apostasy]]

---

## Motives & Tactics

The Warden advances slowly and deliberately, cutting off escape routes before engaging. It absorbs punishment without changing pace. It does not react to pain — it reacts to tactical openings. When a PC overcommits or separates from the group, the Warden closes. Its Crushing Advance punishes any PC who lets it get close without a plan, and its Dead Man's Grip ensures that the character who finally brings it down does not walk away clean.

The Warden is not the most dangerous enemy in the patrol — the Pale Dirge is. But it is the one that demands attention, and it knows it.

---

## Tier 2 — Bruiser

**Type:** Undead / Hollowed | **Role:** Bruiser | **Tier:** 2
**Difficulty:** 14 | **Thresholds:** 13/24 | **HP:** 7 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** +3 | **Crushing Blow:** Melee | 2d8+6 physical
**Experiences:** Hollowed Soldier +2, Immovable +2

### Features

- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Warden critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Relentless (two) - Passive:** The Warden can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn.
- **Crushing Advance - Action:** The Warden moves into Melee with a target and makes a standard attack. On a success, the target is also _Restrained_ until the start of their next turn — pinned by the Warden's mass. A successful Strength Roll (13) ends the condition early.
- **Dead Man's Grip - Reaction:** When the Warden takes damage that crosses its Severe threshold, it immediately makes a standard attack against the PC who dealt the blow.

### Soft Move

The Warden angles its body to cut off a route — one PC of your choice finds their path to Sprint blocked without passing through its Melee range. It hasn't attacked yet. It's waiting.

## Design Notes

- **Relentless (two)** keeps the Warden consistently active without making it Fear-hungry — it does not generate Fear, it simply occupies GM turns, which is the right pressure type for a sustained bruiser in a tutorial encounter.
- **Crushing Advance** is the core punish: _Restrained_ is free to impose (no Mark a Stress required per adversary design rules), and it traps the target for the Dirge or Shambles to exploit on the following action.
- **Dead Man's Grip** ensures the kill is never free. The Severe threshold trigger is clean and standard — no Fear spend, no Stress mark, just a consequence that fires automatically. At slightly easy difficulty it is flavour as much as threat, but it teaches players to be deliberate about who lands the killing blow.
- Thresholds corrected to 13/24 (Major now within Tier 2 Bruiser range 12–14). Stress corrected to 4 (was 3, minimum is 4). Damage corrected to 2d8+6 (avg 15, within 12–16, matches example dice pool from skill table). Experience bonus corrected to +2 for Tier 2.
- **Encounter tip:** The Warden should always move toward whoever holds the party's primary light source. The Umbra-Touched are drawn to warmth — and it creates tactical pressure around the Ashgeld lantern mechanics introduced in this session.
