# Umbra-Horde

*A churning, shrieking tide of shadow-warped vermin, fragmented humanoid husks, and skittering insectoid shapes pours across the ground like a black flood. There are hundreds — thousands — and they move as one ravenous will.*

**Category:** Umbra Swarm / Aberration
**Related:** [[Umbra]], [[The Apostasy]], [[Revenant]]

---

## Motives & Tactics

The Horde does not think. It hungers. It surges toward the nearest source of warmth and light, washing over everything in its path. Individual creatures in the mass are frail — shadow-rotted rats, eyeless crawlers, half-formed Umbra husks no bigger than a child — but their numbers are beyond counting. The Horde drags targets down through sheer weight of bodies, smothering them in darkness. When thinned by fire or magic, the gaps fill within seconds as more pour from every shadow, crack, and crevice. Fighting an Umbra-Horde is like fighting the dark itself.

## Tier 2 — Horde

**Type:** Umbra-Touched | **Role:** Horde | **Tier:** 2
**Difficulty:** 13 | **Thresholds:** 12/18 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +0 | **Claws and Teeth:** Melee | 2d8+4 physical
**Experiences:** None

### Features

- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Horde critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Horde (1d8+2) - Passive:** When the Horde has marked half or more of its HP, its standard attack deals **1d8+2** physical damage instead.
- **Tide of Bodies - Passive:** When the Horde makes a successful standard attack against a target, the target is pulled into Melee range of the Horde as the mass surges around them. The target cannot Sprint until the start of their next turn.
- **Drown in Shadow - Action:** **Spend a Fear** to make a standard attack against all targets within Very Close range. Targets the Horde succeeds against take **2d6+3** physical damage and become _Restrained_ as countless bodies pile on. A target can break free, ending the condition, with a successful Strength Roll (14).
- **Endless - Reaction:** At the start of each GM turn, if the Horde has marked any HP, clear 1 HP. The shadows always bring more.

### Soft Move

The Horde surges and reforms, filling the gaps left by the fallen. It clears 1 Stress.

## Design Notes

- **Endless** is the signature feature — it mechanically represents the "you can't kill them all" fantasy. Clearing 1 HP per GM turn means the PCs need to deal sustained, concentrated damage faster than the Horde regenerates. AoE and fire/light-based attacks should be narratively rewarded (consider an environment feature that grants bonus damage with light sources).
- **Tide of Bodies** prevents kiting. The Horde drags you in and holds you there. Combined with **Drown in Shadow**, a PC caught in Melee can quickly become _Restrained_ and overwhelmed.
- **Horde (1d8+2)** halves the damage output at 3+ HP marked, representing the swarm thinning — but **Endless** fights against this by constantly healing back, creating a tug-of-war.
- **Encounter suggestion:** Pair with a Leader (an Arch-Revenant commanding the tide) or a Support (an Umbra nexus amplifying the swarm). An environment with burnable terrain or light-based interactables gives the PCs tools to counter the Endless feature — e.g., "Ignite the Oil: **Spend a Fear.** All creatures within Close range of the oil take **2d6** magic damage. The Horde cannot use Endless until the end of the next GM turn."
- The Horde's ATK of +0 means it misses often against armored PCs, but **Drown in Shadow** targets everyone in Very Close — quantity over quality.
