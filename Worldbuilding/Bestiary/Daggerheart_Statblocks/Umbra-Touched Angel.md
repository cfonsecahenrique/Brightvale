# Umbra-Touched Angel (Void Seraph)

*A towering six-winged figure of jagged black glass and weeping violet ichor drifts above the ruins, its fractured halo casting inverted light. What was once a divine messenger of a forgotten pantheon now serves only the cold hunger of the [[Umbra]].*

**Category:** Corrupted Divine / Celestial Horror — Umbra-Touched
**Related:** [[Shatter-Wing Angel]], [[The Apostasy]], [[Umbra]]

---

## Motives & Tactics

The Angel hovers above the battlefield, opening with its Dirge of Undoing to weaken clustered PCs before descending to deliver devastating soul-rending strikes. It uses Fractured Divinity to punish attackers who roll with Fear, and its Inverted Hymn countdown steadily builds toward a catastrophic AoE. It fights with cold, alien precision — retreating skyward when pressured, then diving back in with Relentless actions.

---

## Tier 3 — Solo

**Type:** Umbra-Touched | **Role:** Solo | **Tier:** 3
**Difficulty:** 18 (21 while flying) | **Thresholds:** 21/38 | **HP:** 11 | **Stress:** 6
**ATK:** +6 | **Soul-Rending Touch:** Melee | 3d12+6 magic
**Experiences:** Vengeful +3, Fallen Divinity +3

### Features

- **Relentless (two) - Passive:** The Angel can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn. Spend Fear as usual to spotlight it.
- **Flying - Passive:** While flying, the Angel gains a +3 bonus to its Difficulty.
- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Angel critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Fractured Divinity - Passive:** The Angel has resistance to physical damage. PCs that roll with Fear when attacking the Angel lose a Hope.
- **Dirge of Undoing - Action:** **Spend a Fear** to make an attack against all targets within Close range. Targets the Angel succeeds against take **3d10+4** direct magic damage and must mark a Stress as the hymn unravels their sense of self.
- **Soul Sever - Action:** Make a standard attack. On a success, the target must mark an Armor Slot without receiving its benefits. If they can't mark an Armor Slot, they must mark an additional HP.
- **Inverted Hymn - Reaction:** Countdown (Loop 1d6). When a PC marks 2 or more HP from an attack, activate the Countdown. When it triggers, all PCs within Far range **must make** a Knowledge Reaction Roll (17). PCs who fail take **3d8+6** direct magic damage and become _Vulnerable_. PCs who succeed take half damage.
- **Momentum - Reaction:** When the Angel makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

### Soft Move

The Angel rises higher, its fractured halo pulsing with dark resonance. It gains advantage on its next attack roll.

## Design Notes

- **Momentum + Relentless** is deliberately used here — this is meant to be a "truly difficult" adversary per the guide's own advice. The Angel is Fear-positive, making it escalate rapidly.
- **Fractured Divinity** merges the Ghost (resistance to physical) and Unsettling (Fear-roll punishment) archetypes into a single thematic passive. The Hope loss on Fear rolls (instead of Stress) reflects its divine-corruption nature.
- **Soul-Rending Touch** deals magic damage — its strikes bypass mundane defenses as stated in the bestiary lore ("strikes can bypass physical armor, directly unwinding a target's soul").
- **Dirge of Undoing** is the AoE Fear-spend (direct magic damage + Stress), while **Inverted Hymn** is the looping countdown that forces Knowledge Reaction Rolls — both drawn from the "hymns are inverted prayers causing devastating psychological trauma" lore.
- At Difficulty 21 while flying, this is extremely hard to hit. PCs will need to ground it or use magic. Consider pairing with an environment that provides ways to bring it down (collapsed temple pillars, ancient warding circles).
- **Encounter suggestion:** A solo Angel with a damaging environment (crumbling temple, Umbra miasma pools) and 4–6 Revenant minions drawn to its hymn.
