# Rotblood

*A bloated, lurching mass of rotting flesh and dark fluid, its skin stretched to splitting by the Umbra corruption swelling inside it. It does not roar. It gurgles. When it dies, so does anyone standing too close.*

**Category:** Umbra-Touched / Aberration
**Related:** [[Umbra]], [[Apostasy]], [[Slayers]]

---

## Motives & Tactics

The Rotblood does not strategize. It advances, claws, and vomits. But its danger is environmental as much as direct: it pressures clustered parties at range with Acid Spew and punishes melee fighters catastrophically when it drops. Smart players will kill it from a distance; players who haven't seen one before will learn why the Slayers teach "never crowd a Rotblood" on day one of training.

In groups, Rotbloods function as living area-denial — their death bursts carve natural spacing in a battlefield, which makes them excellent pressure tools alongside faster adversaries that herd PCs into melee range.

## Tier 1 — Standard

**Type:** Umbra-Touched | **Role:** Standard | **Tier:** 1
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 6/10 | **HP:** 5 | **Stress:** 3
**ATK:** +1 | **Fetid Claws:** Melee | 1d8+3 physical
**Experiences:** Rot-Bloated +2

### Features

- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Rotblood critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Acid Spew - Action:** Make a ranged attack against a target within Far range, dealing **1d6+2** magic damage.
- **Death Burst - Reaction:** When the Rotblood is reduced to 0 HP, all creatures within Very Close range must make an Agility Roll (11) or take **1d6+2** direct magic damage as the body ruptures.

### Soft Move

The Rotblood lurches into Close range, oozing. A PC of your choice is splattered — they are _Vulnerable_ until they spend an action to clear the corrosive residue.

## Tier 3 — Standard

**Type:** Umbra-Touched | **Role:** Standard | **Tier:** 3
**Difficulty:** 16 | **Thresholds:** 17/29 | **HP:** 6 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** +3 | **Fetid Claws:** Melee | 3d8+3 physical
**Experiences:** Rot-Bloated +3, Unrelenting +2

### Features

- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Rotblood critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Caustic Trail - Passive:** The Rotblood leaves a slick of corrosive fluid wherever it moves. Any PC who enters a space the Rotblood moved through this round takes **1d6** direct magic damage.
- **Acid Spew - Action:** Make a ranged attack against a target within Far range, dealing **3d6+3** magic damage. **Mark a Stress**: the attack instead targets all creatures within Very Close range of the primary target, dealing **3d6+3** magic damage to each target the Rotblood succeeds against.
- **Death Burst - Reaction:** When the Rotblood is reduced to 0 HP, all creatures within Very Close range must make an Agility Roll (14) or take **3d6+4** direct magic damage and become _Vulnerable_ until the start of the next GM turn.

### Soft Move

The Rotblood's skin visibly stretches — the pressure inside it is building toward a rupture. PCs within Very Close range can see it. Whether they act on that information is another matter.

## Design Notes

- **Death Burst** is the defining feature and the core lesson this adversary teaches: do not crowd a Rotblood. It fires at 0 HP without costing the GM anything at Tiers 1–3 — it is automatic and guaranteed, which means PCs learn quickly to finish these from range or at least spread out before landing the killing blow. At Tier 4, the optional Fear spend to expand the blast radius allows the GM to punish a party that has gotten complacent.
- **Acid Spew** scales from a simple ranged attack at Tier 1 to an AoE Fear-spend option at Tier 2+. At Tier 1, it simply teaches that the Rotblood has reach beyond Melee. At Tier 2 the Mark a Stress upgrade introduces the AoE variant and creates a genuine decision point: use a single-target attack for free, or pay a Stress to punish a clustered party.
- **Caustic Trail** appears at Tier 3 as a passive area-control feature. It does not require GM attention to maintain — it simply makes movement around the Rotblood costly, which adds positional texture without slowing the table.
- **Experiences:** Rot-Bloated covers any roll involving the Rotblood's sheer mass, weight, or the physical horror of grappling with it. Unrelenting covers the creature's total absence of self-preservation instinct — it does not slow, flinch, or retreat.
- **Encounter tip:** Rotbloods pair well with faster adversaries (Skulks, Shambles) that herd PCs into melee range and then retreat, leaving the PCs adjacent to a Rotblood as it is killed. A group of 2–3 Tier 2 Rotbloods alongside a Revenant Warden creates a battlefield where the Warden pressures the front line while any Rotblood death threatens everyone nearby.
