# House Ravenna

**Motto:** *"Every road leads somewhere. We know where."*
**Status:** Recent House (Post-Apostasy)
**Seat:** The Wayhouse ([[Medsen]] courier district)
**Heraldry:** A crow in flight over a forked road on a field of charcoal and slate blue.

**Overview:** 
Elevated from common-born scouts who mapped the first stable routes into the Vale during the [[Apostasy]], the Ravenna control Brightvale's definitive communication infrastructure. They trade heavily in movement and information, running an impeccable courier service that doubles as an intelligence network. No one lies in a Ravenna post because their clerks recognize inconsistencies. They rarely blackmail, letting the sheer awareness of their knowledge enforce honesty.

**Strife & Ambition:** 
Their control over correspondence creates universal paranoia; every faction assumes their messages are somehow overseen. Ravenna's core ambition is to legitimize this shadow role, transmuting their corporate courier service into a fully state-funded and protected [[The Council of Seven|Council]] intelligence body.

**Key Relationships:**
- **[[House Drevorn]]:** An active, bitter Cold War as both factions hoard intel and access, underscored by a vicious personal rivalry between house heirs.
- **[[House Iyengar]]:** Professional friction. Iyengar hosts the negotiations, and Ravenna sells the hidden leverage behind them.
- **[[Explorers' Guild]]:** Deep operational ties; [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] shares their name and operates with them intricately.
- **[[Emberguard]]:** Contracted but cautious service, as Commander Varis actively seeks to undo his dependence on them.

**Story Hooks:**
- A sealed courier package sitting dormant for six months suddenly has a colossal bounty tied to its retrieval.
- Explosive documentation leaks suggesting Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna has been actively suppressing certain route maps under House orders.
- A routine courier goes missing near an old ruin linked to a Von Zoltraak artifact, marking the third disappearance on that specific route.
