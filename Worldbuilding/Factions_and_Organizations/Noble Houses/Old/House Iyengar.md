# House Iyengar

**Motto:** *"In every tongue, one word: endure."*
**Status:** Old Armisian House 
**Seat:** The Amber Court (Medsen)
**Heraldry:** A curved blade and a quill crossed over an open fist on a field of amber and deep teal.

**Overview:** 
Originating from an ancient marriage between an Armisian noble and an Orcish Prince, the half-orc Iyengar house blends institutional governance with Orcish directness and the sacred binding of covenants. This dual heritage proved crucial during the Apostasy, enabling diplomacy far beyond human realms. Today, they are the trusted, unshakable neutral arbitrators and highly capable Paladins powered by their internal ideals of covenant.

**Strife & Ambition:** 
Their Orcish heritage causes social friction within the rigid ranks of Medsen’s nobility, while wild Orcish clans remain wary of their high-society integration. The Iyengar aim to become an official Council-chartered arbitration body while extending legal representation to the marginalized Orcenfolk outside Medsen.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Orcenfolk Communities (including Slayers):** Ancestral respect; Makjar and senior members secretly hold deep mutual esteem.
- **House Von Zoltraak:** Cultural alliance built on shared historical rigor and pragmatism.
- **Caretakers of the Beacon:** Quiet tension. The Iyengar view the Beacon practically rather than divinely, empowering their Paladins via ideals, not light.
- **House Ravenna:** Professional competitors; Iyengar offer the meeting ground, whereas Ravenna provides the hidden intel before the meeting begins.

**Story Hooks:**
- An Iyengar Paladin is accused of channeling divine power outside the Caretakers' orthodoxy—a direct attack on the house's independence.
- A major treaty at the Amber Court hinges on granting temporary legal standing to an unrecognized Orcish clan.
- An unearthed ancient treaty implies Iyengar interactions decades before their official establishment, hiding a major secret.
