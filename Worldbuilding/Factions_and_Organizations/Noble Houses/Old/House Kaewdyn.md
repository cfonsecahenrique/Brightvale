# House Kaewdyn

**Motto:** *"The blood remembers what the mind forgets."*
**Status:** Old Armisian House
**Seat:** The Ashen Hall (Medsen)
**Heraldry:** A silver lion rampant holding a pale dragon's scale on a field of deep charcoal and bone-white.

**Overview:** 
Carrying recessive draconic resonance from an ancient union with the extinct House Rjioha, Kaewdyn’s "Ashen-Heirs" share a striking pale physiology and can physically sense the Umbra’s malignant resonance. Choosing to weaponize this terrifying sensitivity, Kaewdyn is the definitive warrior house. Mandatory, grueling service in the Emberguard precedes any claim to Kaewdyn leadership.

**Strife & Ambition:** 
Their draconic sensitivity to the Umbra takes a heavy psychological toll, pushing some to madness. Politically, they find Medsen court life useless and feel more at home at Emberfort. Their core ambition is to officially merge the House into the Emberguard's highest institutional command.

**Key Relationships:**
- **Emberguard:** Deep integration. Varis Lugner respects their prowess, though he prefers keeping clear of noble politics.
- **Slayers:** A sporting, competitive rivalry between Kaewdyn's blood-born sensitivity and the Slayers' chemical augmentations.
- **House Lugner:** High tension due to Lugner's constant clinical attempts to research Kaewdyn draconic genetics.
- **House Von Zoltraak:** Mutual, indifferent detachment.

**Story Hooks:**
- A rookie Ashen-Heir on their mandatory deployment went MIA near a high-density Umbra Warp-Node, possibly drawn in by their resonance.
- A sudden across-the-board intensification in Kaewdyn sensitivity points to shifting Umbra lines or an awakening of the Academy's dormant dragon eggs.
- A Kaewdyn commander interprets "structured patterns" within the Umbra, hinting an intelligence might be broadcasting through the madness.
