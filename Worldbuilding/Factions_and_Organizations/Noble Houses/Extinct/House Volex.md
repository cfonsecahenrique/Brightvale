# House Volex

**Status:** Extinct — Armisian High Nobility
**Heraldry:** A dragon rampant with wings spread on a field of crimson and black.

**Overview:** 
House Volex was the ultimate military linchpin of the old Armisian Empire, boasting a deep, biological bond with war-dragons. They were the very first house to fall during the Apostasy, not strictly because of the Umbra, but due to a mysterious biological breakdown of their draconic bond. As the dragons mutated into erratic, violent entities—later completing their corruption via the Umbra—the Volex riders fell with them, sparking a catastrophic chain of imperial military failure.

**Legacy:**
Their rapid collapse raises a deeply troubling question: Was the draconic mutation the first symptom of the Apostasy, or one of its causes? Now merely an academic warning, Volex's only physical legacy rests in the Aetherweaver Academy’s secured vaults, where the last confirmed surviving dragon eggs reside in stasis—their viability and potential Umbra-taint utterly unknown.
