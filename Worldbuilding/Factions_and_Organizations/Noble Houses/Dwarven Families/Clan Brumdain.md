# Clan Brumdain

**Clan Saying:** *"The seam runs where the seam runs. Follow it."*
**Standing:** Eldest ruling family of [[Hvergi]]
**Hall:** The Root Hall — the deepest residential tier of [[Hvergi]], adjacent to the active crystal seams
**Rune-Mark:** A pickaxe descending into a spiralling seam, ringed by the directional rune for *northeast*

**Overview:**
The Brumdains are the oldest of [[Hvergi]]'s three ruling families, and the ones who read the rock. Where other miners extract crystal, the Brumdains interpret the mountain — tracing seam-lines and pressure gradients the way a scholar reads a manuscript. Their authority is geological as much as political: they know where the crystals run, and they have known for longer than anyone else has been keeping records.

The family's current patriarch, Elder Storvald Brumdain, is the driving force behind the secret northeastern tunnel quietly generating seismic tremors across the eastern peaks. To Storvald this is not a political act — it is the first practical step of [[Heimsfar]], undertaken after forty years of reading the mountain's grain and cross-referencing geological surveys with the ancestral wind-passages carved into [[Hvergi]]'s rune-stones. He is convinced the lands to the northeast hold territory less saturated with [[Umbra]] than anything yet mapped. He is old, and he is not entertaining the possibility of being wrong.

**Strife & Ambition:**
Day-to-day governance interests the Brumdains less than the long project. Other families manage Hvergi's politics. The Brumdains manage its direction. Their ambition is completing the northeastern tunnel before political pressure from [[Medsen]] makes it impossible — and before the seismic tremors become impossible to explain away. Storvald holds no formal authority over the other clans, which means the tunnel is an act of unilateral will, and the fallout of its discovery will land on all of [[Hvergi]] equally.

**Key Relationships:**
- **[[Caretakers of the Beacon]]:** A generations-old, unwritten arrangement. The High Rituals require resonant crystals from the lowest Brumdain seams — ones that vibrate at the Beacon's particular frequency — and the Brumdains supply them without question. In return, the Caretakers provide theological backing whenever [[Medsen]]'s institutions challenge [[Hvergi]]'s autonomy. Neither side would describe this as a transaction. Neither would survive its collapse.
- **[[House Lugner]]:** A source of persistent pressure. Lugner's scholars believe certain deep Brumdain crystals contain pre-[[Apostasy]] resonance — fossilised traces of Deep-Weave spells they call "memory-stones" — and have been seeking exclusive access for years. Storvald finds them useful and distasteful in equal measure: the Lugner want to own the past, while he is trying to use it as a compass. He grants occasional survey access, carefully rationed, to keep them interested without giving them leverage.
- **Clan Hjalrim:** The family responsible for [[Hvergi]]'s defence was not consulted on the tunnel project. This is a wound that has not been addressed.
- **Clan Sigr:** Sigr's [[Aetherweaver Academy]] contacts have occasionally been useful to the tunnel project. Storvald has learned not to refuse useful things on principle, which has made him more tolerant of Sigr's outside relationships than Clan Hjalrim is.

**Heimsfar:**
Of the three families, the Brumdains carry [[Heimsfar]] most literally. The tunnel is an act of prophecy as much as engineering. Storvald does not quote the rune-carvings in conversation — he distrusts what has survived after so many re-carvings — but he acts as though the direction is clear, even where the words are not. Whether his certainty is faith, geological evidence, or stubbornness dressed as both is a question [[Hvergi]] debates quietly, in his absence.

**Story Hooks:**
- The tunnel breaks into a pre-[[Apostasy]] chamber of unknown origin. The carvings inside are not [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarven]].
- Storvald's surveys predict the tunnel will be complete within eight months. The seismic activity has become impossible to conceal, and he approaches the Caretakers to call in the arrangement — but the current High Caretaker was not party to the original compact and does not consider themselves bound by it.
- [[House Lugner]] identifies the specific seam containing the memory-stones and begins negotiating with [[The Council of Seven]] to compel access, framing it as a matter of Vale-wide arcane security.
