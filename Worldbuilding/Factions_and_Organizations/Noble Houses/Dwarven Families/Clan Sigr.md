# Clan Sigr

**Clan Saying:** *"The crystal is worth what someone will pay for it. Know who is paying."*
**Standing:** Ruling merchant family of [[Hvergi]]
**Hall:** The Exchange Hall — the uppermost and most outward-facing tier of [[Hvergi]], closest to the mountain road toward [[Medsen]]
**Rune-Mark:** An open hand holding a crystal shard, palm-forward

**Overview:**
The Sigr are the youngest of [[Hvergi]]'s three ruling families and the most controversial within the enclave. They built their authority not through mining or forging but through negotiation — they are [[Hvergi]]'s commercial interface with the outside world, and older dwarves find this distasteful in a way they struggle to articulate cleanly. The Sigr call themselves pragmatists: a crystal left in the ground is leverage you have chosen not to use.

Their most pressing crisis is one they have not yet shared with the other two families: [[House Thalum]]'s surveyors have registered the eastern seismic tremors from Clan Brumdain's tunnel, and Thalum is precisely the kind of house that files Council paperwork before raising a concern directly. The Sigr are running out of time to contain this before it becomes a formal [[The Council of Seven|Council]] matter.

**Strife & Ambition:**
The Sigr want [[Hvergi]] to remain commercially indispensable — irreplaceable enough that no institution in [[Medsen]] could afford to absorb or antagonise it. Their immediate ambition is holding the line on two fronts simultaneously: preventing [[House Thalum]]'s administrative war from reaching the Council chamber, and keeping their supply relationships with both factions of the [[Aetherweaver Academy]] balanced long enough to extract maximum leverage from each. Whether these two goals are compatible is a question that keeps the Clan's current patriarch, Dain Sigr, awake at night.

**Key Relationships:**
- **[[Aetherweaver Academy]]:** The Sigr supply both the [[House Lugner|Lugner]]-backed Old Magic faction and the [[House Von Zoltraak|Von Zoltraak]]-backed Progressive faction with standard-grade crystals, and they are careful never to grant either side a monopoly. Both factions have offered better terms for exclusivity. The Sigr keep declining, understanding that the moment they choose a side, they become a dependent rather than a supplier. The pressure from [[House Von Zoltraak]] is the harder to resist — their Crucible labs are producing results, and the premium they offer is backed by genuine advances.
- **[[House Von Zoltraak]]:** The closest relationship the Sigr hold with any human institution, and the one that generates the most suspicion within [[Hvergi]]. Both families are unsentimental about the past and direct in their dealings. The Sigr heir who spent three years working in the Crucible laboratories at [[Aurore]] came back with ideas her grandfather does not entirely recognise as dwarven. Clan Hjalrim watches this openly. Clan Brumdain watches it more quietly.
- **[[House Thalum]]:** An entrenched administrative war of overlapping quarry claims, competing land surveys, and bureaucratic filings that accumulate without resolution. Thalum has more lawyers. Sigr has the actual seams. Currently stalemate — but with the seismic activity adding a new legal vector, the balance is shifting.
- **Clan Brumdain:** A cooperative relationship, carefully managed. Sigr's Academy contacts have been useful to the tunnel project, and Storvald has been tolerant in return. The Sigr know more about how close discovery is than Brumdain does. They have not yet decided when to say so.

**Heimsfar:**
The Sigr believe in [[Heimsfar]] — every [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarf]] does, in some form — but they are the family most aware of how many times the prophecy has been re-carved, how much detail bleeds out with each copy, how much of what is quoted in the great halls is interpolation rather than original text. This does not make them dismissive of the voyage. It makes them careful about it. If it is ever going to happen, it will require reliable maps and clear eyes, not inherited assumptions about a shore nobody has seen in living memory. This is, more or less, how the Sigr approach everything.

**Story Hooks:**
- A [[House Thalum]] filing lands at the [[The Council of Seven|Council]] citing "geological instability in the eastern seams" as grounds for a safety audit — which would require access to [[Hvergi]]'s deep tunnels. The Sigr have days to decide whether to warn the other two families or attempt to handle it themselves first.
- [[House Von Zoltraak]] makes a private offer: full political backing against Thalum at the Council in exchange for a ten-year exclusive on premium crystal supply. The terms are the best the Sigr have ever been offered. The strings are exactly as long as Clan Hjalrim always feared.
- The Sigr heir who studied at the Crucible returns with findings suggesting that the ashgeld treatment on Hjalrim blades could be significantly improved using a Von Zoltraak progressive technique. She wants to propose a collaboration between the two families. Neither Clan is certain how the other will receive it — but she believes it could be the first step toward a genuine Hvergi-Academy alliance on dwarven terms.
