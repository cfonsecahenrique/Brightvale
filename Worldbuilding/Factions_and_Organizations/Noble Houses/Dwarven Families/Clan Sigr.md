# Clan Sigr

**Clan Saying:** *"The crystal is worth what someone will pay for it. Know who is paying."*
**Standing:** Ruling merchant family of [[Hvergi]]
**Hall:** The Exchange Hall — the uppermost and most outward-facing tier of [[Hvergi]], closest to the mountain road toward [[Medsen]]
**Rune-Mark:** An open hand holding a crystal shard, palm-forward

**Overview:**
The Sigr are the youngest of [[Hvergi]]'s three ruling families and the most controversial within the enclave. They built their authority not through mining or forging but through negotiation — they are [[Hvergi]]'s commercial interface with the outside world, and older dwarves find this distasteful in a way they struggle to articulate cleanly. The Sigr call themselves pragmatists: a crystal left in the ground is leverage you have chosen not to use.

Where Clan Stiórmund sees crystal as a geological fact to be followed, and Clan Hjalrim sees it as a forging material to be perfected, Clan Sigr has been asking a different question entirely: what is the crystal actually doing, and can it be made to do more of it? The Sigrs have learned, through years of careful experimentation carried out in the Exchange Hall's back rooms, to store Aether in crystal matrix — not merely channel the latent resonance that already exists, but load new Aether into a prepared lattice and draw on it later. The crystal, in their hands, becomes a battery.

This discovery has reoriented the clan entirely. The Sigr are now in active collaboration with [[House Von Zoltraak]]'s Crucible laboratories, [[House Thalum]]'s engineering corps (who immediately grasped the structural implications of portable Aether storage), and researchers at the [[Aetherweaver Academy]] who have been quietly desperate for exactly this technology. The traditional Sigr role as a trading intermediary has not disappeared — but it is now in service of a research agenda, and Dain Sigr has started thinking of his clan less as merchants and more as the people who will make Aether-gadgetry a practical reality.

Their most pressing crisis is one they have not yet shared with the other two families: [[House Thalum]]'s surveyors have registered the eastern seismic tremors from Clan Stiórmund's tunnel, and Thalum is precisely the kind of house that files Council paperwork before raising a concern directly. The Sigr are running out of time to contain this before it becomes a formal [[The Council of Seven|Council]] matter.

**Strife & Ambition:**
The Sigr want [[Hvergi]] to remain commercially indispensable — irreplaceable enough that no institution in [[Medsen]] could afford to absorb or antagonise it. Their immediate ambition is holding the line on two fronts simultaneously: preventing [[House Thalum]]'s administrative war from reaching the Council chamber, and keeping their supply relationships with both factions of the [[Aetherweaver Academy]] balanced long enough to extract maximum leverage from each. Whether these two goals are compatible is a question that keeps the Clan's current patriarch, Dain Sigr, awake at night.

**Key Relationships:**
- **[[Aetherweaver Academy]]:** The Sigr supply both the [[House Lugner|Lugner]]-backed Old Magic faction and the [[House Von Zoltraak|Von Zoltraak]]-backed Progressive faction with standard-grade crystals, and they are careful never to grant either side a monopoly. Both factions have offered better terms for exclusivity. The Sigr keep declining, understanding that the moment they choose a side, they become a dependent rather than a supplier. The pressure from [[House Von Zoltraak]] is the harder to resist — their Crucible labs are producing results, and the premium they offer is backed by genuine advances.
- **[[House Von Zoltraak]]:** The closest relationship the Sigr hold with any human institution, and the one that generates the most suspicion within [[Hvergi]]. Both families are unsentimental about the past and direct in their dealings. The Sigr heir who spent three years working in the Crucible laboratories at [[Aurore]] came back with ideas her grandfather does not entirely recognise as dwarven. Clan Hjalrim watches this openly. Clan Stiórmund watches it more quietly.
- **[[House Thalum]]:** A relationship that has split in two. Their administrative war over quarry claims and land surveys continues unchanged — Thalum has more lawyers, Sigr has the actual seams, and the seismic activity from Clan Stiórmund's tunnel has only added new legal vectors. Simultaneously, Thalum's engineering corps have become some of the Sigr's most productive research collaborators: the moment they understood what portable Aether storage could do for structural reinforcement and precision excavation, they wanted in. The two institutions are currently conducting a territorial dispute and a joint research program at the same time, and both sides are pretending the other thing is someone else's department.
- **Clan Stiórmund:** A cooperative relationship, carefully managed. Sigr's Academy contacts have been useful to the tunnel project, and Storvald has been tolerant in return. The Sigr know more about how close discovery is than Stiórmund does. They have not yet decided when to say so.

**Heimsfar:**
The Sigr believe in [[Heimsfar]] — every [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarf]] does, in some form — but they are the family most aware of how many times the prophecy has been re-carved, how much detail bleeds out with each copy, how much of what is quoted in the great halls is interpolation rather than original text. This does not make them dismissive of the voyage. It makes them careful about it. If it is ever going to happen, it will require reliable maps and clear eyes, not inherited assumptions about a shore nobody has seen in living memory. This is, more or less, how the Sigr approach everything.

**Aether Crystal Research — Player Angle:**
Clan Sigr's work makes them the natural home for characters interested in Aether-gadgetry: artificer-adjacent wizards, engineers who work with magic as a material rather than a discipline, tinkerers who want to store spell-energy, build devices, or craft tools that carry pre-loaded Aether for specific uses. The Sigr are actively recruiting outside expertise — anyone who can help them understand the theory of what they have already empirically discovered. The collaboration with Von Zoltraak and Thalum is productive but still early; there is a great deal of work, and the clan is aware that whoever figures out the theoretical framework first will have enormous leverage over everyone else working in this space.

**Story Hooks:**
- A [[House Thalum]] filing lands at the [[The Council of Seven|Council]] citing "geological instability in the eastern seams" as grounds for a safety audit — which would require access to [[Hvergi]]'s deep tunnels. The Sigr have days to decide whether to warn the other two families or attempt to handle it themselves first.
- [[House Von Zoltraak]] makes a private offer: full political backing against Thalum at the Council in exchange for a ten-year exclusive on premium crystal supply. The terms are the best the Sigr have ever been offered. The strings are exactly as long as Clan Hjalrim always feared.
- The Sigr heir who studied at the Crucible returns with findings suggesting that the ashgeld treatment on Hjalrim blades could be significantly improved using a Von Zoltraak progressive technique. She wants to propose a collaboration between the two families. Neither Clan is certain how the other will receive it — but she believes it could be the first step toward a genuine Hvergi-Academy alliance on dwarven terms.
- A party member is given a Sigr-made Aether-crystal device — a small thing, carved quartz about the size of a fist, that releases a stored spell on a specific trigger. It works exactly as described. On the third use, it does something the Sigr did not intend and cannot yet explain.
