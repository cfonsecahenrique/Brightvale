# Clan Hjalrim

**Clan Saying:** *"The gate holds, or nothing does."*
**Standing:** Ruling warrior family of [[Hvergi]]
**Hall:** The Iron Threshold — the broad fortified hall flanking [[Hvergi]]'s main entrance, doubling as armory and seat of authority
**Rune-Mark:** A gate-arch inlaid with a crossed hammer and blade

**Overview:**
The Hjalrims are [[Hvergi]]'s warriors, gatekeepers, and the finest weaponsmiths in all of Brightvale. Their authority in the enclave is martial and architectural: they decide who enters, they maintain the defences, and they forge the blades that have made [[Hvergi]]'s name known in every guild hall from [[Medsen]] to [[Emberfort]].

During the [[The Brightblood War|Brightblood War]] they backed the Reform Coalition — a decision the current matriarch, Elder Gyrid Hjalrim, still considers correct and the [[Emberguard]] still considers worth remembering. The fracture has never fully closed.

**The Hjalrim Blade:**
A Hjalrim blade is not a weapon. It is an inheritance.

The forging process involves working [[Hvergi]] crystal shards directly into the steel during the smelt — a technique the Clan guards as closely as the enclave's gates. The result is steel that carries the crystal's natural resonance: in the darkness of the Wastes, a Hjalrim blade glows faintly, casting a pale blue-white light at the deep-seam crystal's particular frequency — enough to work by, enough to unsettle [[Umbra]]-touched creatures that recoil instinctively from that resonance.

When the finished blade is enchanted by active ashgeld — the sacred ash of the Beacon's pyre — the effect deepens considerably. The blade becomes actively hostile to Umbra-touched flesh. Its light does not merely illuminate the dark; it *burns* within it. Venturers who carry a fully treated Hjalrim blade describe the experience as fighting with a torch that also cuts.

There are perhaps one hundred such blades in existence across all of Brightvale. Each was made by a Hjalrim smith. Each has a name. The Clan does not sell them — they gift them, rarely, to those they consider worthy, or exchange them in arrangements that carry far more weight than coin.
But they keep tight books about each and every blade.

**Strife & Ambition:**
The Hjalrims do not crave expansion or political dominance. They want [[Hvergi]] to remain sovereign and its people to remain ready — ready for the Wastes, ready for whatever [[Medsen]] attempts, and ready, ultimately, for [[Heimsfar]]. Their current frustration is that they are being forced to manage the fallout of a tunnel project they were never told about, fielding questions from the [[Explorers' Guild]] they cannot answer honestly without betraying [[Hvergi]]'s secrets.

**Key Relationships:**
- **[[Slayers]]:** The deepest external relationship Clan Hjalrim holds. The Slayers' empirical knowledge of [[Umbra]]-touched anatomy meshes naturally with the Hjalrims' obsession with engineering blades that exploit it. It is said [[Makjar One-Eyed, First Slayer]] carries a Hjalrim blade — neither party confirms this publicly, but among Slayers, the weapon she draws in the field is described with the same quiet reverence reserved for the Guild's founding relics.
- **[[Emberguard]]:** Formal and frigid. [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner]] represents an institution the Hjalrims associate with Crown loyalty, and a bloodline they associate with everything in [[Medsen]] that treats [[Hvergi]] as a useful resource rather than a sovereign community. Every interaction is correct and cold.
- **[[Explorers' Guild]]:** A long-standing, uneasy compact. The Guild's elite pathfinders may use certain Technical Routes through Hjalrim-monitored mountain passes in exchange for sharing route logs. [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] tolerates this because she has no better option. Recently, the seismic activity from Clan Brumdain's tunnel has begun disturbing those routes, and the Guild has started asking questions the Hjalrims cannot answer without betraying the secret. The resentment directed at Clan Brumdain is no longer quiet.
- **Clan Brumdain:** The Hjalrims do not oppose the tunnel in principle. They were not told about it. That is not a small distinction.

**Heimsfar:**
The Hjalrims carry [[Heimsfar]] as martial obligation. The Homeward Voyage requires ships — and ships require a people who survive long enough to reach the water. Every blade forged, every generation of [[Hvergi]] that does not get absorbed into [[Medsen]]'s political machinery: to the Hjalrims, this is the prophecy made practical. You keep the people ready. You keep the gate. The sea will still be there.

**Story Hooks:**
- A Hjalrim blade surfaces in [[Medsen]] with its name scratched off — sold, not gifted. Elder Gyrid wants to know how, by whom, and what the receiving party used it for.
- A young Hjalrim smith has developed a technique that significantly improves crystal-shard integration but requires materials only obtainable through [[House Von Zoltraak]]'s Crucible laboratories. The family is divided on whether to make contact.
- [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] arrives at the Iron Threshold in person, without announcement, and asks directly what is causing the eastern tremors. The Hjalrims must decide, immediately, how to answer.
