# Clan Stiórmund

**Clan Saying:** *"The seam runs where the seam runs. Follow it."*
**Standing:** Eldest ruling family of [[Hvergi]]
**Hall:** The Root Hall — the deepest residential tier of [[Hvergi]], adjacent to the active crystal seams
**Rune-Mark:** A pickaxe descending into a spiralling seam, ringed by the directional rune for *northeast*

**Overview:**
The Stiórmund are the oldest of [[Hvergi]]'s three ruling families, and the ones who read the rock. Where other miners extract crystal, the Stiórmund interpret the mountain — tracing seam-lines and pressure gradients the way a scholar reads a manuscript. Their authority is geological as much as political: they know where the crystals run, and they have known for longer than anyone else has been keeping records.

The family's current patriarch, Elder Storvald Stiórmund, is the driving force behind the secret northeastern tunnel quietly generating seismic tremors across the eastern peaks. To Storvald this is not a political act — it is the first practical step of [[Heimsfar]]. He is convinced the lands to the northeast hold territory less saturated with [[Umbra]] than anything yet mapped, and the entire clan has rallied behind him — though not a single one of them knows precisely how this certainty came to him. Storvald does not explain. He points northeast, and the Stiórmund follow. Whether his knowledge is geological, prophetic, or something he found in the deepest rune-stones that he has not shared with anyone is [[Hvergi]]'s most carefully unasked question.

What has silenced any serious internal dissent is wealth. The northeastern tunnel has broken into ore vein after ore vein — copper, iron, and several seams of a heavy dark alloy the Stiórmund have not yet named — all of whose mining rights remain wholly within the clan by virtue of discovery. The Stiórmund are richer than they have been in living memory. Their resolve is funded. Their workforce has expanded. And Storvald's certainty, whatever its source, has not yet been wrong.

**Strife & Ambition:**
Day-to-day governance interests the Stiórmund less than the long project. Other families manage Hvergi's politics. The Stiórmund manage its direction. Their ambition is completing the northeastern tunnel before political pressure from [[Medsen]] makes it impossible — and before the seismic tremors become impossible to explain away. Storvald holds no formal authority over the other clans, which means the tunnel is an act of unilateral will, and the fallout of its discovery will land on all of [[Hvergi]] equally.

**Key Relationships:**
- **[[Caretakers of the Beacon]]:** A generations-old, unwritten arrangement. The High Rituals require resonant crystals from the lowest Stiórmund seams — ones that vibrate at the Beacon's particular frequency — and the Stiórmund supply them without question. In return, the Caretakers provide theological backing whenever [[Medsen]]'s institutions challenge [[Hvergi]]'s autonomy. Neither side would describe this as a transaction. Neither would survive its collapse.
- **[[House Lugner]]:** A source of persistent pressure. Lugner's scholars believe certain deep Stiórmund crystals contain pre-[[Apostasy]] resonance — fossilised traces of Deep-Weave spells they call "memory-stones" — and have been seeking exclusive access for years. Storvald finds them useful and distasteful in equal measure: the Lugner want to own the past, while he is trying to use it as a compass. He grants occasional survey access, carefully rationed, to keep them interested without giving them leverage.
- **Clan Hjalrim:** The family responsible for [[Hvergi]]'s defence was not consulted on the tunnel project. This is a wound that has not been addressed.
- **Clan Sigr:** Sigr's [[Aetherweaver Academy]] contacts have occasionally been useful to the tunnel project. Storvald has learned not to refuse useful things on principle, which has made him more tolerant of Sigr's outside relationships than Clan Hjalrim is.

**Heimsfar:**
Of the three families, the Stiórmund carry [[Heimsfar]] most literally. The tunnel is an act of prophecy as much as engineering. Storvald does not quote the rune-carvings in conversation — he distrusts what has survived after so many re-carvings — but he acts as though the direction is clear, even where the words are not. Whether his certainty is faith, geological evidence, or stubbornness dressed as both is a question [[Hvergi]] debates quietly, in his absence.

**Story Hooks:**
- The tunnel breaks into a pre-[[Apostasy]] chamber of unknown origin. The carvings inside are not [[Dwarvenkind|Dwarven]].
- Storvald's surveys predict the tunnel will be complete within eight months. The seismic activity has become impossible to conceal, and he approaches the Caretakers to call in the arrangement — but the current High Caretaker was not party to the original compact and does not consider themselves bound by it.
- [[House Lugner]] identifies the specific seam containing the memory-stones and begins negotiating with [[The Council of Seven]] to compel access, framing it as a matter of Vale-wide arcane security.
- A player character who asks Storvald directly how he knows northeast is the way will receive a long silence, and then a question in return: *"When did the mountain ever lie to you?"* What he actually found — in the rune-stones, in the grain of the rock, or somewhere else entirely — remains open.
