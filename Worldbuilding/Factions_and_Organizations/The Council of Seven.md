# The Council of Seven

The **Council of Seven** is the supreme governing body of Brightvale, originally forged in the aftermath of royal mismanagement to balance the power of the Monarchy. Evolving alongside the shifting powers of the valley, it serves as the central stage for administration, legislation, and political warfare in [[Medsen]].

## Council Members

The Council consists of seven distinct seats. Five of these seats are permanently held by the leaders of Brightvale’s core institutions, creating the bedrock of the valley's governance:

*   **High Caretaker:** The voice of orthodoxy, representing the [[Caretakers of the Beacon]], religious doctrine, and the preservation of the Light.
*   **Lord Commander of the Emberguard:** Representing internal military power, civil order, and the defense of the settlements.
*   **Arch Aetherweaver:** Representing the [[Aetherweaver Academy]], the study of the Deep-Weave, and the scholastic advancement of Brightvale.
*   **First Slayer:** Representing the [[Slayers]], the defense of the outer walls, and the continuous war against the Umbra.
*   **Grand Explorer:** Representing the [[Explorers' Guild]], outward reclamation of the Wastes, and controversial progressive policies regarding the past.

## The Noble Seats

The final two seats on the Council are rotating positions occupied by patriarchs or matriarchs of the **Noble Houses**. These Noble Seats are filled via a complex, high-stakes election held every **two years**, officially intended to give a voice to the sprawling economic, agricultural, and social interests of the Aristocracy.

### Guild Collusion and Political Proxy

In reality, the Noble Seats are rarely independent. The five permanent factions aggressively collude with the Noble Houses during the election cycles to ensure their preferred candidates win. The Guilds provide wealth, political favors, and public backing to allied Houses, using them to secure one or two extra proxy votes for their side of the table.

This constant manipulation turns the Noble elections into a proxy war. For example, the **Caretakers** and the **Emberguard**—the conservative factions most strongly aligned with the Crown—have historically lobbied for **[[House Aurelian]]** to retain a seat. House Aurelian's extreme piety and unwavering loyalty to the Monarchy makes them an incredibly reliable vote for orthodox interests. 

When a second pious or Crown-loyal House manages to win the other seat at the same time, the conservative bloc on the Council secures a commanding super-majority, resulting in a two-year window of extraordinary power for the Crown. However, the opposition factions (such as the Guilds desiring reform) work tirelessly to prevent this. Because of this aggressive cross-lobbying, a dual-pious majority has not successfully materialized since the days before the **[[The Brightblood War]]**.
