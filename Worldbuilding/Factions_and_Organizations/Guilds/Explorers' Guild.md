# The [[Explorers' Guild]]

While many equate the term **Venturer** with the **[[Explorers' Guild]]**, it is actually a title claimed by members of all five major organizations who take to the Wastes. The Explorers' Guild, however, acts as the administrative and logistical hub for these expeditions. They are the trailblazers, scavengers, and cartographers of the end-times, obsessed with the past and the "Beyond." In the field, while the [[Emberguard]] focus on defense and the [[Slayers]] cull the elite threats, the Explorers' primary concern is the map and the prize.

### Role and Function

*   **Waste-Mapping:** The geography of the Wastes below is notoriously unstable, shifted by [[Umbra]]-flux and lingering spell-scars. The Explorers are tasked with mapping these "shifting sands" to ensure any descent from the plateau has a chance of returning.
*   **Checkpoint Establishment:** They establish and maintain "Checkpoints" — small, magically-shielded outposts in the Wastes where Venturers can find temporary sanctuary, fresh air, or emergency supplies.
*   **Artifact Recovery:** A primary source of the Guild's funding is the recovery of ancient Armisian artifacts and pre-[[Apostasy]] technology. These relics are often essential for the [[Aetherweaver Academy]]'s research into the "Deep-Weave."
*   **Mountain Surveillance:** They explore the treacherous passes of the **Teardrop Mountains**, ensuring the glaciers remain impassable to the [[Umbra]]-touched while searching for routes into the uncharted northwestern regions beyond the mountains.

### The Technical Routes

The Guild maintains tight records on several "Technical Routes" through the peaks. These paths are too technical, sheer, and physically demanding for the (almost) mindless [[Umbra]]-touched or the common citizenry. These secret "Brave-Paths" are the lifeblood of the most elite Venturers, allowing them to bypass traditional checkpoints and enter the Wastes from unexpected angles.

### Institutional Culture

The [[Explorers' Guild]] has the highest mortality rate of any institution in Brightvale. Consequently, they possess the most individual freedom. They are a rowdy, charismatic, and often fatalistic group, bonded by the shared experience of seeing what lies beneath the crimson veil.

### Current Leader: Grand Explorer [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]]

The Guild is currently led by **Grand Explorer [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]]**, a veteran whose name is synonymous with the "Humanity-Loss" gamblers. [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] is a charismatic but reckless leader who pushes the **Council of Seven** to be far more aggressive in reclaiming the ruins of the Old World.

[[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]]’s survival in the most [[Umbra]]-dense regions has come at a steep price. She has participated in the "Memory-Bargains," a desperate technique where a traveler can sacrifice non-essential (and sometimes essential) personal memories to the [[Umbra]]-resonance to avoid Hollowing. [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] has already sacrificed several key memories, including the face of her own child, to ensure she could lead her team back home. She carries this weight with a sharp, dark humor and an unwavering sense of duty.

*"The Wastes don't just take your life,"* [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] is known to joke at the Guild-Hall in [[Emberfort]]. *"They take your Tuesday afternoons and your first kiss, too. At least I've forgotten why I'm supposed to be sad."*
