# [[Aetherweaver Academy]]

Constructed along the precipitous cliffs of **[[Aurore]]'s Peak**, the Academy is a sprawling, blue-roofed edifice of cobalt stone and silver-veined marble dedicated to the mastery of the Arcanum. Its tiered balconies overlook the central valley, providing a serene yet imposing atmosphere for the initiates who walk its halls. Within its tiered libraries and ozone-scented laboratories, Aetherweavers undergo rigorous training in the complexities of Arcane Magic, learning to weave raw environmental energies into stable forms. Simultaneously, senior scholars conduct tireless research into the reinforcement of the valley’s ancient wards and the alchemical production of **Lumen (Blessed Branches)**—an essential process that infuses harvested wood with the Beacon's resonant light to push back the encroaching dark.

Directly below the Academy’s foundations clings the **Town of [[Aurore]]**, a vertical settlement of tiered streets and stone housing providing essential services, warm lodging, and specialized reagent markets for the Enduring. It serves as a bustling hub where mapmakers trade in the shifting geography of the Wilds and shops sell anti-[[Umbra]] talismans. The town acts as a vital bridge between the common citizenry and the esoteric elite, where the clatter of the market constantly competes with the distant, low hum of magical experiments echoing from the cliffs above.

Above the Academy, crowning the very summit of the peak and often piercing the cloud layer, stands **The Tower**. Built in the same majestic, soaring style, it serves as the secluded sanctuary for the Arch-Aetherweavers and is topped by a grand, multi-lensed observatory of ancient glass and brass. From this height, the masters study celestial movements and the vibrant winter Auroras—shimmering curtains of violet light believed to be direct manifestaciones of the **Weave** itself. Their observations are critical, as they interpret these celestial shifts to predict fluctuations in the [[Umbra]]’s strength and the overall health of the valley’s spiritual defenses.

### Two Patrons, One Institution

The Academy is the battleground for the Vale's most significant arcane ideological conflict. Two noble houses fund it from opposing positions, and the tension shapes every research decision made within its halls.

**[[House Lugner]]** backs the **Old Magic** faction — the scholars who believe that the key to surviving the [[Umbra]] lies in recovering and reconstructing the Deep-Weaves of the Armisian Empire. This faction is, at its core, an attempt to recover **unstructured magic**: the pre-Apostasy human tradition of instinct, faith, and raw belief that produced effects the modern Arcanum cannot replicate. Their endowments fund the maintenance of the cobalt stone cliffs and the Tower's multi-lensed observatory, but the price is the pressure to dedicate the Academy's best minds to excavating a past that, their rivals argue, already failed once.

**[[House Von Zoltraak]]** backs the **Progressive Aetherweaving** faction — the scholars who argue that new arcane forms, developed in direct response to current conditions, are categorically more useful than reviving a tradition that did not prevent the [[Apostasy]]. This faction works squarely within the **Elven formulaic tradition**: rigorous, structured, teachable, precise. They fund the Crucible laboratory complex at [[Aurore]]'s mid-tier and protect researchers whose findings antagonize the Lugner endowment.

Individual scholars navigate between these two funding pressures constantly. Some align clearly with one faction; many try to secure grants from both by framing their work to satisfy contradictory priorities. A growing number have quietly relocated to the Crucible's independent laboratories to avoid the politics altogether. The Arch-Aetherweavers of the Tower publicly maintain neutrality, which satisfies no one.

A third path — the **Warlock tradition** of obsessive specialization practiced by the Infernis bloodline's extraplanar ancestors — has no formal faction at the Academy. It is studied as historical curiosity and occasionally as practical model, but it resists institutionalization by nature. See [[Magic_Systems]].
