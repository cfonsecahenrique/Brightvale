# [[Emberguard]]

The **[[Emberguard]]** is the primary internal safety and policing organization of Brightvale. While their comrades—whether fellow Guards, specialist [[Slayers]], or independent **Venturers** from the other guilds—brave the Wastes to cull abominations and explore ruins, the Emberguard themselves serve as the shield that ensures the peace within the Beacon's light. In the field, however, many Guards take up the mantle of Venturer as frontline support for expeditions.

### Base of Operations: The [[Emberfort]]

The guild is headquartered in the **[[Emberfort]]**, the massive bastion that guards the southern entrance to the Vale at the end of the **Venturer’s Trail**. From this vantage point, they monitor the mountain passes for both incoming refugees and descending monsters. While their main force is concentrated at the fort, they maintain "Glow-Stations" (small outposts) along the valley’s major routes and within [[Medsen]] itself.

### Role and Function

*   **Border Defense:** They are the first line of defense against mountain-dwelling horrors and [[Umbra]]-tainted beasts that stray too close to the plateau.
*   **Civil Policing:** Within the settlements, they act as the law of the **Council of Seven**, resolving disputes, enforcing rations, and ensuring the "Sanctity of Light" is maintained.
*   **Mobilization:** When a threat is detected, the [[Emberguard]] acts as the central coordinator. They determine if the threat can be handled by their rank-and-file or if specialized assistance from the **[[Slayers]]** or contracted **Venturers** is required.

### Institutional Traits

*   **The Ashen Oath:** Every member of the Guard undergoes a ritual involving the "Cold-Flame" of the Beacon. This leaves a faint, silvery-gray mark on their forearms—the **Embermark**—which provides a minor resistance to the [[Umbra]]'s Malignant Resonance.
*   **The Chain of Flame:** A tactical doctrine emphasizing tight formations and shared defensive magic. Guards are trained to overlap their protective auras, creating a temporary "Mini-Beacon" in the field.
*   **Vigil of the Zenith:** A tradition where guards remain in total silence for the hour of the solar zenith, symbolizing their unwavering focus on the Beacon's protection.

### Current Leader: Lord Commander [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner]]

The current head of the [[Emberguard]] is **Lord Commander [[Lord Commander Varis Lugner]]**, a veteran of the "Crimson-Gloom" incursions. A prominent **[[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]] ([[Infernis_Tieflings|Tiefling]])** of the "Azure Stain," Varis is easily recognized by his gray-bluish skin and sweeping dark horns. He hails from **[[House Lugner]]**, an Old Armisian lineage known for their rigid adherence to tradition and martial prowess. 

Varis is a polarizing figure; while he is credited with modernizing the [[Emberfort]]’s ballista-defenses, his strict enforcement of the "Common Ration" has led to friction with the newer houses. He is rarely seen without his ancestral plate armor, which is said to be etched with runes that hum in harmony with the Brightvale Beacon.
