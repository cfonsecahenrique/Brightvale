# GM Only Secret
Somewhere in the Century of Loss (first 100 years after Apostasy), within the Imperial City of Armis-Caen, a faction emerged that used the Umbra and manipulated it to dominate other dwindling factions. Their first leader, King Morragh the Hideous, had several arms and a distorted half-giant physique. These people were still unnaturally strong from the Apostasy Wars, so they were able to survive the Umbra and its corruption longer. 

They established a power-first regime, survival of the strong. To become stronger, one had to give into the Umbra. it was brutal and the nail in the coffin for the survival of the Armisian Empire. Survivors either bent the knee to the brutal Order of Blood and Shadow, or fled the city to the ever-more desolate wastes. 

Their resistance to the Umbra dwindled, though. After some decades of brutal rule over an ever-more distorted humanity, they would lose themselves completely to the growing Umbra, slaying themselves in the process.

But remnants and powerful revenants still prowl the ruined city to this day.
