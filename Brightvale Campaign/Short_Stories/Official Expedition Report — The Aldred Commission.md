## Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission

*Issued under seal of the Office of the High Caretaker, 14th of Embertide, 387 AA. Distributed to the parishes of Medsen, Emberfort, and the outer wards. To be read aloud at the Lighting of the Lesser Lanterns.*

*Compiled from the sworn and tended testimony of the surviving Venturers Zorael of House Lugner and Yuriko, formerly of the Order, after their convalescence under the care of the [[The Beacon|Beacon]]'s servants. May their flame yet burn true.*

---

### To the Faithful of Brightvale

Children of the Light — it is with sorrow, and with a measure of righteous indignation, that we set down here the truth of what befell the so-called *Aldred Commission*: the expedition that, in the closing days of last winter, was sent forth from the gates of [[Emberfort]] in search of a relic to feed our dimming flame.

You have heard the rumours. The taverns have spoken, the Guildhalls have whispered, and the lesser presses have inked nonsense onto cheap paper for the price of a tin penny. We address you now to lay these falsehoods to rest, and to honour the memory of the only true son of the Light who walked that path.

We speak, of course, of **Senior Caretaker Aldred Gwyn** — whose ashes will not return to us, and whose name shall be read at every Lighting until the end of the present cycle.

---

### Of the Selection of the Venturers

It is no secret to those who follow the workings of the [[The Council of Seven|Council]] that this expedition was not assembled by the Caretakers alone. It was, regrettably, the product of *compromise* — a compromise pressed upon the Order by parties whose interests lay closer to their own ledgers and their own holdings than to the survival of the Vale.

The Order had nominated a roster of four pious and tempered Venturers, every one of them a known servant of the [[The Beacon|Beacon]]'s warmth. That roster was rejected. In its place, the Council seated a company chosen for its *names*, its *politics*, and its convenience to certain factions who would prefer that the Order's hand be lightened upon the wheel of Brightvale.

Let the record show, plainly, who walked that road, and at whose insistence:

- **Cillian, called the Old** — an elf of unknown allegiance, by his own admission a keeper of pre-Apostatic memory, and a known associate of the [[Concordance]]. A man whose loyalty to the present age was never demonstrated, and whose mind had been hollowed by centuries of [[Umbra]] exposure. Seated at the demand of the Aeldenbarrow caucus.
- **Theodor Stjormund** — second son of [[Storvald Stjormund]] of Hvergi. A brawler of admitted talent, but a man whose ambitions ran ahead of his piety. Seated at the dwarven clans' insistence, despite the Order's reservations regarding his standing among his own people.
- **Yuriko, called Ruinweaver** — once a novice of the Beacon's Flame, *cast out* from the Order in her youth for the very heresies she would, by the end of this expedition, repeat. That she was placed among the Commission at all is a matter the Order has formally protested, and shall continue to protest.
- **Zorael of House Lugner** — cousin to the heir of [[House Lugner|that House]], an Instinctual of the [[Aetherweaver Academy]] of formidable destructive talent, and a young woman who has never, in the public record, knelt at a Lighting. Seated by the Aetherweavers' delegation.

Four names. Not one of them a Caretaker. Not one of them a confirmed communicant of the Flame. This was the company into which our Aldred was sent — alone, as the Order's single voice — to guide them, to counsel them, and, as it transpired, to die for them.

---

### Of the Expedition Itself

The Commission descended the Venturer's Trail on the seventh of Coldglass and crossed into the Wastes without incident. For two days they made progress under the steady direction of Senior Caretaker Gwyn, whose preparations had been exhaustive and whose maps were drawn from the Order's own archives.

On the third day, the company was set upon by a [[Revenant]] patrol of standard tier. The encounter was resolved without loss. We note, for the record, that during this engagement the Venturer Stjormund struck at one of his companions in error, his blood being already up — a small matter, perhaps, but illustrative of the temperament that had been forced upon the Commission.

On the fourth day, the company was beset by a horde of [[Umbra]]-touched dead, drawn in unprecedented density. Senior Caretaker Gwyn ordered the deployment of the [[Ashgeld]] lantern in the prescribed manner. The horde was driven back. The Commission pressed on.

It was on the evening of the fifth day that the disaster occurred.

The party was overrun by a host the like of which the Order has seen only twice in living record: a full descending presence of the [[Shatter-Wing Angels|Shatter-Wing]], in company with two further hordes. The site at which they made their stand has been *struck from the maps*, by the joint order of the Council and the Office of the High Caretaker, that no further Venturer be tempted to repeat their folly. We will not name it here. We will not draw it. We will not speak of it again.

Senior Caretaker Aldred Gwyn fell in the first instant of the assault — not in flight, but standing, with the lantern of the Order still kindled in his hand. The Shatter-Wing took him before any other. We are told, by those who were there, that he met its eye without flinching. He did not run. He did not recant.

The dwarf Stjormund and the elf Cillian fell shortly after, in circumstances which the surviving Venturers have been unable, even now, to recount without falling into the babble that has so afflicted them since their return.

Zorael of House Lugner and Yuriko Ruinweaver were borne back to the gates of Brightvale by the grace of the [[The Beacon|Beacon's]] light alone — for they brought back nothing. No relic. No record. No remains of their companions. Only their breath, and their broken minds.

---

### Of Their Convalescence, and the Rumours

The Order has, at no small expense, undertaken the care and the cleansing of the two survivors. Months of holy treatment, of quiet rooms and tended fires, have restored to them a measure of function. They will not again venture into the Wastes — the kindness of the Order forbids it — but they walk among us, and they breathe, and for that we give thanks.

We address now, directly, the rumours that have circulated in our streets since their return:

> *That the Commission found an ancient artefact, and that its discovery broke them.*

There was no artefact. There was no discovery. There was a horde, and an Angel, and the deaths of three of the bravest souls our compromised politics could place upon that road. The minds of the survivors were broken not by revelation but by **prolonged exposure** to the Umbra — a corruption which any first-year novice of the Order could identify on sight, and which the Caretakers have spent these months patiently drawing back out of them.

> *That the Order arranged the Commission to be rid of certain inconvenient persons.*

This is the most pernicious of the falsehoods, and we name it for what it is: a slander, fed by those same political interests that imposed the Commission's roster upon us in the first place. The Order does not arrange deaths. The Order tends flame. *We did not choose these Venturers.* We **buried** one of our own beside them.

Any further rumour — and we are told there are several, growing in certain quarters — is to be regarded as the ordinary noise of a frightened populace, and is *not* to be repeated in the parishes, in the Guildhalls, or in the markets. The Emberguard have been instructed accordingly.

---

### A Word, in Closing, on the Lesson

Children of the Light — there is a lesson in the Aldred Commission, and the Order would be remiss not to set it before you plainly.

When the [[The Council of Seven|Council]] convenes, and the Guilds press their candidates, and the Houses press theirs, and the Concordance presses theirs, and every faction in the Vale jostles to seat a name of its own choosing upon a venture of the Order's design — *this is what follows*. Four so-called heroes were chosen by every voice in Brightvale save the one that knew the road. Three of them died. One Caretaker died with them. And two were brought back so badly used that they will never again be trusted with a lantern.

The Beacon does not care for our cleverness. It does not care for our compromises. It cares for **fidelity**, and for the kindled flame, and for those who tend it without question.

Let the seats of future Commissions be drawn from the *faithful*. Let those who deny the Beacon, and those who question it, and those who carry old loyalties from before its light, remain at the hearths where they may yet be warmed — but let them not, ever again, be set at the head of an expedition into the dark.

The Beacon endures. The cycle turns. We descend to listen.

*By order of the Office of the High Caretaker,*
*and with the seal of the Council of Seven affixed,*
*this 14th of Embertide, 387 AA.*
