# Pale Dirge

*It does not charge. It drifts — at the edges of the battle, moving between shadows, emitting a sound just below the threshold of a voice. The Hollowed near it move faster. Hit harder. The light in the room feels slightly less bright.*

**Category:** Undead / Hollowed — Corrupted Arcanist
**Related:** [[Revenant]], [[Umbra]], [[Apostasy]]

---

## Motives & Tactics

The Pale Dirge was once someone who understood magic — a scholar, an initiate, a hedge-practitioner. The Umbra has converted that understanding into something else: the ability to broadcast Umbra resonance outward, amplifying the threat of every Hollowed nearby. It has no interest in direct combat. It positions itself at Far range, sustains its Resonance Field, and uses Hollow Keening to pressure anyone it identifies as the party's anchor — the healer, the support, whoever is keeping the others standing.

It is the encounter's real priority target and it knows it. It will attempt to keep the Warden or Shambles between itself and the party at all times.

---

## Tier 1 — Support

**Type:** Undead / Hollowed | **Role:** Support | **Tier:** 1
**Difficulty:** 12 | **Thresholds:** 7/12 | **HP:** 4 | **Stress:** 4
**ATK:** +0 | **Dirge Touch:** Melee | 1d4+2 magic
**Experiences:** Umbra Conduit +2

### Features

- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Pale Dirge critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Resonance Field - Passive:** While the Dirge is alive and within Far range of a Revenant ally, that ally gains +2 to all attack rolls. This bonus applies to all eligible Revenant allies simultaneously.
- **Hollow Keening - Action:** **Mark a Stress.** Target one PC within Far range. That PC must make a Presence Roll (12) or mark a Stress as the keening pulls at their sense of self.
- **Shadow Mend - Reaction:** When a Revenant ally within Close range would mark their last HP, **Spend a Fear**: that ally clears 1 HP instead and remains in the fight.

### Soft Move

The Dirge drifts toward its most damaged ally, repositioning its Resonance Field. Move it to Close range of the ally most in danger — the bonus now applies where it is most needed, and Shadow Mend becomes a live threat.

## Design Notes

- **Resonance Field** is the defining feature: a flat +2 to attack rolls for all Revenant allies makes even the Shambles meaningfully threatening, and it applies passively — no action cost. Killing the Dirge first is the obvious play, but the Warden and Shambles exist to make that difficult.
- **Hollow Keening** now correctly costs **Mark a Stress** (was incorrectly Spend a Fear — per adversary design rules, a single-target Stress-imposing action is a Mark a Stress trigger, not a Fear spend). It targets Presence rather than Agility or Strength, which puts pressure on support-role characters.
- **Shadow Mend** correctly costs Spend a Fear — it gives an ally an effective free action (staying alive) without reducing the benefit, which fits the Fear-spend criteria.
- Difficulty corrected to 12 (was 11, minimum for Tier 1 Support is 12). Stress corrected to 4 (was 2, minimum is 4). Damage corrected to 1d4+2 (avg 4.5, within 3–5 range; was 1d6+2 at avg 5.5, slightly over).
- **Encounter tip:** Place the Dirge at Far range at the start of combat. It should not move into Melee unless forced. If a PC moves to intercept it, the Shambles should use **Flanking Drive** to punish that separation.
