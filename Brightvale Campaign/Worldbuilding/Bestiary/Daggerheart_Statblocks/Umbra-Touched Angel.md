# Umbra-Touched Angel (Void Seraph)

*A towering six-winged figure of jagged black glass and weeping violet ichor, clutching a greatsword of corrupted divine steel so massive it warps the air along its edge. Its fractured halo casts inverted light. It does not speak. It descends, and it ends things.*

**Category:** Corrupted Divine / Celestial Horror — Umbra-Touched
**Related:** [[Shatter-Wing Angel]], [[The Apostasy]], [[Umbra]]

---

## Motives & Tactics

The Angel opens at range — its Dirge of Undoing punishes any clustered approach, and Inverted Hymn begins counting the moment a PC takes serious damage. When it descends, it descends with the greatsword: a Sundering Strike shreds armor on the first pass, and Rending Arc punishes any group that hasn't broken formation. It rises again when pressured, using Flying to reset its Difficulty, and dives back in with its Relentless actions. The sword and the hymns operate on different axes — PCs must choose between closing to stop the divine corruption or staying at range and absorbing the blade's reach through its arc.

## Tier 3 — Solo

**Type:** Umbra-Touched | **Role:** Solo | **Tier:** 3
**Difficulty:** 18 (21 while flying) | **Thresholds:** 21/38 | **HP:** 11 | **Stress:** 6
**ATK:** +6 | **Void Greatsword:** Melee | 3d12+6 magic
**Experiences:** Vengeful +3, Fallen Divinity +3

### Features

- **Relentless (two) - Passive:** The Angel can be spotlighted up to two times per GM turn.
- **Flying - Passive:** While flying, the Angel gains a +3 bonus to its Difficulty.
- **Umbra-Touched - Passive:** The Angel critically succeeds on attacks against a PC on a die roll of 19–20.
- **Fractured Divinity - Passive:** The Angel has resistance to physical damage. PCs that roll with Fear when attacking the Angel lose a Hope.
- **Sundering Strike - Action:** Make a standard attack with the Void Greatsword. On a success, the target must mark an Armor Slot without receiving its benefits. If they have no Armor Slots remaining, they must mark an additional HP instead as the blade cleaves through to the soul beneath.
- **Rending Arc - Action:** **Mark a Stress.** The Angel brings the Void Greatsword down in a sweeping arc. Make an attack against all creatures within Very Close range. Targets the Angel succeeds against take **3d8+4** magic damage.
- **Dirge of Undoing - Action:** **Spend a Fear** to make an attack against all targets within Close range. Targets the Angel succeeds against take **3d10+4** direct magic damage and must mark a Stress as the hymn unravels their sense of self.
- **Inverted Hymn - Reaction:** Countdown (Loop 1d6). When a PC marks 2 or more HP from an attack, activate the Countdown. When it triggers, all PCs within Far range must make a Knowledge Reaction Roll (17). PCs who fail take **3d8+6** direct magic damage and become _Vulnerable_. PCs who succeed take half damage.
- **Momentum - Reaction:** When the Angel makes a successful attack against a PC, you gain a Fear.

### Soft Move

The Angel rises higher, the Void Greatsword trailing violet ichor as it ascends. Its fractured halo pulses. It gains advantage on its next attack roll.

## Design Notes

- **Momentum + Relentless** is deliberately used here — this is meant to be a "truly difficult" adversary per the guide's own advice. The Angel is Fear-positive, making it escalate rapidly.
- **Void Greatsword** replaces Soul-Rending Touch as the standard attack — same damage and magic type (the blade is corrupted divine steel; its strikes still bypass mundane defenses and unwind the soul directly). The rename grounds the threat physically: this thing is carrying a weapon the size of a door.
- **Sundering Strike** is the greatsword's precision application — the armor-shredding move that replaces Soul Sever. Same mechanic, re-tied to the weapon.
- **Rending Arc** is the new sweep action. It costs Mark a Stress (attacks multiple targets at Very Close, dealing regular rather than direct magic damage — distinguished from Dirge of Undoing which deals direct damage and costs a Fear). It gives the Angel a melee AoE that punishes groups who cluster at its feet to avoid its hymns. PCs must choose: spread out and face Rending Arc less, or cluster and face Dirge of Undoing less.
- **Dirge of Undoing** and **Inverted Hymn** remain the Angel's divine corruption axis — range-pressure and countdown threat that operate independently of the greatsword.
- At Difficulty 21 while flying, this is extremely hard to hit. PCs will need to ground it or use magic. The temple environment provides the blue flame as a Very Close deterrent — use that to force the Angel into Rending Arc range if the party is clever enough to exploit it.
- **Encounter suggestion:** The Angel descends through the ceiling and opens on Aldred (instant kill, no roll). Round 1 it is airborne — Dirge of Undoing or Inverted Hymn trigger. It lands on Round 2 and the greatsword comes out.
