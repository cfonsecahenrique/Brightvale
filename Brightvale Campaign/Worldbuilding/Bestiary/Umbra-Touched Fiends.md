# Umbra-Touched Fiend

**Category:** Outsider / Corrupted Fiend
**Habitat:** Deep ravines, forgotten summoning circles, the Chasm

During the long withering of the [[Apostasy]], desperate mortals made pacts with powers at the edges of the collapsing world—Devils of cold contract and Demons of raw will. While these pacts gave rise to the [[Infernis_Tieflings|Tiefling]] lineages, the Fiends themselves found that they were not immune to the world's new reality. Even extraplanar entities of immense malice eventually succumbed to the [[Umbra]].

## Appearance
An **Umbra-Touched Fiend** is a grotesque hybridization of hellish anatomy and void-born mutation. Horns become weeping nexuses; infernal fire burns cold and indigo. Their bodies often twist unpredictably, sprouting the sensory filaments, sightless eyes, and tentacular limbs characteristic of advanced Umbra exposure.

## Behavior and Threat
A Fiend is naturally cunning, deceptive, and deeply individualistic. However, the Umbra is an entropic hive-mind. This conflict causes Umbra-Touched Fiends to be violently erratic. They retain their formidable inherent powers—teleportation, mind-rending, and element-shaping—but deploy them with the relentless, assimilative hunger of the shadow. 

They are particularly dangerous because they often remember the terms of ancient pacts, using that infernal knowledge to bypass wards or manipulate venturing parties before overwhelming them with raw, corrupted magic.
