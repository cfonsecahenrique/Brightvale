# House Kaze

## Overview
**Status:** Recent
**Core Identity:** Controllers of Brightvale's vital messaging network and monopolists of the Vale's few remaining horses.
**Ancestry:** Predominantly [[Orcenfolk]], with some intermarriage.

**House Kaze** is a highly specialized "Recent" noble house that rose to prominence roughly 150 years ago. Their ascension to nobility was not won through combat or ancient imperial bloodlines, but through their absolute mastery and monopoly over the swiftest form of communication in Brightvale: the horse relay system.

## The Value of Horses
In Brightvale, horses are incredibly rare and incalculably expensive. A very small number originally arrived in the valley shortly after the [[Apostasy]], brought by refugees who barely managed to coax the panicked animals up the treacherous, freezing paths of the Venturer's Trail. 

For generations, these few surviving bloodlines were carefully bred and stewarded by Orcish clans. However, due to the small initial population and the devastating casualties suffered during the [[The Brightblood War|Brightblood Civil War]], horses remain one of the most valuable assets in the Vale. While a few of the wealthiest Old Houses possess a token steed for prestige, the vast majority of Brightvale's horses belong exclusively to the Orcish community and House Kaze. It is a common valley idiom that "an Orc's horse is worth more than everything else they own put together."

Due to this extreme rarity and value, horses are never used for mundane labor. The pulling of caravans and carts, or the plowing of the Hearthlands' fields, has completely shifted to sturdy oxen and the endemic mountain alpacas. Furthermore, horses are incapable of making the steep, jagged descent down the Venturer's Trail and are practically useless in the [[Umbra]]-choked, boggy Wastes outside the valley. Therefore, their sole, vital utility is intra-valley communication.

## The Kaze Relay
Recognizing that information is power, the Orcenfolk of House Kaze established Brightvale's foremost messaging network. 

They constructed a series of highly secure, closely guarded stable-checkpoints along all major valley roads, primarily the long stretch between the garrison of [[Emberfort]] and the capital city of [[Medsen]]. By keeping fresh, superbly bred horses ready at each checkpoint and utilizing riders of unmatched skill, the Kaze network can carry a vital message or small, high-value parcel across the nearly 30 kilometers from Emberfort to Medsen in less than four hours. 

In a world where bureaucratic delays or delayed military reports can lead to an Umbra breach or political ruin, this unmatched swiftness made the Kaze indispensable to the [[The Council of Seven]] and the [[Emberguard]], cementing their elevation to nobility.
