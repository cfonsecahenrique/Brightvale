# [[Dwarvenkind]]

### (The Rune-Keepers)

**Dwarvenborne** culture is heavily **Norse-inspired** — a heritage of longships, coastal raids, and the deep conviction that the sea is the only road worth travelling. They remember a "Great Sea" through inscribed stones in **[[Hvergi]]**, and they see the Vale not as a prison, but as a shore they must hold until the tides of shadow recede.

---

## Before the Apostasy

The Dwarves arrived on the Armisian coastline **less than a century before the [[Apostasy]]** — recent enough that the first generation to settle had made the crossing themselves. They came from across a sea whose name they carried in their own tongue, bringing seafaring expertise, deep mining knowledge, and a robust trade culture that the Empire found useful but never fully trusted.

They also brought a strong tradition of militarization. Dwarven communities conducted **Vikingr-like raids** on coastal settlements before diplomatic relations were established, and the memory of those raids persisted in Armisian records long after the raiding stopped. Given how little time elapsed before the Apostasy struck, the rapprochement between the two cultures was still unfinished: trade agreements had been struck, shared mining operations had begun, but the baseline of mutual distrust had never had time to fully dissolve. The Empire considered the Dwarves useful outsiders. The Dwarves considered the Empire arrogant landlords who had barely acknowledged them before the world ended. Neither was entirely wrong.

## During the Apostasy

When the [[Apostasy]] fell and the world collapsed, the Dawnfather united the surviving peoples for the great march north. The Dwarves joined, contributing their knowledge of mountain terrain and their capacity for hard, sustained labour under miserable conditions. Together with the other survivors, they carved a path through the Teardrop Mountains into the plateau that would become Brightvale.

But once settled, the old distrust resurfaced. The Dwarves gravitated toward **isolationism**, pulling away from the predominantly human power structures of [[Medsen]] to establish their own enclave in the eastern peaks: **[[Hvergi]]**. They cooperated where survival required it, but on their own terms, and no more than necessary.

## In the Present

The Dwarves of [[Hvergi]] mine the deep crystal seams beneath the mountains — crystals used by [[Aetherweaver Academy|Aetherweavers]] to focus and manipulate the Beacon's light. Some of these crystals contain latent magical energy of their own, which can be channelled into the creation of magical items or released in a raw, volatile burst. This gives the Dwarves a quiet but significant hold on Brightvale's arcane supply chain — a fact they are well aware of and manage carefully.

Their relationship with [[House Thalum]] remains a formal rivalry over mining rights and quarrying operations. The Thalum want unified extraction. The Dwarves want autonomy. The argument has been running for generations and shows no sign of resolution.
