# Cillian the Old

**Status:** Deceased (387 AA)
**Role:** Elven Druid, Venturer of the [[Official Expedition Report — The Aldred Commission|Aldred Commission]]
**Affiliation:** [[The Concordance|Concordance]] (informally seated by the Aeldenbarrow caucus)

---

## Overview

One of the oldest persons living in Brightvale at the time of his death, Cillian was an [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elven]] druid whose precise age was unknown even to him. His older memories had been hollowed and emptied by the cumulative effects of centuries and prolonged [[Umbra]] exposure — yet what survived was more than enough to make him a walking archive of pre-Apostatic and [[The Concordance|Concordance]] lore.

He was widely regarded as eccentric. He frequently wildshaped into animals while wandering the streets of [[Medsen]] and was often mistaken — even by people who knew him — for an unusually persistent stray. He survived solo expeditions into the Wastes that no one else would have attempted. Whether this was wisdom or whether he had simply forgotten how to be afraid was a matter of long-running debate among the [[Aetherweaver Academy|Academy]]'s younger researchers.

## On the Aldred Commission

Cillian was nominated to the 387 AA expedition by members of the Concordance, who valued him both as a survivor of the Wastes and as a vessel of inherited memory that the [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Order]] could not easily catalogue or contain. He was the rumoured asset the Caretakers most wished to be rid of: a memory the Order did not control.

He died at the temple, holding the threshold with [[Theodor Stjormund]] so [[Zorael Lugner]] and [[Yuriko Ruinweaver]] could flee. What he said as he died, if anything, is not recorded.

The [[Concordance]] has not officially mourned him. They have, however, opened three of his locked memory-chambers in [[Aeldenbarrow]] since 388 AA and have been transcribing the contents continuously. The earliest of those chambers predates the founding of Brightvale.
