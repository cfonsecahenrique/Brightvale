# The Faewilds

The connection between the mortal world and the Faewilds was severed at the [[Apostasy]] — not weakened, not obscured, but **gone**. The [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elven]] communities who recall it describe the change as instantaneous. One day the boundary existed; the next, it did not.

## The Open Questions

- Was the severance caused by the [[Apostasy]], or did it occur in anticipation of it?
- Did the severance originate on the mortal side, the Fae side, or both simultaneously?
- Do the [[Fae]] still exist on the Other Side? Are they aware of the collapse?
- Could a restored connection to the Faewilds represent a strategic resource against the [[Umbra]] — a domain the [[Umbra]] has not penetrated, and allies with no reason to fear it?

## The Concordance Investigation

[[House Aerincorvus]] maintains the **Concordance** — a systematic archive of anomalous reports collected from druidic communities and Elven settlements describing possible Faewilds contact since the [[Apostasy]]. These incidents are rare, often ambiguous, and dismissed by most institutions. The Aerincorvus investigators treat them as data points in a pattern. Three generations of case files now support the working hypothesis that the connection was *closed deliberately* — and may therefore be re-opened.

The question of how is currently unanswered.
