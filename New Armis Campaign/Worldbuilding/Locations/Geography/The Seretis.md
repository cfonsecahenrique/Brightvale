# The Seretis

The great river that is the **lifeblood of [[An'Hiseret]]** — the artery of water and fertility threading the southern desert empire, around which its ancient civilisation grew. As the Nile is to its Egypt, so the Seretis is to An'Hiseret: without it, nothing.

## The Upriver Mystery
The Seretis runs from somewhere **far inland**, beyond the bounds of the empire the Armisians conquered. There is **talk of other, unknown civilisations upriver** — peoples the Empire has never seen, at the river's distant source. *(See hook #4 in [[Plot Hooks]].)*

The pressing questions:
- **Where does the Seretis begin?**
- **Who lives at its source**, and what is their relationship to the deathless empire downstream?

## Hooks (GM)
- A river-journey to the source is a ready-made expedition arc — and a way to introduce a civilisation entirely of your own design, untouched by Armis or An'Hiseret.
