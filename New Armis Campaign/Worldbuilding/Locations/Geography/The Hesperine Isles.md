# The Hesperine Isles

The **westernmost place currently known** to the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]** — a **temperate archipelago** far out past **[[The Contested Isle]]**, on the dim western edge of the charts. *(Armisian name, "the western isles," from* Hesperos*, the west; sailors also call them the **Drifting Isles**, for reasons that become clear below.)*

## What Is (Sort Of) Agreed
- A temperate, seemingly **uninhabited** archipelago.
- **Three large islands and seven smaller ones** — ten in all.
- An unmistakable air of the **mystical**: every expedition comes back changed, or convinced it saw something the others did not.

## The Unreliable Reports
Almost nothing about the Isles is consistent between accounts — and the contradictions are the *defining feature*:
- **Time runs wrong.** One crew swears they spent barely a **week** ashore; Armisian central logs record the same expedition as gone **close to four years**. No two reckonings of time agree.
- **Inhabited or empty?** Many report no living soul. Others describe **strange ancient structures and burial sites** — the work of *someone*, long ago.
- **Where *are* they?** Even the **location is disputed.** Charts contradict one another; ships sent to the same coordinates do not always arrive, and some that never sought the Isles claim to have found them anyway.

## Hooks (GM)
- This is the campaign's note of pure **wonder and dread** — a place that does not obey the rules the Empire has so carefully mastered with [[Aetherweave Mastery]]. Lean into the unreliability; never fully confirm.
- A ready threshold to *something else* — an older world, a liminal realm, a place where time and distance bend. The time-slippage reads like a **[[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Faewilds]]** threshold, and the burial sites may be elven or Fae work. *(Seed — connect or not.)*
- The **westernmost known point** answers the edge of [[Plot Hooks|Hook #2]] only to deepen it: the more the Empire learns, the less it understands.
