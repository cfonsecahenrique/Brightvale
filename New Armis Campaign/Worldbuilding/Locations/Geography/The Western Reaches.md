# The Western Reaches

The colloquial Armisian name for **everything west** — the whole frontier the **[[The Armisian Empire]]** races to explore, map, and colonise, beyond the safe inner **[[The Central Sea]]**. It spans two things:

1. **[[The Titan's Sword]]** — the great western landmass and the *near* frontier, where the Empire built outposts like **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]** and first met the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]**.
2. **The new world** — the lands *beyond* the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]**, west of the Titan's Sword: a realm of exotic artefacts, strange animals, and untapped riches, reached only by the great planar-powered voyages. Across the ocean lie the realms newly found there — the Dwarven **[[Athregal]]** to the north and the crimson empire of **[[Ziramat]]** to the south. At the far western limit of all charts lie the mystical **[[The Hesperine Isles]]**, the westernmost place anyone has returned from.

## Overview
- The destination of the expeditions launched from **[[Pattis]]**.
- A contested colonial frontier where Armisian and Dwarven ambitions first collided, sparking war before settling into uneasy trade.

## Hooks (GM)
- Open canvas for the GM to invent new-world cultures, ruins, beasts, and resources.
- Colonial friction, rival claims, and the question of who — or what — already lived here.
