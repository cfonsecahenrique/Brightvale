# The Topaz Sea

The southern sea **below the [[The Azurian Ocean]]**, in whose waters the dragon-empire of **[[Ziramat]]** is set. Ziramat proper sits on a **great bay** of the Topaz Sea — the stretch of coast and water the dragon-goddess **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]** claims as her own dominion. (See the [[The Known World|world map]].)

## Overview
- The Topaz Sea is the heart of Ziramati waters, as the **[[The Central Sea]]** is to the Armisians.
- Northwest across these waters lies **[[The Contested Isle]]**, which the Ziramati approach from their Topaz bay.

## In Myth
The Ziramati hold that Azhdašar was set to **dominate over this part of the world** — the Topaz Sea and its bay — by her mother, the creator **[[Tiamat]]** (see [[_Ziramati Pantheon|the Ziramati faith]]). Her four sibling-dragons rule, or once ruled, elsewhere; where, no one knows.

## Hooks (GM)
- The Topaz Sea is Azhdašar's *defined* domain. What lies beyond it — south, or deeper into the Topaz — may belong to one of the **four lost dragons**. *(See [[Tiamat]] and [[Plot Hooks]] #1.)*
