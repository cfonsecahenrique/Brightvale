# Caen

The **subjugated** twin of **[[Armis]]**, now slowly merging with its conqueror into a single capital of **[[The Armisian Empire]]**. Once an independent rival; today a proud district-in-the-making that has not entirely forgotten its sovereignty.

## Overview
- Absorbed by [[Armis]] but not yet fully digested — its own customs, families, and grudges persist as the walls between the two cities come down.

## Hooks (GM)
- A reservoir of resentment and faded glory: ideal ground for conspiracies, separatist sentiment, or old bloodlines clinging to lost status.
