# Zergas-by-Azure

A far-western Armisian **outpost town** near the **northernmost point** of the **[[The Titan's Sword]]**, facing the open **[[The Azurian Ocean]]** — as its name ("by-Azure") attests. A rough frontier settlement at the very edge of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Empire]]'s** reach.

## Significance
- **Site of First Contact.** Here the Empire first met the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]**, as both powers pushed to colonise the Titan's Sword. The meeting began in bloodshed and slowly cooled into the uneasy trade that now binds the two peoples. (See [[Timeline (Armis)]].)

## Character
- A remote colonial port — supply-runs, prospectors, soldiers, and opportunists; the Empire's wonders thin out this far from the [[The Central Sea]].

## Hooks (GM)
- The natural starting town for a frontier or expedition campaign: where Armisian and Dwarven interests rub closest, and old grievances are a tavern brawl away from reigniting.
