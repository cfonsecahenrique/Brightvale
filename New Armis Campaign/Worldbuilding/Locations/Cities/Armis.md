# Armis

The imperial capital and namesake of **[[The Armisian Empire]]**. The seat of power, law, and the great houses — and now, increasingly, of the first **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarven]]** communities to settle within the Empire's heart.

## Overview
- One half of the **twin capital**, slowly merging with the subjugated city of **[[Caen]]** into a single metropolis.
- The political and religious centre of the Empire; where returning expeditions ultimately answer.

## Hooks (GM)
- The merger with [[Caen]] breeds resentment, opportunity, and street-level tension between old rivals.
- Dwarven enclaves bring new craft, new wealth, and new suspicion.
