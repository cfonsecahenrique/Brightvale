# Ziramat

A foreign **empire** set on a great **bay of the [[The Topaz Sea]]**, **south of the [[The Azurian Ocean]]**, only just discovered by the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian Empire]]** — contact has barely begun. Where the Dwarven realm of **[[Athregal]]** sits to the ocean's north, Ziramat holds its southern reaches. (See the [[The Known World|world map]].)

> **Cultural inspiration (GM):** **Akkadian** crossed with **ancient Persian**, under a heavy **draconic** overlay — ziggurat-and-cliff grandeur, deep-time god-kingship, and the cult of the dragon woven through everything.

## The Dragon-Goddess
Ziramat is ruled by **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar, the Unwaning Flame]]** — an **Ancient Red Dragon** and living **Dragon-Goddess** of immense age and power, sovereign over the **[[Ziramati Dragonkin|dragon-folk]]** who are reckoned her brood and lesser kin. Her rule is the axis the entire empire turns upon.

In Ziramati faith (the **[[_Ziramati Pantheon|Ziramati Pantheon]]**), Azhdašar is a **daughter of [[Tiamat|Tiamat, the World-Mother]]** — one of **five dragons** the creator set to dominate the world, granted the **[[The Topaz Sea]]** and the bay of Ziramat as her domain. Ziramat is a **matriarchy** in her image: descent, authority, and priesthood pass through the female line, and the cliff-cities are governed by dragon-blooded matriarchs who answer to the Crimson Mother.

## The People
Ziramat is a realm of **[[Ziramati Dragonkin|dragon-folk]]** — a dragonborn people who practise **strange forms of Aetherweaving** unlike the Empire's planar [[Aetherweave Mastery]]. What powers their weaving is unknown, and the [[The Armisian Empire|Empire]] is keenly interested in finding out — and in learning how to make them weaker. *(See hook #3 in [[Plot Hooks]].)*

## The Land
A **crimson, desertic** realm of red rock and sandstone. The Dragonkin build **into the cliffs themselves** — cities carved from red stone faces and sandstone canyons rather than raised on open ground.

*(GM: the red land is no coincidence — it is the living mark of a Red **[[Primordial Dragons|primordial dragon]]** reshaping her domain. Azhdašar's magic has remade Ziramat in her own image, exactly as the dead primordial has warped [[The Contested Isle]].)*

## Status
- **First contact, just begun.** The Empire knows Ziramat only at its edges; little of its power, culture, or intentions is yet understood.
- **Known cities** (besides the eponymous capital, **Ziramat**): **[[Elikrys]]**, **[[Hiraks]]**, **[[Arakses]]**, and **[[Yaviks]]**.

## The Race for the Isle
Ziramat is one of three powers — with **[[The Armisian Empire]]** and **[[Athregal]]** — colonising **[[The Contested Isle]]** to the **northwest**, each from its own base-town. The Ziramati approach the Isle from the southeast.

## Hooks (GM)
- An unknown empire is the campaign's open frontier — potential trade partner, rival, threat, or all three. Define what the Ziramati *want* from the strangers who have just sailed up out of the Azurian.
- Cliff-cities and a red-desert interior invite ruins, hidden depths, and a people very unlike either the Empire or [[An'Hiseret]].
