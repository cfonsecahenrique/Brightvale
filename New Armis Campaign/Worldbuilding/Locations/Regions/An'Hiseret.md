# An'Hiseret

The **subjugated empire** of the **southern coasts** of the **[[The Central Sea]]** — an ancient, powerful civilisation in its own right, only recently brought under **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian]]** dominion. Where the Empire's northern heartland is bright, Hellenic, and Phoenician, **An'Hiseret is its ancient-Egyptian counterweight**: older, stranger, and steeped in death.

## The Necromantic Weave
An'Hiseret's power rested on mastery of the **necromantic Weave** — a tradition entirely unlike the Armisians' planar **[[Aetherweave Mastery|Aetherweave]]**. Its society deliberately **blurred the line between life and death**:
- **God-princes** are known to have ruled for **hundreds of years**, their reigns outlasting whole Armisian dynasties.
- Its **higher priesthood** likewise extended their lives — and unlives — far beyond mortal span.
Death, in An'Hiseret, was not an ending but a station one could hold, bargain with, and rule from.

## The River Seretis
The lifeblood of An'Hiseret is the great river **[[The Seretis|Seretis]]** — the artery of water and fertility around which the whole civilisation grew. It runs from far inland, and there is **talk of unknown civilisations upriver**, at a source no Armisian has seen. *(See hook #4 in [[Plot Hooks]].)*

## Religion
The **[[_An'Hiseretian Pantheon|An'Hiseretian Pantheon]]** — an ancient-Egyptian faith of the river, the sun, and the deathless, bound up with the necromantic Weave and the cult of the [[The Seretis|Seretis]].

## The Subjugation
For all its antiquity and power, An'Hiseret fell to the Armisians after the **[[Aetherweave Mastery|Aetherweave Awakening]]** (744 AF). The Armisians' newfound command of *strongly flowing* Aetherweave gave them the edge to finally overcome the deathless empire of the south — one of the defining conquests of the golden age.

## Hooks (GM)
- A conquered necromantic empire whose god-princes measure time in centuries does not forget, and does not truly die. Its resentment is patient.
- The clash of **planar Aetherweave** vs **necromantic Weave** — and a society built on blurring life and death — is fertile ground to foreshadow the [[Apostasy]].
