# Aetherweave Mastery

The engine of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian]]** golden age. Two and a half centuries before the campaign present — in **744 AF** (see [[Timeline (Armis)]]) — Armisian arcanists achieved mastery over **strongly flowing Aetherweave**, the deep currents of planar magic. Everything that defines the Empire's height — its fleets, its wealth, its wonders — flows from that single breakthrough.

## The Awakening (744 AF)
Where earlier ages could only *channel* faint resonances, the Armisians learned to *command* the Weave at full flood — drawing power directly and reliably from the elemental planes. This is **250 years** of accumulated mastery by the time play begins in **994 AF**.

## Planar Seafaring
The Empire's command of the sea is, at root, command of **two planes**:
- **Water** — calming swells, parting currents, drawing ships through the deep.
- **Air** — summoning and steering winds, lifting vessels, filling sails on a windless sea.

Together they make the **[[The Azurian Ocean]]** a highway rather than a barrier, and make the annual **[[The Western Reaches|new-world]]** voyages possible at all. Armisian ships are as much works of aetheric engineering as of carpentry.

## Where It Lives
- **[[Pattis]]** is the Empire's great work of Aetherweaving — a *moving* island-city built both **above and beneath the waves**, carried across the [[The Central Sea]] by constant planar control of Water and Air.

## What It Conquered
The Awakening was not only a wonder but a weapon. It was the decisive edge that let the Empire finally subdue **[[An'Hiseret]]** — the ancient, deathless empire of the southern [[The Central Sea]], whose mastery of the **necromantic Weave** had kept it powerful for centuries. Planar Aetherweave overcame the magic of the deathless; the conquest of An'Hiseret is among the defining triumphs of the golden age.

## The Residuum of the Isle
**[[The Contested Isle]]** bleeds a powerful **Aether-heavy residuum** — raw Aether in concentrations found nowhere in the known world. To the Empire it promises Aetherweaving beyond anything achieved at home, and it is the true prize in the three-way race to colonise the Isle.

**(GM truth:** the residuum is not a mineral but the magic of a **dead [[Primordial Dragons|primordial dragon]]** still bleeding into the earth — see [[The Contested Isle]]. Harnessing a corpse-god's leaking power is a prime candidate for the seed of the [[Apostasy]].**)**

## Looking Forward (GM)
A civilisation this dependent on commanding planar forces is a civilisation with a long way to fall. This mastery is both the Empire's crown and, for the GM seeding the [[Apostasy]], its fault line.
