# Glacier Giants

The ancient enemy of the **[[Dwarven Clans|Dwarves of Athregal]]** — **giants of the northern glaciers**, pressing down out of the ice upon the Dwarven homeland of **[[Athregal]]**. Their long war with the Dwarves is one of the defining struggles of the far north, and one of the few forces that can make the feuding clans **unite**.
