# Athregali Pantheon

The gods of the **Dwarves of [[Athregal]]** — a **Norse** faith of forge, frost, war, and fate, sung in mead-halls and carved into stone. A hard pantheon for a hard people, shaped by the endless war against the **[[Glacier Giants|giants]]** of the ice.

> **Flavour:** ancestor-veneration, oaths sworn to gods, doom and fate (a *wyrd* the clans cannot escape), gods who are mighty but mortal-feeling and themselves at war with giants.

## Hooks (GM)
- The **giant-war** is mythic as well as military — the giants may be the gods' ancient enemies too, raising the stakes of the [[Glacier Giants|northern struggle]].
- Clan feuds may be partly religious: rival clans claiming different divine patrons or ancestral lines.
