# Elven Faith & the Fae

The faith of the **[[Elves]]** — older than any human pantheon, and unlike them all. The Elves do not worship a family of gods so much as keep **communion with the Fae**: the spirits, powers, and seasons of the **Otherworld** that lies alongside the waking world. **Brythonic-Celtic** in character — druidic, seasonal, oral, and bound to place.

> **Flavour:** standing stones and barrow-thresholds, druid-keepers, the turning of the year, oaths and bargains, the Otherworld a footstep away rather than a heaven above.

## The Fae
The **Fae** are the denizens of the Otherworld — not gods and not mortals, but powers of nature, glamour, and bargain: beautiful, perilous, and bound by their own laws of debt and courtesy. To the Elves they are kin, patrons, and danger all at once.

## The Faewilds (the Otherworld)
The **Faewilds** are the realm the Fae keep — a green, glamour-drenched world that touches ours at **thresholds**: ancient groves, standing stones, barrow-mounds, and the turning-points of the seasons. In this age the way between is **open and walked**; elven druids cross it, and the Fae come through.

## In This Age (GM)
- The bond is **alive and vital** now — central to elven magic and identity, and a power the [[The Armisian Empire|Empire]] neither controls nor understands.
- **Liminal echoes:** the time-bending **[[The Hesperine Isles]]** (a week ashore, four years in the logs) read exactly like a Faewilds threshold. Their ancient structures and burial sites may be elven or Fae work. *(Seed — connect or not, as you wish.)*
- A different magic entirely from **[[Aetherweave Mastery]]** — bargain and glamour, not planar command.
