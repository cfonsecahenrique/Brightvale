# An'Hiseretian Pantheon

The gods of **[[An'Hiseret]]** — an **ancient-Egyptian** faith of the river, the sun, and above all **death and the deathless**. This is the theology that let god-princes and high priests rule for centuries: in An'Hiseret, death is not an ending but a throne one can hold. Worship centres on the great river **[[The Seretis|Seretis]]** and the necromantic mysteries woven through it.

> **Flavour:** animal-headed gods, mortuary cults, divine kingship, the weighing of souls — and a uniquely literal traffic between the living and the dead.
