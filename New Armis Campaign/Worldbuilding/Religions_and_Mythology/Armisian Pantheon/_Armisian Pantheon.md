# Armisian Pantheon

The gods of **[[The Armisian Empire]]** — a **Bronze Age Mediterranean** faith in the **Phoenician-Hellenic** mould: a family of powerful, flawed, very human gods presiding over sea, sky, craft, trade, and war, worshipped through civic temples, festivals, and seafarers' rites.

> **Flavour:** Olympian-style anthropomorphic gods; city-patron cults; oracles; sacrifice before a voyage. The sea-gods loom largest in a thalassocracy.
