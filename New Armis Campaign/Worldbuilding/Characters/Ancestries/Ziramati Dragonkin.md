# Ziramati Dragonkin

The people of **[[Ziramat]]** — a **dragon-folk** (dragonborn) race native to the crimson deserts south of the **[[The Azurian Ocean]]**. The **[[The Armisian Empire|Empire]]** has only just made contact, and knows them barely at all. *(Mechanically: Daggerheart **Drakona** ancestry.)*

## What Little Is Known
- **A dragon people.** Scaled, draconic kin who carved their cliff-cities into the red rock and sandstone of [[Ziramat]]. Their culture reads as **Akkadian-Persian** beneath a heavy draconic overlay.
- **Brood of the Dragon-Queen.** They are reckoned the brood and lesser kin of Ziramat's ruler, **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]**, an **Ancient Red Dragon** god-queen (see [[Ziramat]]). Their society is **matriarchal** in her image — descent and authority pass through the female line.
- **Strange Aetherweaving.** They practise forms of the Weave **unlike the Armisians' planar [[Aetherweave Mastery]]** — methods the Empire's scholars cannot yet account for. What *powers* their weaving is an open and pressing question (the Dragon-Queen [[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]? draconic blood? the red desert itself?).

## The Imperial Question (GM / [[Plot Hooks]])
Armisian interest is not friendly curiosity. The standing directive is threefold:
1. **Learn** who the Dragonkin are and the source of their power.
2. **Understand** their strange Aetherweaving.
3. **Find how to make them weaker** — a frank intelligence-and-conquest agenda. *(See hook #3 in [[Plot Hooks]].)*

## Hooks (GM)
- Decide what *truly* powers Ziramati weaving — draconic blood, the red desert itself, something beneath their cliff-cities? Make the answer something the Empire would kill to control.
