# Elves

The **elder people** of the world — the great civilisation that flourished **before humankind emerged**, and the first to raise cities, sing law, and walk the threshold of the **[[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Fae]]**. By the Armisian golden age they are a conquered people: **subjugated and assimilated** into the **[[The Armisian Empire]]**, their elder glory folded into a younger empire's story. *(Mechanically: Daggerheart **Elf** ancestry.)*

> **Cultural inspiration (GM):** **ancient Celtic**, specifically **Brythonic** — tribal and clannish, druidic and oral rather than imperial and written; raisers of standing stones and barrows; bound to the seasons and to the Otherworld.

## The Elder Age
Long before Armis was founded, the world was the Elves'. Theirs was the first great culture, and the deep ruins, megaliths, and barrow-mounds scattered across the land are the bones of it. Then **humankind rose** — numerous, restless, and quick — and over ages the Elves were overtaken, then conquered, then absorbed.

## Under the Empire
- **Subjugated and assimilated.** Elves live as subjects of the Empire, their tongue and rites surviving in the seams of a human world.
- **The northeastern reaches.** They remain most numerous in the **north-eastern reaches** of the Empire — older, greener, more thinly-Romanised country, where the standing stones still stand and the old ways are kept.

## The Fae Bond (still living)
The Elves' defining trait is their **connection to the [[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Fae and the Faewilds]]** — the Otherworld of spirits and seasons. In this age that bond is **still open and vital**, a living wellspring of elven magic and identity. *(GM: a striking contrast with later ages — treasure it; its fate is a deep thread.)*

## Hooks (GM)
- A proud elder people under a young empire: assimilation, resentment, lost sovereignty, and keepers of secrets older than Armis.
- Elven druids and Fae-touched are the Empire's quiet rivals to the [[Aetherweave Mastery|Aetherweavers]] — a wholly different magic.
