# Dwarven Clans

The **Dwarves of [[Athregal]]** — a people only **recently** known to **[[The Armisian Empire]]**. First contact came at **[[Zergas-by-Azure]]**, on the **[[The Titan's Sword]]**, where both cultures reached to colonise the western frontier and met as rivals. The early encounters were **warlike**; diplomacy has since produced an **uneasy collaboration and trade**.

## Overview
- **New neighbours.** Until recently, the Empire and the Dwarves of [[Athregal]] had no contact at all.
- **From war to trade.** Bellic first contact at [[Zergas-by-Azure]] gave way to cautious treaties and commerce.
- **Settling in.** Only now are Dwarven communities appearing within Empire cities — most visibly in **[[Pattis]]** and **[[Armis]]**.
- **A people forged in war.** Back home in [[Athregal]], the clans wage an endless struggle against the **[[Glacier Giants|giants]]** of the northern glaciers — a hard, cold war that shapes Dwarven character and politics alike.

## Hooks (GM)
- The relationship is fragile: old grievances, mismatched customs, and competing colonial claims could reignite conflict.
- Dwarven craft and goods are a new luxury — and a new dependency — for Armisian society.
