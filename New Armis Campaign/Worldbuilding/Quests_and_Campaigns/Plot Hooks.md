# Plot Hooks — The Imperial Agenda

The driving engines of the campaign. Most are expressions of the **[[The Armisian Empire|Armisian]]** agenda of **expansion and domination** at the height of its golden age — the Empire reaching outward in every direction, certain of its right to do so. Player venturers are the instruments (witting or not) of that reach.

> The campaign present is **994 AF**. See [[Timeline (Armis)]] and [[The Known World]].

## The Frontiers of the Map
**1. South of [[Ziramat]] — what lies there?**
Beyond the crimson dragon-empire, the map runs blank. The Empire wants it filled — and claimed.

**2. West of the [[The Azurian Ocean|Azure]] — what lies there?**
Past the open ocean and **[[The Contested Isle]]** lie the **[[The Hesperine Isles]]**, the westernmost known place — a mystical archipelago whose own expeditions return with contradictory accounts of time, inhabitants, even location. What lies *beyond* them, no Armisian ship has charted. The far west is the great unknown, and the great temptation.

## The Rival Powers
**3. The Dragonkin of [[Ziramat]].**
The **[[Ziramati Dragonkin]]** practise strange Aetherweaving unlike the Empire's own. The directive: learn who they are, discover **what powers their weaving**, and then — **find how to make them weaker**. Intelligence in service of conquest — though the honest answer to "weaken them" may be their **living god-queen** herself (see **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]**), a task no Armisian yet grasps the scale of.

**4. The source of the [[The Seretis|Seretis]].**
[[An'Hiseret]]'s life is its river. There is talk of **unknown civilisations upriver**. Where does the Seretis begin, and who lives at its source? The Empire means to find out before anyone else does.

**5. The divided Dwarves of [[Athregal]].**
The Dwarves are **bellicose and fractured**, their clans at each other's throats — yet when they unite, they wreck Armisian plans. Send envoys upriver of their politics: **back rival clans against the ruling ones** to keep Athregal too divided to ever stand as one.

## Cracks Within and Threats Without
**6. The intrigue of [[Pattis]] and [[Armis]]–[[Caen]].**
The halls of the capital and the moving city seethe with politics. Some hold that the **Pattisians are a separate people** who would **secede** from the greatest empire of any age. A fracture at the Empire's heart.

**7. The pirates of [[Borkum Reef]].**
Two days west of the [[The Titan's Sword]]'s tip, deserters and outcasts are hardening into a real pirate faction — the **[[The Reef-Born]]** — astride the western sea-lanes. They **must be dealt with**: crushed, infiltrated, or turned against themselves.

## The Powers Older Than the Empire
*Late additions — threads older than Armis, and beyond its command.*

**8. [[The Contested Isle]] — the prize beneath everything.**
Three empires race for one island: Armisian *Adamantis*, Ziramati *Tiâmtu*, Athregali *Reiðrey*. Its **[[Aetherweave Mastery|Aether-heavy residuum]]** promises weaving beyond anything achieved at home, and the Empire means to hold it first. *(GM truth: the residuum is the leaking magic of a **dead [[Primordial Dragons|primordial dragon]]**, and the likeliest seed of the [[Apostasy]].)*

**9. The [[Elves]] and the Fae they keep.**
The conquered **elder people** still hold an open road to the **[[_Elven Faith and the Fae|Faewilds]]** — a magic of bargain and glamour the Empire neither controls nor understands, and a quiet rival to its own Aetherweavers. Subjects with a power the throne cannot command are subjects worth watching — and the liminal **[[The Hesperine Isles]]** may be a Fae threshold by another name.

**10. The three lost dragons.**
Ziramati myth names **[[Tiamat|five primordial dragons]]**; **[[Azhdashar|Azhdašar]]** rules [[Ziramat]] and one lies dead beneath the Isle. **Three** world-shaping powers remain unaccounted for — sleeping, ruling, or fallen somewhere on the very map the Empire is so determined to fill.
