 The main inspiration of the Armisian Empire is the Hellenic golden age, mixed with ancient Phoenician empire. It's a sea-faring, exploring, bright and vibrant culture, sponsoring many new-world discovering expeditions. 

Every year, ships arrive from the distant west, bringing exotic artefacts, animals and riches. The capital of the empire is the now-merging twin cities of Armis and its subjugated Caen. 

But many city states maintain their independence, namely Pattis, the western-most city of the Empire. It is from Pattis that most seafaring expeditions begin. Partis is an island-metropolis, in the large Azurian Ocean, inspired in Numenor and Atlantis.

Dwarven clans have recently started to make contact with the Armisian Empire. This contact started when both cultures met in western reaches of the continent, both keen on colonizing. Although the first contacts were bellic in nature, recent diplomatic attempts have set an uneasy collaboration and trading relationship. Only recently have Dwarven communities started to become present in Pattis and Armis.
