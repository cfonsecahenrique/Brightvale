# An Age of Azure and Gold
### An introduction to the Armisian Empire

It is the **994th year of the Empire**, and the world has never been brighter.

From the white harbours of the **Central Sea**, the banners of **Armis** fly over the greatest civilisation the world has ever known — a culture of merchants, philosophers, explorers and shipwrights, golden and restless and sure of itself. Every year, fleets sail out past the horizon and return laden with the marvels of distant shores: strange beasts, stranger artefacts, and riches enough to make poets of accountants. The twin capital of **Armis–Caen** glitters. The wandering island-city of **Pattis**, half above the waves and half beneath them, drifts the western waters under a sky it commands. This is the age everyone will one day remember as the height of the world.

It was not always so. Two and a half centuries ago, the Empire's arcanists achieved the **Aetherweave Awakening** — mastery over the deep, strong currents of planar magic. With command of the **Water and Air**, Armisian ships made a highway of the impassable ocean; with that same power, the Empire broke the ancient deathless realm of **An'Hiseret**, whose god-princes had ruled the southern coasts for centuries. The Awakening did not merely enrich the Empire. It made it certain that the world was *theirs to take*.

And so they are taking it. Beyond the great landmass of the **Titan's Sword** lies the open **Azurian Ocean**, and across it, the edges of the map are filling fast: the warlike Dwarven clans of **Athregal** to the north; the crimson, cliff-carved dragon-empire of **Ziramat** to the south. Three proud powers — and between them, ringed by all their landings, the **Contested Isle**: a vast, unexplored island that bleeds raw Aether and *fights back*, its elusive natives moving in time with powers older than any empire. Everyone wants what the Isle holds. No one yet understands what it is.

This is where you come in.

You are **venturers** — sent west by the Empire's hunger, into waters no chart describes and onto a shore that does not want you. You will map the unmappable, treat with peoples your masters mean to conquer, and reach for a power your masters do not comprehend. The Empire's agenda is plain enough: *learn everything, claim everything, divide every rival who might stand in the way.* Whether you serve that agenda, subvert it, or merely survive it is yours to decide.

There is no shortage of work for those willing to sail. Among the designs in motion:

- **The Contested Isle** — break into the interior of the island that fights back, and claim its raw Aether before the rivals do.
- **The Dragonkin of Ziramat** — learn the secrets of their strange weaving, and how to turn it against them.
- **The Source of the Seretis** — follow An'Hiseret's sacred river inland, to the unknown civilisations at its head.
- **The Divided Dwarves** — keep Athregal at war with itself, lest its clans ever unite against the Empire.
- **The Fracture at Home** — smother the whispers of secession in the halls of Pattis and Armis–Caen.
- **The Reef-Born** — deal with the pirates gathering on Borkum Reef, astride the western trade.
- **The Edges of the Map** — chart what lies south of Ziramat, and west of the Azure, where no Armisian ship has returned with answers.

> A note for the table: this is a story of **discovery, wonder, and ambition** — a golden age in full bloom. But golden ages end. There is something at the heart of this world's brilliance that will, in time, undo it. The characters cannot see that ending. You, perhaps, can feel it coming.

The sails are full. The horizon is open. **Where will you go first?**
