# Campaign Primer

# Brightvale, A West Marches Campaign

This primer outlines the core mechanics and known lore of the Brightvale campaign, functioning as a starting point for a collaborative worldbuilding process. The setting, based on Matt Mercer's Age of [[Umbra]] campaign frame, is intentionally designed with gaps that will be populated through player-driven character creation. Your choices regarding background, social ties, and personal history will be used to establish NPCs, events, and the campaign's political landscape, religious structures, and organizational factions.

The aim is that the complexity of the world—including its rumors, key NPCs, and historical events—is the fruit of the characters you build, making the setting a truly emergent storytelling narrative. While the setting is system-agnostic, it is primarily designed around the Daggerheart TTRPG system.

It draws inspiration from the lore of Dark Souls, Elden Ring, and Enshrouded; the Forgotten Realms and Dark Sun settings; Attack on Titan and Destiny; the magic of Frieren: Beyond Journey's End; the dungeon delving of Dungeon Meshi; and the grit of The Witcher and Curse of Strahd.
