## Player characters:
### Zorael Lugner
Cousin of Varis Lugner. Tiefling sorceress. Rocky relationship with Varis, the House Heir. She excelled in the Aetherweaver academy as one of the Instinctuals -- old magic wielders that visualize magic into existence. Became a venturer for her innate and expert destructive weaving abilities, and became known as a Horde razer: her spells rained wide destruction on multiple targets, often clearing a path through hordes. She is impatient, and often seen as cold.
### Theodor Stjormund
Dwarven Guardian. Second son of Storvald Stjormund, was chosen by Hvergi to be sent for this prestigious expedition. On one hand, because he was expendable, being a second son. On the other hand, because of his vast experience with the Umbra and the Wastes, being a renowned warrior. Was presented with a Hjalrim Crystal Gauntlet, made with Sigr crystal, as a gesture of goodwill from the other two dwarven clans. Brash, always happy to fight and throw hands.
### Yuriko Ruinweaver
Yuriko is an Orcen Seraph. Grew up in the Beacon's religious order, away from Orcen nomadic tradition, to be a Paladin of the Beacon's Flame. She was eventually expelled from the Beacon, due to her questions about the Oracle Cycle and the Sealed Chamber, the ruin she weaved herself. Became a very successful freelancing Venturer, and discovered her divine wings and powers somehow remained. It is unknown which political machinations led to Yuriko being picked for this expedition, besides her fighting capabilities.
### Cillian
Old bearded Elvish druid -- one of the oldest persons in Brightvale. Older memories corrupted and emptied by the centuries and the umbra. Vast experience in surviving the Umbra, even alone. Often seen as eccentric and crazy, and often seen roaming around Brightvale wildshaped as an animal. Sent by members of the Concordance.

## Events
Party met an old caretaker, Aldred, selected to accompany the party. He had little experience in the outside besides a couple of Ventures. He was seen suspiciously by the party members.

The trek down the Venturer's Trail happened without problems.
In the Umbra-bog below, they started being stalked by monsters, eventually fighting them.
After a full day travelling in the mist, they arrived an ancient temple at the top of a hill.
The ruined chapel-temple was dedicated to some ancient forgotten beautiful goddess.
Aldred mentions that he's seen the Goddess's face in dreams, as well as the interior of the chapel.
He then lit an ashgeld fire in the centre brazier, which started a blue fire and a communication channel with the sealed oracles, who revealed to the party the true nature of the Beacon and that the caretakers have been lying to them for centuries.
Unfortunately, an Umbra-touched angel descended from the rafters, killed Aldred and attempted to kill all others, while the Umbra hordes outside fought to enter the chapel.
Theodor and Cillian fought to let Zorael and Yuriko flee, giving up their lives.
Upon Zorael and Yuriko's return to Brightvale, they were greeted coldly by Emberguards and Caretakers, who deemed their mission a failure: they return empty-handed and with heavy casualties. Besides that, they bent protocol -- to quarantine and evaluate Umbra-corruption on returning Venturers -- to question and incarcerate the survivors for a few months.
Meanwhile, word spread out about the failed expedition, disseminating an carefully planted lie: that the survivors returned insane, and their minds and memories corrupted by the Umbra horrors they faced.

## What is known of this famous expedition
4 popular and capable heroes were selected to find an artefact that would boost the Beacon's light, severely needed due to its late flickerings.
The expedition ended in failure, with the loss of a Senior Caretaker (Aldred Gwyn), Storvald's Stjormund's second son Theodor, and the old elf Cillian the Old. Zorael Lugner and Yuriko Ruinweaver returned injured and with their minds heavily corrupted by the Umbra, spouting insane things typical of minds heavily claimed by the Umbra. The Caretakers managed to save their minds through quarantine and holy treatment -- making them somewhat functional members of society. Despite this, they were barred from participating in future expeditions due to the weakened state of their minds.
Within the common populace, no one knows what truly happened in that expedition. But many rumours are whispered: that they found an ancient pre-apostasy artefact that broke their minds, that the Caretakers planned an impossible expedition to get rid of certain people -- Cillian was a walking compendium of Pre-Apostasy and Concordance history, Theodor was the most ambitious and charismatic figure of Hvergi (feared that he could unite the dwarven clans), Yuriko was expelled from the Caretaker society early in her life, and Zorael was too good of an Aetherweaver, uninspired by the Beacon's light.
However, the most hidden of these rumours was the one that in 13 years generated the greatest following: that they found ancient divinity, rivalling that of the Beacon. Ever since then, expeditions of careful and curious Venturers highlighted multiple temples of a Forgotten Goddess, beautiful, eerie, serene and austere, but kind. This information is kept by the Explorer's Guild, much to the displeasure of the Caretakers. Concordance members and even Aetherweavers rival the Caretakers on this matter. Some Venturers have started wearing symbols or even ink in their bodies related to symbology found in the Goddess's temples. However, Caretakers call this heresy -- creating a fracturing tension in Brightvale.
