# The Call of the Wilds

The [[Venturers]] exist because [[Brightvale]] cannot survive in isolation. Given recent turns of events, to stay inside is to slowly starve; to go outside is to risk a fate worse than death. Expeditions are not executed by the Vanguard — [[Slayers]], [[Emberguard]], and [[Explorers' Guild|Explorers]] — alone, but are often accompanied by members of the [[Aetherweaver Academy|Aetherweave]] and/or [[Caretakers of the Beacon|Light Bearers]], bringing support from arcane and divine magic and drastically enhancing their survivability rates.

## Task Boards

The [[Explorers' Guild]], with the approval of the [[Order in the Vale|Council]], sets boards of tasks to be taken by groups of Venturers, who among themselves decide the composition of their adventuring parties. Large parties bring manpower but attract more attention and more complex survival logistics, so the [[Explorers' Guild]] sets the optimal number at **4–6 members** for the completion of announced tasks.

## Expedition Structure

Venturing parties are briefed on their tasks before leaving, but most expeditions maintain a similar structure:

1. **Explore** — move through the target area and assess conditions
2. **Map** — record the terrain, hazards, and points of interest as accurately as possible
3. **Complete Task** — fulfill the assigned objective
4. **Return** — withdraw to [[Emberfort]] or the nearest checkpoint
5. **Report** — submit maps and findings to the [[Explorers' Guild]]

It is the responsibility of the venturing party to map their exploration as accurately as possible. Upon returning, their maps and reports are incorporated into the [[Explorers' Guild]]'s master cartography, forming the foundation for all future expeditions.

## Checkpoints

Venturing parties can rest and resupply at **Checkpoints** — outposts deep in the [[Umbra]]-choked lowlands, carefully maintained by a permanent garrison of [[Emberguard]], [[Slayers]], [[Aetherweaver Academy|Aetherweavers]], and [[Caretakers of the Beacon]]. Identifying and establishing new safe havens to expand the checkpoint network is itself a standing priority, as a stronger logistical network directly increases the range and effectiveness of all Venturing operations.
