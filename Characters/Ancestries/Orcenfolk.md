# [[Orcenfolk]]

### [[Orcenfolk|Orcs]] (The Nomadic Senshi)

**Orcish-borne** are a blend of nomadic **Mongolian** traditions and disciplined **Senshi** warrior-culture. They grow restless with sedentary life; their yearly feasts revolve around the **Great Relocation**, where they move their settlements across the valley. They maintain **Horse Archery** as a high artistic tradition, though it is largely obsolete in the swampy, tree-ridden lowlands outside the Vale. They primarily station near **[[Emberfort]]**.

**Naming Traditions (Japanese/Korean):** *Musha, Ryuujin, Orochi, Kasai, Kenshi, Haru, Rin, Hana, Hama, Goro, Goryo, Sakae, Nari, Jin, Nantai.*
