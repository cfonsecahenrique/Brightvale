# [[Orcenfolk]]

### [[Orcenfolk|Orcs]] (The Nomadic Senshi)

**Orcish-borne** are a blend of nomadic **Mongolian** traditions and disciplined **Senshi** warrior-culture. While they possess deep equestrian roots, horses are incredibly rare and expensive in Brightvale, as only a small number survived the Apostasy and the trek up the Venturer's Trail. As a result, the Orcish community stewards what few horses remain, treating them as priceless assets rather than beasts of burden. Through the predominantly Orcish **[[House Kaze]]**, they operate Brightvale's vital high-speed messaging relay. In battle, they primarily station near **[[Emberfort]]**.

**Naming Traditions (Japanese/Korean):** *Musha, Ryuujin, Orochi, Kasai, Kenshi, Haru, Rin, Hana, Hama, Goro, Goryo, Sakae, Nari, Jin, Nantai.*
