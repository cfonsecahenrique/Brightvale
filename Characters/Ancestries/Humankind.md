# [[Humankind]]

### [[Humankind|Humans]] (Heirs of the @Armisian Empire)

The most numerous and hierarchical. [[Humankind|Humans]] obsess over the **Noble Houses** and the lost glory of the **Armisian Empire**. Nobles maintain a feudal system of fiefs and palazzos, though their short lifespans drives the less blood-obsessed ones toward the "Mathematical Arcanum" to achieve in decades what [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]] do in centuries.

**Naming Traditions (Central European):** *Kaleb, Florian, Lucien, Petyr, Liana, Jacek, Storme, Dawn, Lilianne, Aelfred, Florenz, Harald, Narcissa, Nidas, Essek, Matthias, Wyll, Aelyn, Kobyl, Dawn, Eldris, Reinhardt.*

**Less noble names** *Azi, Nayar, Thyazi, Thalia, Shadia, Kari, Neera.*
