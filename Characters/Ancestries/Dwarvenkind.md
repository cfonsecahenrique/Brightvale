# [[Dwarvenkind]]

### (The Rune-Keepers)

**Dwarvenborne** culture is heavily **Norse-inspired**. Descendants of seafaring communities, they remember a "Great Sea" through inscribed stones in **[[Hvergi]]**. They see the Vale not as a prison, but as a shore they must hold until the tides of shadow recede.

**Naming Traditions (Norse):** *Storvald, Hjalrim, Merlos, [[Hvergi]], Brumdain, Dain, Thormor, Tyr, Uthin, Brigitte, Dyanna, Freyr., Eivor*
