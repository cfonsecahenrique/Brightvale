# [[Elves (Faefolk)]]

**Elvenborne** are split into two cultures. The majority are **Druidic [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]]**, living in hidden glades where they seek to reconnect with the **[[Fae]]**. They view the urban world as a distraction from the true tragedy of the [[Apostasy]]. **Urban [[Elves (Faefolk)|Elves]]** live in [[Medsen]], often married into the High Houses or serving as high-ranking academics at [[Aurore]]’s Peak.

**Naming Traditions (Celtic):** *Cillian, Ciara, Eira, Maeve, Aodhán, Faolan, Fíadh, Elowen, Enriel, Cirdan, Hwynfer, *Taliesin*.*
