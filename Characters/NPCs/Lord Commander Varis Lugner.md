
**Role:** Lord Commander of the **[[Emberguard]]**
**Ancestry:** [[Infernis_Tieflings|Infernis]] (Blue [[Infernis_Tieflings|Tiefling]])
**Affiliation:** **[[House Lugner]]** (estranged)

### Appearance
Varis is a striking figure, standing tall with the light gray-bluish skin characteristic of the **Azure Stain** in the [[House Lugner|Lugner]] bloodline — one of the visible marks of the ancestral Vile-Pact. He possesses a pair of sweeping, matte-black horns that curve back from his brow, often etched with defensive runes of his own design. His eyes are a piercing, pale gold, contributing to what field soldiers call his **Iron-Gaze**. He is almost always armored in heavy, rune-etched plate that hums in faint cadence with the Brightvale Beacon — a resonance he learned to induce through the [[Emberguard]]'s Ashen Oath ritual, not through house instruction.

### Personality
A man of few words and absolute discipline. Where [[House Lugner]] produces scholars who deliberate endlessly before acting, Varis decides and moves. He is pragmatic to the point of austerity — the strict enforcement of ration policies, the refusal to debate operational security with civilian nobles, the preference for [[Emberfort]]'s ramparts over [[Medsen]]'s courts. He does not view his service as glorious. He views it as necessary. There is a difference, and he finds it important.

Despite his demonic heritage — or perhaps because of it — he is a fervent believer in the **Sanctity of Light**. He has spoken, on rare occasions, of his service as a personal penance for the Vile-Pact. The Pale Sanctum considers this dramatic. Varis has not been inside the Pale Sanctum since he was nineteen.

### Background
Varis was identified young as a potential arcanist of the Old Magic sect, the expected trajectory for a Lugner of his generation. He abandoned this path in his youth to enlist in the [[Emberguard]], an act the Pale Sanctum has never publicly acknowledged and has never forgiven. His reasons were his own, and he has not elaborated on them.

He rose through the Guard's ranks during the brutal **Crimson-Gloom** incursions — large-scale [[Umbra]] surges that tested the northern passes for nearly three years. His tactical coordination with [[House Kaewdyn]] field officers during the third incursion is credited with preventing a breach that would have reached [[Medsen]]. The Lord Commander position was offered to him over several candidates with stronger noble connections, because nobody else had his record.

He views the growing commercial ambitions of the Recent houses with quiet suspicion, believing that any softening of the Vale's defensive posture will be visible to whatever is building pressure in the Wastes before the Council notices it themselves.

### The House Question
The relationship between Varis and [[House Lugner]] is glacially cold and carefully managed by both parties. The house does not claim him publicly. He does not disavow his lineage. They communicate through intermediaries when absolutely necessary. The Pale Sanctum is aware that Varis is one of the most famous people in the Vale, and that publicly repudiating him would invite questions they do not wish to answer.

Varis is aware that the Pale Sanctum's deepest research involves artifacts and formulae tied to the same Vile-Pact that produced his Azure Stain. He has not yet decided what to do about this knowledge.
