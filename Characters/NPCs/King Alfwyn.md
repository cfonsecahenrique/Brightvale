# [[King Alfwyn]]

**Role:** Sovereign of **Brightvale** (Sun-Born King)
**Ancestry:** [[Humankind|Human]] (claimed Dawnfather lineage)
**Status:** Recluse (The "Silent Sovereign")

### Appearance
No subject has seen the King’s face in seven years. Those who remember him from his coronation describe a man of pale, luminous skin and eyes the color of molten gold. Nowadays, he is a ghost in his own palace, living within the **Central Temple-Castle** built directly over the Beacon's heart.

### Personality & Current State
Once known for a quiet, contemplative nature, Alfwyn has fully withdrawn from the world. He communicates solely through written decrees or the words of his **High Steward**. 

The prevailing theories on his state are:
1.  **Resonance Sickness:** Prolonged exposure to the Beacon's core has physically drained him, leaving him unable to survive outside the castle's specialized shielding.
2.  **The Vigil:** He is engaged in a constant, hidden ritual to stabilize the flickering Beacon, sacrificing his life force to keep the Vale safe.
3.  **Profound Grief/Fear:** He has seen something in the depths of the Beacon — perhaps the true nature of the Dawnfather's sacrifice — that has shattered his will to lead.

### Governance
He occupies a symbolic seat on the **Council of Five**, but never attends. His **High Steward** acts as his proxy, managing the administrative tasks of [[the monarchy]] and ensuring the "Sun-Born" mythos continues to provide a sense of stability to the populace.
