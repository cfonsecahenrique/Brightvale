**Role**:** Grand Explorer — head of the **[[Explorers' Guild]]**
**Ancestry:** [[Humankind|Human]]
**Status:** Humanity-Loss Gambler

### Appearance
[[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] has the look of a woman who has spent too much time in the wind and shadow. Her skin is toughened by mountain air, and her eyes have a constant, restless quality, always scanning the horizon. She is often covered in dust and grease from her climbing gear and artifact-extraction tools. She wears a patchwork of armor and utility belts, each pouch containing something essential for survival in the Wastes.

### Personality
Reckless, charismatic, and profoundly fatalistic. [[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] is the kind of leader who inspires loyalty through sheer audacity. She is a woman of action who finds the political maneuvering of [[Medsen]]'s Noble Houses to be a tedious waste of time. She uses dark humor to mask the profound loss she has suffered, often making light of the memories she has sacrificed to the [[Umbra]].

### "Humanity-Loss" and Memory Bargains
[[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] is a veteran of the "Memory-Bargains"—a forbidden or perhaps just desperate practice known to some experienced Venturers. To survive the mental pressure of the most [[Umbra]]-dense regions, she has "gambled" pieces of her soul. She has traded away deep, personal memories to create a buffer against Hollowing. Most notably, she can no longer remember the face of her own child—a sacrifice she made to navigate her team out of a collapsing spell-scar. She views these losses as a necessary cost of her duty ("The Price of Pathfinding").

### Ambitions
[[Grand Explorer Mira Ravenna]] is a loud voice on the **Council of Five** (representing the interessi of the Venturers). She is an "Aggressionist," believing that Brightvale's best chance for long-term survival is to retake the Old World ruins and the knowledge they contain, rather than simply hiding behind the Beacon's light.
