
**Role:** First Slayer — head of the **[[Slayers]]** guild
**Ancestry:** [[Orcenfolk|Orc]]
**Epithet:** Slayer of Rotbloods

### Appearance

Makjar is a broad-shouldered, unhurried presence — the kind of person a room adjusts to rather than the reverse. She is tall even by [[Orcenfolk|Orc]] standards, with dark olive-green skin weathered by years in the Wastes, and she carries her body with the deliberate economy of someone who has learned not to waste movement. The scar where her left eye was is smooth and old, worn openly. She has never replaced it. She does not explain why, and no one who knows her asks.

Her gear is well-maintained to the point of ceremony — she treats her weapons the way a musician treats an instrument.

### Personality

Makjar is difficult to know and not trying to be easy. She has little tolerance for ceremony, political maneuvering, or the performance of authority. She is direct, sometimes bluntly so, and expects those around her to mean what they say.

Beneath this, she is a philosopher in the most practical sense — someone whose ideas emerge not from study but from the accumulated weight of what she has survived. Her belief that **artistic expression is the only thing separating a warrior from a hollow beast** is not a poetic flourish. It is the conclusion she arrived at after watching what happens to soldiers who fight without it.

### The Verse of the Blade

Makjar is a master practitioner of the **"Verse of the Blade"** — a philosophical tradition deeply rooted in her **[[Orcenfolk]]** heritage. It approaches combat as a form of personal expression where each technique is chosen, not merely executed.

As First Slayer, she champions this tradition within the guild, encouraging all initiates to maintain some form of non-martial creative practice — verse, carving, music, or calligraphy — as a parallel discipline. While not all [[Slayers]] adopt the full Orcish philosophy, her influence is felt across the guild. *"You cannot express what you have not bothered to feel,"* she explains. *"And a warrior who cannot express themselves in the fight has already started to Hollow."*


### Background

Makjar rose through the Slayer ranks over a long career in the Wastes. Her defining engagement — the one that gave her both her epithet and her scar — was a three-day pursuit and kill of a **Rotblood** deep in the lowland ruins, which she completed alone after the rest of her unit was lost. She has never spoken in detail about those three days.

She was named First Slayer by guild vote, not appointment — a distinction that matters to her considerably.
